# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Monthly Report (January 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In January 2026, the `superchain-registry` focused on maturing its operational tooling and streamlining the integration process for new Layer 2 chains. A major milestone was achieved in enhancing the "add chain" functionality to support embedded contract artifacts, significantly improving the robustness of the registry's onboarding pipeline. The month was also characterized by a substantial "spring cleaning" of the repository, where dozens of long-standing issues related to configuration management, validation consistency, and developer experience were resolved, ensuring the registry remains a reliable source of truth for the expanding Superchain ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Enhanced Chain Onboarding & Configuration
The primary technical advancement this month centered on improving how new chains are integrated into the registry.
*   **Embedded Contract Artifacts:** The `add-chain` operation was upgraded to support embedded contract artifacts. This change impacts various internal `ops` components, including deployer configurations and reporting tools, allowing for more flexible chain integrations ([#1172](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1172)).
*   **Granular Address Mapping:** To improve readability and organization, the `[addresses]` key in configuration files was refactored into more granular roles and protocol sections ([#437](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/437)).
*   **Registry Consolidation:** The registry moved toward a more unified data structure by exporting a single mapping instead of three separate ones ([#391](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/391)) and adopting a consistent identifier for all OP chains ([#381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/381)).

### Tooling & CI Improvements
Significant effort was directed toward the developer experience and automated maintenance.
*   **New CLI Commands:** The registry's utility was expanded with the addition of `remove-chain` and `clean-bytecodes` commands ([#341](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/341)).
*   **Automated Maintenance:** A new CI job was implemented to automatically tidy configuration files, ensuring long-term repository health ([#303](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/303)).
*   **Validation Refinement:** Validation logic was strengthened by adding default timeouts for tests ([#413](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/413)) and refactoring the `readAddressesFromChain` function ([#427](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/427)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
The team successfully cleared a backlog of technical debt and feature requests:
*   **OP Stack Compatibility:** Resolved inquiries regarding minimal Solidity contract compatibility, confirming standard Ethereum smart contract support while highlighting L1/L2 differences ([#18709](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18709)).
*   **Fault Proofs & Hardforks:** Addressed validation checks for fault proof game prestates to accept any `op-program` release ([#706](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/706)) and finalized the strategy for storing hardfork times ([#751](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/751)).
*   **Infrastructure Scoping:** Several acceptance tests related to Flashblocks and Rollup-Boost (e.g., sequencer failover and kill-switches) were closed as they were determined to be outside the current scope of `op-acceptance-tests` due to required infrastructure manipulation ([#316](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/316), [#314](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/314)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **SyncStatus Logic:** A significant discussion is underway regarding the `Revise SyncStatus CurrentL1 Selection`. The community is converging on a fix that consolidates channels rather than adding new ones, referencing existing design documents for architectural alignment ([#18673](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18673)).
*   **Bug Reports:** Three new bug descriptions were logged early in the month ([#1190](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/1190), [#1191](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/1191), [#1192](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/1192)), which are currently awaiting further triage.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month showed a high level of maintainer activity focused on repository health and architectural consistency. Collaboration was particularly evident in the technical discussions surrounding `SyncStatus`, where contributors engaged in deep-dive analysis of L1 selection logic. The closure of various "good first issue" style tasks—such as Java bindings for OP Stack components ([#312](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/312)) and diff tool improvements ([#284](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/284))—indicates a healthy balance between core protocol work and ecosystem-facing developer tooling.
