# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Monthly Report (June 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
June was characterized by intensive debugging and stabilization of the interop stack, particularly focusing on synchronization reliability between `op-node`, `op-supernode`, and `op-reth`. Significant progress was made in validating the interop proof path on devnets, including the successful proving of withdrawals. However, the month also revealed critical edge cases in Consensus Layer (CL) sync recovery and transaction pool behavior following invalid interop messages, leading to the development of new recovery runbooks and observability metrics.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Registry Maintenance & Automated Tooling
The project maintained high configuration consistency through daily automated code generation.
*   **Automated Codegen Updates:** Multiple routine updates were performed to `addresses.json` within the `superchain/extra` directory to ensure the registry remains synchronized with the latest chain configurations ([#1250](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1250), [#1251](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1251), [#1252](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1252), [#1253](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1253)).

### Interop Proofs & Devnet Progress
Significant milestones were reached in testing the security and deployment of the Superchain interop features.
*   **Withdrawal Proof Validation:** A major milestone was achieved with the successful proving of a withdrawal on the Interop Proofs Devnet, confirming the validity of the withdrawal path in a forked environment ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20489)).
*   **Devnet Infrastructure:** Progress continued on establishing long-running devnets, moving from local environments toward stable `glamsterdam-devnet-4` configurations ([#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20691)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Infrastructure Refinement:** Resolved a devstack configuration error where the Execution Layer (EL) was incorrectly shared between the light node sequencer and the supernode ([#21158](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21158)).
*   **Operational Strategy:** The team decided to manage `op-conductor` recovery from interop reorgs manually via runbooks rather than implementing complex auto-recovery logic ([#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20006)).
*   **Security Audit:** Feedback from the Lumi Security Audit regarding `doc.go` was addressed and closed ([#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21124)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sync Deadlocks & Stalls:** A critical cyclic deadlock was identified in `reth`'s `StateTrieOverlayManager`, causing CL sync recovery to stall when referencing missing blocks. While two patches showed promise in testing, the issue has been escalated to the upstream `reth` team for a permanent fix ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21076)).
*   **Interop Transaction Pool Failures:** A new issue was identified where `op-reth` fails to resume accepting interop transactions after a failsafe is disabled, currently requiring a full restart of both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21149)).
*   **Protocol Safety:** Discussions are ongoing regarding adding protocol-level upper bounds for sequencer rebates to prevent potential DoS vectors, effectively capping computation even with 100% refunds ([#20709](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20709)).
*   **Testing Flakiness:** A new issue was opened to investigate hangs in the `op-e2e-fuzz` CI jobs, with diagnostics being added to bound RPC and engine calls ([#21191](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21191)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was heavily focused on cross-component debugging between the Optimism and Paradigm (`reth`) teams. The identification of the `rayon`-related deadlock in `reth` demonstrates deep technical coordination between the Layer 2 and Execution Client developers. Additionally, the community showed strong interest in the upcoming Amsterdam hardfork support in `revm` (v111), prompting the team to commit to more frequent dependency updates and improved documentation for staying in sync with upstream releases ([#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21069)).
