### `op-reth` Bootnode Configuration
The issue [#20453](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20453) regarding `op-reth` shipping zero default bootnodes for OP Stack chains, preventing discv5 bootstrapping without explicit `--bootnodes`, was closed. The team decided to implement a solution.

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test Investigation
Discussion continued on the flaky test `TestStorageProofUsingMultiStorageContract` [#19978](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19978). A new CI run was provided as evidence, showing the flake reoccurring on a chainspec-only change. The `op-reth-e2e-sysgo-tests` job has a ~9% flake rate, indicating the test fails on `develop` without PR involvement.

### `op-core` Extraction Progress
The issue [#20257](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20257), focused on decoupling the monorepo from `op-geth` to use upstream `go-ethereum`, saw a comment suggesting the inclusion of `PostExecTx` alongside `DepositTx` in the extraction process.

### Missing Events on `eth_getLogs`
The long-standing issue [#667](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/667) concerning missing events on `eth_getLogs` calls immediately after `newHeads` and occasional high latency received a new comment. The comment suggested that the issue might be related to backend freshness or propagation lag rather than `eth_getLogs` itself. It recommended verifying if websocket subscriptions and RPC calls are pinned to the same node and comparing block data before and after delays.
