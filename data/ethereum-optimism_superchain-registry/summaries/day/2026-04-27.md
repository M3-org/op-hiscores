## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestReorgUnsafeHead`
The issue [#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20198) regarding the flaky `TestReorgUnsafeHead` test continues to be active. A recent comment from pauldowman on April 27, 2026, indicates that the proposed fix in `ethereum-optimism/optimism#20201` does not fully resolve the flakiness, as the test recurred on PR #20250. The test fails with an assertion that "Expected to get different heads on divergence block number, but got the same hash, so no reorg happened on chain A." The root cause, as identified by ajsutton on April 21, 2026, is the asynchronous update of `StatusTracker.data.UnsafeL2` and `Sequencer.latestHead` compared to the synchronous EL updates, leading to stale data being used. The proposed fix involves replacing `time.Sleep` with a poll on `SyncStatus().UnsafeL2.Hash`.

### LightCL `safe_l2`/`local_safe_l2`/`finalized_l2` Zero on Fresh-Genesis Boot
Issue [#20295](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20295) describes an observation where `safe_l2`, `finalized_l2`, and `local_safe_l2` are reported as zero on a fresh-genesis OP chain when one op-node is configured with `--l2.follow.source` and another as the sequencer. karlfloersch commented on April 27, 2026, suggesting that the likely root cause is that genesis `L2BlockRef`s are being treated as unset state because several follow-source/status paths compare only `Number`. This leads to valid upstream genesis refs failing "is this ahead?" checks. A related sync-status case also fails for genesis. The recommended fix is to distinguish "unset" from "genesis" using a full zero-value check/block identity, not just block number, and to add a regression test for this scenario. It was also questioned if `ethereum-optimism/optimism#20352` fixes this, but it appears not to cover this specific genesis/follow-source case.
