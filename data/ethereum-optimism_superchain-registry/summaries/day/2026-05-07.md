## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona and op-node Brotli Channel Disagreement ([#19333](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19333))
Discussion continued on the divergence between Kona and op-node regarding brotli-compressed channels. The latest comments from `sebastianst` provide a detailed analysis, identifying a Kona wrapper bug as the dominant cause of divergence, eliminating 95% of discrepancies with a 4-line fix. Further investigation into residual corruption-only divergences and strict-mode parity is recommended. The Rust `brotli` crate used by Kona was found to be non-spec-compliant on certain corrupted inputs, accepting streams that Go libraries correctly reject.

### Kona Frame Parsing Mismatch ([#19335](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19335))
The issue regarding Kona's frame parsing mismatch against `op-node` and OP Stack Specs was acknowledged by `sebastianst`, who confirmed a fix is being implemented via a separate pull request ([https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20590](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20590)). This indicates active progress towards resolving inconsistencies in frame parsing.
