### vm-runner: Support ZK execution ([#18325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18325))
Pauldownman commented on this issue, suggesting that extracting relevant parts of the proposer might be a good approach to minimize new code when updating `vm-runner` to support ZK proof programs. This follows an earlier comment by Inphi noting that actual ZK execution would need to be in Rust due to `sp1` dependency, but the `vm-runner` could drive the executor.
