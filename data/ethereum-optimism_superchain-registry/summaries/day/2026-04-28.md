## CLOSED ISSUES
### Kona Test Stability Improvements
A bug causing the `kona-host-client-offline-cannon` test to crash due to an incorrect target specification in `cannon-builder:v1.0.0` was resolved. The fix involved bind-mounting a corrected `mips64-unknown-none.json` and refreshing the committed witness tar. ([#19895](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19895))

### L2CM Upgrade Process Refinement
The implementation of the Bundle Canonicalization process for L2CM Upgrades has been completed. This process aims to provide a manual trigger for updating bundled NUTs per hardfork, with automation and CI checks to minimize human error. ([#19307](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19307))
