Context was added regarding a related PR that updated documentation on supported pruning configurations. The conversation also touched upon whether to remove the "minimal" pruning option or adjust its defaults and clarify its risks.
*   **`kona-sp1-proposer`: Which games should the proposer defend when challenged** ([#22111](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22111))
    *   A comment suggests not making changes at this point, noting that the challenger's bond is kept for every valid game proven, which is higher than the proving cost. It also mentions that if overwhelmed, it's a "proof of whale" attack, a known weakness, and until bond economics improve, the defense is to blacklist or retire games.
*   **`op-reth`: update `reth` dependency to 2.4.2 / 2.5.0** ([#22115](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22115))
    *   A detailed comment explains that `op-reth-premium` is waiting for an upstream `reth` change to address a breaking change introduced by `optimism-premium`. This upstream fix would allow for a cleaner solution to the state-root wait issue, removing a proxy channel and detached OS thread, and preventing potential unbounded resource leaks.
