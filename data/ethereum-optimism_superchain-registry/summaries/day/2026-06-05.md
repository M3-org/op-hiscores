## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `op-supernode`/`op-reth` CL Sync Recovery Stalls
Discussion on [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21076) continued with significant updates regarding a candidate fix from `reth`. Testing revealed that `paradigmxyz/reth#24875` did not resolve the wedge and in some cases reproduced it more reliably. The root cause was identified as a cyclic deadlock in `reth`'s `StateTrieOverlayManager` / `DeferredTrieData` machinery, with two distinct "lock held across a `rayon` compute" sites. Two patches were proposed and tested, both successfully preventing the wedge during continuous CL-sync for extended periods. The issue is now flagged for the `reth` team for an upstream fix.

### `op-supervisor`/`op-supernode` Cross-Safe Backlog
The discussion on [#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21117) focused on whether the observed 12-hour delay in cross-safe catching up after an interruption was expected behavior due to the sequencer window expiring. It was noted that if the sequencing window expires, manual intervention is required for recovery. The latest comment suggests that if the observed behavior is indeed the expected outcome of sequencer window expiry, then no further action might be needed on this ticket.
