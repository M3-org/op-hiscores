## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SuperDisputeGame Deployment Support
Discussion on [#19652](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19652) suggests that support for deploying `SuperDisputeGame` via `op-deployer` and `netchef` is largely complete. Ajsutton believes the task is done, as a devnet was successfully deployed to `PermissionedSuperDisputeGame` and the upgrade to `SuperDisputeGame` has also been performed. The issue is being reviewed for closure.

### Flaky Test: `TestFollowSource_HeadsDivergeThenConverge`
Issue [#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19821) was reopened due to a resurfaced flake with a different signature. Wwared reported a convergence timeout on `memory-all-opn-op-reth`, where `local-safe` never caught up to `cross-safe` on the follower. The error indicates the EL (op-reth) is stuck in `SYNCING`, causing the CL to reset repeatedly. This flake has a history of recurrence, suggesting it's not a one-off.

### Flaky Test: `TestFlashblocksTransfer`
Issue [#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19934) continues to be active, with sebastianst reporting another flake. The test failed with an "upstream dep failed: operation failed permanently after 5 attempts: not found" error, indicating a potential sequencer or txpool issue. While a previous PR made the test fail faster, the underlying `op-rbuilder` flashblock-emission bug remains, and the actual fix needs to be on the builder side.

### Interop + Batcher: Use Verification-Aware CurrentL1
Discussion on [#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19944) highlights concerns about the batcher's handling of `CurrentL1` going backwards, especially in the context of L1 reorgs. Geoknee suggests the problem lies with `op-node` / `op-supernode` not correctly progressing `CurrentL1` after a reorg, causing the batcher to stall. Ajsutton notes that the batcher should ideally handle `CurrentL1` going backwards, as it does with `op-node`. A related PR [#20694](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20694) was linked.

### op-supernode: cross-safe head stalls after deep L1 reorg
Issue [#20134](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20134) describes a critical problem where `op-supernode`'s `cross-safe` and `local-safe` heads permanently stall after a deep L1 reorg. Geoknee points to [#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19944), suggesting the fix should be in `op-node` / `op-supernode` rather than the batcher.

### Flaky Test: `TestWithdrawal_Permissioned`
Issue [#20345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20345) continues to report flakes. Geoknee noted another instance where the test ran for over 20 minutes before failing.
