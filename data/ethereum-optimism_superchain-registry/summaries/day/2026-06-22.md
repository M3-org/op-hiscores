Discussion continued on adapting the Rust stack release process to the monorepo. A comment highlighted that the `op-revm` changelog is outdated, stopping at `19.0.0` despite `20.0.0` being published. The question was raised whether the scope of this issue includes keeping the changelog up to date going forward. ([#18926](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18926))

The runbook is intended to cover several existing issues and ensure the failsafe is disabled after recovery. ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21161))
