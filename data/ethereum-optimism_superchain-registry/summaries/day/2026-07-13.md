### Simplifying `op-challenger` Dispute Game Handling
Discussions around simplifying `op-challenger`'s handling of permissioned dispute games ([#21678](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21678)) saw pauldowman suggesting adding `closeGame()` to the generic `IDisputeGame` interface. This is in response to the need for `op-challenger` to close games and update the anchor state registry after finalization, a task currently tracked in a related issue ([#21708](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21708)).

### Advancing Anchor for Finalized `SuperPermissionedDisputeGame`
The issue of `op-challenger` advancing the anchor for finalized `SuperPermissionedDisputeGame` games ([#21708](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21708)) also saw pauldowman suggesting adding `closeGame()` to the generic `IDisputeGame` interface, mirroring the discussion in issue #21678, to address the lack of a `closeGame()` method in `SuperPermissionedDisputeGame` and ensure the `AnchorStateRegistry` is updated.
