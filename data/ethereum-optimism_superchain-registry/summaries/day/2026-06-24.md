### Flaky Test in Blob Encoding/Decoding
Discussion continued on the flaky test `FuzzDetectNonBijectivity` ([#21539](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21539)). The latest comment clarifies that the test's premise might be incorrect due to how the blob encoding/decoding functions in `op-node` and `kona` handle the top two bits of the first field element. These bits are masked out during reassembly, meaning flipping them does not change the decoded data, leading to the test's failure. The comment suggests that a fix might involve either adjusting the test harness or implementing a hardfork to change the encoding/decoding behavior to return errors for malformed blobs, though the latter has implications for existing on-chain blobs.
