## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona-node SafeDB Implementation
- Discussion on [#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20278) highlights the need for a SafeDB in `kona-node` that works well with interop and proofs, emphasizing the importance of a well-designed solution rather than blindly adopting existing `op-node` designs.

### Kona-node Syncmode Parity
- Issue [#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21243) focuses on implementing `syncmode.offset-el-safe` in `kona-node` for parity with `op-node`. The latest comments suggest that while `kona` doesn't persist the safe head, its EL node will, potentially mitigating the issue. It also notes that `kona` will eventually need a SafeDB, which should prioritize the SafeDB head over the EL's safe head after EL sync.

### SDM Operator Flag Consistency
- The discussion on [#21360](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21360) revolves around improving the consistency of the SDM opt-in operator flag across `op-node`, `op-reth`, and `op-rbuilder`. The latest comments confirm that the scope will be narrowed to `op-reth` and `op-node`, and the flag will be named `operator_sdm_opt_in` to clearly convey its purpose.
