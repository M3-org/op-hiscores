## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes` ([#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19804))
The `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes` test continues to be flaky. A fix was submitted in `ethereum-optimism/optimism#20580`. The root cause was identified as `freezeChains` stopping sequencers and batchers in parallel without ensuring `LocalSafe == LocalUnsafe`, leading to `nextTimestampAfterSafeHeads` computing `endTimestamp` from lagging safe heads. The proposed fix reorders `freezeChains` to drain `LocalSafe` up to `LocalUnsafe` before stopping batchers. Previously, a different root cause was identified and fixed in #19805, related to `l1BlockWithLocalSafeBlocks()` polling supernode's `RequiredL1` values.

### Interop + Batcher: Use Verification-Aware CurrentL1 ([#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19944))

### Flaky test: `TestChallengerRespondsToMultipleInvalidClaims` ([#20574](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20574))
A new flaky test, `TestChallengerRespondsToMultipleInvalidClaims`, has been reported. A fix has been opened in `ethereum-optimism/optimism#20575`. The root cause was identified as `StartSuperCannonKonaGame` computing a synthetic honest root claim identical to the canonical super root, leading to `GameAlreadyExists` errors when the test's `safeTimestamp()` collides with a value already used by the proposer. The fix introduces a `presets.WithoutHonestProposer()` option to prevent proposer races during game creation in tests that don't exercise the proposer.
