## CLOSED ISSUES

### OpChainConfig Structure Refinement
An issue concerning the removal of the `proxyAdmin` input from the `OpChainConfig` struct used for `OPCM.upgrade()` was closed as a duplicate. The `proxyAdmin` is no longer required as an input since it can be retrieved from `systemConfigProxy.proxyAdmin()`. ([#16728](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/16728))

### SystemConfig Override Removal
An issue tracking the removal of the `SystemConfig::isCustomGasToken` override allowance for OPCMv2 was closed. This override was a temporary measure until U18 ships and `isCustomGasToken` becomes available on the SystemConfig. ([#18502](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18502))
