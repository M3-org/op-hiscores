# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Daily Update (Jun 23, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on refining the Superchain registry by removing deprecated testnet chains and enhancing operational robustness. Key updates include addressing intrinsic gas issues on Optimism Sepolia and improving atomic file writing mechanisms.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Superchain Configuration and Hardfork Management
A new per-chain configuration flag, `keep_karst_upgrade_gas`, was introduced and enabled for Sepolia and Mainnet superchains to manage upgrade gas behavior following the Karst hardfork. This change impacts various chain configurations across both networks, ensuring consistent hardfork handling ([#1259](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1259)).

### Testnet Chain Cleanup and Registry Maintenance
Two Sepolia testnet chains, Ethernity Testnet (ID 233) and Creator Chain Testnet (ID 66665), were removed from the registry. This cleanup involved deleting their respective configuration TOMLs, genesis files, and codegen artifacts, streamlining the registry and removing outdated entries ([#1263](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1263)).

### Operational Tooling Enhancements
The `AtomicWrite` function in the operational tooling was updated to stage temporary files within the destination directory. This resolves issues with cross-filesystem renames that previously caused `EXDEV` errors, improving the reliability of file operations, particularly during codegen processes ([#1264](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1264)).

## CLOSED ISSUES

### SDM Test Hardening and Design Finalization
Significant progress was made in hardening SDM tests, with issues related to ETH supply conservation, post-execution settlement underflow, and mid-block payload handling being addressed and covered by new tests ([#21324](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21324)). Additionally, the design document for PCD Infra support was approved and marked as complete ([#21327](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21327)).

## NEW ISSUES

### Reth Integration and Slot-Preimage Seeding
A new feature request proposes reusing an upstream slot-preimage seeding helper in `op-reth` to eliminate duplication and improve snapshot importer functionality. This aims to leverage existing `reth` support for `init-state` and `init_from_state_dump` ([#21523](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21523)).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona-node: SafeDB Implementation
Discussion on the `kona-node: SafeDB` issue ([#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20278)) continues, with `einar-oplabs` providing a preview of an implementation based on the design document. The approach involves defining a `SafeDBv1` trait for a 1-to-1 copy, with plans to experiment with `SafeDBv2` for interface and implementation improvements. `geoknee` emphasized the importance of ensuring compatibility with interop and proofs, while also encouraging better design considerations beyond simply adopting existing `op-node` patterns.

### Optimism Sepolia Intrinsic Gas After Karst Hard Fork
The issue of "intrinsic gas too high" on Optimism Sepolia after the Karst hard fork ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21457)) is actively being discussed. `yuercl` provided detailed observations, noting that `eth_estimateGas` returns an error, while `Tenderly` provides a gas estimate, `staticCall` succeeds, and sending with an explicit `gasLimit` works on-chain. This issue started after Karst on OP Sepolia and has been reproduced across multiple RPC providers. `geoknee` confirmed it as a known issue, with a fix anticipated in the next release.
