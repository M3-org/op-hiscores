### chore(ci/kona): simplify kona-proof's ci using kona's monorepo ([#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18773))
Discussion revolves around simplifying `kona-proof`'s CI by utilizing Kona's monorepo. A key point of discussion is the dual use of `kona/version.json` for both acceptance tests (where the latest Kona from the monorepo is desired) and for the `kona-host` release included in the `op-challenger` docker image. The complexity arises from ensuring consistent release management for `kona-host` within `op-challenger` to avoid confusion if separate `Kona-host` releases exist but are not the ones integrated into `op-challenger`.
