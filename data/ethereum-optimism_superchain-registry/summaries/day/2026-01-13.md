### chore(ci/kona): simplify kona-proof's ci using kona's monorepo ([#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18773))
Discussion revolves around the implications of simplifying `kona-proof`'s CI. A key point raised is the dual use of `kona/version.json` for both acceptance tests in `cannon+kona` and the release of `kona-host` in the `op-challenger` docker image. The current thinking suggests that for acceptance tests, the latest Kona from the monorepo should be used. For `kona-host` releases, the ideal scenario would be for it to be released by tagging `op-challenger`, as standalone `kona-host` releases might not add value and could lead to confusion if they don't align with what's included in `op-challenger`.
