## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Interop Expiry Window Stress Test
Discussion on [#15285](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/15285) focused on the results of live devnet stress testing for the interop expiry window. Inphi reported successful completion of stress tests on Kona interop running in Cannon/MIPS, even with old initiating messages, after fixing a Kona EIP-2935 proof decoding issue. The tests showed linear scaling of cost with message count, suggesting that extending the expiry window to 7 days is unlikely to cause completion issues. Ajsutton agreed that the analysis is sufficient and that the RLP decoding fix, now merged, addresses the main concern.

### L2CM Acceptance Testing on Betanet
Issue [#20089](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20089) regarding L2CM acceptance testing on Betanet was identified as a duplicate of #18839 by maurelian.

### Runbook for Super Root Chain Update
On [#20473](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20473), ajsutton suggested deferring the runbook update for moving a super root chain from permissioned to permissionless until after interop has shipped. The rationale is that this documentation is only needed for chains that have upgraded to SuperDisputeGame (U20 at least) and then want to transition, making interop a precondition. He asked for guidance on how to track this as follow-up work without blocking the current release.
