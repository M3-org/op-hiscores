## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Fatal Panic in op-reth StateRootTask During Upgrades
Issue [#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20462) details a fatal panic in `StateRootTask` when upgrading `op-reth` from 1.x to 2.x on OP Stack chains. The latest update confirms that the `--engine.legacy-state-root` flag does not resolve the panic. New findings indicate that the panic, an `assertion 'left == right' failed` in `alloy-trie-0.9.5`, occurs in both `StateRootTask` and `Parallel` strategies, suggesting a shared bug in the parallel trie code paths. The crash is not block-specific but triggered non-deterministically during parallel trie computation against 1.x database state.
