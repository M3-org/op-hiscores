### `op-supernode` Behavioral Observations and Model Discrepancies
Discussion on [#22636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22636) detailed numerous discrepancies between `op-supernode`'s Go implementation and its Dafny model, surfaced during the `lokahi` mirroring work. Key points include:
*   **Verification Gaps:** Several areas where the Go implementation deviates from the model's verification logic, such as skipping verification for the first sealed block's executing messages, differing handling of same-timestamp initiating messages, and an unverified axiom regarding frontier view correctness.
*   **Logs Store Inconsistencies:** A violation of the `UpdatedLogsDB` axiom due to virtual-parent first-block sealing in Go.
*   **Rewind Plan Mismatches:** `buildRewindPlan` in Go produces plan shapes that the Dafny model's `ValidRewindPlan` cannot express, and there's a difference in rewind apply order (engines-first in model vs. engines-last in Go).
*   **Observation and Decision Logic:** `observeRound`'s two-flag L1 split in Go has no model analog, and `obs.L2sConsistent` is never set in Go, leading to vacuously discharged obligations.
*   **Commit and Lifecycle Differences:** The model requires strictly-next timestamp commits, while Go allows idempotent replay. The all-chain VN freeze during invalidation in Go is not modeled.
*   **I/O Error Handling:** Go's I/O-error paths are outside the model's scope, leading to partially-written states that the model's invariants cannot describe.

### `kona-node` RPC Namespace Gaps
Further discussion on [#22636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22636) highlighted two significant RPC namespace gaps in `kona-node` compared to `op-node`:
*   **Missing `superroot` Namespace:** `kona-node` lacks the `superroot` RPC namespace, which `op-node` registers unconditionally. This causes "Method not found" errors when dispute infrastructure points to a standalone `kona-node` RPC.
*   **Missing `opstack` Block-Building Namespace:** `kona-node` does not implement `op-node`'s experimental `opstack` namespace (`opstack_openBlockV1`, `sealBlockV1`, etc.), which is used by `op-supernode`'s virtual nodes and `op-test-sequencer` for CL-side block building. This results in "Method not found" errors for block-building calls.

### CI Job Naming and Reporting Inaccuracies
An observation on [#22636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22636) noted that the CI job `memory-all-kona-lokahi-op-reth-<fork>` incorrectly advertises running `lokahi` as the supernode, when it actually runs `op-supernode`. This has led to false positive reports of `lokahi` passing acceptance tests. The issue highlights the need for accurate job naming and reporting of the actual implementation measured in test runs.
