## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `op-supernode` Observations and `kona-node` Discrepancies ([#22636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22636))
Discussions continued regarding observations from mirroring `op-supernode` behavior in `lokahi`, revealing several key discrepancies and issues:
- **Missing `superroot` Namespace in `kona-node`**: It was noted that `op-node` unconditionally registers a `superroot` RPC namespace, which is entirely absent in `kona-node`. This means any dispute infrastructure pointing to a standalone `kona-node` RPC will encounter "Method not found" errors when expecting `superroot` calls.
- **`kona-node` Lacks `opstack` Block-Building Namespace**: `kona-node` does not implement `op-node`'s experimental `opstack` block-building namespace (`opstack_openBlockV1`, `sealBlockV1`, etc.). This leads to "Method not found" errors when the `op-test-sequencer` attempts to drive block building through `kona-node`'s CL, causing test failures.
- **Verifier Behavior with Safe-Head Stability Gate**: A residual case was identified where a deep post-commit L1 reorg below the first *stable* SafeDB entry still permanently halts verification, mirroring existing `op-supernode` behavior.
- **Misleading CI Job Naming**: A significant issue was highlighted where a CI job named `memory-all-kona-lokahi-op-reth-<fork>` was actually running `supernode_kind: op-supernode` instead of `lokahi`. This led to false-positive test results for `lokahi` and underscored the need for CI job names to accurately reflect the implementations being tested. Recommendations include renaming the job and reporting the selected `supernode_kind` within test records.
