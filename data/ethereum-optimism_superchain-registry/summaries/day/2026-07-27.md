### Precompile Gas Reservation for Glamsterdam (EIP-8037)
Discussion continued on increasing `PreimageOracle.PRECOMPILE_CALL_RESERVED_GAS` before Glamsterdam ([#19380](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19380)). A recent comment suggests the issue might be stale as written, arguing that `PRECOMPILE_CALL_RESERVED_GAS` is primarily for precompile call setup, not whole-function completion. The current EIP-8037/8038 drafts indicate that even a `200_000` value would be insufficient for whole-function completion, with estimates closer to `400_000` needed. The recommendation is to either close the issue as unnecessary for correctness (while adding Amsterdam gas-estimation coverage) or redefine it to explicitly guarantee whole-function success at the guard threshold with an updated value.

### Flaky Test Investigation: `TestUnsafeChainNotStalling_CLSync`
Investigation into the flaky `TestUnsafeChainNotStalling_CLSync` family of tests continued ([#21041](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21041)). A recent occurrence on `develop` showed a total EL-backfill stall, where the follower `op-reth` failed to backfill blocks because its only EL peer (the sequencer's `op-reth`) disconnected at the exact moment backfill started and never re-established. This suggests an EL peer-management defect rather than a budget or CI contention issue, as the test sat at `stage=Headers checkpoint=12 target=None connected_peers=0` for the entire 60-second budget with no progress.
