### Supernode Unsafe SuperRoots
A new issue, [#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19059), was opened to discuss the potential for `op-supernode` to return "SuperRoots" for unsafe data, even when no verified or safe data exists at a referenced timestamp. The discussion revolves around the utility and implications of returning such mechanical, non-semantically meaningful roots.

## ACTIVE ISSUES
### op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness
Discussion on [#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/17631) continued, focusing on the behavior of `op-node` marking the target of initial EL Sync as finalized, even if it's not truly finalized. `trianglesphere` clarified that while EL sync with `op-reth` can follow a finalized L2 as a maximum target, `op-geth` in snap sync cannot, as snap sync only works with state snapshots within 128 blocks of the tip. `axelKingsley` emphasized that EL sync should only drive to the tip of the chain for finalized content, suggesting that snap sync should follow the finalized L2 as a maximum target. `sebastianst` agreed that the current behavior is buggy and proposed running derivation from a prior L1 block and then consolidation to verify alignment with the EL-synced unsafe chain.
