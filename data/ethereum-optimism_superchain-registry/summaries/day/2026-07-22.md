*   **flaky test: `TestNotTruncateDatabaseOnRestartWithExistingDatabase`** ([#20649](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20649))
    *   Claude reported a new occurrence of this flake on July 22nd, with an error indicating the safe head database did not go back far enough. The failure occurred at the baseline sanity check before any restart, due to a race condition between the live safe head and the L1 origin-resolved safe head. A new PR ([#21975](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21975)) has been opened to re-land a previous fix for this issue.

*   **[flake] TestSupernodeInterop_SafeHeadProgression — EL safe label race with CL cross-safe** ([#20851](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20851))
    *   sebastianst reported a new occurrence of this flake on July 22nd, with the error "operation failed permanently after 15 attempts: expected head to advance: safe". This occurred during work on an unrelated Go dependency change, and a workflow rerun was triggered.

*   **chore(op-node): Fix light-sequencer follow-source ↔ sequencer oscillation during recovery** ([#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21964))
    *   Claude logged an occurrence that upgraded this from a convergence-time issue to a correctness problem. The oscillation can lead to the sync-start recovery path relabeling a local fork as safe/finalized, even if it's not. An instance of `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` failed because a transaction was missing from the light sequencer's EL, despite the cross-safe gate passing. This highlights that height-based safety-label checks are insufficient to distinguish forks once recovery has relabeled the local fork.
