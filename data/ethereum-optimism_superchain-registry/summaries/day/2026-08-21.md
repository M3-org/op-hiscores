### Kona Node Service: EngineActor Becomes ChainController [#22527](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22527)
Discussion on renaming `EngineActor` to `ChainController` and extending its functionality continued. The latest comment from claude (2026-08-21T04:47:42Z) provided detailed insights into the implementation of a read-only query handler. Key points include:
- The single read for state was already available, but its significance was enabled by a related PR (#22596).
- A structural gap in the Go side's `OptimisticAt` function was identified, where atomicity is not fully guaranteed despite comments suggesting otherwise.
- The query handler needs to distinguish four verdicts for timestamp queries (ahead, head's timestamp, behind, below genesis) rather than just two, to avoid misinterpretations by consumers.
- An incidental fix was made to `engine.mdx` documentation regarding `InsertTask` snippets.
- The proposed changes do not require or implement any part of #22528 (SafeDB) and do not introduce new wire names, focusing on in-process queries.

Earlier, claude (2026-08-21T01:44:41Z) noted that "Mediate derivation-bound signals and own reset initiation" and depth-1 lockstep confirmation were already implemented. The discussion also clarified that the `descends-from-local-safe` check cannot reside in the controller due to potential state changes between enqueue and execution, leading to its implementation as a predicate on `InsertTask`. The query handler for "Atomic local-safe-at-timestamp + L1-at-safe snapshot" was deferred, with a detailed correction on why the controller cannot reliably form the L2→L1 pairing, concluding that the write hook belongs in `kona-engine`'s sync state.
