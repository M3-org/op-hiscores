## ACTIVE ISSUES
- **Flaky Test Investigation and Fix Draft**
  The flaky test issue, [#20944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20944), continues to be a focus. A draft fix has been proposed in `ethereum-optimism/optimism#21002`. The root cause was identified as `verifiedDB` closing during supernode shutdown, leading to `PayloadSuccessEvent` draining issues and incorrect L2 genesis reporting. The fix involves propagating read errors and signaling use-local fallback on verifier errors. Follow-up actions include addressing op-reth's acceptance of non-monotonic `finalized_block_hash` in `engine_forkchoiceUpdated`.

- **Dev Tooling: Support for Deployment Factories**
  Discussion on [#12](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/12) regarding supporting deployment factories in dev tooling saw detailed proposals from Hobie1Kenobi. The approach involves researching and designing a `FactoryDependency` link type, building an indexer for `CREATE`/`CREATE2` opcodes, and integrating factory links into the dependency graph with verification gates. Deliverables include a schema PR, a factory indexer, verification integration, documentation, and a test suite, with an estimated completion of 11 days.

- **Onchain Builders: Expand User Metrics Beyond Farcaster IDs**
  Issue [#13](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/13) received further input on expanding user metrics. Hobie1Kenobi proposed a blended user score incorporating Gnosis Pay/KYC-linked addresses (weight 1.0), CEX-linked addresses (weight 0.6), and organic onchain activity (weight 0.3), with Sybil resistance via graph clustering. The approach includes researching methodologies, creating SQL queries for Dune/Flipside, developing Sybil heuristics, and providing final recommendations, with an estimated completion of 5-7 days.
