## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SDM Performance Benchmarking and Rollout
Discussions on [#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20176) and [#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21273) continue to focus on the rollout of an alpha/beta devnet for SDM and its performance benchmarking. A recent comment on [#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20176) suggests pointing the `op-benchmark` tool at the SSD devnet with SDM for performance measurement. For [#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21273), a comment highlighted the availability of rudimentary scripts to drive traffic at an SDM network, which could be used to generate transactions for testing.

### op-node Synchronization Issues
Issue [#20974](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20974) details problems with `op-node` failing to sync when `--syncmode=execution-layer` is enabled with an `op-reth` backend. The latest comment describes a fullnode pod eventually establishing two peers after continuous errors and retries, noting the high number of `Ecies(ECIESError(UnreadableStream))` errors.

### Reth-Optimism-Node Integration Test Flakiness
The discussion on [#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20973) concerns a flaky `reth-optimism-node` integration test (`peers_negotiate_eth_69`) that SIGSEGVs on teardown. The latest update indicates that the issue is on pause, with CI instrumented to provide notifications and stack traces for future flakes to aid in debugging.
