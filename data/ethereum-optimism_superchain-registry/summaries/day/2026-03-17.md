## ACTIVE ISSUES

### op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/17631))
Discussion continues on the behavior of `op-node` marking the reached target as finalized after initial EL Sync, even if the block is not actually finalized. The latest comments propose solutions to address this, including:
- Continuing EL sync to the unsafe head but using a different finalization gadget to set the finalized head.
- Options for the finalization gadget include a command-line flag, an RPC URL for a trusted checkpoint provider, looking at L1 for the last finalized head, or an arbitrary delay for the finalized head.
- The impact on `op-geth` (being deprecated) and `opp-reth` is also considered.
- Questions were raised about whether this bug is specific to light CL/interop or present even without it, and how it's currently mitigated.
