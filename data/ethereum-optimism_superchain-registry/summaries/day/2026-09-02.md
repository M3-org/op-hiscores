### Addressing Go Module Consumability for Superchain Registry Data
Discussion on [#22678](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22678) continued with significant updates regarding the issue of `op-core/superchain` being unconsumable by downstream Go modules.

- **Clarification on Scope:** It was clarified that recent PRs ([#22700](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22700) and [#22709](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22709)) only partially resolved the issue by reducing the number of affected packages. The core problem remains: `superchain-configs.zip` is still gitignored and not included in published Go modules, making genuine registry consumers unbuildable.
- **Impacted Consumers:** Key registry consumers like `op-deployer`, `op-conductor`, `op-challenger`, `op-dispute-mon`, `op-supernode`, `op-interop-filter`, op-node's non-`rollup` packages, and `op-service/flags` are still affected.
- **Correction on Supersim:** An earlier assessment that `supersim` was a genuine registry consumer was corrected. It was determined that `supersim` is unblocked by reachability work and does not require the `superchain-configs.zip` to be shipped within the module.
- **Git LFS Not a Solution:** It was confirmed that `git-lfs` is not a viable option for shipping the zip due to `cmd/go` and `proxy.golang.org` limitations.
- **Import Graph Analysis:** An analysis of the import graph revealed that initially 206 of 560 monorepo packages reached the bundle. After proposed cleanups (splitting `depset` loader, deleting dead code, and addressing `opprof` dependencies), this number is expected to drop to ~109, primarily genuine data consumers.
- **Cost of Committing the Zip:** Measurements from `op-geth`'s history indicate that committing the `superchain-configs.zip` would incur a marginal cost of approximately 245 KB per bump, totaling around 3 MB/year, which is significantly less than previously assumed. This suggests that committing the zip directly might be a more feasible solution than initially thought.
