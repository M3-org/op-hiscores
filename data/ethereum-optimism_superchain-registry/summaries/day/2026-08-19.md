## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Flaky Test: `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract`** ([#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20376)):
    - The issue was reopened due to a resurfacing flake with a different symptom but the same underlying race condition.
    - The `WaitForProofsStoreBlock` gate, added previously, now explicitly times out after 30 seconds instead of a "no state found" error.
    - Evidence from the CI run shows the validator's proofs ExEx store never advanced past genesis for 30 seconds, while the validator EL processed 17 blocks.
    - The proofs indexing engine on the sequencer appeared starved, flushing 19 blocks in a single burst during teardown.
    - The issue is considered a flake, not a regression, as the PR diff was CI-only, the job passed previously, and sibling tests passed in the same run.
    - Suggested directions include raising the `require.Eventually` timeout in `WaitForProofsStoreBlock` and investigating why the proofs indexing worker makes no progress for extended periods.

- **[PCD] Default-`X` benchmarking** ([#20916](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20916)):
    - The issue was reopened twice on this day because it was closed while still being referenced by TODOs in the `op-deployer` codebase.
    - The TODOs relate to enforcing a reasonable minimum for the `X` offset (between L1 anchor timestamp and L2 `genesis_time`) and replacing a value with a benchmark based on L2 genesis deployment time.
    - The CI job `todo-issue-checks` will continue to fail until these TODO comments are updated or removed, or the issue is properly resolved.
