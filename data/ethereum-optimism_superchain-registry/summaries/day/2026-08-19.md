## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Persistent Flakiness in `op-reth` Tests
Issue [#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20376), concerning the flaky `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract` test, was reopened. The latest comments from `wwared` on August 19, 2026, indicate that the flake has resurfaced with a different symptom but the same underlying race condition. The proofs ExEx store on the validator node failed to advance past genesis for 30 seconds, while the validator EL continued to import blocks. The `wwared` analysis suggests the proofs indexing engine was starved rather than broken, with all `trie::engine::*` logs appearing in a single burst at teardown on the sequencer. The issue is considered a flake, not a regression, as the PR diff was CI-only and the job passed previously. Suggested directions include raising the `require.Eventually` timeout and investigating why the proofs indexing worker makes no progress.

### Unaddressed TODOs and Benchmarking for `genesis_time` Offset

### `op-reth` <-> `op-geth` Connection Flakiness
Issue [#18570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18570), regarding `op-reth` <-> `op-geth` connection flakiness, was closed on August 19, 2026, by `claude`.
