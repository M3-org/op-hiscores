## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **op-supernode/op-supernode cross-safe cannot drain a backlog faster than real-time after a stall** ([#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21117))
    - The latest comment questions whether the local safe truly caught up to unsafe, suggesting that a 12-hour lag indicates the sequencing window expired. It's highlighted that if the sequencing window elapses due to an extended batcher outage, manual intervention is required for recovery, as the safe head's force-increasing nature invalidates batcher inclusions.

- **op-supernode/op-reth: CL sync recovery can stall when SafeDB/bookkeeping references EL-missing block** ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21076))
    - The latest update confirms the issue can be reproduced without `op-supernode`, using a standalone `op-node` and `op-reth` setup. The failure pattern involves `reth` logging "Block added to canonical chain" but not "Received new payload from consensus engine" or "Canonical chain committed" for a block, leading to `op-node` timing out. This points to an immediate failure in the `op-node`/`op-reth` CL derive/build Engine API path, with `op-supernode` potentially amplifying the stall but not being required to trigger it. Follow-ups include capturing traces, focused regression testing, and fixing historical blob fetching.
