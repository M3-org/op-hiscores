## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestReorgUnsafeHead` ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20198))
The `TestReorgUnsafeHead` test continues to be flaky. The latest comment from joshklop on 2026-07-10 highlights another CI occurrence where the test failed because the expected reorg did not happen. The issue appears to stem from the op-node sequencer reasserting its original head due to an in-flight sequencing/forkchoice action racing `StopSequencer()`. A suggested fix involves applying a quiescence guard before the first `ReorgExact` to ensure the op-node has no pending actions that could reassert the original head.
