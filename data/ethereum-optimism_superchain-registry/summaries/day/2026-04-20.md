## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Flaky Test: `TestFlashblocksStream`** ([#19883](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19883)): The test was reopened due to a resurfacing flake with a different failure mode. The latest comment from ajsutton indicates a fix has been proposed in ethereum-optimism/optimism#20166, identifying the root cause as an assertion mismatch with the flashblock protocol where sequences are keyed by `payload_id` not `block_number`. Previous comments from ajsutton and wwared detailed different failure modes, including a port collision and a context cancellation error during sequencer stop.
- **`op-supernode`: SuperRoots for the unsafe** ([#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19059)): The discussion revolves around whether `supernode` should return super roots for unsafe or unverified data. axelKingsley suggests finding alternate means to determine if a node has "observed" a given height or hash, proposing to mark the task as "deferred" due to low urgency. He also highlighted the introduction of Safe/Finalized retraction when EL syncing `op-node` ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19972](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19972)). Inphi previously clarified that the returned "super root" would be purely mechanical without semantic meaning, only indicating observation.
- **`kona-node`, `interop`: Conditionally deploy CrossL2Inbox** ([#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19311)): The issue concerns the unconditional generation of CrossL2Inbox transactions by `kona`'s `Hardforks::INTEROP.txs()`. Inphi confirmed an e2e test is in place to check this behavior as part of [#19010](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19010) and passes with changes in [#20147](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/20147). The focus is on ensuring `kona-derive` (and `kona-proof`) do not generate spurious CrossL2Inbox deployments.
