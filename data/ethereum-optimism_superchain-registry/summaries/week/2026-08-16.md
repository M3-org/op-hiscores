# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Weekly Report (Aug 16 - 22, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on expanding the Superchain ecosystem through the addition of new network targets and refining the stability of the development stack. A significant milestone was reached in resolving long-standing P2P connection flakiness between execution clients, enabling the re-enabling of P2P verification in end-to-end testing. However, the project continues to grapple with persistent race conditions in storage proof testing and CI failures triggered by legacy TODO references in the codebase. The overall theme of the week was one of ecosystem growth balanced against the rigorous maintenance of testing infrastructure.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Superchain Expansion
The registry continues to grow as the foundational source of truth for the Superchain ecosystem.
*   **New Network Targets:** Work began on integrating the "Plataberget superchain target" into the registry via PRs [#1309](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1309) and [#1308](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1308).
*   **Registry Maintenance:** Automated maintenance of the registry's generated code was performed to ensure consistency across the data set ([#1307](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1307)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Interoperability Stability:** A major fix was finalized regarding connection flakiness between `op-reth` and `op-geth`. By normalizing peer-ID formats and removing the `SKIP_P2P_CONNECTION_CHECK` workaround, the team has successfully re-enabled P2P connection verification in Rust e2e sysgo jobs ([#18570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18570)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Storage Proof Flakiness:** Issue [#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20376) remains a high-priority concern. Recent observations indicate a race condition where the proofs indexing engine appears "starved," failing to advance past the genesis block for extended periods. Current investigations are focused on the proofs indexing worker and potential adjustments to `require.Eventually` timeouts.
*   **CI Blockers & Technical Debt:** The `todo-issue-checks` CI job is currently failing due to persistent TODO references in `op-deployer`. This led to the reopening of issue [#20916](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20916) regarding L2 genesis deployment benchmarking, highlighting a need to either complete the referenced work or clean up the codebase to unblock the CI pipeline.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by active debugging and infrastructure maintenance. Contributors like `wwared` provided deep technical analysis into execution engine behavior to address test flakiness, while `claude` managed the lifecycle of several critical issues related to deployment benchmarking and P2P networking. The reopening of issues due to CI checks indicates a rigorous automated governance process that requires contributors to actively manage technical debt and documentation within the code.
