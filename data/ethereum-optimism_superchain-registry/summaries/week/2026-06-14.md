# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Weekly Report (Jun 14 - 20, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on maintaining the integrity of the Superchain configuration through automated registry updates and refining the technical roadmap for ZK-related infrastructure. Significant progress was made in streamlining issue tracking by consolidating ZK dispute game tasks into broader Optimism repositories. Discussions also matured regarding the integration of ZK execution within the `vm-runner` and the cost-effective implementation of proof generation for CI/CD environments.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Registry Maintenance & Automation
The core registry remains synchronized with the evolving Superchain ecosystem through automated tooling.
*   **Automated Configuration Updates:** A codegen update was successfully merged to refresh `addresses.json` within the `superchain/extra` directory. This ensures that the registry accurately reflects the latest contract addresses and configurations across the network ([#1257](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1257)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues: ZK Dispute Game Refinement
The team focused on cleaning up the backlog by moving specific ZK implementation details to more appropriate tracking locations.
*   **Bond Credit Claiming:** Issues related to handling `address(0)` bond credit claiming in `ZKDisputeGame` were closed ([#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21420)). The consensus was that these fixes are better tracked within the primary Optimism repository. Technical debate suggested that a specific fix for "unlikely" scenarios—such as a faulty proposer on an invalid game—might be unnecessary as the existing `delayedweth` mechanism provides sufficient disincentives.

### New & Active Issues: ZK Infrastructure & Tooling
Ongoing discussions are shaping how ZK proofs will be handled in development and testing workflows.
*   **ZK Execution in `vm-runner`:** Work continues on supporting ZK execution within `vm-runner` ([#18325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18325)). Current proposals suggest extracting logic from the proposer to minimize code duplication, though the dependency on `sp1` necessitates a Rust-based executor driven by the `vm-runner`.
*   **CI/CD Proof Strategies:** To manage costs and efficiency, there is a strong recommendation to utilize mock provers for local development and CI environments ([#21409](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21409)). Proving on `op-sepolia` is being considered as a secondary path for production-level testing once infrastructure matures.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by high-level technical coordination between core contributors like **Inphi** and **Pauldowman**. Their collaboration centered on architectural decisions for ZK integration, specifically balancing the need for robust testing against the practical costs of ZK proof generation. The closure of several issues in favor of tracking them in centralized repositories indicates an effort to reduce fragmentation across the Optimism Collective's various codebases.
