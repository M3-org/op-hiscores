# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Weekly Report (Aug 9 - 15, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused heavily on the operationalization of State-Dependent Mining (SDM) and critical stability fixes for node connectivity. The team successfully transitioned the SDM devnet (`sdm-net-0`) into a functional state with continuous workloads and verified monitoring, marking a significant milestone for protocol testing. Efforts also addressed deep-seated synchronization issues in `op-reth` and `op-supernode`, while scheduling upcoming infrastructure upgrades for World Chain Karst. Despite these gains, the team is currently navigating complex race conditions in P2P testing that remain a priority for CI stability.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### SDM Devnet Operationalization
The primary technical focus this week was the stabilization and benchmarking of the SDM environment.
*   **Workload Deployment:** Successfully deployed a standing, continuous SDM-exercising workload to ensure the devnet remains under consistent stress for testing purposes [#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22343).
*   **Performance Benchmarking:** Completed baseline performance benchmarks to establish a telemetry foundation for future SDM optimizations [#22344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22344).
*   **Observability:** Verified and integrated SDM-specific dashboards within OPE Grafana to provide real-time visibility into devnet health [#22345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22345).

### Infrastructure & Chain Upgrades
*   **World Chain Karst:** Initiated the scheduling for upgrades to World Chain Karst via a new pull request [#1305](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1305), ensuring the Superchain registry stays aligned with the latest chain configurations.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Node Connectivity & Sync:** Resolved a critical issue where `op-reth` was missing hardfork override flags, which previously blocked p2p connections [#20224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20224). Additionally, a fix was implemented for `op-supernode` to prevent repeated reset cycles during invalidation recovery when unsafe sync resumes before Execution Layer (EL) convergence [#21948](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21948).
*   **Documentation:** Clarified the status of Base configuration in `op-reth` documentation, confirming that Base is no longer supported in the current software release [#20597](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20597).

### Active & New Issues
*   **P2P Stability:** A significant investigation is underway regarding flaky failures in `TestP2PFull` [#22381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22381). Current findings suggest a race condition between disconnect cleanup and immediate reconnection, though a definitive root-cause fix is still pending further logging and analysis.
*   **SDM Test Coverage:** Discussions continue regarding test coverage for invalid PostExec transactions in `rust/kona` [#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21344). There is a proposal to close this issue as the specific scenario is currently untestable until the harness engine is migrated to `op-reth`.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by deep technical troubleshooting between core contributors. Notably, @wwared provided a detailed deterministic model to reproduce P2P race conditions, demonstrating a rigorous approach to CI stability. Collaboration also touched on the intersection of SDM, Lagoon, and Kona, with contributors like @einar-oplabs and @Yomibito-Shirazu-jp coordinating on the limitations of current test suites and the necessity of secure channels for discussing sensitive technical concerns related to new protocol features.
