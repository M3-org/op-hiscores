# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Weekly Report (Jul 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on streamlining the Superchain Registry's data model and advancing the decoupling of `op-geth` from core services. Significant progress was made in resolving infrastructure flakes, particularly identifying the root cause of interop-related sequencer failures. The team also successfully simplified the registry by removing legacy classification concepts and updated migration runbooks to support new super-root game types.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Registry Data Model & Maintenance
- **Removal of `superchain_level`:** A major refactoring effort was completed to eliminate the `superchain_level` concept. This classification was no longer utilized by validation or tooling logic; its removal from configuration files, documentation, and Go code reduces technical debt and simplifies the registry's internal logic [#1292](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1292).
- **Automated Maintenance:** Routine automated code generation updates were performed to maintain consistency across the codebase [#1289](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1289), [#1291](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1291).
- **Super-Root Support:** New operations support was added for super-root game types, ensuring the registry can accommodate evolving governance and security models [#1290](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1290).

### Protocol & Infrastructure Decoupling
- **`op-geth` Decoupling:** The project reached a milestone in swapping call-sites to `op-core/*` across `op-node`, `op-service`, and `op-batcher` [#20263](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20263). 
- **Batcher Migration Strategy:** Discussions regarding `op-service/sources` led to a pivot in the `op-batcher` migration. It was determined that the batcher will move to a "payload-native" approach using `PayloadByNumber` rather than `InfoAndUserTxs`, as the latter inadvertently drops critical L1-info deposit transactions required for batch building [#20264](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20264).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
- **Kona Improvements:** Several issues related to the Kona sequencer were resolved. `TestSequencerPreset` was updated to reflect current support, and a request for `syncmode.offset-el-safe` parity was closed after determining that Kona’s existing safety properties were sufficient and that the change would cause finalization regressions [#18675](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/18675), [#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21243).
- **Documentation:** Fixed errors in the super-root migration runbooks specifically regarding `SUPER_PERMISSIONED` game types [#21709](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21709).

### Active & New Issues
- **Sequencer Interop Flake:** The root cause for `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` was identified as a race condition where `FollowSource` could canonicalize a block while a timer was armed against an old parent. A fix is currently being verified to reject non-derived build starts with mismatched parent block IDs [#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21635).
- **Reth Integration Stability:** Investigation continues into SIGSEGVs during `reth-optimism-node` teardowns. While initial hypotheses regarding "dead runtimes" were refuted, the team is now looking to capture core dumps in CI to pinpoint the exact cause, while considering a workaround patch in the interim [#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20973).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw deep technical coordination between contributors regarding the `op-geth` decoupling strategy, specifically involving the nuances of transaction derivation and receipt roots. There is also active engagement regarding the security and coverage of Rust/Kona proofs, with contributors coordinating private channels to discuss sensitive technical concerns related to SDM and Lagoon [#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21344). The collaborative effort to squash long-standing CI flakes demonstrates a strong commitment to developer experience and system reliability.
