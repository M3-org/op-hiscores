# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Weekly Report (Apr 12 - 18, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on post-upgrade maintenance and refining the technical requirements for cross-chain interoperability. A primary focus was the synchronization of proxy configurations following the Jovian hardfork to ensure registry accuracy. Additionally, technical discussions intensified regarding the deployment logic of the `CrossL2Inbox`, specifically focusing on preventing spurious deployments within the Kona stack. The week's activity underscores a commitment to protocol security and the precise coordination of Superchain infrastructure during major network transitions.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol Maintenance & Hardfork Support
Following the recent Jovian hardfork, work began to ensure the registry reflects the updated state of the network.
*   **Proxy Updates:** A new pull request, [#1207](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1207), was initiated to update proxies across the registry. This is a critical step in maintaining the integrity of the Superchain's configuration data post-upgrade.

### CI/CD & Infrastructure
Efforts to enhance the security and verifiability of the project's build pipeline were noted.
*   **Provenance Attestation:** Work was proposed to add a GitHub Actions workflow for verifying `ci-base-clang` provenance attestation. While the initial issue ([#20122](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20122)) was consolidated into existing efforts, it highlights an ongoing focus on supply chain security for the registry's tooling.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The most significant technical discourse this week centered on the conditional deployment of interoperability components.
*   **Conditional CrossL2Inbox Deployment:** Discussions continued on [#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19311) regarding the logic for deploying the `CrossL2Inbox`. The conversation clarified that the primary objective is to ensure that `kona-derive` and `kona-proof` do not generate unnecessary deployments. This requires specific adjustments to the core crates used for post-interop derivation, ensuring that the `kona-node` and its associated proof systems remain lean and accurate.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by deep technical alignment between maintainers regarding the Kona stack. The dialogue between contributors (notably Inphi) regarding issue [#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19311) demonstrates a highly coordinated approach to cross-chain architecture. By refining the requirements for `kona-derive` and `kona-proof`, the team is actively preventing technical debt in the Superchain's interoperability layer. The immediate consolidation of new issues into existing ones also suggests an efficient triage process and a well-maintained backlog.
