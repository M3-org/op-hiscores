# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Weekly Report (Aug 23 - 29, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on critical infrastructure maintenance and the refinement of the Superchain ecosystem's footprint. Significant progress was made in repository consolidation and archiving strategies to streamline the developer experience, alongside successful testing of L1 chain deployments on the Plataberget testnet. While routine automated maintenance remained steady, the team also addressed complex stability issues within the Kona node and began navigating monorepo updates required for the Glamsterdam Defense. The week was characterized by a transition toward more rigorous configuration standards, notably with a new policy to reject Alt-DA chain configurations.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Infrastructure & Maintenance
*   **Automated Codegen Updates:** The repository maintained a consistent cadence of routine maintenance with daily automated code generation updates ([#1315](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1315), [#1316](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1316), [#1317](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1317), [#1319](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1319)).
*   **Alt-DA Configuration Policy:** A new operational pull request was introduced to reject new Alt-DA chain configurations ([#1318](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1318)), signaling a shift in how alternative data availability layers are integrated into the registry.

### Testnet Operations
*   **Plataberget Deployment:** Successful testing was conducted on the Plataberget (Glamsterdam) testnet. This involved bootstrapping the OP Contracts Manager (OPCM), deploying a new L1 chain, and verifying deposit and withdrawal paths. A simulated withdrawal was successfully executed by impersonating a proposer and creating a permissioned game, with finalization on the devnet expected around September 2, 2026.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Repository Archiving & Cleanup:** The team completed an "archive sweep" of abandoned repositories, including `optimism-starter` and `scaffold-op`. Audits confirmed that no live documentation links to these repos ([#1014](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/1014), [#1017](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/1017)).
*   **Protocol Stability:** An issue regarding `op-supernode` cross-safety wedges ([#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22325)) was closed after being identified as a known chain oscillation issue rather than a new bug. Additionally, a Kona genesis issue regarding unknown JSON keys ([#22682](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22682)) was closed as "not planned" to maintain backward compatibility with retired keys.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Kona Node Stability:** New reports emerged regarding `kona-node` sequencers getting stuck in `forkchoiceUpdated` loops ([#22681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22681)). There is also ongoing work to address performance stalls caused by remote-signer HTTP calls ([#22646](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22646)).
*   **Glamsterdam Defense Updates:** A significant new effort is underway to update the monorepo for Glamsterdam Defense ([#22698](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22698)). This includes upgrading Foundry and fixing CI failures across various contract tests (e.g., `OptimismPortal2`, `PreimageOracle`).
*   **Developer Tooling Gaps:** Following the archive sweep, a new issue was opened to find a permanent home for SuperchainERC20 compatibility checking, as developers currently lack a supported method for these audits ([#1020](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/1020)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was centered on organizational cleanup and technical troubleshooting. Maintainers coordinated closely on the "Starter Examples" consolidation ([#1015](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/1015)), debating the merits of plain copies versus subtree merges to preserve readability for users. There was also active engagement from developers offering to resolve Kona node performance issues, demonstrating a healthy feedback loop between protocol users and maintainers regarding P2P swarm stability and remote signer latency.
