# ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry Weekly Report (Apr 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on streamlining the Superchain’s upgrade path and refining core interoperability components. A significant shift occurred in the management of L2CM (L2 Chain Manager) milestones, which have been transitioned into the broader "Upgrade 19" process to ensure better alignment with governance and deployment schedules. Technical efforts were concentrated on resolving flaky test environments for flashblocks and finalizing the conditional deployment logic for cross-L2 infrastructure. The registry also saw maintenance updates for World Chain Jovian, signaling continued expansion and support for diverse network participants within the ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Network Upgrades & Maintenance
Work continued on ensuring the registry accurately reflects the latest chain configurations and upgrade paths.
*   **World Chain Jovian:** A new pull request ([#1208](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1208)) was initiated to handle chore-related upgrades for World Chain Jovian across both Sepolia and Mainnet environments.
*   **SuperDisputeGame Integration:** The integration of the SuperDisputeGame for new chain launches was finalized, marking a step forward in the security and fault-proof architecture for the Superchain ([#19014](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19014)).

### Interoperability & Core Infrastructure
Significant progress was made on the technical requirements for cross-chain communication and data availability.
*   **Conditional CrossL2Inbox Deployment:** Developers worked on the conditional deployment of the `CrossL2Inbox` for `kona-node` and interop features ([#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19311)). This includes ensuring end-to-end tests pass for `kona-derive` and `kona-proof` before merging core crate fixes.
*   **Supernode Data Handling:** Discussions progressed regarding how `op-supernode` handles "unsafe" data. The team is evaluating mechanical "super roots" to indicate data observation without requiring full semantic validation, potentially allowing EL-synced nodes to support proofs more rapidly ([#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19059)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **L2CM Milestone Transition:** Several high-level milestones were closed as they moved from the L2CM-specific tracker to the "Upgrade 19" process. This includes the Interop Betanet deployment ([#20088](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20088)), Sepolia activation ([#20090](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20090)), and associated governance documentation ([#20091](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20091)).
*   **Upgrade Testing:** Weekly fork testing for L2CM upgrades across all supported chains was successfully concluded ([#19766](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19766)).

### Active Issues & Blockers
*   **Flaky Test `TestFlashblocksStream`:** This issue ([#19883](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19883)) was reopened due to a new failure mode. Investigations revealed a mismatch in the flashblock protocol where sequences were incorrectly keyed by block number instead of `payload_id`. A fix is currently being tracked in the main `optimism` repository.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week demonstrated tight coordination between protocol engineers (notably **ajsutton**, **Inphi**, and **axelKingsley**) regarding the nuances of supernode functionality and interop testing. The transition of L2CM milestones suggests a high degree of organizational synchronization between technical teams and governance contributors, ensuring that the "Upgrade 19" rollout is cohesive across the Optimism Collective. Discussions around `kona` and `op-supernode` indicate active cross-repository collaboration to stabilize the foundational layers of the Superchain.
