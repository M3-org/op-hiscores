### Reopened Issue: Deploy Input for Anchor Root and Prestate Hashes
Issue [#20912](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/20912), concerning the addition of starting anchor roots and prestate hashes to the deploy input, was reopened. The issue was initially closed but still has outstanding TODOs referencing it in the codebase. Specifically, deploy pipelines continue to use placeholder starting anchor roots, and both the interopgen deploy path and the OPCM V2 initial-deployment guard refer to this issue as the tracker for providing real anchor roots. The TODOs are located in `op-chain-ops/interopgen/deploy.go` and `packages/contracts-bedrock/src/L1/opcm/OPContractsManagerV2.sol`.
