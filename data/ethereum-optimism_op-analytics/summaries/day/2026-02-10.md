### Support for Multiple Sequencer RPC Endpoints
Discussion continued on [#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/763) concerning support for multiple sequencer RPC endpoints for transaction forwarding. The conversation revolves around the trade-off between user-friendliness and reliability. While the proposed change offers optional support for multiple comma-separated URLs to prevent outages due to single endpoint failures, concerns were raised about whether this is the most user-friendly approach compared to ensuring high reliability within a single URL.
