## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **`op-conductor` SEGFAULT during Leader Election:** Discussion continues on a critical bug where `op-conductor` experiences SEGFAULTs when a peer is killed during leader re-election. A recent comment confirms the problem is still present in the current version, particularly when sequencers are paused during migration from single to HA setups, as the system attempts to snapshot. ([#13363](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/13363))

*   **Follow-up for Blob Tip Oracle Activation:** This issue was reopened due to a remaining `TODO` reference in the codebase, indicating that the blob tip oracle activation is still pending confirmation in production regarding `newBlobSavings`. The `TODO` specifically asks to confirm that `newBlobSavings == true` occurs most of the time before activating the oracle. ([#18618](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/18618))

*   **Support for Multiple Sequencer RPC Endpoints:** The discussion revolves around the need for supporting multiple sequencer RPC endpoints for `op-geth` to enhance reliability. Recent comments highlight that the proposed change is backward compatible and aims to prevent outages caused by single endpoint failures. However, there's a counter-argument suggesting that building redundancy into a single URL, similar to web2 standards, might be a more user-friendly approach. ([#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/763))
