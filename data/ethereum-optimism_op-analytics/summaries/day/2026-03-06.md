### BigQuery Data Synchronization Hotfix
A hotfix was deployed to address an issue where the Dagster `chain_metadata` asset was not uploading data to BigQuery. This update ensures that the `api_table_uploads.op_stack_chain_metadata` table is now correctly updated, preventing data discrepancies that arose from the previous reliance on a defunct legacy CircleCI notebook runner. This fix involved modifications to `src/op_analytics/coreutils/bigquery/load.py`, `src/op_analytics/dagster/assets/chainsdaily.py`, and `src/op_analytics/datapipeline/chains/upload.py`.
