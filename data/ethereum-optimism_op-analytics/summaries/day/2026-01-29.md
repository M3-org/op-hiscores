## NEW ISSUES

### Migration of Historical Proofs Support
A new issue has been opened to track the migration of historical proofs support from `op-rs/op-reth` into `op-reth`. This migration is crucial for maintaining compatibility and ensuring that the code remains in both stable and unstable branches until cyclic dependencies with upstream reth are resolved. ([#18984](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/18984))

### Integration of op-reth Snapshots
A new issue proposes adding op-reth snapshots to `datadirs.optimism.io`. This initiative aims to provide essential resources for verifiers and node operators utilizing Reth. ([#18473](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/18473))
