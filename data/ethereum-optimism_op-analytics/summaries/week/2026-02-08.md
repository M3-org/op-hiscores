# ethereum-optimism/op-analytics Weekly Report (Feb 8 - 14, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused heavily on refining data integrity and transaction qualification logic within the `other_chains_tracking` and `helper_functions` modules. Significant progress was made in protocol-level tracking with the activation of blob priority fee monitoring, while discussions continued regarding the reliability of sequencer RPC infrastructure. The team successfully addressed several critical bugs impacting EVM-related data retrieval, ensuring more accurate cross-chain analytics for the Superchain ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Data Integrity & Quality Assurance
The team addressed a critical quality issue within the `other_chains_tracking` module. This fix ensures the reliability of scripts responsible for tracking Total Value Locked (TVL) trends, contract labeling, and general EVM data retrieval ([#1790](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1790)).

### Transaction Qualification Refinement
A series of updates were implemented to improve how transactions are qualified across various data sources, including Dune Analytics and Google BigQuery.
*   **Logic Corrections:** Fixed qualification logic specifically within the `get_qualified_txs.ipynb` notebook ([#1792](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1792)).
*   **Cross-Module Synchronization:** Addressed qualification issues across multiple utility files and notebooks, including `duneapi_utils.py`, `google_bq_utils.py`, and transaction retrieval scripts ([#1791](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1791)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Blob Priority Fee Tracking:** The project successfully activated `op-batcher/txmgr` blob priority fee tracking, marking the completion of a significant monitoring milestone ([#18737](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/18737)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Blob Tip Oracle Activation:** Issue [#18618](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/18618) was reopened. While the logic exists in `op-batcher`, activation is currently pending production confirmation that the oracle consistently provides tangible benefits.
*   **Sequencer RPC Infrastructure:** Ongoing discussions persist regarding support for multiple sequencer RPC endpoints for transaction forwarding ([#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/763)). The debate centers on the trade-off between providing a user-friendly single URL versus the increased reliability of allowing multiple comma-separated URLs to prevent outages.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week's activity reflects a strong focus on internal protocol stability and data accuracy. Collaboration was most evident in the technical debates surrounding the `op-batcher` and sequencer reliability. The reopening of the Blob Tip Oracle issue suggests a cautious, data-driven approach to protocol changes, prioritizing production evidence over immediate deployment. Contributors are actively balancing the need for infrastructure robustness with the goal of maintaining a seamless developer experience.
