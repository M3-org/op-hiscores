# ethereum-optimism/op-analytics Weekly Report (May 31 - 6, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on critical infrastructure stability and the verification of cross-chain interoperability milestones. A major achievement was the successful verification of the Interop Proofs Devnet, confirming the withdrawal path on a forked Sepolia network. However, development efforts were primarily concentrated on diagnosing complex synchronization stalls within the `op-supernode` and `op-node`/`op-reth` stack. The team is also pivoting to address external dependencies, specifically the upcoming deprecation of the Blocknative API, to ensure continuity in market data collection.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no new code changes were merged during this specific reporting window, technical efforts were centered on deep-dive diagnostics of the Superchain's execution and derivation layers.

*   **Interop Proof Verification:** The team successfully validated the end-to-end withdrawal process for the Interop Proofs Devnet. This confirms that withdrawals can be proven and finalized, a vital step for the security and reliability of the Superchain's cross-chain architecture.
*   **Supernode Initialization Refinement:** Technical planning is underway to refactor how `op-supernode` handles execution layer connectivity. The proposed architectural change involves decoupling engine-controller initialization from the virtual node restart loop and implementing mutex guards to prevent permanent "engine client not initialized" hangs when the execution layer is temporarily unreachable.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The week saw significant activity in triaging complex synchronization bugs and responding to security audit feedback.

### Closed Issues
*   **Devnet Milestones:** The Interop Proofs Devnet verification issue ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/20489)) was closed following the successful proof of a withdrawal.
*   **Security & Documentation:** Feedback from the Lumi Security Audit regarding `doc.go` was addressed and closed ([#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21124)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sync Stalls and Engine API Failures:** Investigation continues into a stall where CL (Consensus Layer) sync recovery fails when blocks are missing from the EL (Execution Layer) ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21076)). A/B testing suggests the failure is localized within the `op-node`/`op-reth` Engine API path, characterized by incomplete block transitions that lead to frozen queryable states.
*   **Cross-Safe Backlog Recovery:** There is an ongoing investigation into why `op-supervisor` fails to drain backlogs faster than real-time after a stall ([#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21117)). Current analysis suggests that extended batcher outages may cause sequencing windows to expire, requiring manual intervention.
*   **API Deprecation:** A new high-priority issue ([#1797](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/1797)) was opened to replace the Blocknative gas estimation API before its shutdown on June 19, 2026, to prevent disruptions to market data scripts.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by intensive technical troubleshooting and collaborative debugging. Discussions on issues [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21076) and [#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21117) show a high level of coordination between contributors to reproduce edge cases in the synchronization logic. The use of A/B testing and detailed log analysis indicates a rigorous, data-driven approach to resolving protocol-level bottlenecks within the Superchain ecosystem.
