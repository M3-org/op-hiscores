# ethereum-optimism/op-analytics Weekly Report (Jul 5 - 11, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the team focused on stabilizing the network following the Karst hard fork and optimizing CI infrastructure. A critical intrinsic gas estimation error on Optimism Sepolia was successfully resolved and deployed via `op-reth`. Significant efforts were also directed toward improving developer experience and CI reliability by auditing fuzzing strategies and addressing flakiness in interop testing. These actions collectively aim to reduce maintenance overhead while ensuring protocol stability across the Superchain.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol & System Optimization
Efforts were made to streamline system operations and prepare for dependency updates.
*   **SystemConfig Efficiency:** A new initiative was launched to optimize the system by eliminating redundant calls to `SystemConfig` batch inbox getters, which aims to reduce unnecessary overhead in data retrieval [#1798](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1798).
*   **Dependency Management:** The team is currently monitoring the release cycle for `revm` to update dependencies to version 2.4.0 / 2.3.1 within `op-reth` [#21524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21524).

### CI Infrastructure & Testing Strategy
A comprehensive review of the testing suite led to a strategic shift in how fuzzing and integration tests are handled.
*   **Fuzzing Strategy Pivot:** Following a detailed audit, the team decided to retire PR-time Go fuzzing jobs. The audit concluded that time-bounded exploration without a committed corpus provided minimal value. While the fuzz functions remain as deterministic seed tests, the removal of these CI jobs reduces maintenance overhead and CI minutes [#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21636), [#21639](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/21639).
*   **Interop Test Reliability:** Engineers identified a root cause for flakiness in the `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` test. The issue stemmed from an ordering conflict in `FollowSource` processing, leading to a proposed fix involving force-resets of active sequencers on upstream head advances [#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21635).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Sepolia Gas Estimation Fix:** The most significant resolution this week addressed an issue where `eth_estimateGas` failed with "intrinsic gas too high" on OP Sepolia post-Karst hard fork. The fix was integrated into the `op-reth/v2.3.3` release, restoring functionality for RPC providers and developers [#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21457).
*   **Fuzzing Audit:** The discussion regarding the efficacy of current fuzzing jobs was concluded with a decision to move toward more meaningful, nightly differential fuzzing targets rather than PR-level checks [#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21636).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Flaky Test Tracking:** The `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` remains a point of focus. A draft PR is currently in progress to implement the proposed fix for the "missing parent header" errors caused by stale queued build work [#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21635).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by deep technical audits and proactive troubleshooting. The resolution of the Sepolia gas issue involved monitoring multiple RPC providers, demonstrating a coordinated response to network-level regressions. Furthermore, the transparent evaluation of the fuzzing suite highlights a collaborative effort to balance rigorous testing with CI efficiency, ensuring that contributor workflows remain fast and reliable.
