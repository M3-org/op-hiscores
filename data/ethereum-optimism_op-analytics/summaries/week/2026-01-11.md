# ethereum-optimism/op-analytics Weekly Report (Jan 11 - 17, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on refining the data infrastructure for the Superchain and resolving critical post-fork execution bugs. A primary achievement was the update to the System Config Transform within Clickhouse, ensuring accurate tracking of system configurations across the ecosystem. Additionally, the team successfully addressed technical debt and bugs related to the `eth_simulateV1` functionality following the Jovian fork, improving the reliability of transaction simulation for developers.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Data Infrastructure & Clickhouse Transforms
The team focused on maintaining the integrity of Superchain metadata by updating the System Config Transform processes.
*   **System Configuration Updates:** Significant modifications were made to the creation and update scripts for `dim_superchain_system_configs_v1`. This work spanned both the core `op_analytics` source code and the `dagster` Helm charts, ensuring that Clickhouse accurately reflects the current state of the Superchain ecosystem ([#1782](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1782)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
The week saw the resolution of key issues centered around transaction simulation and protocol upgrades:

*   **`eth_simulateV1` Stability:** A critical error where `eth_simulateV1` failed to calculate the DA footprint (returning a "missing deposit transaction" error) following the Jovian fork was resolved. This fix was integrated and released in `op-geth` v1.101605.0 ([#739](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/739)).
*   **Testing & Validation:** To prevent future regressions in simulation logic, the team closed the tracking issue for implementing `eth_simulateV1` tests within the `op-e2e` framework ([#14690](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/14690)).

### New & Active Issues
No new issues were reported or reached high levels of activity (3+ comments) during this period.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by cross-component synchronization, particularly between the analytics data models and the execution layer (`op-geth`). The resolution of the Jovian fork-related simulation error demonstrates active monitoring of protocol upgrades and their downstream effects on developer tooling. The updates to the Helm charts and Dagster configurations indicate ongoing coordination between data engineering and infrastructure operations to maintain the Superchain's analytical backend.
