# ethereum-optimism/op-analytics Weekly Report (Feb 1 - 7, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the `op-analytics` team focused on hardening the reliability of core data infrastructure, specifically targeting data integrity within the BigQuery pipeline. A major fix was deployed to resolve upload failures that spanned multiple components of the stack, from local build configurations to cloud orchestration. Additionally, the team is actively investigating RPC-related failures in CI, coordinating with infrastructure teams to improve error handling for block ranges across the Superchain.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Data Pipeline & Infrastructure Reliability
The primary technical achievement this week involved a comprehensive fix for BigQuery data uploads. This effort addressed a critical bug that hindered the reliable loading of analytics data into the warehouse.
*   **BigQuery Upload Fixes:** The resolution required synchronized changes across the `Makefile`, Helm charts (`helm/dagster/values.yaml`), and core Python logic within `src/op_analytics`. These updates ensure that the Dagster-orchestrated pipelines can successfully interface with BigQuery, maintaining the consistency of the project's data products ([#1789](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1789)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The team is currently managing a significant blocker regarding CI stability and RPC reliability.
*   **CI RPC Failures:** Investigation continues into failing RPC calls within the `superchain-ops` repository, specifically regarding "block out of range" errors ([#17688](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/17688)). 
*   **Cross-Repo Coordination:** Technical discussions have identified that the issue may stem from how `proxyd` handles requests for future blocks. Instead of returning an error, the consensus is that the service should return `null`. This has led to active coordination with the infrastructure team to implement a fix at the proxy level.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by cross-functional troubleshooting between the analytics and infrastructure domains. The resolution of the BigQuery upload issue ([#1789](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1789)) demonstrated a holistic approach to debugging, involving both software engineering and DevOps configurations. Furthermore, the ongoing dialogue regarding RPC errors shows a healthy feedback loop between `op-analytics` contributors and the broader Optimism infrastructure maintainers to ensure Superchain data remains accessible and accurate.
