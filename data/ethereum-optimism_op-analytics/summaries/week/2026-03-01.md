# ethereum-optimism/op-analytics Weekly Report (Mar 1 - 7, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the primary focus was on stabilizing core data infrastructure and ensuring the reliability of the Superchain's metadata pipeline. The team successfully deployed a critical hotfix to the Dagster orchestration layer, resolving a failure in BigQuery data ingestion. This work marks a significant step in migrating away from legacy CI-based runners toward a more robust, automated pipeline for tracking OP Stack chain metadata.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Data Pipeline & Infrastructure
The technical priority this week was the restoration of automated metadata updates to ensure accurate reporting across the ecosystem.

*   **BigQuery Metadata Hotfix:** A critical update was implemented to the Dagster `chain_metadata` asset. Previously, the `op_stack_chain_metadata` table in BigQuery had stopped updating due to the failure of a legacy CircleCI notebook runner. The fix ensures that the Dagster pipeline now correctly handles the upload to the `api_table_uploads` schema, centralizing the process within the modern data stack and removing dependencies on deprecated infrastructure ([#1795](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1795)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The week was characterized by targeted maintenance rather than a high volume of new bug reports or feature requests.

*   **Closed Issues:** The primary resolution involved the successful transition of metadata uploads from legacy runners to the active Dagster pipeline, effectively closing the gap in chain metadata availability.
*   **New & Active Issues:** No new major issues were reported during this period, and there were no active discussions on existing issues exceeding the three-comment threshold, suggesting a period of stability following the infrastructure hotfix.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week centered on infrastructure reliability and the deprecation of legacy systems. The successful deployment of the BigQuery hotfix ([#1795](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1795)) demonstrates a continued effort by maintainers to streamline the developer experience by moving toward more maintainable, automated tooling. The lack of new reported issues indicates that the current focus remains on hardening existing data pipelines that support the broader Optimism Collective.
