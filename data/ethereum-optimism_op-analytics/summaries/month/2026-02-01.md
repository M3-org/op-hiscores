# ethereum-optimism/op-analytics Monthly Report (February 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
During February 2026, development efforts for `op-analytics` focused on hardening the reliability of core data infrastructure. The primary achievement involved resolving critical data pipeline failures to ensure the integrity of BigQuery uploads. Additionally, the team addressed ongoing infrastructure challenges related to RPC stability across the Superchain, specifically targeting errors in block range requests that impact Continuous Integration (CI) workflows.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Data Pipeline Reliability
The month's primary technical focus was ensuring the stable transfer of data into the project's analytical warehouse.
*   **BigQuery Upload Fixes:** A comprehensive bug fix was implemented to resolve issues hindering reliable data uploads to BigQuery. This work involved cross-file modifications to the data pipeline and loading logic, ensuring that downstream analytics remain accurate and up-to-date ([#1789](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1789)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The project is currently managing a significant infrastructure-related issue that affects the stability of automated testing and data retrieval.
*   **CI RPC Failures:** Investigation continued into failing RPC calls within the `superchain-ops` repository, specifically regarding "block is out of range" errors ([#17688](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/17688)). 
*   **Infrastructure Coordination:** Technical discussions have identified a dependency on `proxyd` behavior. Contributors noted that `proxyd` should ideally return `null` for future blocks rather than an error, indicating that a permanent resolution may require coordination with broader infrastructure teams.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month centered on troubleshooting complex interactions between the analytics pipeline and the underlying Superchain infrastructure. Technical discussions involved key contributors like `sebastianst`, who provided critical insights into how proxy behavior affects CI reliability. The focus remains on cross-repository coordination to ensure that infrastructure errors do not block the analytics development lifecycle.
