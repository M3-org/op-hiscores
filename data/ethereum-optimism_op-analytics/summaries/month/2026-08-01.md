# ethereum-optimism/op-analytics Monthly Report (August 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In August 2026, the `op-analytics` project focused on hardening the stability of the Superchain ecosystem, specifically targeting node sequencer reliability and infrastructure migrations. Key efforts included resolving long-standing interoperability issues in `op-supernode` and initiating a transition from Blocknative to public RPCs for blob base fee tracking. The month was also characterized by a significant audit and consolidation of developer resources, ensuring that starter examples and documentation are streamlined as the ecosystem matures toward enhanced token interoperability.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Infrastructure & Data Migration
Efforts were initiated to modernize how the project tracks network fees and handles Ethereum-related data.
*   **RPC Migration:** A significant pull request, [#1800](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1800), was opened to replace Blocknative with a public RPC for blob base fee tracking and to migrate Eth-related data handling. This move aims to reduce dependency on specific third-party providers while maintaining data integrity for analytics.

### Repository Consolidation & Documentation
A major audit of the developer experience was conducted to clean up legacy repositories and improve the discoverability of starter tools.
*   **Starter Example Consolidation:** Based on a detailed migration analysis in [#1015](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/1015), the team is moving to consolidate five disparate `superchain-starter` repositories into a single primary repository. 
*   **Documentation Audit:** The team confirmed that archiving abandoned repositories (such as `superchainerc20-compatibility`) will not break live documentation, as current builds have successfully transitioned to the monorepo structure ([#1014](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/1014), [#1017](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/1017)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Supernode Stability:** The `op-supernode` cross-safe wedge issue ([#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/22325)) was closed. Investigation into a parent-hash mismatch on chain 901 determined the event was a rare occurrence during invalidation recovery rather than a systemic flaw.
*   **Genesis Configuration:** Issue [#22682](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/22682) regarding `kona-genesis` leniency was closed as "not planned." The decision was made to maintain support for retired keys, with existing registry tests deemed sufficient to cover unmodeled fields.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sequencer Reliability:** A critical new issue was identified in `kona-node` ([#22681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/22681)), where sequencers can become permanently wedged in a `forkchoiceUpdated` loop during engine reorgs. Additionally, work is ongoing to address `NetworkActor` stalling in `kona-node` ([#22646](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/22646)).
*   **SuperchainERC20 Compatibility:** A gap was identified regarding how developers verify `SuperchainERC20` compatibility ([#1020](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/1020)). This has become a high-priority item due to the potential revival of the `SuperchainTokenBridge` and the requirements for token interoperability on the Ink network.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month saw focused collaboration between contributors like `jefr90` and `jdodinh`, particularly in the areas of repository maintenance and node performance. `jefr90` led the effort to audit the repository landscape, providing detailed migration paths for legacy code. Meanwhile, technical discussions around `kona-node` stability showed active engagement in parity-testing overflow policies and latency injection to harden sequencer performance. The community is currently navigating a transition period, waiting for upstream protocol changes (like the revival of `SuperchainERC20`) to finalize the next steps for developer tooling.
