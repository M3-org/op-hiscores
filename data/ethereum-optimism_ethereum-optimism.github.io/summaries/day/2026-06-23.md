## CLOSED ISSUES

This included coverage for ETH supply conservation, post-execution settlement underflow paths, and handling of 0x7D mid-block/out-of-order index payloads.
- **PCD Infrastructure Design:** The design document for PCD Infrastructure support, [#21327](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21327), was closed after approval.

## NEW ISSUES

- **Reth Slot-Preimage Seeding:** A new feature request, [#21523](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21523), was opened to reuse the upstream slot-preimage seeding helper in `op-reth`, aiming to remove duplication from a previous issue. This is planned for the next reth release.

## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Kona-node SafeDB Development:** Discussion on [#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20278) indicates progress on the SafeDB implementation. A trait `SafeDBv1` has been defined as a 1-to-1 copy based on the design document, with plans to introduce `SafeDBv2` for interface and implementation experimentation. The importance of interoperability and proofs, while also considering improved designs over `op-node`'s approach, was highlighted.
- **Optimism Sepolia Intrinsic Gas Issue:** Issue [#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21457) continues to be active, with a user confirming the "intrinsic gas too high" error on OP Sepolia after the Karst hard fork. The issue affects `eth_estimateGas` on various RPC providers, though sending with an explicit `gasLimit` succeeds. This is a known issue and a fix is anticipated in the next release.
