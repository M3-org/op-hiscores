### Kona Hint Format
Issue [#19125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19125) discusses changing the Kona hint format from string serialization to a binary format. While kien-rise proposed the change for correctness and potential performance, ajsutton questioned the performance benefits and highlighted the backward compatibility requirements for the host, emphasizing that string-based hints must continue to be supported.
