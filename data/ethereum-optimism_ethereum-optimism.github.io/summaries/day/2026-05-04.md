## ACTIVE ISSUES

**Interop Expiry Window Stress Testing**
- In [#15285](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/15285), the latest update from Inphi details live devnet stress testing on `interop-jnt-v1`. A critical Kona EIP-2935 proof decoding issue was identified and fixed, which previously caused "unexpected RLP length" failures. The stress test involved repeatedly validating old messages, with the largest landed transaction repeating 4096 executing messages. Cannon/MIPS results showed linear scaling of cost with message count, indicating that the validation is expensive but terminates cleanly. The observed cost was roughly 5.77M VM steps per repeated executing message, with the practical upper bound appearing to be the L2 gas/block limit rather than interop validation. Ajsutton agreed that the analysis is sufficient to be confident about performance, especially after the RLP decoding fix was merged.

**L2CM Acceptance Testing on Betanet**

**Runbook for Super Root Chain Update**
- In [#20473](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20473), ajsutton suggested deferring the runbook update for changing a super root chain from permissioned to permissionless with OPCMv2 until after interop has shipped.
