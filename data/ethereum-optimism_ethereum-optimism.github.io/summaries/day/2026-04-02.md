## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Database Resync Tests
- [#19661](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19661): This issue, concerning flaky `TestPreserveDatabaseOnCLResync`, `TestTruncateDatabaseOnELResync`, and `TestCLUnsafeNotRewoundOnInvalidDuringELSync` tests, was reopened today after a resurfacing of the flake. The latest failure, `expected head to match: safe`, has been identified as having a different root cause than the original stale RPC address issue, prompting the creation of a separate issue to track it. Previously, the issue was closed due to consistent passes, with two of the three tests appearing fixed.

### Flaky SuperCannon Step Test
- [#19892](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19892): This newly opened issue details a flaky `TestSuperCannonStepWithPreimage_nonExistingPreimage` test. Deep investigation revealed the root cause to be the challenger stepping at the wrong trace index (one position before the preimage load) due to intermittent disagreement. This disagreement is attributed to resource contention in CI, causing kona execution to be prolonged or truncated, leading to incorrect claim values. A fix involving serializing subtests and sharing one interop system, combined with a recent kona fix, is recommended to mitigate the flake. Local testing also identified a consistent failure in the blob variant, likely due to stale prestate artifacts.

### Flaky ExecutePayloadSuccess Test
- [#19898](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19898): This new issue reports a flaky `TestExecutePayloadSuccess` test. Root cause analysis indicates a race condition where the test queries the validator immediately after sequencer funding, but the validator's proofs ExEx may not have indexed block 1 yet. This leads to "no state found for block number 1". The proposed fix involves adding `WaitForBlockNumber` synchronization between the sequencer and validator.
