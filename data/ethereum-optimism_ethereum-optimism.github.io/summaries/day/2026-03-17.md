## ACTIVE ISSUES

### op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness [#17631]
Discussion continues on the behavior of `op-node` marking the initial EL Sync target as finalized, even if not truly finalized. The latest comments propose solutions for a finalization gadget, including using a command-line flag, an RPC URL for a trusted checkpoint provider, looking at L1 for the last finalized head, or an arbitrary delay (e.g., 12 hours) for the finalized head after EL sync. The idea of delaying safe/finalized by the sequencing window is favored for its operational simplicity. The discussion also touched upon the implications for sequencer nodes and light CL clients.
