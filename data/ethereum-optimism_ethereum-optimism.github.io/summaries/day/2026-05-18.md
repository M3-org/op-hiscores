### Flaky Test Analysis and Potential Fixes
Discussions on [#20334](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20334) regarding the flaky test `TestELSyncSafeRetractedByOffset` continued. The latest comment from Ilya0527 suggests that a configuration issue with `OffsetELSafe = 10s` being globally active during the preset's baseline sync gate might be the cause, making the equality target unreachable. This could explain the "failed permanently" timeouts. The comment proposes a way to falsify this hypothesis by logging the delta between `sequencer.safe` and `verifier.safe` at timeout. Previous comments from wwared, ajsutton, and geoknee highlighted repeated occurrences of the CrossSafe-during-ELSync timing issue and the test failing even with increased retry attempts.
