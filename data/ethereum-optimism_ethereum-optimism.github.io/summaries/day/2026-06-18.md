## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona-node Synchronization and SafeDB
Discussions on [#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21243) (kona-node: implement syncmode.offset-el-safe) highlighted the need for `kona-node` to achieve parity with `op-node`'s `syncmode.offset-el-safe` behavior. It was noted that while `kona-node` doesn't persist the safe head, its EL node will after initial sync, potentially mitigating the issue. The conversation also linked to [#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20278) (kona-node: SafeDB), emphasizing that `kona-node` will eventually require a SafeDB, which should prioritize its own SafeDB head over the EL's safe head. Requirements for the new SafeDB design are to be gathered from Interop and Proofs.

### SDM Operator Flag Consistency
The issue [#21360](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21360) (SDM: improve consistency of opt in operator flag) saw further discussion on standardizing the naming and implementation of the SDM opt-in flag across `op-node`, `op-reth`, and `op-rbuilder`. The scope was narrowed to `op-reth` and `op-node`. A consensus was reached to use `operator_sdm_opt_in` as the consistent name for the flag, distinguishing it from `protocol_active`, which is a consensus/fork check.
