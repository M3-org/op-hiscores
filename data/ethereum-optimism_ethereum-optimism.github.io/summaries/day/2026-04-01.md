## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **Interop Transaction Pool Behavior**
    *   Discussion on [#19351](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19351) clarifies that `op-geth` drops all interop executing messages from the transaction pool on any reorg for intentional simplicity and stronger safety guarantees. The canonical behavior with `op-reth` will also drop all interop transactions. Future re-evaluation of this eviction behavior might consider re-verifying interop transactions before re-adding them to the mempool.
