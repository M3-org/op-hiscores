### op-node stuck in EL sync mode after restart ([#18468](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/18468))
The issue of `op-node` getting stuck in an initialization loop when configured with `--syncmode=execution-layer` after restarting with a synced Reth instance is being actively investigated. A temporary workaround suggested is to change the sync mode from EL sync to CL sync and restart `op-node`. The issue is affecting Base Mainnet nodes.
