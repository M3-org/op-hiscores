### SDM: add coverage in `rust/kona/tests/proofs` ([#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21344))
The discussion on this issue revolves around the necessity of adding further test coverage for SDM in `rust/kona/tests/proofs`. The latest comments suggest that existing coverage for the scenario (invalid PostExec tx leading to a deposit-only block) is already present in separate components. It's noted that the missing glue for testing an invalid PostExec payload specifically triggering the deposits-only fallback is currently not testable in `rust/kona/tests/proofs` due to the `op-geth` engine not having SDM execution. The current recommendation is to close the issue as the executor-level rejection and derivation-level fallback are covered elsewhere, and the combined scenario is dependent on a future change to `op-reth`.

### flaky test: `TestP2PFull` ([#22381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22381))
Investigation into the flaky `TestP2PFull` test has been conducted, with a detailed summary provided. The CI evidence shows two types of failures: `failed to dial ...: no addresses` and `noise: message is too short`. A strong candidate for the `no addresses` failures is a race condition involving disconnect cleanup and immediate reconnect. While a deterministic model reproduced this symptom, a broader validation with real bare libp2p hosts still showed failures, indicating the proposed TTL-only fix was insufficient. The root cause for the `noise: message is too short` error remains unknown. The conclusion is that there is insufficient evidence for a root-cause fix, and the issue should remain open for further investigation with improved logging and testing fixtures.
