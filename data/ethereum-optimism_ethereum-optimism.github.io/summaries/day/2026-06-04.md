## CLOSED ISSUES

### Sequencer Stability and Sync Testing Improvements
Several issues related to sequencer stability and sync testing were closed. This includes a fix for a follow-mode sequencer's `latestHead` failing to re-anchor past a `forceReset` under EL-sync, as detailed in [#21125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21125). Additionally, the integration of `op-supernode` with `op-sync-tester` and the addition of an extended nightly sync test were addressed and closed in [#21096](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21096).

### Transaction Pool and Metric Issues
A critical issue where `op-reth`'s interop transaction pool did not resume after a failsafe was disabled, requiring a restart, was resolved and closed in [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21149). Furthermore, a bug concerning very high cardinality for exported metrics in Kona was fixed and closed in [#19927](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19927).

### Test Infrastructure and Flake Resolution
Flaky tests and test infrastructure issues were also addressed. A persistent flake in `op-e2e-fuzz` related to "Too long with no output" was investigated and a root cause identified, leading to its closure in [#21191](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21191). An issue regarding `op-up`'s `TestRun` not being executed in CI due to binary dependencies was closed in [#21202](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21202) as `op-up` was deleted. A reported flaky test `TestL2OutputSubmitter/honest` was closed as filed in error, as it was not actually run for the referenced PR, as noted in [#21231](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21231).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### op-reth-test-engine Development
Issue [#20415](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20415) discusses the development of `op-reth-test-engine` for minimal in-test EL for `op-e2e/actions`. The latest comment indicates that implementation is underway as a stacked series of PRs. The first PR, [#21216](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/21216), establishes the foundation with an `EphemeralChain` and `TestEngine` wrapper. The approach confirms reuse of the real `op-reth` stack, avoiding reimplementation of storage, validation, or block building. The remaining stack involves one-shot block execution, a stateful in-flight payload builder, lib API finalization with a UDS JSON-RPC binary, and OP-semantics golden-vector tests.
