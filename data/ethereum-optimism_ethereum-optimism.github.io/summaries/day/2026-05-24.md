### Addressing Flaky Test in Supernode Resync Logic
Discussions on [#20944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20944) continued regarding a flaky test related to `TestSupernodeResyncResumesAtActivation_PostActivation`. A draft fix has been proposed in `ethereum-optimism/optimism#21002`. The root cause was identified as `verifiedDB` being closed prematurely during supernode shutdown, leading to incorrect cold-start interpretations and subsequent issues with `op-reth` accepting regressed finalized blocks. The proposed fix involves propagating read errors from `VerifiedBlockAtL1` and `LatestVerifiedL2Block` and signaling use-local fallback on verifier errors. Follow-up actions include addressing `op-reth`'s acceptance of non-monotonic finalized blocks.

### Expanding User Metrics Beyond Farcaster IDs
