## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SDM Devnet Rollout and Benchmarking
Discussion on the rollout of an alpha/beta devnet for SDM ([#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21273)) provided a detailed update. The `sdm-net-0` spec has been merged, and the network creation workflow is awaiting approval of generated artifacts and k8s manifests. A gap analysis against the `kupcake` devnet identified needs for contracts and transaction load, an SDM Grafana dashboard, and a policy hot-enable/disable feature (which requires protocol code). A risk was noted regarding `rpc-2/sync-2` running `kona-node` CLs and their ability to derive post-exec transactions. The plan includes finishing the rollout, verifying SDM live, standing up load, porting the SDM dashboard, reviving the hot-toggle with the Core team, and conducting baseline benchmarks.

### Auditing `op-reth` CLI Flags
The issue concerning auditing `op-reth` CLI flags and removing unsupported combinations ([#21687](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21687)) received further context. The discussion revolves around whether to remove the "minimal" pruning option or adjust its defaults and clarify its usage, as it's a preset of different pruning settings. This is partially motivated by a related issue and recent updates to documentation on supported pruning configurations.

### Kona-SP1 Proposer Game Defense Strategy
On the newly opened issue regarding which games the `kona-sp1-proposer` should defend when challenged ([#22111](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22111)), a comment suggested that no changes should be made at this point. The current mechanism of keeping the challenger's bond for every valid game proven is considered sufficient, as it will be higher than the proving cost. It was noted that if overwhelmed, it would be a "proof of whale" attack, a known weakness, and until bond economics improve, the defense would be to blacklist or retire games.
