## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Light-Sequencer Recovery Oscillation:** Discussion continued on an issue concerning the `op-node`'s light-sequencer configuration, where recovery from divergence between its unsafe head and the upstream safe chain oscillates, making net progress of approximately one block per two epochs ([#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21964)).
    - A new occurrence of the oscillation was logged, where the height-based gate was defeated. This happened during a `memory-all-opn-op-reth-prague` job on `develop`, resulting in a `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` failure.
    - The log showed a `Follow Source: Reorg onto upstream chain` followed immediately by the sequencer sealing its own-fork block, leading to an `Invalid forkchoice state` error and a deriver system reset.
    - The issue highlights that the oscillation can lead to the `sync-start` recovery path relabeling a local fork as safe/finalized, which is a correctness problem rather than just a convergence-time issue.
    - The previous fix (a gate at `sys.L2BCL.Reached(safety.CrossSafe, settledBlock, 45)`) was deemed insufficient because after a force-set, the CL reports cross-safe (and finalized) at the correct height but on the wrong chain, making height-based safety-label checks unable to distinguish forks.
    - A "smell" was noted where a locally-built, never-derived fork block is marked as `finalized` in the engine forkchoice, which is intended for EL-sync bootstrap but is reachable in normal light-sequencer operation due to the oscillation.
