Joshklop reported that a local devnet is now running, with plans to establish a long-running devnet. The current strategy involves waiting for `glamsterdam-devnet-4` images and attempting a local devnet using `bal-devnet-6` images. ([#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20691))

### `op-revm` Synchronization and Updates
Discussions continued regarding `op-revm` support and its synchronization with new `revm` releases. Wodann expressed interest in REVM's latest release (v111) for its Amsterdam hardfork support and shared type definitions. Sebastianst confirmed the aim to stay in sync with new `revm` releases, noting that dependency updates often coincide with `reth` updates. He also mentioned plans to update guides for more eager dependency updates. ([#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21069))

### Interop Transaction Pool Recovery
Jelias2 provided an update on interop-bench-3 remediation, clarifying that restarting both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` on sequencers is necessary to restore interop after clearing a failsafe. A failsafe-state gauge metric is proposed in [#21151](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21151) to improve observability. ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21149))

### Invalid Interop Message Recovery Runbook
Ajsutton outlined key areas for the invalid interop message recovery runbook, including addressing issues [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21149), [#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20006), and [#21125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21125), as well as ensuring the failsafe is disabled.
