### Investigating Light Sequencer Oscillation
A new issue, `chore(op-node): Fix light-sequencer follow-source ↔ sequencer oscillation during recovery` ([#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21964)), was opened to address an oscillation problem during recovery in the light sequencer configuration. An occurrence was logged where this oscillation led to a correctness problem, with the sync-start recovery path relabeling a local fork as safe/finalized, causing a test failure. The mechanism involves engine resets flip-flopping between follow-source references and locally-built blocks.
