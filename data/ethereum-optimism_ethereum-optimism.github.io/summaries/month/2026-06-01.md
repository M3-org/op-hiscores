# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Monthly Report (June 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In June 2026, development focused heavily on hardening the **Superchain interop layer** and improving the stability of the **op-reth** execution client. Significant progress was made in resolving critical synchronization deadlocks and transaction pool stalls that previously hindered sequencer reliability. The team also prioritized developer experience by streamlining Docker build processes and expanding token metadata for the ecosystem. These efforts collectively move the protocol toward a more resilient and observable multi-chain infrastructure.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
The technical work this month centered on infrastructure stability and ecosystem expansion through the following initiatives:

### Infrastructure & Build Optimization
Efforts were made to stabilize the build pipeline and dependency management for core services.
*   **Docker & Dependency Management:** Addressed a critical bug in Dockerfiles to ignore lifecycle scripts during dependency installation, which improved build reliability. This included restoring the `@eth-optimism` registry in `.npmrc` and `Dockerfile.bot` ([#1324](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1324)).
*   **Token Metadata Expansion:** New pull requests were merged to expand the supported assets on Base and other chains, including the addition of WRP (Warp) and wTXM token metadata ([#1326](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1326), [#1327](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1327)).

### Client Stability & Performance
Significant engineering hours were dedicated to the `op-reth` stack to ensure it meets the demands of the Superchain.
*   **CL Sync Recovery:** Resolved a cyclic deadlock in `op-reth`'s `StateTrieOverlayManager` that caused Consensus Layer (CL) sync to stall when Execution Layer (EL) blocks were missing. A two-patch fix was implemented to prevent the engine from wedging during `rayon` compute operations ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21076)).
*   **Metric Cardinality:** Fixed a bug in Kona that caused excessively high cardinality for exported metrics, which previously impacted observability performance ([#19927](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19927)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The project saw a high volume of activity in resolving blockers related to interop testing and sequencer synchronization.

### Closed Issues
*   **Sequencer & Sync Testing:** Resolved issues where follow-mode sequencers failed to re-anchor past a `forceReset`. The team also successfully integrated `op-supernode` with `op-sync-tester` and added extended nightly sync tests ([#21125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21125), [#21096](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21096)).
*   **Interop Transaction Pool:** Fixed a critical issue where the `op-reth` interop transaction pool failed to resume after a failsafe event without a full restart ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21149)).
*   **Test Infrastructure:** Investigated and resolved a persistent "Too long with no output" flake in `op-e2e-fuzz` by bounding RPC/engine calls ([#21191](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21191)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Interop Recovery Runbook:** Work has begun on a formal runbook for recovering from invalid interop messages and handling failsafe events ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21161)).
*   **op-reth-test-engine:** Development is underway for a minimal in-test EL for end-to-end actions. The foundation has been laid with `EphemeralChain` and `TestEngine` wrappers to reuse the real `op-reth` stack ([#20415](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20415)).
*   **Security & Gas Limits:** Ongoing discussions regarding a potential state-persistent DoS in `OptimismPortal2` ([#20820](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20820)) and the implementation of `MAX_GAS_LIMIT` enforcement for SDM refunds to cap sequencer computation ([#20709](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20709)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was characterized by deep technical synchronization between the Optimism and Reth teams. Notable interactions include:
*   **Upstream Coordination:** The identification of the `op-reth` deadlock led to a coordinated effort to flag the issue for an upstream fix in the main `reth` repository.
*   **Dependency Alignment:** Discussions between contributors like `wodann` and `sebastianst` highlighted a commitment to keeping `op-revm` in sync with the latest `revm` releases (v111) to support the Amsterdam hardfork.
