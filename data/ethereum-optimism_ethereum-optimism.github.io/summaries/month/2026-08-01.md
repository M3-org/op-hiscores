# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Monthly Report (August 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
During August 2026, development efforts focused on expanding the Base ecosystem through new asset integrations and addressing critical stability issues within the `op-node` infrastructure. The month was characterized by routine maintenance of token lists alongside deep technical investigations into sequencer recovery mechanisms. A primary challenge emerged regarding light-sequencer oscillation, where divergence between unsafe heads and upstream safe chains hindered recovery progress, highlighting a need for more robust fork-choice derivation.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The most significant technical hurdle identified this month involves the stability of the light-sequencer configuration during chain recovery.

*   **Light-Sequencer Recovery Oscillation ([#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21964)):** Developers are investigating a persistent issue where the `op-node` oscillates when attempting to recover from a divergence between its unsafe head and the upstream safe chain. 
    *   **Technical Detail:** Analysis of CircleCI logs revealed that the light sequencer's Execution Layer (EL) block was creating a local fork, triggering `AssertTxInBlock` failures. 
    *   **Impact:** Even when the `follow-source` resets onto an upstream chain, the sequencer immediately seals its own-fork block, leading to an inconsistent-forkchoice deriver reset. This cycle defeats current height-based gates and represents a significant blocker for consistent light-sequencer synchronization.
