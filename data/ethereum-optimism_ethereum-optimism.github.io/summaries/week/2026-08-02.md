# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Weekly Report (Aug 2 - 8, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development efforts focused heavily on hardening the OP Stack's reliability, specifically targeting sequencer failover stability and interop testing frameworks. A major milestone was reached in the Conductor system with the completion of user-visible behavior porting to modern testing suites. While the team successfully mitigated high-probability test failures related to `op-conductor` availability, new investigations into cross-chain safety stalls under merge-queue load have emerged as a primary technical focus for the coming week.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no major feature completions were finalized this week, significant progress was made in refining the protocol's derivation logic and ecosystem expansion:

### Protocol Derivation & Validation
*   **Post-Execution Transaction Logic**: The team finalized a technical determination regarding `PostExecTxs` validation. It was confirmed that both `op-node` and Kona will treat these transactions as opaque bytes during the derivation process, delegating the responsibility for deeper validation to the Execution Layer (EL). This clarifies the boundary between the rollup node and the execution engine.

### Ecosystem Expansion
*   **Token Integrations**: The Superchain ecosystem continues to grow with the proposal to integrate the AAH (AliensAreHere) token on Base ([#1347](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1347)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The week was characterized by a deep dive into testing infrastructure and the resolution of flaky CI behaviors that impact developer velocity.

### Closed Issues
*   **Testing Infrastructure Migration**: The project successfully ported Conductor system coverage from `op-e2e` to the `op-acceptance-tests` and `op-devstack` DSL ([#21902](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21902)). This move improves the maintainability of sequencer failover tests.
*   **Sequencer Failover Stability**: A persistent flaky test in the Conductor RPC suite ([#22094](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22094)) was resolved by removing problematic rapid back-to-back leadership transfers, providing a more stable baseline for testing `op-conductor` availability.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Supernode Interop Races**: Significant effort is being directed toward resolving `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` ([#22234](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22234)). Investigators identified a race condition where the `safe-label` gate fails to stabilize by-number reads. A fix is currently in draft ([#22321](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22321)) to implement a polling mechanism for canonical block assertions.
*   **Cross-Safety Stalls**: A new critical investigation ([#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22325)) was opened to address instances where cross-safe progress appears to stop following local-safe post-replacement, particularly when the `ResendUntilSafe` budget is exhausted under heavy load.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was centered on rigorous debugging and protocol architecture discussions. The resolution of the `PostExecTxs` issue involved cross-component coordination between `op-node` and Kona maintainers to ensure consistency across different implementations of the OP Stack. The active investigation into interop testing ([#22234](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22234)) demonstrates a high level of technical engagement, with contributors identifying distinct failure signatures (Signature A and B) to systematically dismantle complex race conditions in the Superchain's interoperability layer.
