# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Weekly Report (May 10 - 16, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on hardening the Superchain infrastructure, specifically addressing critical vulnerabilities in interoperability verification and refining deployment tools for the `SuperDisputeGame`. Significant effort was directed toward diagnosing persistent flakiness in the end-to-end test suite, particularly regarding sequencer behavior and L1 reorg handling. While some retroactive improvements for the L2CM project were deferred to prioritize core stability, the team successfully synchronized cross-client behavior between `op-program`, `kona-client`, and `op-supernode` to ensure secure block transitions.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interoperability & Protocol Security
The team focused on ensuring consistency across the various components of the Optimism stack to prevent invalid state transitions.
*   **Vulnerability Remediation:** A critical issue where Kona accepted invalid interop activation-block transitions was resolved. This fix ensures that `op-program`, `kona-client`, and `op-supernode` maintain consistent validation logic ([#20684](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20684)).
*   **Circulating Supply Tracking:** Initiated a dry run for circulating supply rehearsals ([#1314](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1314)), a necessary step for financial transparency and governance monitoring.

### Deployment & Infrastructure
Progress continued on streamlining the deployment of complex governance and dispute mechanisms.
*   **SuperDisputeGame:** The deployment of `SuperDisputeGame` via `op-deployer` and `netchef` is nearing completion. Successful devnet deployments to `PermissionedSuperDisputeGame` and subsequent upgrades have been verified ([#19652](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19652)).
*   **L2CM Retroactive Improvements:** Documentation for Go vs. EVM gas accounting was finalized, though global gas accounting changes were deferred for deeper investigation ([#20388](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20388)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **L2CM Invariants:** Discussions regarding invariant definitions for the L2CM project were closed as they transitioned into broader architectural conversations ([#20389](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20389)).
*   **Protocol Security:** As noted in Key Developments, the Kona interop activation vulnerability was successfully addressed.

### Active Issues & Blockers
*   **L1 Reorg & Head Stalls:** A significant investigation is underway regarding `op-supernode` stalling after deep L1 reorgs. Current findings suggest the issue lies within `op-node` or `op-supernode` rather than the batcher, specifically involving `currentL1` references getting stuck at zero ([#20134](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20134), [#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19944)).
*   **Persistent Test Flakiness:** Several high-priority tests continue to fail intermittently, impacting CI reliability:
    *   `TestFollowSource_HeadsDivergeThenConverge`: Reopened due to convergence timeouts where `op-reth` remains stuck in a syncing state ([#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19821)).
    *   `TestFlashblocksTransfer`: Evidence suggests an underlying `op-rbuilder` bug persists despite improved error reporting ([#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19934)).
    *   `TestWithdrawal_Permissioned`: Reporting extreme runtimes exceeding 20 minutes before failure ([#20345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20345)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week's activity highlights a strong collaborative effort between protocol engineers to align different client implementations (Kona, Reth, and the OP Stack). The decision to defer certain L2CM retroactive tasks ([#20390](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20390)) reflects a pragmatic approach to project management, prioritizing immediate protocol safety and the completion of the current L2CM milestone over non-blocking enhancements. Technical discussions remain heavily focused on the nuances of L1/L2 synchronization and the reliability of the sequencer's transaction pool.
