# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Weekly Report (Jan 11 - 17, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on refining protocol simulation capabilities and expanding the ecosystem's asset support. A significant milestone was reached with the resolution of issues surrounding the `eth_simulateV1` functionality, ensuring stability following the Jovian fork. Additionally, the project continued its steady growth through the integration of new community tokens, maintaining the repository's role as a central hub for the Optimism ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Ecosystem Asset Integration
The repository continues to serve as a registry for the expanding Superchain ecosystem. 
*   **Token Additions:** A new pull request was initiated to add the Kleros Pinakion (PNK) token to the platform ([#1254](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1254)). This reflects ongoing efforts to support diverse governance and utility tokens within the Optimism ecosystem.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues: Protocol Simulation & Stability
The primary technical focus this week was the successful resolution of bugs related to transaction simulation, specifically addressing regressions introduced during protocol upgrades.

*   **`eth_simulateV1` Resolution:** Two critical issues regarding the `eth_simulateV1` function were closed. 
    *   A bug that caused the error "error calculating DA footprint: missing deposit transaction" following the Jovian fork was identified and fixed. The resolution was officially released in `op-geth` version `v1.101605.0` ([#739](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/739)).
    *   To prevent future regressions, a new end-to-end test for `eth_simulateV1` was implemented in `op-e2e` ([#14690](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/14690)).

### New & Active Issues
There were no new issues opened or high-activity discussions reported during this period, suggesting a stable week for the repository's maintenance.
