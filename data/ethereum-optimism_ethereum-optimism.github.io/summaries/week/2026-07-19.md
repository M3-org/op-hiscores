# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Weekly Report (Jul 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week’s activity was headlined by the successful completion of the `op-geth` decoupling project, a major milestone in streamlining the OP Stack’s core architecture. By migrating call-sites to `op-core`, the team has significantly reduced the dependency of `op-node`, `op-service`, and `op-batcher` on the `op-geth-fork`. Parallel efforts focused on stabilizing the testing environment, specifically addressing persistent integration flakes in `reth-optimism-node`, and maintaining ecosystem branding through metadata updates.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol Architecture & Decoupling
The primary technical achievement this week was the finalization of the `op-geth` decoupling initiative. This work ensures that core components no longer reference transaction or parameter symbols from the Geth fork outside of specific differential tests.
*   **Core Refactoring:** The scope of work involving the transition to `op-core/*` across the stack was completed. The final changes were landed via PRs [#21791](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/21791) and [#21904](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/21904), effectively isolating the protocol's core logic from specific execution client implementations.

### Ecosystem & Branding
*   **Metadata Updates:** A new pull request, [#1341](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1341), was introduced to update the ENSC logo and description, ensuring accurate representation of ecosystem partners within the project's public-facing repository.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **op-geth Decoupling (Issue [#20263](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20263)):** This major architectural issue was marked as done. All sub-tasks related to swapping call-sites for `op-node`, `op-service`, and `op-batcher` have been resolved.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Reth Integration Stability:** Discussion persists regarding issue [#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20973), which tracks a `reth-optimism-node` integration test flake causing SIGSEGVs during teardown. Developers are currently evaluating a workaround proposed in PR [#20983](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/20983) that bypasses a race condition in upstream `reth`.
*   **Security & SDM Coverage:** On issue [#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21344), which focuses on expanding test coverage for `rust/kona/tests/proofs`, a contributor has requested a secure communication channel with core developers to discuss a non-public technical concern regarding SDM and Kona components.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week demonstrated strong internal coordination among core maintainers, particularly in the final push to close out the decoupling project. Technical collaboration is also evident in the cross-component troubleshooting of the `reth` integration tests, where contributors are weighing the trade-offs between upstream fixes and local workarounds. The request for private disclosure on the Kona/SDM issue highlights a mature approach to security and technical sensitivity within the contributor community.
