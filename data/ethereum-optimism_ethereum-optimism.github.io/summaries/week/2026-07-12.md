# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Weekly Report (Jul 12 - 18, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on expanding the Superchain ecosystem through new asset listings on Base and Base Sepolia, alongside critical refinements to the OP Stack's dispute and execution layers. Significant technical discussions centered on optimizing `op-challenger`'s dispute game handling and ensuring `op-revm` synchronization for upcoming Hardhat 3 support. While the team addressed infrastructure stability by investigating integration test flakes in `reth-optimism-node`, they also made strategic decisions to streamline the `op-e2e` testing suite by retiring legacy pre-Regolith tests.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Ecosystem Expansion: New Asset Listings
The project continues to grow its supported assets on the Superchain, specifically focusing on synthetic dollars and testnet tokens.
*   **Synthetic Dollar Integration:** Two new pull requests were introduced to add synthetic dollar assets to the Base network: skUSD (Staked Kerne Synthetic Dollar) [[#1337](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1337)] and kUSD (Kerne Synthetic Dollar) [[#1336](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1336)].
*   **Base Sepolia Updates:** Support for NMR was proposed for the Base Sepolia testnet [[#1338](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1338)].

### Protocol & Tooling Refinement
Work progressed on maintaining the foundational technology of the OP Stack, focusing on synchronization and interface consistency.
*   **`op-revm` Synchronization:** Efforts are underway to ensure the `op-revm` crate remains compatible with Hardhat 3, specifically regarding Glamsterdam support. This includes a push for more frequent releases and the implementation of a formal changelog.
*   **Dispute Game Interface:** Developers explored simplifying `op-challenger` by adding `closeGame()` to the generic `IDisputeGame` interface. This change aims to streamline permissioned dispute game handling and facilitate advancing the `AnchorStateRegistry` after finalization [[#21678](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21678), [#21708](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21708)].

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **RPC Binding Defaults:** The community resolved a debate regarding RPC binding defaults [[#16487](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/16487)]. The decision was made to maintain `0.0.0.0` as the default to ensure Docker compatibility, noting that security risks are mitigated by the opt-in nature of privileged APIs.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Integration Test Stability:** A recurring `SIGSEGV` flake in the `reth-optimism-node` integration tests [[#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20973)] remains a priority. The issue appears linked to the payload builder service and runtime teardown, occasionally blocking merge-queue checks.
*   **Testing Suite Technical Debt:** The team is folding pre-Regolith test issues [[#21451](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21451)] into a broader effort to retire the in-process `op-geth` L2 execution engine in `op-e2e` tests. This will likely involve requiring bedrock/regolith co-activation.
*   **Data Fetcher Reliability:** Issue [#18430](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/18430) was reopened to address a TODO regarding the implementation of retries for all requests within the `OPSuccinctDataFetcher`.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by active technical coordination between maintainers and contributors like **Wodann** and **pauldowman**. Discussions were highly focused on protocol-level consistency, such as the alignment of `op-revm` releases with developer tooling and the standardization of dispute game interfaces. The collaborative decision-making process was evident in the resolution of the RPC binding issue, where maintainers balanced security concerns with operational requirements for containerized environments.
