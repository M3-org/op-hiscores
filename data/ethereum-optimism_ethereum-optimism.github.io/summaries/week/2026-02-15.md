# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Weekly Report (Feb 15 - 21, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on the continued expansion of the Superchain ecosystem and the investigation of critical performance bottlenecks within the node infrastructure. A significant effort was initiated to integrate GNET by Galactica.com into the ecosystem's official documentation and tracking. Meanwhile, technical attention remains heavily focused on a persistent memory leak affecting `op-reth` nodes, which appears to be impacting a broad range of operators. The week's activity underscores the dual priority of scaling the network's reach while maintaining the stability of its underlying execution clients.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The primary focus of the community's troubleshooting efforts this week centered on protocol stability and client performance.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Persistent Memory Leak in op-reth:** Significant discussion continues regarding a memory leak in `op-reth` ([#19212](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19212)). The issue is triggered when database read transactions remain open for excessive periods, resulting in a specific system warning. Recent reports indicate that this is not an isolated incident but is recurring across all nodes for certain operators, suggesting a widespread issue that may require a deep architectural fix in how the execution client handles long-running transactions.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by active troubleshooting between node operators and maintainers. The engagement on the `op-reth` memory leak issue highlights a high degree of transparency and communication, as contributors work to identify the scope of the performance degradation. The submission of new ecosystem participants like GNET also demonstrates the steady influx of external contributors looking to align their infrastructure with the Optimism Stack.
