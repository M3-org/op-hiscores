# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Weekly Report (May 31 - June 6, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week’s development focused heavily on hardening the infrastructure for interoperability and refining the Superchain's execution layer. Significant effort was directed toward resolving recovery challenges in the interop transaction pool and establishing a long-running devnet using `bal-devnet-6` images. The team also prioritized protocol stability through critical Dockerfile fixes and strategic planning for `op-revm` synchronization with upstream releases to support the Amsterdam hardfork.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Build System & Infrastructure Stability
The team addressed critical bottlenecks in the containerization process to ensure more reliable deployments across the ecosystem.
*   **Dockerfile Dependency Optimization:** A fix was implemented to correctly ignore lifecycle scripts during dependency installation. This update also restores the `@eth-optimism` registry in `.npmrc`, leading to more robust and efficient Docker builds ([#1324](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1324)).

### Execution Layer & `op-revm` Integration
Discussions centered on the evolution of `op-revm` following its migration to the `ethereum-optimism` repository.
*   **Upstream Synchronization:** The team is planning to align `op-revm` updates with `reth` dependency bumps. A primary focus is the integration of REVM v111, which introduces Amsterdam hardfork support and refactors that allow for shared type definitions across REVM crates ([#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21069)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The week saw a surge in activity related to interoperability failure modes and devnet orchestration.

*   **Interop Recovery Challenges:** A significant issue was identified where `op-reth` fails to resume accepting interop transactions after the `op-interop-filter` failsafe is disabled. Investigation revealed that `op-rbuilder` also holds evicted state, necessitating a full restart of both components on affected sequencers. To improve observability, a new failsafe-state gauge metric has been proposed ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21149)).
*   **Operational Runbooks:** Work has begun on a formal runbook to guide recovery after invalid interop messages trigger large reorgs. This documentation aims to consolidate fixes for known issues like state eviction and failsafe management ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21161)).
*   **Devnet Deployment:** Progress continues on the devnet front. While a local devnet is now operational using `bal-devnet-6` images, the team is transitioning toward establishing a stable, long-running devnet environment ([#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20691)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by deep technical troubleshooting regarding the interop stack. The interaction between developers on issues [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21149) and [#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21161) demonstrates a tight feedback loop between identifying protocol-level bugs and creating operational documentation. There is also clear evidence of cross-project coordination as the team aligns its internal `op-revm` roadmap with the broader Ethereum execution client ecosystem (REVM and Reth).
