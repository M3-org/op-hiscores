# ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io Weekly Report (Jul 26 - Aug 1, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on advancing the infrastructure for the Superchain ecosystem, specifically through the rollout of the SDM (Superchain Dependency Manager) devnet and refining the security posture of ZK-fault proof components. Significant technical discussions centered on optimizing the `op-reth` execution client and defining defense strategies for the `kona-sp1-proposer`. While the week was characterized by planning and architectural refinement rather than high-volume code merges, the progress on `sdm-net-0` and the integration of new ecosystem participants like B0x signals continued momentum in scaling Ethereum's Layer 2 infrastructure.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no major pull requests were finalized during this specific window, the groundwork for ecosystem expansion and protocol optimization was laid through several new initiatives:

### Ecosystem Expansion
Efforts are underway to integrate B0x (B Zero X) into the Optimism ecosystem. This involves multi-chain support to ensure availability across both the Superchain and Ethereum L1.
*   **B0x Integration:** New pull requests were opened to add B0x to Base and Ethereum Mainnet ([#1344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1344), [#1343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1343)).

### Protocol & Execution Client Optimization
Technical planning focused on improving the efficiency of `op-reth` and the robustness of the ZK-proving stack.
*   **Reth Dependency Management:** A new initiative was launched to track the update of `op-reth` to version `2.4.2` or `2.5.0`. This update is intended to streamline the state-root wait process by leveraging upstream `reth` changes, potentially eliminating the need for proxy channels and detached OS threads ([#22115](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22115)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The week saw active engagement in resolving architectural bottlenecks and defining security parameters for new protocol features.

### New & Active Issues
*   **SDM Devnet Rollout:** Significant progress was reported on the `sdm-net-0` alpha/beta devnet ([#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21273)). The team is currently navigating a gap analysis involving contract deployment, transaction load generation, and the implementation of a "hot-toggle" feature for protocol policies. A potential risk was identified regarding `kona-node` CLs and their ability to derive post-execution transactions.
*   **Kona-SP1 Proposer Strategy:** A critical discussion is ongoing regarding the `kona-sp1-proposer` game defense strategy ([#22111](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22111)). The current consensus leans toward maintaining the existing bond-claim mechanism to deter attackers, acknowledging that while "proof of whale" attacks remain a theoretical risk, current bond economics provide a sufficient baseline defense.
*   **CLI Flag Audit:** The team is auditing `op-reth` CLI flags to remove unsupported configurations, specifically focusing on the "minimal" pruning option to prevent user misconfiguration and align with updated documentation ([#21687](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21687)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was marked by deep technical coordination between the Core team and developers working on the ZK-stack and SDM. The discussions surrounding the `kona-sp1-proposer` defense strategy highlight a transparent approach to protocol security, where contributors are weighing economic incentives against technical proving costs. Furthermore, the cross-team effort to align `op-reth` with upstream `reth` developments demonstrates a commitment to maintaining a lean and performant execution layer for the Superchain.
