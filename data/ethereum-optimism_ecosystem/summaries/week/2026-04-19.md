# ethereum-optimism/ecosystem Weekly Report (Apr 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the Optimism ecosystem focused on stabilizing interoperability features and refining protocol security infrastructure. Significant progress was made in finalizing Interop specifications and advancing the **SuperDisputeGame** roadmap, with Phase 1 Kona proof work nearing completion. While the team successfully resolved several critical bugs related to block replacement and contract feature flags, efforts were heavily concentrated on triaging persistent flaky tests in the CI pipeline. Infrastructure security also saw a boost through updated ownership protocols for sensitive cloud-security components.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interoperability & Protocol Logic
The team reached several milestones in the development of cross-chain functionality and node behavior:
*   **Specification Finalization:** Interop specifications were officially updated and closed, signaling a transition from design to stable implementation ([#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/905)).
*   **Kona & Supernode Refinement:** Resolved conditional deployment issues for `CrossL2Inbox` in `kona-node` and finalized application behaviors for `op-supernode` ([#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19311), [#18742](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/18742)).
*   **Block Replacement Fixes:** Addressed a critical bug in `kona` where output root mismatches occurred during message expiry consolidation ([#19848](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19848)).

### Contract Infrastructure & Security
*   **OPSuccinct Integration:** Successfully integrated `FeatureFlag` for `OPSuccinct` within the contract system and added the flag to the `OPCM _devFeatureBitmap` ([#18419](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/18419), [#18420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/18420)).
*   **Infrastructure Oversight:** Updated `CODEOWNERS` to include `@ethereum-optimism/cloud-security` for critical infrastructure files, ensuring tighter security reviews for sensitive changes ([#1001](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/pull/1001)).

### Maintenance & Dependency Management
*   **Security Patches:** Updated `axios` to version 3.0.0 to resolve security findings and bumped `postcss` to 8.5.10 ([#1002](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/pull/1002), [#1003](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/pull/1003)).
*   **Cleanup:** Removed unused `toml` dependencies from documentation to streamline the repository ([#20017](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/20017)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Validation Reviews:** Concluded the review of `kona-interop` internal block creation, confirming correct validation handling ([#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19266)).
*   **CI Stability:** Closed a recurring Out-Of-Memory (OOM) error affecting `rust-tests` during node compilation ([#19935](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19935)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Dispute Game Roadmap:** Work continues on **Interop Phase 1 & 2**. Phase 1 (SuperDisputeGame adoption) is targeting completion of Kona proofs this sprint, while Phase 2 (Shared Dispute Game) has successfully merged main contract PRs ([#19690](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19690), [#19691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19691)).
*   **Flaky Test Investigations:** Multiple tests are under heavy scrutiny due to flakiness, including `TestUnsafeGapFillAfterUnsafeReorg` (race conditions), `TestTruncateDatabaseOnELResync` (CrossSafe promotion bugs), and `TestFlashblocksTransfer` (nil channel panics) ([#19936](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19936), [#19897](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19897), [#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19934)).
*   **At-Risk Tasks:** Development on "Verification-Aware CurrentL1" for the Batcher and `op-conductor` recovery from interop reorgs are currently flagged as "at risk" due to shifting priorities toward immediate devnet sync issues ([#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19944), [#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/20006)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw active technical coordination regarding the transition of execution clients. Maintainers clarified the deprecation timeline for `op-geth`, confirming its support until May 31st while encouraging the transition to `op-reth` ([#20203](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/20203)). Collaboration remains high between the protocol and security teams, evidenced by the restructuring of file ownership and the rapid response to CI failures that impact the broader contributor workflow.
