# ethereum-optimism/ecosystem Weekly Report (Jan 18 - 24, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week’s activity focused on critical infrastructure maintenance and architectural planning for the Superchain ecosystem. Key efforts centered on resolving technical debt within the `op-supernode` reset logic and advancing the conversion of deployment tools to Forge. Additionally, the team prioritized security and performance through a significant version bump of the Hono library across multiple interoperability services.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Dependency Management & Security
The team executed a major update to the project's web framework dependencies to ensure stability and access to the latest features.
*   **Hono Library Upgrade**: Updated `hono` from version 4.7.5 to 4.11.4. This change impacted core ecosystem applications, specifically `ponder-interop` and `sponsored-sender`, ensuring consistent middleware and routing performance across interop services ([#973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/pull/973)).

### Infrastructure & Tooling Evolution
Ongoing work continues to refine how the Superchain is deployed and managed at the protocol level.
*   **Forge Deployer Conversion**: Development continues on the transition of `DeploySuperchain` to a Forge-based workflow. Recent activity provided historical context on the original implementation, aiding the current effort to modernize the deployment stack ([#17215](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/17215)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Active Issues & Blockers
The most significant technical discussions this week revolved around node consensus and head management within the Superchain.

*   **Supernode Reset Handling**: A primary focus remains on the challenges of resetting the "unsafe head" in `op-supernode`. Current limitations in `op-geth`'s `FCU` (Fork Choice Updated) behavior prevent moving the unsafe head back to a canonical block. 
    *   **Status**: The team is currently pursuing a tactical workaround involving a synthetic block update to force the desired state. 
    *   **Future Outlook**: Long-term resolution will likely require a new Engine API method to handle these resets more gracefully ([#18740](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/18740)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by deep technical troubleshooting regarding protocol-level constraints. The discussions on issue [#18740](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/18740) highlight active coordination between developers to find a balance between immediate "hotfixes" and sustainable long-term API changes. Furthermore, the involvement of original contributors like @bitwiseguy in providing context for the Forge conversion demonstrates a strong continuity of knowledge within the Optimism Collective.
