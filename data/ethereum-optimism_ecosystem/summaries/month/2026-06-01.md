# ethereum-optimism/ecosystem Monthly Report (June 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In June 2026, development focused heavily on hardening the infrastructure for interoperability and refining the build pipeline. Key efforts were directed toward resolving critical Docker build reliability issues and addressing edge-case recovery scenarios for interop transactions. Significant progress was also made in local devnet stability, transitioning from initial setup to the establishment of long-running environments, while the team formalized synchronization strategies for `op-revm` to ensure compatibility with the Amsterdam hardfork.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Build System & Infrastructure Reliability
Efforts this month focused on improving the consistency of the development environment and build processes.
*   **Docker Build Optimization:** A critical fix was implemented to ensure Docker builds ignore lifecycle scripts during dependency installation. This change improves build reliability and prevents unexpected script execution during the containerization process ([#1007](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/pull/1007)).
*   **Devnet Evolution:** The project successfully transitioned from initial local devnet testing to the next phase of infrastructure deployment. Following the availability of `glamsterdam-devnet-4` images, the team has established a running local devnet and is now moving toward a long-running devnet configuration ([#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/20691)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The month saw the identification of several technical hurdles related to the Superchain's interoperability layer and execution engine synchronization.

*   **Interop Recovery & Failsafes:** Two significant issues were raised regarding chain recovery. One involves a bug where the `op-reth` transaction pool fails to resume accepting interop transactions after the `op-interop-filter` failsafe is disabled, currently requiring a full restart of `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/21149)). Additionally, work has begun on a runbook to handle large reorgs triggered by invalid interop messages, targeting systems that do not currently handle these events gracefully ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/21161)).
*   **`op-revm` Synchronization:** Ongoing discussions are centered on keeping `op-revm` in sync with upstream `revm` releases (specifically v111). This is critical for supporting the Amsterdam hardfork and maintaining shared type definitions. The team is moving toward a strategy of more aggressive dependency updates, particularly when `reth` dependencies are bumped ([#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/21069)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was characterized by high-level technical coordination between maintainers regarding protocol upgrades. Contributors like **Wodann**, **Sebastianst**, and **Joshklop** were instrumental in aligning the project's execution engine dependencies with the broader Ethereum roadmap. The discussions around `op-revm` and devnet infrastructure indicate a strong focus on cross-team synchronization as the project prepares for upcoming hardforks and more complex interoperability testing.
