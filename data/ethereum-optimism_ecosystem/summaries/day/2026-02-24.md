### op-supernode SLA Updates
The issue [#19180](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19180) addresses the ill-defined and underperforming SLA of the Superroot object in `op-supernode`. Recent comments include ajsutton noting that some tests had to be skipped in [#19302](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19302) due to missing partial `OptimisticAtTimestamp` data. axelKingsley also commented that Optimistic Roots should be a best-effort attempt, with any unobtainable roots simply being omitted or given empty values.
