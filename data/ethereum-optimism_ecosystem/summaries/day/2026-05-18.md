**Flaky Test Investigation and Analysis**
Discussions continued on the flaky test `TestELSyncSafeRetractedByOffset` ([#20334](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/20334)). The latest comment suggests a potential configuration issue where `OffsetELSafe = 10s` is globally installed before a baseline sync gate, making the equality target unreachable. This could explain the consistent timeout at 72s. The commenter proposed logging the delta between `sequencer.safe` and `verifier.safe` to confirm if the offset is indeed blocking the gate or if it's a genuine ELSync convergence issue under load.
