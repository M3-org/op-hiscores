- **op-reth Proofs-History SIGBUS Crash**: Issue [#20934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/20934) was reopened as the SIGBUS crash during deep FCU rewinds and the infinite retry loop remain unresolved. The current hypothesis points to a potential race condition during unwind/reorg flows in Reth, specifically with `File::set_len(ftruncate)` truncating mmap pages while another thread is reading them.
