## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona Outdated Just Recipes [#18654](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/18654)
Discussion continues regarding the removal of outdated `just` recipes for building `kona-host/client`. @Inphi questioned the removal, stating the recipes are still convenient for local testing and prestate building, especially with recent changes in #18879. This suggests a need to retain or adapt these recipes rather than fully remove them.

### L2CM Upgrades Risk Modeling [#18869](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/18869)
@maurelian provided templates and examples for performing risk modeling (FMA or threat model) for the L2CM design, referencing existing FMA documents for OPCMv2. This indicates progress in establishing a structured approach to risk assessment for L2CM.

### Use binary format for Kona hints instead of String serialization [#19125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/19125)
The discussion revolves around the proposal to switch Kona hints from string serialization to a binary format. @ajsutton expressed skepticism about the necessity, questioning the performance benefits and highlighting backward compatibility requirements for the host. @kien-rise clarified that the primary motivation is correctness rather than speed, but acknowledged potential performance improvements and offered to propose an approach that preserves compatibility. This indicates a debate between technical purity, performance, and practical backward compatibility.

### Fix devfeatures cyclic dependency [#19151](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/19151)
Discussion on this newly opened issue suggests moving shared, OP protocol-specific code, such as development feature flags, to `op-core/devfeatures`. This aims to centralize and simplify the management of these flags, improving code organization and reducing cyclic dependencies.
