## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona Hint Format Optimization
Discussion continued on [#19125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/19125) regarding the proposal to use a binary format for Kona hints instead of string serialization. The primary motivation is to "make things right" rather than solely for performance, though performance improvements are anticipated. Concerns were raised about backward compatibility, with ajsutton questioning the necessity of the change if performance benefits are not significant and highlighting the need for the host program to continue supporting string versions for existing games. kien-rise acknowledged the backward compatibility concern and expressed willingness to propose an approach that preserves compatibility.

### Kona Proofs Outdated Recipes
On [#18654](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/18654), Inphi commented that the "outdated just recipes" for building kona-host/client are still needed for local testing and prestate generation, despite the migration of Kona in the monorepo. This indicates that the issue, which was previously reopened by ajsutton due to a TODO in the code, remains active as these recipes serve a practical purpose for developers.

### L2CM Upgrades Risk Modeling
On [#18869](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/18869), maurelian provided links to a FMA template and the OPCMv2 FMA, offering resources for performing risk modeling for the L2CM design. This suggests that the task of performing a risk assessment or threat model for the L2CM design is progressing with guidance on methodology.
