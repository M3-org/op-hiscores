## ACTIVE ISSUES
### Supernode Reset Handling Discussions
Discussions continued on [#18740](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/18740) regarding `op-supernode` reset handling. The latest comments from axelKingsley and geoknee outline potential solutions. The current plan is to initially implement a workaround by updating to a synthetic block and then back to the desired block, as described by geoknee. Other options, such as modifying EL FCU behavior or adding a new engine API method, are being considered for the long term. The complexity arises from the `forkChoiceUpdated` (FCU) behavior not resetting the unsafe head to a canonical block on `op-geth`.

### Forge Deployer Conversion
