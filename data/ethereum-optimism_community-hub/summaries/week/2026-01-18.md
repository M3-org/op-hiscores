# ethereum-optimism/community-hub Weekly Report (Jan 18 - 24, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development efforts focused on refining the Superchain deployment infrastructure and addressing critical synchronization logic within the `op-supernode`. Key discussions centered on resolving complex reset handling issues between the execution layer and the supernode to ensure protocol stability. Additionally, the project moved forward with standardizing data presentation for Retroactive Public Goods Funding (RetroPGF) and refactoring deployment scripts to leverage modern tooling like Forge.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While much of the week was dedicated to architectural discussions and ongoing refactors, the following technical progress was made:

### Data Standardization & UI Refinement
*   **Retro Funding History Formatting:** Work began on standardizing how numerical data is displayed within the retro funding history. PR [#1201](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/pull/1201) was introduced to ensure consistency across the community hub’s financial reporting interfaces, improving readability for governance participants.

### Deployment Infrastructure
*   **Forge Deployer Conversion:** The team continued the refactor of the `DeploySuperchain` process. This effort, tracked in [#17215](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/17215), aims to transition deployment logic to Forge, streamlining the developer experience and aligning the Superchain's deployment patterns with industry-standard Ethereum tooling.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The week's activity was dominated by deep technical triage regarding the interaction between the Supernode and the Execution Layer (EL).

### Active Issues & Technical Blockers
*   **Supernode Reset Handling:** Significant discussion occurred on [#18740](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/community-hub/issues/18740) regarding how `op-supernode` handles resets. The core challenge involves `forkChoiceUpdated` (FCU) behavior in `op-geth`, which currently does not reset the "unsafe head" to a canonical block as expected. 
    *   **Short-term fix:** The team is moving toward a workaround that involves updating to a synthetic block before reverting to the desired canonical block.
    *   **Long-term strategy:** Contributors are evaluating more robust solutions, such as modifying EL FCU behavior or proposing a new Engine API method to handle these edge cases more gracefully.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by high-level technical problem-solving among core contributors. The dialogue between **axelKingsley** and **geoknee** on the supernode reset issue highlights a collaborative approach to protocol-level debugging, weighing immediate workarounds against long-term API changes. There is also evidence of historical knowledge sharing, as seen in the Forge conversion task where contributors are tracing original implementations by **bitwiseguy** to ensure continuity during the refactoring process.
