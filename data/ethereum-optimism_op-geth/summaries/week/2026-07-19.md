# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Weekly Report (Jul 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on critical protocol integrity and the continued architectural decoupling of the OP Stack. A significant fix was implemented to correct transaction hashing for EIP-2718 types, ensuring proper canonical encoding for `PostExecTx`. Simultaneously, the project reached a milestone in its refactoring efforts by closing a major issue related to swapping call-sites to `op-core/*`, further isolating `op-geth` internals from other services. Meanwhile, the team continues to investigate complex integration flakes and sensitive technical concerns regarding the proof stack.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol Integrity & Transaction Encoding
- **Transaction Hashing Correction:** Developers resolved a bug where `PostExecTx` was generating incorrect hashes. The issue stemmed from transaction data being written verbatim during encoding rather than following the canonical EIP-2718 format. The fix ensures that non-legacy transactions are correctly routed through `prefixedRlpHash(type, inner)` for proper RLP encoding ([#805](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/805)).

### Architectural Refactoring
- **Op-Geth Decoupling:** Significant progress was made in the long-term goal of decoupling `op-geth` from other OP Stack components. By transitioning call-sites in `op-node`, `op-service`, and `op-batcher` to use `op-core/*`, the project is moving toward a cleaner, more modular dependency graph ([#20263](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20263)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
- **Service Decoupling:** The primary issue tracking the migration of internal service dependencies to `op-core` was closed, with remaining edge cases transitioned to specific follow-up tasks ([#20263](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20263)).

### New & Active Issues
- **Reth-Optimism Integration Flakes:** Investigation continues into a persistent SIGSEGV occurring during the `peers_negotiate_eth_69` test teardown. While initial theories regarding the `payload-builder` thread were refuted, current hypotheses focus on potential races between RocksDB background threads and C++ static destructors or issues with `mdbx_env_close_ex`. A workaround involving a different architectural approach is being considered to bypass the upstream race condition ([#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20973)).
- **Proof Stack Coverage:** A technical concern regarding SDM, Lagoon, and Kona has been raised. Due to the sensitive nature of the topic, discussions are being moved to secure private channels between core contributors ([#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21344)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week's activity highlights deep technical collaboration between maintainers, particularly in the debugging of integration tests. Detailed forensic analysis by @sebastianst and @einar-oplabs on the `reth-optimism-node` issue demonstrates a high level of rigor in maintaining CI stability. Additionally, the coordination between @nonsense, @geoknee, and community members regarding proof-related concerns indicates an active and security-conscious contributor environment.
