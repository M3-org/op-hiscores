# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Weekly Report (Aug 16 - 22, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week’s activity focused heavily on refining protocol stability and addressing technical debt within the networking and testing layers. A significant effort was directed toward resolving persistent P2P connection flakiness between `op-geth` and `op-reth`, alongside a deep dive into stalled storage proof tests that suggest underlying engine synchronization issues. The team also initiated a migration of STUN networking components to Pion v3 and continued benchmarking efforts for L2 genesis deployment to improve the `op-deployer` experience.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Networking Infrastructure
Work has begun on modernizing the networking stack to improve NAT traversal and peer discovery.
*   **STUN Migration:** A new pull request, [#807](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/807), was opened to migrate the `p2p/nat` STUN networking implementation to Pion v3. This update is expected to provide better maintenance and compatibility for peer-to-peer connectivity.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **P2P Interoperability:** The team resolved long-standing connection flakiness between `op-reth` and `op-geth` ([#18570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18570)). The fix involved normalizing peer-ID formats. Consequently, the `SKIP_P2P_CONNECTION_CHECK` workaround was removed, re-enabling standard P2P verification in Rust-based end-to-end system tests.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Storage Proof Stalls:** Investigation continues into a flaky `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract` ([#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20376)). Current findings suggest the issue is not due to concurrent devnet contention but rather an `EngineHandle` worker that fails to make progress despite receiving chain commitment notifications. The engine appears to "buffer" blocks and only flush them during teardown, indicating a potential synchronization bottleneck.
*   **Genesis Benchmarking:** Issue [#20916](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20916) remains active and was reopened to address remaining TODOs in `op-deployer`. The goal is to establish a data-driven minimum for the L1 anchor timestamp offset based on actual L2 genesis deployment durations, ensuring more reliable network launches.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw focused technical troubleshooting involving contributors like `claude` and `wwared`. There is a clear emphasis on automated quality assurance, evidenced by the `todo-issue-checks` CI job actively flagging incomplete tasks in the codebase. The collaboration between the Geth and Reth implementation contexts remains a high priority, as seen in the cross-client P2P stabilization efforts. The detailed analysis of the storage proof engine's behavior during teardown highlights a sophisticated level of debugging regarding the interaction between the sequencer and the execution engine.
