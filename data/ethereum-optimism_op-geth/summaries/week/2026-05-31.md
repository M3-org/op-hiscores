# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Weekly Report (May 31 - June 2, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused heavily on refining the **Superchain interoperability** stack, specifically addressing recovery mechanisms and stability in devnet environments. Significant progress was made in validating interop proofs, with the successful proving of initiated withdrawals on Sepolia forks. However, the team also identified critical edge cases in `op-supernode` and `op-reth` where unreachable execution layers or invalid interop messages can lead to permanent stalls or "stuck" states. Parallel to these protocol efforts, the team continued to streamline the CI/CD pipeline by removing deprecated publishing tools and preparing for future hardforks via `op-revm` updates.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### CI/CD & Infrastructure Optimization
Efforts were made to clean up the development pipeline and manage the lifecycle of deprecated components.
*   **CI Streamlining**: The team removed diff publishing to `op-geth.optimism.io` ([#801](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/801)). This process was identified as broken and no longer useful given the current deprecation status of certain `op-geth` components.
*   **Consensus Management**: A new pull request was introduced to add a "Karst deprecation kill switch" to the consensus/beacon module ([#802](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/802)), providing better control over legacy consensus paths.

### Interoperability & Devnet Progress
The project is moving toward more stable, long-running testing environments for the Superchain.
*   **Interop Proofs**: Successfully closed the Interop Proofs Devnet milestone by proving an initiated withdrawal and confirming its finalization on a Sepolia Anvil fork ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20489)).
*   **Devnet Scaling**: Local devnets are now operational, with the team shifting focus toward establishing long-running devnets once `glamsterdam-devnet-4` images are available ([#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20691)).
*   **Execution Layer Sync**: Discussions regarding `op-revm` support ([#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21069)) highlighted a move toward tighter synchronization with `revm` v111 to support the upcoming Amsterdam hardfork and shared type definitions.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Security & Documentation**: A security audit feedback issue regarding `doc.go` was resolved after being identified as noise from an unsolicited review tool ([#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21124)).
*   **Protocol Milestones**: As noted above, the Interop Proofs Devnet issue ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20489)) was successfully concluded.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Supernode Recovery Stalls**: A critical issue was identified where `op-supernode` becomes permanently stuck if the Execution Layer (EL) is unreachable at startup ([#21136](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21136)). Relatedly, stalls in CL sync recovery were traced to the `op-node`/`op-reth` Engine API path, where blocks are added to the canonical chain but not fully committed ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21076)).
*   **Interop Transaction Pool Failures**: Testing on `interop-bench-3` revealed that `op-reth` fails to resume its transaction pool after a failsafe is disabled, requiring a full restart of both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21149)).
*   **Recovery Runbooks**: Work has begun on a formal runbook to handle chain recovery following invalid interop messages that trigger reorgs ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21161)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week's activity shows high-intensity collaboration between developers working on the Interop stack. Discussions around issues [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21076) and [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21149) demonstrate a deep dive into cross-component debugging (involving `op-node`, `op-reth`, and `op-rbuilder`). The community is actively using A/B testing and trace captures to isolate failures in the Engine API, indicating a robust, data-driven approach to resolving complex synchronization bugs in the Superchain ecosystem.
