# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Weekly Report (Jan 11 - 17, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on enhancing protocol stability and aligning with upstream `go-ethereum` standards. A critical fix for `eth_simulatev1` was deployed in release `v1.101605.0`, resolving issues with transaction simulations on OP Stack chains. The team also initiated preparations for the "Karst" feature and the Osaka L2 upgrade, while continuing efforts to reduce technical debt through upstream synchronization. Significant progress was also made in the dispute monitoring and challenger infrastructure, streamlining how the Superchain handles multi-node metrics and provider integrations.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Upstream Synchronization & Maintenance
A primary focus this week was reducing the "diff" between `op-geth` and upstream `go-ethereum`. This ensures easier maintenance and faster adoption of new Ethereum features.
*   **Core Alignment:** Modifications were made to `core/txpool`, `crypto/ecies`, and `eth/fetcher` to align the codebase more closely with upstream changes ([#748](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/748)).
*   **Test Re-integration:** Plans were set to re-add an upstream test during the v1.16.8 merge that was previously removed due to compilation errors ([#749](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/749)).

### Transaction Simulation Improvements
The team resolved a high-impact issue affecting developers and searchers using simulation tools on Layer 2.
*   **`eth_simulatev1` Fix:** Resolved a bug where simulations failed due to missing deposit transactions. This fix is essential for backrunning strategies and accurate EVM state prediction ([#739](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/739)).
*   **End-to-End Testing:** To prevent regression, a new test for `eth_simulateV1` was integrated into the `op-e2e` suite ([#14690](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/14690)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Dispute Monitoring:** The Grafana dashboard for `op-dispute-mon` was updated to support new multi-node metrics, improving visibility into the health of the fault proof system ([#18205](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18205)).
*   **Challenger Infrastructure:** The integration of a supernode provider into the challenger was completed, marking a step toward replacing the legacy supervisor provider ([#18524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18524)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Karst & Osaka Upgrades:** Two new issues ([#746](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/746), [#747](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/747)) were opened to track the integration of the "Karst" feature and its enablement on Osaka L2s, signaling the next phase of protocol evolution.
*   **FCU Message Aggregation:** Discussion persists regarding excessive `FCU` (Fork Choice Update) messages. Recent analysis suggests a previous fix may have failed to properly buffer these calls, causing the system to perform an FCU on every safe head update ([#15393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/15393)).
*   **Kona CI Complexity:** There is an ongoing debate regarding the CI simplification for `kona-proof`. The core tension lies in whether `kona-host` should be released alongside `op-challenger` to ensure version consistency or maintained as a separate release ([#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18773)).

The focus on upstream synchronization and simulation accuracy reflects a community-wide commitment to maintaining a high-quality, developer-friendly environment for the Superchain.
