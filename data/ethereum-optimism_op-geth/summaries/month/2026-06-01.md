# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Monthly Report (June 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In June 2026, development focused heavily on hardening the Superchain’s interoperability layer and streamlining the legacy codebase. Significant milestones were reached in interop proofs, including the successful proving of initiated withdrawals on test forks. However, the month also revealed critical edge cases in cross-chain synchronization and node recovery, leading to the development of new failsafe mechanisms and recovery runbooks. Efforts to modernize the repository continued with the deprecation of legacy CI/CD components and the introduction of consensus-level kill switches.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### CI/CD & Repository Maintenance
Efforts were made to clean up the repository as `op-geth` transitions through its lifecycle. 
- **Legacy Component Removal:** The team removed diff publishing to `op-geth.optimism.io` in the CI pipeline, as the service was no longer functional and the project moves toward deprecation ([#801](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/801)).
- **Consensus Kill Switches:** A new pull request was introduced to add a Karst deprecation kill switch to the beacon consensus layer, providing more granular control over protocol transitions ([#802](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/802)).

### Interoperability & Supernode Infrastructure
The project made strides in cross-chain functionality while identifying infrastructure bottlenecks.
- **Interop Proofs:** Successfully demonstrated the end-to-end proving of an initiated withdrawal on an Anvil fork of Sepolia, confirming that the finalization logic functions correctly outside the dispute window ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20489)).
- **Devnet Evolution:** Local devnet environments are now operational, with the team shifting focus toward establishing long-running devnets following the integration of `glamsterdam-devnet-4` images ([#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20691)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
- **Security & Documentation:** A security audit feedback item regarding `doc.go` was resolved and closed after being identified as an unsolicited advertisement for a review tool ([#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21124)).
- **Withdrawal Finalization:** The Interop Proofs Devnet issue was closed following the successful validation of withdrawal flows ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20489)).

### New & Active Issues
- **Sync & Recovery Stalls:** A critical investigation is underway regarding `op-node`/`op-reth` CL sync recovery stalls. A/B testing suggests that when SafeDB references a block missing from the Execution Layer, the Engine API path fails to commit transitions, leading to timeouts ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21076)).
- **Interop Resilience:** New issues were opened to address the `op-reth` transaction pool's failure to resume after an `op-interop-filter` failsafe is triggered. Current remediation requires a full restart of both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21149)).
- **Supernode Initialization:** A bug was identified where `op-supernode` becomes permanently stuck if the Execution Layer is unreachable at startup, as it currently lacks a retry mechanism for engine client initialization ([#21136](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21136)).
- **Recovery Runbooks:** Work has begun on a formal runbook for recovering chains after invalid interop messages trigger large reorgs, which currently stress multiple system components ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21161)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month saw deep technical coordination between contributors working on different layers of the OP Stack. Discussions regarding `op-revm` support highlighted a commitment to staying synchronized with upstream `revm` releases (specifically v111) to leverage Amsterdam hardfork support and shared type definitions ([#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21069)). Collaboration was also evident in the troubleshooting of the `interop-bench-3` environment, where cross-team debugging identified that `op-rbuilder` was holding evicted state, necessitating a broader restart strategy than initially anticipated.
