# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Monthly Report (August 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In August 2026, development focused on hardening the networking layer and addressing persistent flakiness within the end-to-end testing suite. A significant effort was initiated to modernize P2P infrastructure by migrating STUN networking to Pion v3. While the team successfully resolved long-standing peer-ID normalization issues between `op-reth` and `op-geth`, new challenges emerged regarding indexing latency in the proofs Execution Extension (ExEx) under heavy CI loads. The month was characterized by a rigorous cleanup of technical debt, specifically targeting unaddressed benchmarking TODOs that were impacting CI stability.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Networking Infrastructure Modernization
The project began a transition to improve NAT traversal and peer discovery mechanisms.
*   **STUN Migration:** A new pull request, [#807](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/807), was opened to migrate the `p2p/nat` STUN networking implementation to Pion v3. This update is intended to provide a more robust and maintained foundation for handling network address translation in P2P connections.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **P2P Compatibility:** The team successfully resolved issue [#18570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18570), which involved flakiness in connections between `op-reth` and `op-geth`. The resolution involved normalizing peer-ID formats (via #18684) and removing the `SKIP_P2P_CONNECTION_CHECK` workaround (via #22490), effectively re-enabling full P2P verification in Rust-based E2E system tests.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Proofs ExEx Latency:** Issue [#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20376) was reopened due to recurring failures in `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract`. Investigations suggest a race condition where the proofs indexing engine fails to advance past genesis during concurrent-devnet CI runs. This appears to be a latency issue rather than a protocol bug, requiring further investigation into why the indexing worker stalls until teardown.
*   **Benchmarking Technical Debt:** Issue [#20916](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20916) remains active and was reopened to address lingering TODOs in `op-deployer`. These TODOs concern the enforcement of minimum values for L1 anchor timestamp offsets and the implementation of benchmarks based on L2 genesis deployment times. The automated CI TODO checker currently blocks progress here until these references are reconciled.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month saw active technical coordination between contributors (notably `claude` and `wwared`) to diagnose complex interactions between the Execution Layer (EL) and the proofs indexing engine. The reopening of issues based on automated CI failures (such as the TODO checker) indicates a high level of reliance on automated governance and code quality tools to maintain the integrity of the OP Stack. Collaboration also extended across the Superchain ecosystem, as evidenced by the cross-client work to ensure `op-geth` and `op-reth` interoperability.
