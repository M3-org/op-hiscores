### ZK Game Integration Clarification
Issue [#18692](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18692) discusses the relationship between `OptimisticZkGame` and `op-succinct`'s `OPSuccinctFaultDisputeGame`. The latest comment from ajsutton clarifies that `OptimisticZkGame.sol` is not yet production-ready and its integration with `OPContractsManager` is incomplete. The team is working on integrating `op-succinct` contracts into the standard OP Stack with modifications for multiple ZK engines, but there's no firm timeline. For current ZK support, `op-succinct` or Kailua are suggested, though they are not yet Governance approved or part of the standard OP Stack.
