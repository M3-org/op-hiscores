### ZK Game Integration Clarification
Issue [#18692](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18692) regarding the relationship between `OptimisticZkGame` and `op-succinct`'s `OPSuccinctFaultDisputeGame` was closed today. The latest comment from ajsutton clarified that `OptimisticZkGame.sol` is not yet production-ready and its integration with `OPContractsManager` is ongoing. It was noted that while work is underway to integrate `op-succinct` contracts into the standard OP Stack with modifications for multiple ZK engines, there is no firm timeline. For current ZK support, `op-succinct` or Kailua were suggested, with the caveat that these are not yet Governance approved or considered part of the standard OP Stack.
