## CLOSED ISSUES

**CI Optimization and Flake Investigation**
- An issue regarding enabling `eatmydata` in CI to potentially speed up builds was opened and subsequently closed, as it was determined that `eatmydata` would not be effective for statically linked Go binaries, which constitute the majority of `op-geth`'s CI fsync pressure ([#20787](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20787)).

**Kona Interop Activation Gate Fix**
- A bug where the Kona init-chain interop activation gate was disabled when `interop_time` was `None` was closed, with a fix implemented in a related `optimism` pull request ([#20578](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20578)).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

**Flaky Test Investigations**
- **`TestWithdrawal_CannonKona` Flakiness:** Discussions continued on the `TestWithdrawal_CannonKona` flaky test ([#19964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19964)). The test was reopened due to a new flake variant, where the L2 EL (op-reth) fails to provide state for a block when a dispute game is published, suggesting a race condition. A fix is being drafted to retry `GetProof` calls with a bounded `Eventually` mechanism.
- **`TestELSyncSafeRetractedByOffset` Flakiness:** The `TestELSyncSafeRetractedByOffset` test continues to be flaky ([#20334](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20334)). Recent occurrences show the same preset-setup failure pattern, where the verifier's safe head lags the sequencer, leading to a timeout. This suggests a persistent CrossSafe-during-ELSync timing issue, even after increasing retry attempts.

**Interop Proofs Devnet Progress**
- The "Interop Proofs Devnet" issue ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20489)) received an update, stating that this network will be used for long-term internal testing and will not be spun down soon. The "Definition of Done" for this initiative includes successful superdispute games, withdrawals, proofs team approval, flashblocks with interop, and conductor re-orgs.

**Missing "base-mainnet" in op-node networks**
- An issue regarding the absence of "base-mainnet" in `op-node`'s available networks ([#20810](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20810)) was discussed. A user confirmed they had to downgrade to `v1.17.0` to resolve the issue.

**op-challenger-runner and op-reth for OP Mainnet**
- An update on the `op-challenger-runner` using `op-reth` for OP Mainnet issue ([#19517](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19517)) indicated that the team is still awaiting `op-reth` nodes with proofs history enabled for mainnet.

**Delete op-supervisor**
- The issue to delete `op-supervisor` ([#20124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20124)) was updated, noting that its deletion depends on a related issue in the `optimism` repository.
