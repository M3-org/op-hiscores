## ACTIVE ISSUES
- **Flaky Test: `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes`** ([#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19804)): The root cause of this flaky test was identified. The issue stemmed from `l1BlockWithLocalSafeBlocks()` polling the supernode's `RequiredL1` values, which could shift due to sequencer activity. A fix has been submitted in #19805, replacing supernode polling with direct L1 head reads to maintain invariants.
- **Flaky Test: `TestSuperCannonRootBeyondProposedBlock_ValidRoot/mt-cannon-next`** ([#19806](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19806)): This issue, reported by Claude due to a local test failure, was noted as not genuinely flaky.
- **Flaky Test: `TestFollowSource_HeadsDivergeThenConverge`** ([#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19821)): The root cause for this flaky test was identified. During `Supernode.Stop()`, `Interop.Stop()` cancels its context but doesn't wait for the `Start()` goroutine to exit, leading to panics when accessing a closed SafeDB. A fix has been proposed in #19824, which adds `ErrClosed` guards to SafeDB read/write methods to convert panics into graceful error returns.
