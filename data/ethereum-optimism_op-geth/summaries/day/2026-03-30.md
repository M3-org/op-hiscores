## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Flaky Test: `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes` ([#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19804))**: The root cause of this flaky test has been identified. The issue stems from `l1BlockWithLocalSafeBlocks()` polling the supernode's `RequiredL1` values, which can shift due to continuous block production during Stage 2's batcher choreography. A fix has been submitted in #19805, which replaces supernode polling with direct L1 head reads at each batcher choreography stage to maintain invariants.
- **Flaky Test: `TestFollowSource_HeadsDivergeThenConverge` ([#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19821))**: The root cause for this flaky test has been identified and a fix proposed in #19824. The problem occurs during `Supernode.Stop()`, where `Interop.Stop()` cancels its context but doesn't wait for the `Start()` goroutine to exit. This can lead to calls to `SafeHeadAtL1()` on a closed SafeDB, causing a panic. The proposed fix adds `ErrClosed` guards to SafeDB read/write methods, synchronized via `sync.RWMutex` and a `closed` flag, to convert the panic into a graceful error return.
