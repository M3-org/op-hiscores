### Flaky Fault Proof Tests
Discussions on [#19829](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19829) and [#19828](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19828) highlight new flaky tests in the `TestInteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes_FasterChainB` suite. The latest comments from @wwared and @pcw109550 confirm additional occurrences of these flakes, providing CI run contexts and error messages. The root cause is suspected to be a missing L1 head check in the Kona no-op transition path, as noted by @ajsutton. The issues describe the Kona interop program either exiting validly when an error is expected or failing with an invalid claim due to a mismatch in post-state commitments. These flakes were encountered during work on `ethereum-optimism/optimism#19253`.
