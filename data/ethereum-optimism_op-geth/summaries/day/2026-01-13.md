### Kona CI Simplification
Discussion on [#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18773) revolves around simplifying `kona-proof`'s CI by leveraging information from Kona's monorepo, following a related pull request in the `optimism` repository. Key points include the dual use of `kona/version.json` for both acceptance tests and `kona-host` releases within the `op-challenger` docker image. There's a debate on whether `kona-host` should be released as part of `op-challenger` to ensure consistency, similar to `op-program`, or if separate `Kona-host` releases are viable, despite potential confusion if they don't align with `op-challenger`'s included version.
