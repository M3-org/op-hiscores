### Simplifying Kona-Proof's CI
Discussion on [#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18773) revolves around simplifying `kona-proof`'s CI by leveraging information from Kona's monorepo, following the merge of a related PR in the `optimism` repository. A key point of discussion is the dual use of `kona/version.json` for both acceptance tests for `cannon+kona` and the release of `kona-host` in the `op-challenger` docker image. There's a desire for the acceptance tests to use the latest Kona from the monorepo. The management of `kona-host` releases is also being considered, with a preference for it to be built from the same commit as `op-challenger` to avoid confusion with separate `Kona-host` releases.
