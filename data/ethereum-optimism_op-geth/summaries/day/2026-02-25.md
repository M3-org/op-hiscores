## ACTIVE ISSUES

### L2 Contracts Manager (L2CM) Upgrade Enhancements
- **L2ContractsManager Contract Implementation** [#18830](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18830): Discussions focused on ensuring upgrade atomicity by preventing error swallowing patterns and confirming that `L2ContractsManager` avoids declaring storage variables to prevent `L2ProxyAdmin` storage corruption during `DELEGATECALL` execution.
- **L2CM Upgrade Gas Mechanism in op-node** [#18832](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18832): The conversation revolved around adding metadata to the JSON NUT bundle, specifically suggesting the inclusion of a `generatedFromCommit` field to track the source commit when the bundle is updated.
- **Solidity Script for Deterministic JSON NUT Bundles** [#18834](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18834): A key point raised was the need for a CI job to regenerate and verify the NUT bundle on every PR to prevent malicious or incorrect bundles from being merged.
- **L2 Upgrade Fork Testing Infrastructure** [#18837](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18837): Discussions emphasized the importance of including a test in the fork testing infrastructure to verify the initialization state of all predeploys post-upgrade, ensuring they are not left uninitialized or partially initialized.
- **L2CM Acceptance Tests** [#18870](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18870): It was highlighted that acceptance tests should verify the existence of the deterministic-deployment-proxy on all target L2 chains before the upgrade fork activates to prevent `ConditionalDeployer` failures.

### op-supernode SLA Updates and Reorg Handling
- **Superroot SLA Updates** [#19180](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19180): Progress was noted with a database update ready for review to allow fetching of optimistic vs. verified outputs. There was also a mention of skipping tests due to missing partial `OptimisticAtTimestamp` data.
- **Prune Denylist on L1 Reorg and Alert** [#19289](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19289): Discussions clarified that the `Supernode` level `Reset(chain, ts)` callback should be triggered for uncontrolled regressions of the Local Safe, and Chain Containers may need to prune their Denylists if the Local Safe regresses due to an L1 reorg.

### Geth Miner Historical State Fallback
- **Miner makeEnv historical state fallback unreachable** [#767](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/767): The discussion is ongoing, with a question raised about whether this issue is specific to `op-geth` or if it's an upstream Geth issue that should be addressed there.
