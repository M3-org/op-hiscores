## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Addressing L1/L2 Fork Mapping Duplication**: In [#21338](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21338), a detailed analysis by sebastianst revealed that the mapping between OP (L2) hardforks and their implied L1 (Ethereum) hardforks is duplicated in seven different locations within the Rust stack, not just three as initially thought. This duplication has led to a latent bug where the `Karst → Osaka` mapping is missing in one instance. A comprehensive plan has been proposed to centralize this mapping in `alloy-op-hardforks` as a canonical table, derive views from it, and rewire direct consumers. The plan also includes eliminating OP-fork knowledge from `kona-engine` and adding a consistency test for `op-revm` to prevent future divergences without adding unnecessary dependencies.

- **Optimizing Withdrawal Proof Constants**: Discussion continued on [#20774](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20774) regarding increasing `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and reducing air gap constants for faster ZK withdrawals. Inphi commented on the unbounded nature of max history for proofs, suggesting that with an hourly cadence of proposals, the max history for 1-day withdrawals would not exceed one day. They also noted that challenging does not require history as the withdrawal root in Isthmus allows for reconstructing the correct output root without touching state.
