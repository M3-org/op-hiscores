## ACTIVE ISSUES

### op-node: Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/17631))
Discussion continues on the behavior of `op-node` marking the EL sync target as finalized, even if not truly finalized.
- **Latest Proposal:** `trianglesphere` suggests continuing EL sync to the unsafe head and using a different finalization gadget. Options for this gadget include a command-line flag, an RPC URL for a trusted checkpoint provider, looking at L1 for the last finalized head, or an arbitrary delay (12 hours to 10 days) for the finalized head after EL sync finishes. `trianglesphere` leans towards a command-line flag or a 12-hour fallback.
- **Operational Simplicity:** `karlfloersch` finds the idea of delaying safe/finalized by the sequencing window operationally simple and effective for most of the issue.
- **Context on Flakiness:** `trianglesphere` clarifies that this issue is more likely to be triggered with light CL and that sequencer nodes are usually fully synced before service.
- **Historical Context:** `trianglesphere` previously noted that the current behavior was the intended design for EL sync, as re-running derivation on a snap-synced node doesn't work due to state availability.
- **Consolidation Approach:** `sebastianst` suggests running derivation from a prior L1 block and then consolidation to verify alignment with the EL-synced unsafe chain. They also propose fixing the code to only advance the unsafe ref, keeping safe/finalized at genesis initially.
