## NEW ISSUES

### RPC Performance: `eth_getLogs` Memory Exhaustion
Two new issues, [#768](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/768) and [#769](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/769), were opened to address a critical RPC performance vulnerability. Both issues describe how `op-geth` is susceptible to `eth_getLogs` memory exhaustion, similar to upstream `go-ethereum`, where wide block ranges can cause unbounded state scans and lead to OOM errors on RPC nodes. Issue [#769](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/769) was subsequently closed as a duplicate of [#768](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/768).
