### LightCL Genesis Boot Observation
The observation regarding `safe_l2`, `local_safe_l2`, and `finalized_l2` being zero on a fresh-genesis boot for LightCL [#20295](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20295) received significant attention. karlfloersch identified the likely root cause: genesis `L2BlockRef`s are being treated as unset state because several follow-source/status paths compare only the block `Number`. This leads to valid upstream genesis refs failing "is this ahead?" checks when the current local value is unset and the upstream value is the real genesis block. A recommended fix involves distinguishing "unset" from "genesis" using a full zero-value check/block identity, not just the block number, and adding a regression test. It was also noted that a related bug fix in #20352 might not cover this specific genesis/follow-source case.
