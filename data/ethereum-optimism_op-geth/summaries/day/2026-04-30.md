# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Daily Update (Apr 30, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
The day saw significant progress in transaction encoding with the addition of post-execution transaction support. Discussions continued on existing issues, including a flaky test and `eth_getLogs` behavior.

Encoding and decoding support for post-execution transactions has been added, introducing a new `PostExecTxType = 0x7d` and its implementation. This includes JSON marshal/unmarshal support and RPC metadata handling. ([#789](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/789))

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestStorageProofUsingMultiStorageContract` ([#19978](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19978))
The `TestStorageProofUsingMultiStorageContract` test continues to be flaky, with a recent failure on a PR unrelated to the ExEx manager. The job `op-reth-e2e-sysgo-tests` has a 91.3% success rate, indicating a ~9% flake rate on `develop`. The error consistently points to "no state found for block number 2" and a panic in the 'exex manager'.

### op-core Extraction: Decouple monorepo from op-geth ([#20257](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20257))
A new comment suggests including `PostExecTx` alongside `DepositTx` in the decoupling effort, indicating ongoing work to transition the monorepo's Go services from `op-geth` to upstream `go-ethereum`.

### Missing events on eth_getLogs calls immediately after newHeads + occasional high latency ([#667](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/667))
The latest comment suggests that the observed behavior of missing events might be due to backend freshness or propagation lag, rather than an `eth_getLogs` issue itself. It recommends verifying that websocket subscriptions and RPC calls are pinned to the same node and comparing block and log data before and after a delay to isolate the problem. BlockReq is also suggested as a tool for side-by-side comparison.
