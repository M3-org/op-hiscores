## OVERVIEW 
Today's focus was on a critical bug fix related to transaction encoding, ensuring correct hashing of PostExecTx. An important issue regarding op-geth decoupling was closed, marking significant progress in refactoring efforts.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Transaction Encoding Bug Fixes
- A bug was fixed where `PostExecTx` was incorrectly hashed due to its `Data` being written verbatim during encoding, leading to an incorrect canonical EIP-2718 encoding. The fix ensures that non-legacy transactions are routed through `prefixedRlpHash(type, inner)` for proper RLP encoding ([#805](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/805)).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Reth-Optimism-Node Integration Test Flake ([#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20973))
The discussion continues around the `reth-optimism-node` integration test `peers_negotiate_eth_69` which experiences SIGSEGVs on teardown.
- **Latest Update:** @einar-oplabs noted that a different approach proposed in a separate PR might solve the problem without directly addressing the upstream reth race condition, presenting an attractive workaround.
- **Previous Analysis:** @sebastianst provided an in-depth analysis, refuting the initial hypothesis that the SIGSEGV was caused by a detached payload thread polling a torn-down runtime. Experiments showed that the `select(on_shutdown, task)` wrapper prevents "polling a dead runtime" and that payload-builder threads are gone before `exit_group`. The analysis suggests the crash's actual mechanism is still unidentified, and the "own-runtime patch" is not being pursued as a fix.
- **Recommended Next Step:** The primary recommendation is to capture a core dump/faulting stack in CI to definitively identify the cause, as local reproductions have been unsuccessful.
