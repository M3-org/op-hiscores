## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Supernode/Reth CL Sync Recovery Stall
- Discussion on [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21076) continued with an update from a new A/B test. The issue, where CL sync recovery can stall when SafeDB/bookkeeping references an EL-missing block, was reproduced without `op-supernode`, indicating the immediate failure lies with the `op-node`/`op-reth` CL derive/build Engine API path. The key pattern observed is a half-completed transition where a block is added to the canonical chain but not fully committed, leading to timeouts. Further follow-ups include capturing traces and adding focused regression tests.

### Cross-Safe Backlog After Stall
- Issue [#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21117), concerning `op-supervisor`/`op-supernode` cross-safe not draining a backlog faster than real-time after a stall, saw a comment questioning if the local safe truly caught up to unsafe. The commenter suggested that a 12-hour delay implies the sequencing window expired, which would require manual intervention and a sequencer recovery mode to resolve.
