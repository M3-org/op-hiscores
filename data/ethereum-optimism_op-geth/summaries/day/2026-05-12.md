### Flaky Withdrawal Test Investigation
The flaky test `TestWithdrawal_Cannon` ([#19963](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19963)) was re-opened after flaking again with a different failure mode. The new failure occurred mid-test while polling on the prove transaction, with the error "Condition never satisfied: Sending prove transaction". An investigation by Inphi revealed that the failing assertion was triggered by `Eventually`'s 30-second timer, not a suite-level shutdown. The `Failed to send prove transaction` errors were due to `OptimismPortal_InvalidProofTimestamp()` because `estimateGas` was evaluated against the current L1 head while `block.timestamp <= disputeGameProxy.createdAt()`. A draft fix PR, [#20677](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20677), has been created to explicitly wait for `l1.head.Time() > game.createdAt()` before entering the prove-tx retry, ensuring the retry is correctly scoped to transient submission/confirmation.
