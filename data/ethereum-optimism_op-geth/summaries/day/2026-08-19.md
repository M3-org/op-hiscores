### Persistent Flakiness in P2P Connections and Proofs ExEx
Issue [#18570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18570), concerning flakiness in `op-reth <-> op-geth` connections, was closed today. The latest comment from claude at 2026-08-19T12:46:13Z indicates that the peer-ID format mismatch was fixed by normalization in #18684, and `op-geth` is no longer supported in devstack. The `SKIP_P2P_CONNECTION_CHECK` workaround was removed by #22490, which re-enabled P2P connection verification in rust e2e sysgo jobs.

Issue [#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20376), regarding a flaky test `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract`, was reopened. The latest comments from wwared at 2026-08-19T10:41:52Z and 2026-08-19T10:36:04Z highlight that the flake has resurfaced with a different symptom but the same underlying race condition. The proofs ExEx store on the validator consistently failed to advance past genesis for 30 seconds, while the validator EL imported blocks normally. The proofs indexing engine on the sequencer node also showed no activity during the test, only flushing blocks in a single burst during teardown. The issue is attributed to proofs-ExEx indexing latency under concurrent-devnet CI load, not a correctness bug. Suggested directions include raising the `require.Eventually` timeout and investigating why the proofs indexing worker makes no progress.

### Unaddressed Benchmarking TODOs Lead to Reopened Issue
Issue [#20916](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20916), related to default-`X` benchmarking for the L1 anchor's timestamp offset, was reopened twice today. The latest comment from claude at 2026-08-19T16:07:35Z indicates that the issue was reopened because two TODOs in `op-deployer` still reference it, despite the issue being previously closed. These TODOs relate to enforcing a minimum `X` value and replacing it with a benchmark based on L2 genesis deployment time. The automated TODO checker in CI will continue to fail until these comments are updated or removed.
