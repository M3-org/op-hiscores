### Persistent Flakiness in P2P Connections and Proofs ExEx
Issue [#18570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18570), concerning flakiness in `op-reth` <-> `op-geth` connections, was closed today. The latest comment from `claude` at 2026-08-19T12:46:13Z indicates that the peer-ID format mismatch was fixed by normalization in #18684, and `op-geth` is no longer supported in devstack. The `SKIP_P2P_CONNECTION_CHECK` workaround was removed by #22490, which re-enabled P2P connection verification in rust e2e sysgo jobs.

Issue [#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20376), regarding a flaky test `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract`, was reopened. `wwared` at 2026-08-19T10:41:52Z provided a correction, stating that the tests run sequentially, not concurrently, meaning the previous explanation of "starved under parallel load" is incorrect. The key signal is that the proofs engine flushed blocks 1-19 in a single ~50ms burst during teardown, suggesting something prevented the proofs indexing worker from being driven until shutdown. The `latest` value was stuck at `0`, indicating the worker might not have been scheduled at all. `wwared` at 2026-08-19T10:36:04Z initially reopened the issue, noting that the flake resurfaced with a different symptom (30s timeout in `WaitForProofsStoreBlock`) but the same underlying race condition. The validator's proofs ExEx store never advanced past genesis for 30s while the EL imported blocks normally. The `trie::engine::*` logs on the sequencer appeared in a single burst at teardown, suggesting the proofs indexing engine was starved. The issue is considered a flake, not a regression, as the PR diff was unrelated to ExEx indexing, and the job passed on the same branch previously. Suggested directions include raising the `require.Eventually` timeout and investigating why the proofs indexing worker makes no progress.
