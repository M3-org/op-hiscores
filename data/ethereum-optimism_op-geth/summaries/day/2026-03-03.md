### Glamsterdam Gas Pricing Impacts
- Issue [#19375](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19375) is actively discussing the impact of Glamsterdam's gas repricing on precompile acceleration and fault proofs. Key points include the need to adjust `requiredGas` to match L1 costs and considering increased call data costs for large preimages and precompile inputs. A Notion document on "Precompile Oracle Gas Cost Risks in OP Stack Fault Proofs" was shared for further context. An AI-generated plan for `requiredGas` changes is being tracked in a separate issue.
