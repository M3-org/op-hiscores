# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Daily Update (Mar 3, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today, a critical update was implemented to enhance transaction pool stability by dropping interop transactions during reorgs. Several issues related to L2 contract upgrades and VM runner support were closed, primarily due to being duplicates or having been addressed elsewhere. A new issue was opened to investigate the impact of Glamsterdam gas pricing on precompile acceleration.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Transaction Pool Stability
A significant change was introduced to the transaction pool to improve stability during blockchain reorgs. Interop transactions, which depend on cross-chain state that can be invalidated by reorgs, are now filtered and removed from the transaction pool when discarded blocks are walked. This prevents potential issues arising from invalid cross-chain state. ([#772](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/772))

## NEW ISSUES

### Glamsterdam Gas Pricing Impact
A new issue has been opened to investigate the potential impacts of Glamsterdam gas pricing on precompile acceleration ([#19375](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19375)). The Glamsterdam upgrade is expected to significantly increase gas costs for several precompiles, which could affect the current acceleration methods that execute on L1. The team needs to ensure this does not break proofs and also consider the increased call data costs for large preimages and precompile inputs.
