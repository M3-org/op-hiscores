## ACTIVE ISSUES
### Discussion on Multi-Sequencer Endpoint Support
Issue [#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/763) saw active discussion regarding the implementation of multiple sequencer RPC endpoints. Initial concerns were raised about the user-friendliness of requiring multiple URLs for reliability, suggesting that built-in redundancy for a single URL might be a better approach. However, it was clarified that the proposed change would be optional and fully backward compatible, allowing users to continue with single URLs while providing an option for operators needing redundancy to prevent production incidents caused by single endpoint failures.
