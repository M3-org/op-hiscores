# ethereum-optimism/op-geth Daily Update (Feb 26, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's focus was on refining consensus mechanisms for Optimism genesis blocks, specifically addressing `extraData` validation. Discussions continued on `op-supernode` issues related to Superroot SLA updates and denylist pruning during L1 reorgs, alongside an ongoing `miner` issue.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Consensus Mechanism Refinement
- **Optimism Genesis Block Validation**: Implemented a skip for `extraData` validation in Optimism genesis blocks to accommodate their non-empty `extraData` field, which was previously rejected by a recent validation change. This involved adding `ChainConfig.IsOptimismGenesisBlock` to correctly identify these blocks. ([#770](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/770))

### Superroot SLA and Data Consistency
- **Superroot SLA Updates** ([#19180](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19180)): axelKingsley has prepared a database update to address the ill-defined SLA of the Superroot object, aiming to enable fetching of optimistic versus verified outputs. - **Pruning Denylist on L1 Reorg and Alert** ([#19289](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19289)): axelKingsley clarified that this issue's suggestions supersede a previous ticket, emphasizing the need for downstream components to prune data during L1 reorgs. The discussion highlights the importance of notifying activities via the `Reset` callback and ensuring Chain Containers eliminate data from their Denylists if the Local Safe regresses due to an L1 reorg.
