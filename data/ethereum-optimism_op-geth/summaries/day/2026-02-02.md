## ACTIVE ISSUES

### op-supernode: Safe Label Adjusting
Discussions continued regarding the `op-supernode`'s handling of safe labels and its interaction with the Execution Layer (EL) and Consensus Layer (CL). A key point of discussion is whether the CL should remain the sole controller of the EL's engine, with the `op-supernode` controlling the engine via the virtual node/CL. This approach aims to prevent multiple stateful controllers from getting out of sync and leverages the existing interop-aware data structures in the `op-node` engine controller. The potential downside is that the `op-node` would have some awareness of interop, which might break a desirable invariant. ([#18976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/18976))

### Feedback: `BlockNotFoundError` in Step 5 (Prove the withdrawal on L1)
Further analysis was provided on the `BlockNotFoundError` encountered during the ETH bridge tutorial. The discussion highlighted that the Optimism SDK packages include a validation step for the output root (`isValidOutputRoot`), which appears to be missing in viem's `Withdrawals guide` and related actions. This lack of validation can lead to issues when a withdrawal is initiated on L2 and `waitToProve` is executed immediately, as the corresponding state root might not yet be published to L1. ([#1956](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/1956))
