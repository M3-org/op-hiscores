# ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding Monthly Report (May 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In May 2026, development activity focused on stabilizing core infrastructure components and refining the operational logic of the Superchain ecosystem. Key efforts were directed toward diagnosing a persistent fatal panic in `op-reth` during version upgrades and addressing logic discrepancies within `op-supernode`. The month also saw a focus on security posture through the initiation of new branch scanning protocols and the clarification of monitoring tool capabilities for dispute games.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no new features or pull requests were finalized during this reporting period, technical efforts were concentrated on deep-dive debugging and architectural clarification:

### Infrastructure Stability & Debugging
*   **op-reth Upgrade Path:** Significant investigation was conducted into a fatal panic occurring in the `StateRootTask` when upgrading `op-reth` from 1.x to 2.x. Developers identified that the issue persists across different strategies (including `Parallel`) and is linked to an assertion failure in `alloy-trie`. The investigation suggests a non-deterministic bug in parallel trie computation when interacting with legacy database states.
*   **Supernode Logic Refinement:** Technical analysis of `op-supernode` revealed a potential logic error in how `VerifiedRequiredL1` is calculated. Current findings suggest the system may be incorrectly using the minimum of per-chain L1 values rather than the maximum, which impacts how `Superroot.atTimestamp` functions.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The month's issue tracking reflected a balance between resolving architectural questions and managing critical system bugs.

### Closed Issues
*   **Tooling Clarification:** Issue [#20476](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/20476) was resolved, confirming the versatility of `op-dispute-mon`. It was established that a single instance can monitor both standard CANNON and SUPER_CANNON_KONA games, and that `op-supernode` is capable of serving as the rollup-rpc.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Security Monitoring:** A new security initiative was launched with issue [#100](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/100), establishing a Wiz 'main' Branch Scan Overview to enhance repository security.
*   **Critical Blockers:** The `op-reth` panic ([#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/20462)) remains the most significant active technical blocker, as the `--engine.legacy-state-root` flag has been ruled out as a viable workaround.
*   **Testing Gaps:** Discussion on the `op-supernode` L1 calculation ([#20481](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/20481)) noted that while a bug is suspected, definitive verification is pending the migration of specific acceptance tests.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was characterized by high-level technical troubleshooting and knowledge sharing regarding the Superchain's monitoring stack. The interaction surrounding the `op-dispute-mon` and `op-supernode` functionality indicates an active effort to streamline the operator experience by clarifying how different components of the OP Stack interoperate. Despite the technical hurdles identified in `op-reth`, the community remains focused on rigorous root-cause analysis and improving the security infrastructure through new scanning overviews.
