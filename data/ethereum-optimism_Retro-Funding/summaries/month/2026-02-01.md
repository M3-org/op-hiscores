# ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding Monthly Report (February 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
During February 2026, development activity focused on essential dependency maintenance and addressing infrastructure inconsistencies within the data layer. The primary focus was on refining the `op-reth` snapshot distribution system to ensure better reliability for node operators. While the month saw routine updates to core libraries, significant community attention was directed toward improving the clarity and accuracy of archival data symlinks and naming conventions.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Dependency Management & Maintenance
Work this month included routine maintenance to ensure the security and stability of the project's communication protocols.
*   **Protobuf Update:** The project updated its protocol buffers dependency to the latest patch version. This included the transition from version 5.29.5 to 5.29.6 in PR [#98](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/pull/98).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The most significant technical discussion this month centered on the reliability of the Optimism data directory infrastructure.
*   **op-reth Snapshot Inconsistencies:** A critical discussion is ongoing regarding the setup of `op-reth` snapshots ([#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/536)). Users identified several points of friction at `datadirs.optimism.io`, including:
    *   Inconsistent naming patterns for Reth archival files.
    *   Discrepancies between specific `mainnet-reth` files and the `latest-mainnet-reth` symlinks.
    *   Outdated symlinks that do not point to the most recent data.
    *   A lack of clear metadata in filenames to distinguish between "archival" and "full" node types.

This issue represents a potential blocker for contributors and operators who rely on accurate snapshots for rapid node synchronization.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was characterized by active feedback from the operator community regarding infrastructure usability. The detailed reporting on issue [#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/536) demonstrates a high level of engagement from users who are auditing the project's public data resources. This feedback loop is currently driving the requirements for better documentation and more standardized file-naming conventions within the Superchain's data distribution layer.
