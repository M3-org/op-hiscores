# ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding Monthly Report (January 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In January 2026, development activity for the Retro-Funding repository centered on the finalization and documentation of Round M11 results. The primary focus was on refining data sets for onchain builders and developer tooling, ensuring that reward distributions are backed by updated queries and transparent weightings. This work represents a critical step in the Optimism Collective's mission to provide sustainable, data-driven funding for public goods within the Superchain ecosystem.
