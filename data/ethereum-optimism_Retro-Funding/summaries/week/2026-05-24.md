# ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding Weekly Report (May 24 - 30, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week was characterized by a rigorous audit of technical debt and the initiation of new feature sets for user metrics. A systematic review of the codebase led to the reopening of over a dozen issues due to lingering TODO comments, signaling a shift toward higher code quality and completion standards. While the project successfully finalized the deletion of the `op-supervisor` component, the primary focus remained on refining interoperability logic and cleaning up legacy upgrade transactions to ensure a more stable foundation for the Superchain ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Metrics and Deployment Infrastructure
Work began on enhancing the core functionality of the Retro-Funding repository to better track project impact and streamline deployments.
*   **User Metrics & Factory Dependencies:** A new feature PR was introduced to implement deployment factory dependencies and diversify user metrics [#101](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/pull/101). This effort aims to provide a more nuanced view of project engagement and technical lineage within the ecosystem.

### Protocol Refactoring and Cleanup
Significant effort was directed toward removing legacy code and standardizing contract patterns across the OP Stack.
*   **L2ProxyAdmin Initialization:** Developers are working to refactor the `L2ProxyAdmin` to utilize the Initializable pattern, moving away from older deployment scripts [#19182](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/19182).
*   **Event System Refinement:** A cleanup of the event system refactor is underway to remove direct call notes and deprecated `ForkchoiceRequestEvent` references across `op-node` and `op-e2e` [#16917](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/16917).
*   **Predeploy Safety:** Investigations are ongoing into creating a single source of truth for predeploys within the `L2ContractsManager` to increase upgrade safety [#19260](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/19260).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Component Deletion:** The removal of `op-supervisor` was successfully completed through a coordinated series of pull requests, streamlining the repository's architecture [#20124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/20124).

### New & Active Issues (Technical Debt Audit)
The week saw a massive "re-triage" event where multiple issues were reopened to address outstanding TODOs that prevented full productionization:
*   **Interoperability Blockers:** Issues related to interop test-sequencing [#14129](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/14129) and the migration of upgrade transactions to the NUT bundle [#19239](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/19239) remain active. These are critical for the "Test-Sequencing Milestone 2" productionization.
*   **Legacy Cleanup:** The removal of Karst-specific upgrade transactions [#19369](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/19369) and the transition to v2 dispute game blueprints [#17262](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/17262) were identified as incomplete due to remaining skips in the testing suite.
*   **Sync & Integration:** Lingering tasks persist in `op-node` sync-start logic [#16141](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/16141) and the integration of `txplan`/`txintent` [#16371](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/16371).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The activity this week suggests a high level of internal scrutiny and maintainer-led quality control. The reopening of numerous issues by maintainers (notably `ajsutton`) indicates a collaborative push to ensure that "completed" features meet the project's definition of done, specifically regarding the removal of internal TODOs and temporary feature flags. This rigorous approach to technical debt highlights a commitment to long-term maintainability as the Superchain ecosystem scales.
