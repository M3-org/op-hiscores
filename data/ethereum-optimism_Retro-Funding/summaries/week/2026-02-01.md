# ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding Weekly Report (Feb 1 - 7, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on essential maintenance and addressing infrastructure inconsistencies within the Retro-Funding repository. The primary technical activity involved dependency management to ensure protocol stability, alongside a significant community-driven audit of the `op-reth` snapshot distribution system. Efforts are currently centered on resolving discrepancies in data availability and naming conventions for archival files, which are critical for node operators and ecosystem transparency.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Dependency Management & Maintenance
The team continues to prioritize the security and reliability of the codebase through regular dependency updates.
*   **Protobuf Update:** A pull request was initiated to bump the `protobuf` dependency from version 5.29.5 to 5.29.6 [#98](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/pull/98). This ensures the project remains aligned with the latest patches in data serialization standards.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The most significant area of active discussion involves the **op-reth snapshot setup [#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/536)**. This issue highlights several critical inconsistencies in how snapshots are served via `datadirs.optimism.io`:
*   **Naming Inconsistencies:** Users reported that naming patterns for `reth` archival files do not follow a predictable format.
*   **Symlink Discrepancies:** There are noted mismatches between specific files (e.g., `mainnet-reth...`) and their corresponding `latest-mainnet-reth` symlinks, as well as outdated links for other `latest-reth` pointers.
*   **Metadata Requests:** Community members have requested clearer distinctions between "archival" and "full" node files within the filenames themselves to improve the developer experience for those syncing nodes.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by high-quality feedback from the community regarding infrastructure accessibility. The detailed reporting on issue [#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/536) demonstrates active engagement from users who are technical stakeholders in the Optimism ecosystem. This dialogue between maintainers and users is currently serving as a diagnostic phase for improving the reliability of public data directories, ensuring that the infrastructure supporting Retro-Funding and the broader Superchain remains robust and user-friendly.
