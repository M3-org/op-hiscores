### Fatal panic in StateRootTask when upgrading op-reth
Issue [#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/20462) continues to be actively investigated. The latest update confirms that the `--engine.legacy-state-root` flag does not resolve the panic when upgrading op-reth from 1.x to 2.x. The panic, an assertion failure in `alloy-trie-0.9.5/src/nodes/branch.rs`, now occurs in the `Parallel` strategy as well, suggesting a shared bug in parallel trie computation. The crash block is not specific, indicating a non-deterministic trigger during parallel trie computation against 1.x database state.

### op-supernode: VerifiedRequiredL1 uses MIN of per-chain L1s instead of MAX
Issue [#20481](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/20481) highlights a potential bug where `Superroot.atTimestamp` in `op-supernode` uses the minimum of per-chain `verifiedL1` values instead of the maximum. A note was added clarifying that the referenced acceptance tests are not yet available but will be migrated soon.
