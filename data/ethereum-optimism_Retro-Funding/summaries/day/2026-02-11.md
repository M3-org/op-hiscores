**Kona Just Recipes and Prestate Building**
- Discussion on [#18654](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/18654) (chore(kona/proofs): remove outdated just recipes once kona's migration is complete) continued with Inphi questioning the removal of custom just recipes, stating they are convenient for developers during local testing and that with [#18879](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/pull/18879), the monorepo is used to build the prestate. **L2CM Upgrades Risk Modeling**
- On [#18869](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/18869) ([L2CM Upgrades] Risk Modeling (FMA or threat model)), maurelian provided links to an FMA template and the OPCMv2 FMA document, indicating progress in defining the methodology for risk modeling.

**Kona Hint Format Optimization**
- The discussion on [#19125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/19125) (Use binary format for Kona hints instead of String serialization) saw kien-rise clarify that the motivation for a binary format is primarily correctness rather than speed, though performance improvements are expected. They also expressed willingness to propose a compatibility-preserving approach. ajsutton raised concerns about the necessity of performance gains to justify the change, given the backward compatibility requirements for the host program, which must continue to support string versions for existing games.

**`op-alloy` Documentation Deployment**

**Fixing `devfeatures` Cyclic Dependency**
- On [#19151](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/19151) (Fix devfeatures cyclic dependency), maurelian suggested creating a new `op-core/devfeatures` package for shared, OP protocol-specific code, which would allow for shorter function and variable names.
