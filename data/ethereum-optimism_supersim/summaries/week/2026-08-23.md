# ethereum-optimism/supersim Weekly Report (Aug 23 - 29, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on critical infrastructure maintenance and a strategic audit of the project's repository ecosystem. A major effort was launched to address technical debt by upgrading the OP Stack dependency to `v1.16.13` and proposing automation for future upgrades to prevent the 14-month stale periods seen previously. Development also centered on refining the Superchain developer experience through a comprehensive audit and consolidation plan for starter repositories. While progress was made in resolving complex cross-chain interoperability "wedges," new challenges emerged regarding `kona-node` sequencer stability and P2P networking stalls.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Dependency Management & Infrastructure
The primary technical focus was modernizing the project's foundation by addressing outdated dependencies.
*   **OP Stack Upgrade:** A new pull request, [#424](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/pull/424), was initiated to upgrade the OP Stack dependency to `op-node/v1.16.13`. 
*   **Upgrade Automation:** To prevent future technical debt, issue [#425](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/425) was created to establish a formal cadence for these upgrades, noting that the previous monorepo pin had remained stale for over a year.

### Repository Consolidation & Documentation Audit
Significant work was done to streamline the developer onboarding experience by auditing and merging redundant resources.
*   **Starter Repo Migration:** Analysis in [#1015](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/1015) recommended consolidating five `superchain-starter*` repositories into a single primary repo to reduce fragmentation.
*   **Documentation Integrity:** A comprehensive audit ([#1014](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/1014)) confirmed that archiving abandoned repositories would not break live documentation, clearing the path for a cleaner ecosystem.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Supernode Interoperability:** Issue [#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/22325) was resolved after an investigation into `op-supernode` cross-safe wedges. The root cause was identified as a parent-hash mismatch following a reorg on chain 901, linking the behavior to known oscillation issues rather than a new cross-safety bug.
*   **Configuration Leniency:** The team decided to maintain current behavior in `kona-genesis` regarding unknown `rollup.json` keys ([#22682](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/22682)). This leniency is intentional to support legacy configurations.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sequencer Stability:** A critical new issue ([#22681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/22681)) reports that `kona-node` sequencers can become permanently wedged in a `forkchoiceUpdated` loop during engine reorgs.
*   **Networking Stalls:** Discussion continues on [#22646](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/22646), where the `NetworkActor` is reportedly stalling the P2P swarm during remote-signer HTTP calls. Contributors are currently investigating overflow policies and latency injection to confirm the behavior.
*   **Compatibility Gaps:** The archiving of older tools has highlighted a gap in developer tooling for checking `SuperchainERC20` token compatibility ([#1020](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/1020)), which will require a new supported solution.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by high-level strategic coordination regarding the project's footprint. Contributors engaged in detailed migration analysis for the "Archive Sweep," demonstrating a cautious approach to repository management that prioritizes preserving history while improving maintainability. There is active technical collaboration on the `kona-node` networking issues, with contributors stepping up to volunteer for complex debugging tasks involving P2P swarm behavior and remote-signer integration.
