# ethereum-optimism/supersim Weekly Report (Apr 26 - 2, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on refining interoperability configurations and addressing critical stability issues within the OP Stack. A significant effort was directed toward improving the reliability of interop autorelay mechanisms through configurable retry logic. However, the project is currently navigating a critical technical hurdle involving a fatal panic in `op-reth` during version upgrades, which appears linked to parallel trie computation. Additionally, logic discrepancies in `op-supernode` calculations were identified, signaling a focus on hardening the Superchain's foundational synchronization and verification layers.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interoperability & Autorelay Enhancements
Efforts this week focused on making the interoperability layer more resilient and customizable for developers.
*   **Configurable Autorelay:** Work began on making autorelay retries and backoff strategies configurable for interop. This update also includes a fix for a dead retry path, ensuring more robust message delivery across chains. [#421](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/pull/421)

### Protocol & Node Logic
Refinements to the `op-supernode` logic are underway to ensure accurate cross-chain state tracking.
*   **Verification Logic Adjustments:** Developers identified a calculation error in `op-supernode` where `VerifiedRequiredL1` was incorrectly tracking the minimum of per-chain values rather than the maximum. Correcting this is essential for the accuracy of `SuperRootResponseData`.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Supernode Querying:** An issue regarding the ability of `op-dispute-mon` to query the supernode's Virtual Nodes (VNs) was successfully resolved and closed this week.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Critical Panic in op-reth ([#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20462)):** A high-priority issue persists involving a fatal panic in `StateRootTask` when upgrading `op-reth` from 1.x to 2.x. Investigations reveal that the panic occurs within the `Parallel` strategy and is linked to an assertion failure in `alloy-trie`. Current findings suggest the `--engine.legacy-state-root` flag is not a viable workaround, indicating a deeper bug in parallel trie computation against legacy database states.
*   **Supernode Calculation Errors ([#20481](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20481)):** A new issue was opened regarding the `VerifiedRequiredL1` calculation in `op-supernode`. This is a potential blocker for accurate state verification, though full resolution is pending the migration of associated acceptance tests.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week's activity shows a strong focus on protocol-level stability and cross-component integration. The detailed technical discussion surrounding the `op-reth` panic ([#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20462)) highlights active collaboration between developers to diagnose non-deterministic failures in the trie computation layer. There is also evidence of ongoing maintenance work to align testing frameworks with recent code changes, particularly regarding supernode acceptance tests.
