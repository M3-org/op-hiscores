# ethereum-optimism/supersim Monthly Report (May 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In May 2026, development focused on refining interop configurations and addressing critical stability issues within the OP Stack ecosystem. Key efforts were directed toward making the interop autorelay more resilient through configurable retry logic and backoff strategies. However, the month was also marked by significant technical challenges, including a critical panic in `op-reth` during version upgrades and logic errors in `op-supernode` regarding L1 verification calculations. These developments underscore a period of stabilization and fine-tuning as the project scales its cross-chain interoperability features.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interop & Autorelay Enhancements
Efforts to improve the reliability of cross-chain message passing were a primary focus this month.
*   **Configurable Autorelay:** A significant update was introduced to make autorelay retries and backoff strategies configurable for interop. This work also included a fix for a dead retry path, ensuring more robust message delivery between chains ([#421](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/pull/421)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The project is currently navigating several high-priority technical hurdles related to node stability and protocol logic:

*   **Critical Panic in op-reth:** A fatal panic has been identified in the `StateRootTask` when upgrading `op-reth` from 1.x to 2.x on OP Stack chains ([#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20462)). Investigations reveal that the panic persists even with the `--engine.legacy-state-root` flag and occurs within the `Parallel` strategy. The issue appears linked to an assertion failure in the `alloy-trie` library, suggesting non-deterministic behavior during parallel trie computation against legacy database states.
*   **op-supernode Logic Errors:** A new issue was identified in `op-supernode` where the `VerifiedRequiredL1` calculation is incorrect ([#20481](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20481)). The system is currently tracking the minimum of per-chain `verifiedL1` values instead of the maximum, leading to inaccurate `SuperRootResponseData`. Resolution is pending the migration of associated acceptance tests.

### Closed Issues
*   **Monitoring Improvements:** The team successfully resolved an issue regarding `op-dispute-mon`'s ability to query the Virtual Nodes (VNs) of the supernode, improving the observability of the dispute monitoring system.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The activity this month reflects a deep technical focus on the core protocol and its dependencies. Discussions around the `op-reth` panic ([#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20462)) indicate active troubleshooting and collaboration between contributors to diagnose complex state-root issues across different versions of the stack. The opening of PR [#421](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/pull/421) shows a proactive approach to developer experience by allowing more granular control over interop configurations.
