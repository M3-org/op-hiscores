# ethereum-optimism/supersim Monthly Report (August 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In August 2026, development activity focused on refining the interoperability layer and addressing stability issues within the testing infrastructure. A key priority was resolving environment variable mismatches to ensure proper configuration of L2-to-L2 Cross-Domain Messenger (CDM) overrides. The project also began streamlining its technical debt by closing long-standing trackers in light of the upcoming `op-node` deprecation, while simultaneously investigating recurring port collision flakes in the acceptance test suite.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interoperability & Configuration
Efforts this month focused on ensuring the simulation environment correctly handles cross-chain communication overrides.
*   **Environment Variable Matching:** A fix was introduced to address an issue where the `SUPERSIM_INTEROP_L2TOL2CDM_OVERRIDE` variable was not being correctly recognized by the system ([#423](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/pull/423)). This ensures that developers can reliably override the Cross-Domain Messenger addresses during interop testing.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Pipeline Improvements & Deprecation:** The "Holocene-D: Pipeline improvements tracker" ([#12490](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/12490)) was closed as "not planned." This decision stems from the strategic shift toward the deprecation of `op-node`, which renders the previously identified code todos and pipeline optimizations obsolete.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Test Flakiness (Metrics Port Collisions):** A significant investigation is underway regarding a recurring flake in the `op-acceptance-tests` ([#22299](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/22299)). The `kona-sp1-proposer` has been intermittently failing to boot due to "Address already in use" errors on the metrics server port. This has been observed specifically within the `memory-all-kona-op-reth-prague` jobs on the `develop` branch, indicating a potential resource contention issue in the CI environment.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was characterized by active maintenance and strategic triaging. Maintainers `sebastianst` and `ajsutton` coordinated on the lifecycle of protocol-level trackers, ensuring the project's roadmap aligns with the broader evolution of the OP Stack (specifically the transition away from `op-node`). Technical discussions also involved `claude`, who provided critical diagnostic data regarding the proposer failures, helping to narrow down the impact of metrics port collisions on the `develop` branch.
