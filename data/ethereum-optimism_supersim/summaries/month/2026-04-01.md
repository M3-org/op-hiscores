# ethereum-optimism/supersim Monthly Report (April 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In April 2026, the `supersim` project focused heavily on advancing the Interop roadmap, specifically transitioning through Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the SuperDisputeGame. While the team successfully finalized Interop Specifications and resolved critical CI infrastructure bottlenecks, development encountered headwinds due to persistent flaky tests and synchronization issues on devnets. These challenges have shifted priorities toward stabilizing the Execution Layer (EL) sync recovery and addressing regression risks, placing some Batcher and `op-conductor` milestones at risk for the immediate term.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interop Infrastructure & Dispute Games
The project made significant strides in the multi-phase rollout of interoperability features. 
*   **Phase 1 (SuperDisputeGame Adoption):** Work progressed on enabling each chain to use the SuperDisputeGame independently. Kona proof work remained a primary focus, with completion targeted for late April ([#19690](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/19690)).
*   **Phase 2 (Shared Dispute Game):** The team successfully merged the main contract pull requests required to consolidate multiple chains into a shared SuperDisputeGame framework ([#19691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/19691)).
*   **Specification Finalization:** Interop Specs were officially updated and closed, signaling a transition from design to full implementation ([#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/905)).

### Dependency Management & Maintenance
*   **Security & Updates:** A significant upgrade to `axios` (v3.0.0) was initiated to resolve existing findings and maintain library security ([#420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/pull/420)).
*   **CI Optimization:** The team resolved a recurring Out-of-Memory (OOM) error during `reth-optimism-node` compilation within the `rust-tests` suite, improving CI reliability ([#19935](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/19935)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Kona-Interop Validation:** Internal block access reviews concluded that Kona is correctly handling interop validation, leading to the closure of [#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/19266).
*   **Reorg Support:** Initial work regarding `op-conductor` support for reorgs was finalized ([#20214](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20214)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sync & Regression Risks:** A new high-priority issue was opened to implement a regression guard for EL restart synchronization. This addresses a specific stall in the `EngineController` when an EL returns a `SYNCING` status after a restart ([#20307](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20307)).
*   **Persistent Flakes:** Two major flaky tests are currently impeding the development velocity:
    *   `TestTruncateDatabaseOnELResync`: Reopened after `CrossSafe` failed to advance past genesis, suggesting a deeper bug in EL Sync mode ([#19897](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/19897)).
    *   `TestFlashblocksTransfer`: Reporting panics due to nil channel closures ([#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/19934)).
*   **Project Blockers:** Tasks related to "Verification-Aware CurrentL1" for the Batcher ([#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/19944)) and graceful recovery from interop reorgs in `op-conductor` ([#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20006)) are currently queued behind urgent devnet sync issues and are considered at risk.
