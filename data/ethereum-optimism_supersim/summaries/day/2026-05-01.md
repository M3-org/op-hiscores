### Critical Panic in op-reth StateRootTask [#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20462)
Discussion on this issue highlights a fatal panic in `StateRootTask` when upgrading `op-reth` from 1.x to 2.x on OP Stack chains. Latest updates confirm that the `--engine.legacy-state-root` flag does not resolve the panic, which now occurs in the `Parallel` strategy as well. The panic is linked to an assertion failure in `alloy-trie-0.9.5/src/nodes/branch.rs`, suggesting a non-deterministic trigger during parallel trie computation against 1.x database state.

### op-supernode: Incorrect VerifiedRequiredL1 Calculation [#20481](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/20481)
This new issue describes a problem where `Superroot.atTimestamp` in `op-supernode` incorrectly tracks `minVerifiedRequiredL1` as the minimum of per-chain `verifiedL1` values instead of the maximum, leading to an incorrect `SuperRootResponseData.VerifiedRequiredL1`. A note indicates that referenced acceptance tests are pending migration.
