# ethereum-optimism/design-docs Monthly Report (July 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
July was a month focused on organizational maintenance and the refinement of protocol documentation standards. The primary activity centered on streamlining the repository by closing out legacy issues and ensuring terminology alignment across the OP Stack. Key efforts included reconciling bond sizing models with core repository developments and cleaning up security-related feedback to maintain a high-signal environment for contributors.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no new technical features or code refactors were merged into the design documents this month, the project focused on maintaining the integrity of existing specifications.

*   **Protocol Specification Refinement**
    *   Efforts were made to ensure technical clarity within the documentation, specifically regarding the categorization of network upgrades. This included finalizing discussions on the consistent application of "soft vs. hard-fork" terminology to prevent ambiguity in future design docs [#342](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/342).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The month saw a significant effort in triaging the backlog and directing technical discussions to their appropriate venues within the Optimism ecosystem.

*   **Closed Issues:**
    *   **Economic Modeling:** An issue regarding the bond sizing model (calculated as a function of dependency-set size and total gas) was closed. It was determined that this work is better tracked as a subset of a larger initiative within the primary `optimism` repository [#21417](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21417).
    *   **Infrastructure & Nodes:** Documentation tracking for the Unichain Mainnet Replicas Supernode was closed, with the technical details transitioned to core team tracking [#21177](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21177).
    *   **Security Maintenance:** The team actively moderated the repository, quickly identifying and closing non-constructive security feedback related to `flags.go` that originated from external audits [#21623](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21623).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was characterized by cross-repository coordination. Maintainers focused on ensuring that design discussions in this repository remained aligned with the active development occurring in the core `optimism` codebase. By migrating specific technical discussions (like bond sizing and Unichain infrastructure) to core repositories, the team is maintaining a clear distinction between high-level design specifications and implementation-specific tracking.
