# ethereum-optimism/design-docs Monthly Report (April 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In April 2026, the Optimism design-docs repository focused on refining protocol efficiency and advancing the next generation of gas and metering models. Key achievements included the introduction of a streamlined span batch processing design for the Karst hardfork and the initial drafting of the "opgas" model to address fee rebates. Significant effort was also dedicated to system stability, with a concerted push to resolve complex race conditions and resource contention issues within the CI/CD pipeline, particularly affecting interop and flashblock testing suites.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
The month’s technical work centered on protocol optimizations and foundational designs for future network upgrades.

### Protocol Efficiency & Hardfork Planning
- **Span Batch Optimization:** A new design was proposed for the Karst hardfork to drop overlapping span batches. This change eliminates the need for expensive `SafeBlockFetcher` verification, significantly improving derivation efficiency ([#371](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/371)).
- **Kona Prestate Native Build:** Documentation was introduced for the Kona prestate native build, supporting the evolution of the Optimism proof stack ([#372](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/372)).

### Gas Modeling & Metering
- **Opgas Design:** An initial draft for "opgas" was submitted, synthesizing concepts for fee rebates and a revised gas model for the ecosystem ([#361](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/361)).
- **Sequencer-Defined Metering (SDM):** The design phase for SDM was completed, providing a framework for problem definition, implementation planning, and benchmarking ([#360](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/360)).

### Infrastructure & Tooling
- **Reactive Runtime Config:** Discussions progressed on enabling `op-node` to reactively reload configurations when unsafe block signer addresses rotate, potentially reducing the current 10-minute reload interval to improve UX during key transitions ([#19981](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19981)).
- **Template Updates:** A reminder regarding third-party service integration was added to the design document templates ([#373](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/373)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The project saw a high volume of activity related to stabilizing the testing environment and finalizing service level agreements (SLAs).

- **Closed Issues:**
    - **Superroot SLAs:** Updates for Full Outputs in Denylist and the use of Denylist for OptimisticOutput were finalized and merged ([#19180](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19180)).
    - **Database Resync:** Issues involving database truncation and preservation during EL/CL resyncs were resolved, though they required multiple rounds of triage as new failure modes appeared ([#19661](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19661), [#19897](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19897)).

- **New & Active Issues (Flaky Tests):**
    - **Race Conditions:** `TestExecutePayloadSuccess` and `TestFlashblocksTransfer` highlighted persistent race conditions between sequencers and validators, often caused by block indexing delays or timing mismatches in CI ([#19898](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19898), [#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19934)).
    - **Resource Contention:** `TestSuperCannonStepWithPreimage` revealed non-deterministic failures in the `mt-cannon-next` suite, attributed to resource contention in CI causing truncated execution ([#19892](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19892)).
    - **Interop Migration:** Work is ongoing to migrate `op-e2e` interop action tests to `op-acceptance-tests` to streamline the testing architecture ([#18974](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18974)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was characterized by deep technical troubleshooting between maintainers. Discussions regarding the **Reactive Runtime Config Reload** ([#19981](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19981)) showed active engagement in balancing protocol security with user experience. Furthermore, the iterative debugging of flashblock and interop tests involved significant data sharing (CI logs and trace data) between contributors like `wwared` and `maurelian`, demonstrating a highly collaborative approach to maintaining the stability of the Superchain's foundational code.
