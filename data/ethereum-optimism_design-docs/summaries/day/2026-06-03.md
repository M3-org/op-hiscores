## ACTIVE ISSUES

### op-supernode/op-reth CL Sync Recovery Stalling ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21076))
The issue of CL sync recovery stalling due to `op-supernode`/`op-reth` bookkeeping referencing EL-missing blocks has seen significant progress. A fix has been implemented and successfully soaked for 6 hours, with both jnt-v2 chains syncing continuously without wedges. The root cause was identified as a cyclic deadlock in reth's `StateTrieOverlayManager` / `DeferredTrieData` machinery, specifically due to locks being held across `rayon` compute operations. Two patches were developed to address this by ensuring locks are not held during blocking computations.

### SDM: Add a Limit for SDM Refunds ([#20709](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20709))
Discussion continues on implementing a protocol-level upper bound for sequencer rebates. The current suggestion is to enforce `MAX_GAS_LIMIT` regardless of the refund amount, effectively capping the maximum computation the sequencer can perform. It is believed this can be hardcoded into the node software.
