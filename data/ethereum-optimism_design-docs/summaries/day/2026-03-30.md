### Flaky Test: TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes
Discussion on this flaky test ([#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19804)) indicates that the root cause has been identified and a fix submitted in PR #19805. The flakiness was attributed to `l1BlockWithLocalSafeBlocks()` polling the supernode's `RequiredL1` values, which could shift due to sequencer activity during batcher choreography. The proposed fix involves replacing supernode polling with direct L1 head reads at each batcher choreography stage to maintain invariants.
