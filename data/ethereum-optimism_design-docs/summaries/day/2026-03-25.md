### Flaky Test: TestCLELDivergence
Discussion on [#19681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19681) continues regarding the `TestCLELDivergence` flakiness. The latest comments indicate that a mitigation was implemented in https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19733, but the issue remains open to confirm the flakiness has truly disappeared. Earlier analysis suggested the test's comment about EL-sync causing resets was incorrect, and the observed resets were part of the initial derivation pipeline lifecycle rather than an in-progress EL sync. The core question remains whether the derivation pipeline resetting the unsafe head is correct behavior or a bug, as the test expects the CL's unsafe head to persist despite the EL returning SYNCING.
