### Flaky Test: `TestChallengerCountersPreGenesisGame`
- Issue [#20869](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20869) is investigating a flaky test. Investigation is complete, and a proposed fix has been submitted in PR #20874. The root cause was identified as a timing issue where `plannedRollupStartBlockNumber` could collide with the receipt block due to varying migration wall-clock times. The fix involves increasing the `preGenesisRollupStartBlockDelay` and adding a post-migration L1-head re-read to prevent future flakiness.
