*   **ZKDisputeGame: Handle address(0) bond credit claiming via monitoring or contract change** ([#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19743))

    The discussion concluded that a mock prover should be used for proofs in CI and local development. It was also suggested that proving on op-sepolia could be considered at a set cadence if production infrastructure for local proving is established, but proving in CI is deemed too expensive for little benefit.
