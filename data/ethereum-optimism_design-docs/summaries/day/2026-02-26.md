### L1 Reorg Handling and Denylist Pruning
Issue [#19289](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19289) discussed the need for downstream components to prune data, specifically Chain Containers' Denylists, when the Virtual Node responds to an L1 reorg. axelKingsley clarified that this suggestion supersedes a previous ticket, emphasizing the need for `Reset(chain, ts)` callbacks to trigger even for uncontrolled regressions of the Local Safe, and for Chain Containers to eliminate data from their Denylists if the Local Safe regresses.
