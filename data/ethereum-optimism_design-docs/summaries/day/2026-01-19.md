### P2P Connection Flakiness in `op-reth` and `op-geth`
Discussion on [#18570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18570) continued with `forkfury` mentioning a related pull request has been made and asking `theochap` to review it. This issue concerns the instability of P2P connections between `op-reth` and `op-geth` nodes in system tests, which was previously highlighted as a major bug or high-priority chore due to its impact on test stability and potential production compatibility.

### `op-supernode`: Reset Handling
On [#18740](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18740), `geoknee` provided an analysis of prior art for reset handling, specifically referencing `op-wheel` and `op-node`. The comment details how `op-node` uses `forkChoiceUpdate` (FCU) triggered by `op-supervisor` to reset local fork choice state. However, `geoknee` expressed confusion as tests indicate FCU does not move the unsafe head back to a canonical block on `op-geth`, questioning how this mechanism previously functioned.

### `op-reth`: Batcher Throttling
Regarding [#18764](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18764), `einar-oplabs` noted that for `op-reth`, both throttling mechanisms appear to be implemented within the payload builder. The comment also highlighted that no testing related to this implementation has been found so far. This issue focuses on the absence of an equivalent to `miner_setMaxDASize` in `op-reth` for batcher-sequencer throttling, which is available in `op-geth`.
