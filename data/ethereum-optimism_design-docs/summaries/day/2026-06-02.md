A new issue has been opened to write and test a runbook for recovering a chain after an invalid interop message triggers a reorg [#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21161). This runbook should cover issues such as [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21149), [#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20006), and [#21125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21125), and ensure the failsafe is disabled.

### Interop Tx Pool Issue
A new issue highlights that `op-reth`'s transaction pool does not resume accepting interop transactions after the `op-interop-filter` failsafe is disabled, requiring a restart of `op-reth` [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21149).

## ACTIVE ISSUES
### Devnet Spin-up Progress
Discussion on spinning up a devnet [#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20691) indicates that a local devnet is now running, with the next step being a long-running devnet. Previously, the plan was to wait for `glamsterdam-devnet-4` images before attempting a full devnet.

### `op-revm` Support and Syncing
The issue regarding `op-revm` support [#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21069) confirms that the team aims to stay in sync with new `revm` releases, with dependency bumps expected when `reth` has new releases. There is particular interest in REVM's latest release (v111) for its WIP support for the Amsterdam hardfork and shared type definitions.

### Interop Tx Pool Failsafe Remediation
Regarding the `op-reth` interop tx pool not resuming after failsafe is disabled [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21149), an update from remediation on `interop-bench-3` indicates that restarting `op-reth` alone was insufficient. Restarting both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` (the flashblocks block builder) on the sequencers was required to restore interop. A failsafe-state gauge metric is proposed in #21151 to make this state observable.
