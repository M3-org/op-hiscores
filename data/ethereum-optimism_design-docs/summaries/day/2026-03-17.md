## ACTIVE ISSUES

### op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/17631))
Discussions continued regarding the behavior of `op-node` when finishing initial EL Sync, specifically the potential for it to mark a block as finalized even if it's not truly finalized.
- **trianglesphere** proposed several options for a finalization gadget, including a command-line flag, an RPC URL for a trusted checkpoint provider, looking at L1 for the last finalized head, or an arbitrary delay of the finalized head (12 hours to 10 days). They leaned towards a command-line flag or a 12-hour fallback.
- **karlfloersch** expressed favor for the operational simplicity of delaying the finalized head by the sequencing window.
- **trianglesphere** also clarified that this behavior was the intended design when the EL sync feature was created, and that re-running derivation on a snap-synced node does not work as expected.

### non-terminating CI job: memory-all-opn-op-geth ([#19563](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19563))
Further occurrences and investigations into the non-terminating CI job were reported.
- **ajsutton** noted another timeout for `TestChallengerPlaysGame` and launched an investigation. They also opened a draft fix ([#19634](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19634)) for the root cause, which was identified as RPC calls within polling loops using the base test context with a long deadline, leading to indefinite blocking when L1 geth hangs. The fix involves creating a context-bound copy of the `FaultDisputeGame` binding for polling loops to ensure RPC calls respect per-wait timeouts.
- **geoknee** mentioned that several PRs are in progress that may address this issue and suggested re-evaluating the problem in a few days.

### flaky test: `TestAddressValidDeployment` ([#19591](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19591))
The investigation into this flaky test was completed.
- **ajsutton** identified the root cause as intermittent timeouts and connection resets during live RPC calls to `publicnode.com` endpoints under CI load. A fix PR ([#19592](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19592)) was opened, which adds retry logic, consolidates RPC clients, and increases context timeouts.
