# ethereum-optimism/design-docs Weekly Report (May 24 - 30, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on expanding the architectural roadmap for the Superchain and addressing persistent technical debt. Significant progress was made on the design front with the introduction of proposals for the **Super ZK Dispute Game** and **Predictive Chain Deployments (PCD)**. A major theme of the week was a comprehensive audit of the codebase for lingering `TODO` comments, leading to the reopening of over a dozen issues to ensure that technical debt—ranging from Interop load test performance to contract refactors—is systematically tracked and resolved. Meanwhile, engineering efforts continue to troubleshoot complex `op-reth` stability issues involving Engine API stalls and race conditions.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### New Design Proposals
The repository saw the introduction of two major design documents aimed at enhancing the scalability and deployment efficiency of the ecosystem:
*   **Super ZK Dispute Game ([#384](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/384)):** A new design document outlining the integration of Zero-Knowledge proofs into the dispute resolution mechanism for the Superchain.
*   **Predictive Chain Deployments ([#385](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/385)):** A proposal for Predictive Chain Deployments (PCD), focusing on streamlining the deployment process across the network.

### Infrastructure & Tooling Refinement
*   **Supervisor Cleanup:** Successfully completed the deletion of the `op-supervisor` component as part of a broader architectural cleanup ([#20124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20124)).
*   **L2CM Feature Flags:** Established a new documentation-based approach to prevent silent drift between feature flag definitions in `DevFeatures.sol` and the CI matrix ([#20340](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20340)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Technical Debt & TODO Cleanup
A significant effort was made to align the issue tracker with the actual state of the codebase. Many issues were reopened by maintainers after identifying unresolved `TODO` tags in the source code:
*   **Interop & Load Testing:** Issues regarding `sync-start` awareness ([#16141](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/16141)), `txplan` integration ([#16371](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/16371)), and budget depletion in load tests ([#16536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/16536)) were reopened to address performance anomalies and composition logic.
*   **Contract Refactoring:** Reopened issues include the `L2ProxyAdmin` initialization ([#19182](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19182)), the migration of Interop upgrade transactions to the NUT bundle ([#19239](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19239)), and the removal of Karst-specific upgrade logic ([#19369](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19369)).
*   **System Maintenance:** Reopened tasks for the event system refactor ([#16917](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/16917)) and `op-program` dependency set consolidation ([#14415](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/14415)).

### Critical Stability Investigations
*   **`op-reth` Crashes:** Investigation continues into SIGBUS crashes and infinite retry loops in the `proofs-history` exex ([#20934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20934)). Current hypotheses point to a race condition involving `ftruncate` and `DataReader` during reorgs.
*   **Engine API Stalls:** Flaky tests in `TestUnsafeChainNotStalling_CLSync` ([#21041](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21041)) have been traced to `op-reth` Engine API timeouts rather than test budget issues.
*   **Nondeterministic Builds:** A bug in `generate-semver-lock` ([#21032](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21032)) was identified where multiple Forge artifacts map to the same key, causing inconsistent output.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by high-intensity maintenance activity, particularly by contributor **ajsutton**, who led the effort to synchronize the issue tracker with the codebase's internal `TODO`s. Collaboration with the **Reth core team** is currently a high priority as the Optimism team seeks to resolve upstream Engine API race conditions and mmap-related crashes. Additionally, a security-conscious culture was reinforced through new warnings regarding the use of AI tools for contract debugging and the potential for data exfiltration.
