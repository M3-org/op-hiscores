# ethereum-optimism/design-docs Weekly Report (Mar 15 - 21, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the development team focused heavily on protocol stability and the refinement of the Superchain's interoperability layer. Significant effort was directed toward resolving a surge of flaky tests in the CI pipeline to ensure reliable development velocity. Key technical discussions centered on improving the `op-node` initial execution layer (EL) sync logic and advancing the Supernode’s reporting accuracy. Additionally, the introduction of a new feature for ZK dispute games signals continued progress toward diversifying the Optimism ecosystem's proof systems.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol & ZK Proof Enhancements
The project is expanding its fault-proof capabilities with new technical proposals for zero-knowledge components.
*   **ZK Dispute Game:** A new feature was proposed to implement Floating-Point Multiply-Add (FMA) functionality specifically for ZK dispute games ([#369](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/369)).
*   **Gas Pricing Impacts:** The team concluded an investigation into how Glamsterdam gas pricing affects precompile acceleration, ensuring that protocol economics remain aligned with performance goals ([#19375](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19375)).

### Supernode & Interoperability
Work continued on the foundational components of the Superchain to ensure seamless cross-chain interactions.
*   **Reporting Accuracy:** A discrepancy where the Supernode reported the current derived L1 block instead of the verified L1 block was resolved, improving the reliability of node status data ([#18651](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18651)).
*   **Kona Interop:** Developers are refining the `kona-interop` block hash oracle, with a current focus on creating integration tests to verify block lookups that occur prior to the current history window ([#18288](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18288)).
*   **Test Suite Consolidation:** Efforts to deduplicate tests between `op-e2e` and `op-acceptance-tests` are underway. Three tests were successfully migrated to the shared `superfaultproofs` package, reducing maintenance overhead ([#19010](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19010)).

### CI Stability and Infrastructure
A major portion of the week's activity involved hardening the codebase against non-deterministic test failures.
*   **Flaky Test Mitigation:** The team addressed a wide array of race conditions, nonce races, and timeout issues across the CI suite (e.g., [#18137](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18137), [#19588](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19588), [#19583](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19583)).
*   **Resource Management:** Investigations are ongoing regarding a non-terminating CI job (`memory-all-opn-op-geth`), with several pending PRs expected to resolve the underlying resource exhaustion ([#19563](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19563)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Test Reliability:** Multiple issues tracking flaky tests related to message expiration and transaction indexing were closed following successful root-cause analysis and patching ([#19589](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19589), [#19584](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19584)).
*   **Component Scoping:** An issue regarding the blob-archiver's beacon endpoint was identified as repository-specific and moved to the dedicated `blob-archiver` repo to maintain clean project boundaries ([#19623](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19623)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Initial EL Sync UX ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/17631)):** A high-priority discussion is active regarding how `op-node` handles finalization during initial sync. The team is weighing several "finalization gadgets," such as trusted checkpoint providers or L1-based head verification, to prevent nodes from incorrectly marking blocks as finalized.
*   **Chain Lag Tracking:** New issues were opened to investigate specific flakiness in Supernode interop chain lag and unsafe chain stalling, which are critical for real-time network health ([#19610](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19610), [#19608](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19608)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week showed strong collaborative problem-solving, particularly in the area of infrastructure. The rapid identification and movement of the blob-archiver issue ([#19623](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19623)) demonstrates efficient triaging across the Optimism monorepo ecosystem. Furthermore, the deep technical debate surrounding the `op-node` finalization logic ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/17631)) highlights an active and engaged maintainer group focused on protocol correctness and user experience for node operators.
