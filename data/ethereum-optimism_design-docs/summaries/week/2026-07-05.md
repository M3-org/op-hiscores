# ethereum-optimism/design-docs Weekly Report (Jul 5 - 11, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on refining the project's documentation and administrative hygiene by closing several long-standing issues and addressing feedback from external audits. Key activities centered on standardizing protocol terminology and delegating complex technical specifications, such as bond sizing models and supernode configurations, to core repository tracking. The week's theme was one of consolidation and clarity, ensuring that design documentation remains aligned with the evolving OP Stack and Superchain architecture.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no new technical features or pull requests were merged during this period, the repository underwent significant maintenance to ensure the accuracy of the design specifications.

### Protocol Specification Refinement
Efforts were made to ensure the technical language used in the design docs is precise and consistent across the ecosystem.
*   **Terminology Standardization:** The team addressed inconsistencies regarding "soft vs. hard-fork" terminology, ensuring that future protocol upgrades are described using a unified vocabulary to prevent developer confusion [#342](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/342).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The week saw the resolution of several issues ranging from protocol economics to infrastructure scaling.

### Closed Issues
*   **Economic Modeling:** An issue regarding the **Bond Sizing Model**—specifically calculating bonds as a function of dependency-set size and total gas—was closed. It was determined that this work is better tracked as a subset of a larger initiative within the primary `optimism` repository [#21417](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21417).
*   **Infrastructure & Supernodes:** Discussions regarding the **Unichain Mainnet Replicas Supernode** were concluded in this repository, with the technical details being moved to core team tracking for implementation [#21177](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21177).
*   **Security Audit Cleanup:** A feedback issue related to `flags.go` originating from a Lumi Security Audit was triaged and closed after being identified as spam [#21623](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21623).

### New & Active Issues
No new issues were opened during this period, and there were no active discussions exceeding the three-comment threshold, indicating a period of focused internal review rather than high-volume public debate.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by cross-repository coordination. The closure of issues related to Unichain and Bond Sizing indicates active synchronization between the design-docs maintainers and the core OP Stack engineering teams. By moving specific implementation details to the main `optimism` repository, the community is maintaining a clear distinction between high-level design philosophy and low-level execution.
