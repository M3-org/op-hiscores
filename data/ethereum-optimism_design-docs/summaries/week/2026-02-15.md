# ethereum-optimism/design-docs Weekly Report (Feb 15 - 21, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on strengthening the protocol's security framework and refining sequencer performance within the Superchain ecosystem. Key milestones included the introduction of a migration strategy for the SuperDisputeGame and the formalization of Feature Management Agreements (FMa) for L2CM. Development discussions also pivoted toward optimizing the `SequencerActor` to reduce blocking operations, alongside ongoing refinements to gas mechanisms for L2CM upgrades.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol & Security Documentation
The repository saw significant additions to its core protocol documentation, focusing on migration paths and security governance.
*   **SuperDisputeGame Migration:** A new design document was integrated outlining the transition to the SuperDisputeGame, a critical component for the protocol's security and dispute resolution evolution ([#365](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/365)).
*   **L2CM Feature Management:** The team added a Feature Management Agreement (FMa) for L2CM. Located in the security directory, this document establishes the governance and operational standards for managing L2CM features ([#366](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/366)).

### Performance & Infrastructure Refinement
Efforts were made to streamline sequencer operations and clean up legacy architectural components.
*   **Sequencer Optimization:** A new initiative was launched to address performance bottlenecks in the `SequencerActor`. The goal is to move away from a blocking initial engine reset in favor of a non-blocking approach to improve overall system responsiveness ([#19249](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19249)).
*   **Legacy Cleanup:** The team evaluated the removal of Chugsplash and Resolved Delegate proxy code from `ProxyAdmin`. While the issue was closed due to a change in implementation plans, it reflects an ongoing effort to audit and simplify the codebase ([#18842](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18842)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Proxy Infrastructure:** Work on removing legacy proxy code ([#18842](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18842)) was concluded as the team opted for a different implementation path than originally proposed.

### New & Active Issues
*   **L2CM Upgrade Gas Mechanisms:** Active discussion continues regarding the implementation of additional upgrade gas mechanisms in `op-node`. Recent technical debates centered on the metadata requirements for `current_nut_bundle.json`, specifically debating the utility of `forkName` versus `generatedFromCommit` fields to ensure better traceability of contract changes without introducing redundancy ([#18832](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/18832)).
*   **Engine Reset Performance:** The newly opened issue regarding the `SequencerActor` ([#19249](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19249)) represents a priority for improving the efficiency of the sequencing layer.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by deep technical scrutiny of protocol metadata and upgrade safety. Maintainers like **maurelian** have been instrumental in driving the conversation around L2CM upgrades, ensuring that the technical implementation of gas mechanisms and bundle tracking aligns with long-term maintenance needs. The dialogue suggests a high level of rigor regarding how contract changes are canonicalized and tracked across the Superchain.
