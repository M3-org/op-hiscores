# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (May 14, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
The day saw significant progress in integrating `rollup-boost` into the monorepo and enhancing `op-node` observability. Critical bug fixes addressed sequencer stalls, ELSync verifier issues, and Kona proof interop. Several issues related to dispute games and interop strategy were closed, while new issues focused on ZKDisputeGame enhancements and supernode stability.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Rollup-Boost Integration & Observability
The `rollup-boost` library, previously an external submodule, has been fully vendored into the monorepo, bringing its development under the `monorepo:/rust` directory. This move streamlines dependency management and development workflows for the `op-rbuilder` component. Additionally, the `op-node` now includes a new Prometheus counter to track successful follow source updates, improving observability alongside existing error metrics.
- [#20669](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20669)
- [#20763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20763)

### Critical Bug Fixes for Node Stability
Several crucial bugs were addressed, enhancing the stability and resilience of the Optimism stack. Fixes include resolving a deterministic deadlock in `--sequencer.max-safe-lag` that caused permanent sequencer stalls and a batcher-side deadlock preventing recovery. Improvements were also made to `op-devstack` for progress-aware initial sync checks in ELSync verifiers, and `op-node` was made more resilient to deep L1 reorgs beyond the cross-safe head.
- [#19758](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19758)
- [#20652](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20652)
- [#20769](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20769)

### Kona Proof Interoperability & Validation
The Kona client received important fixes related to proof interoperability and output root validation. This includes rejecting future-timestamped prestate in `BootInfo::load` to ensure honest actor behavior and validating the output-root version word in `fetch_safe_head_hash` and `fetch_output_block_hash` to prevent incorrect L2 block hash slicing.
- [#20727](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20727)
- [#20724](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20724)
