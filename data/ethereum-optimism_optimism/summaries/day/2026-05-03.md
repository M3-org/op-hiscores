### Fault Proof Test Enhancements
Significant work was done to improve the `SuperFaultProofs` acceptance suite. This change also allowed for the removal of `MarkFlaky` from certain tests. ([#20482](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20482))

### `op-challenger` Trace Dispatching Issue
A new issue has been identified where `op-challenger run-trace` cannot properly exercise the `super-cannon-kona` game type. The trace-provider factory dispatches both `CannonKonaGameType` and `SuperCannonKonaGameType` through a single-chain `vm.NewKonaExecutor()`, leading to incorrect behavior.
