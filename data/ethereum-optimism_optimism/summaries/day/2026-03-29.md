## ACTIVE ISSUES
- **Flaky Test Resolution:** Issue [#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19804) regarding a flaky test has been actively discussed. The root cause was identified as `l1BlockWithLocalSafeBlocks()` polling the supernode's `RequiredL1` values, which shifted due to sequencer block production during Stage 2 batcher choreography. A fix has been submitted in [#19805](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19805), replacing supernode polling with direct L1 head reads at each batcher choreography stage to maintain invariants.
