# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (May 22, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on critical bug fixes across the stack, particularly addressing stability in Kona node finalization and op-reth sync, alongside significant refactoring of interop error handling. Testing infrastructure saw improvements with CI optimization and removal of an AI contracts job.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Node Stability and Sync Fixes
Several crucial bug fixes were implemented to enhance the stability and synchronization of Optimism nodes. This includes carrying the L2 hash through finalization in the Kona node to prevent silent disagreements ([#20877](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20877)), preventing engine sync-target spin after deep unwinds in `op-reth` ([#20949](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20949)), and stabilizing fuzz targets in `op-service/eth` to prevent CI hangs ([#20940](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20940)).

### Interoperability Refactoring and Contract Adjustments
The interop module underwent significant refactoring to centralize shared error sentinels ([#20896](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20896)) and log helpers ([#20946](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20946)), improving code organization. Additionally, contract-bedrock saw adjustments to increase gas limits for `l2ProxyAdmin.upgradeExecution()` and NUT bundle deployments ([#20986](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20986), [#20977](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20977)), and a revert was made to a fix concerning XLayer LiquidityController minters ([#20981](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20981)).

### CI and Testing Infrastructure Improvements
Efforts were made to optimize the CI pipeline and testing suite. This included disabling Go test coverage and Codecov uploads to reduce overhead ([#20972](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20972)), unblocking CI with verbose test streaming and capped package concurrency ([#20965](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20965)), and speeding up `depreqres` tests in `op-acceptance-tests` ([#20950](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20950)). An AI contracts test job and its supporting system were also removed ([#20954](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20954)).

Critical issues impacting node stability and test reliability were resolved. This includes fixing `FinalizeTask` dropping the L2 hash ([#20811](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20811)), addressing a `proofs-history exex` SIGBUS crash and infinite retry loop during deep FCU rewinds ([#20934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20934)), and resolving flaky fuzz tests `FuzzEncodeDecodeBlob` ([#20935](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20935)) and `FuzzDetectNonBijectivity` ([#20936](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20936)).

A bug in L2CM that dropped liquidity minters on non-standard CGT Chains was also addressed ([#20804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20804)).
