# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (May 28, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on enhancing Superchain stability and developer experience, with significant bug fixes in `op-node` and `contracts-bedrock`, alongside documentation improvements and new acceptance tests.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Superchain Stability and Interoperability
Improvements were made to the `op-node` to enhance cross-safe head caching and redesign the SuperAuthority's safe/finalized head as a tri-state result, addressing transient verifier outages and reorg signals ([#21079], [#21049]). The `op-supernode` also received updates for interop cold start progress logging ([#20961]) and removal of virtual-parent indirection from logsDB ([#21083]).

### Smart Contract Refinements and Documentation
Bug fixes in `contracts-bedrock` ensured the use of a single source of truth for predeploy addresses ([#21010]) and corrected the interface usage in `L2ContractsManagerUtils` ([#21068]). Documentation was enhanced to clarify feature flag wiring in contracts ([#21061]) and to update the `L2ProxyAdmin` semver ([#21062]).

### Developer Experience and Documentation
The developer experience was improved through restructured execution client documentation, making `op-reth` the primary EL and archiving legacy-geth navigation ([#21001]). Guides for `op-challenger` were updated with warnings against restoring SafeDB from snapshots ([#21082]), and the Rust update guide was extended to cover shared `revm`/`alloy` sync in reth updates ([#21086]).

### Testing and Security Enhancements
New acceptance tests were added for L2CM upgrades and fork tests on the Karst betanet, including withdrawal scenarios ([#20668]). A security review from Cantina for Upgrade 19 was also added to the documentation ([#21075]).

## NEW ISSUES

New issues include the migration of `op-alloy-flz` to the monorepo ([#21087]) and a critical problem where `op-node` restarts with `--syncmode=execution-layer` against `op-reth` wipe the SafeDB ([#21089]).

### Testing and Dispute Monitoring
There is a new issue to integrate `op-supernode` with `op-sync-tester` and add extended nightly sync tests ([#21096]). `Dispute-mon` also requires work to support the simplified super permissioned game ([#21098]).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestFollowL2_WithoutCLP2P` ([#20718])
The `TestFollowL2_WithoutCLP2P` test continues to be flaky, with recent failures showing "expected head to advance: local-safe" errors. This indicates a persistent issue with the test's ability to reliably track L2 head advancement without CL P2P.

### Proofs: Increase `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and Reduce Air Gap Constants ([#20774])
Discussions are ongoing regarding increasing `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and reducing air gap constants for faster ZK withdrawals, while maintaining a 7-day total. The team is considering the implications on max history needed and the shared contract implementation for finality and proof maturity delays.

### `op-revm` Support and Dependency Sync ([#21069])
The community is seeking clarity on the synchronization cadence for `op-revm` within the monorepo, especially concerning `revm` and `alloy` crate updates. The team aims to stay in sync with new `revm` releases and periodically update shared dependencies when `reth` is updated, with a recent update to `alloy-*` crates.

### `op-supernode`/`op-reth`: CL Sync Recovery Stalls ([#21076])
A critical issue where CL sync recovery can stall when SafeDB/bookkeeping references an EL-missing block is under active investigation. Recent observations indicate that `op-supernode`'s local safe head can advance, but cross-safe/finalized remains pinned at the activation anchor due to interop cold start being gated on all dependency-set chains. The problem appears to stem from two distinct bugs: `op-node`'s `onPayloadSuccess` advancing `localSafeHead` despite FCU timeouts, and `op-reth`'s engine actor stopping mid-canonicalization. Potential fixes include classifying sustained FCU timeouts as `ResetEvent`s or detecting "EngineTemporaryError with not found" on blocks within the `[finalizedHead, localSafeHead]` range.
