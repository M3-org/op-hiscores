### Rollup-Boost RPC Port-Binding Race Fix
A critical bug in `op-devstack` was addressed, eliminating a race condition where the rollup-boost RPC port could be pre-allocated in Go and then handed to the Rust subprocess, leading to "Address already in use" errors. The fix ensures the port is dynamically assigned and read back, preventing conflicts.
[#20938](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20938)

### Resolution of Flaky Flashblocks Transfer Test
The long-standing flaky test `TestFlashblocksTransfer` was closed after the fix for the rollup-boost RPC port-bind race was merged. The issue had accumulated several distinct root causes over time, including wall-clock assertions, goroutine safety issues, speculative-flashblock divergence, and a close-of-nil-channel panic. The final fix addressed the `EADDRINUSE` error on rollup-boost RPC, which was identified as the root cause of recent failures.
