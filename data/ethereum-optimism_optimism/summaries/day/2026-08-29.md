- **`op-core/superchain` is unconsumable by downstream Go modules: superchain-configs.zip is gitignored** [#22678](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22678)
  User geoknee provided a detailed analysis of the problem, confirming the diagnosis and quantifying the impact of gitignoring `superchain-configs.zip`. The analysis suggests that committing the zip would incur a minimal storage cost (~3 MB/year) compared to the current issue of downstream modules being unable to build. The discussion also explored alternatives like loose files and embedding directly from the `superchain-registry` submodule, concluding that these do not resolve the core problem. A significant finding was that many packages accidentally reach the bundle without needing registry data, and a new PR ([#22700](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22700)) was opened to address this by moving `PathFlag` to a leaf package. A `depguard` test is also proposed to prevent future accidental dependencies.
