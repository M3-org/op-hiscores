  - geoknee provided a detailed analysis of the problem, confirming the diagnosis and offering measurements regarding the impact of gitignoring `superchain-configs.zip`.
  - The analysis quantifies the storage cost of committing the zip file (approximately 3 MB/year) and compares it to the current issue of downstream modules being unable to build.
  - It also explores alternatives like loose files versus a zip and explains why embedding directly from the `superchain-registry` submodule is not a viable solution.
  - A fourth direction is proposed: reducing the number of packages that reach the bundle, citing examples where `op-service` packages unnecessarily pull in registry data. This includes the fix implemented in [#22700](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22700).
  - The comment concludes by summarizing the findings and offering to create a PR for the chosen solution.
