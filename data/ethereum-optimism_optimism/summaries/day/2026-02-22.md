# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (Feb 22, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on a CI configuration update to address publishing permissions for Kona prestates. Several new pull requests were opened, primarily concerning contract documentation and audit fixes.
