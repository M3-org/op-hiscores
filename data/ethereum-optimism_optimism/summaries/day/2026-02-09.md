# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (Feb 9, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on enhancing the Supernode's processing efficiency and robustness, alongside addressing critical issues related to chain rewinding. Several new pull requests were opened, indicating active development across various components.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Supernode Performance and Stability Improvements
The Supernode received updates to improve its processing speed and prevent busy-waiting. The interop activity ticker rate was lowered for faster progress, and a backoff period was introduced to prevent excessive resource consumption when chains are not ready. Additionally, a critical fix was implemented to prevent the Supernode from rewinding over finalized heads, addressing a potential crash scenario.
- Faster Processing with Backoff ([#19037](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19037))
- Never Rewind Over Finalized Head ([#19109](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19109))
