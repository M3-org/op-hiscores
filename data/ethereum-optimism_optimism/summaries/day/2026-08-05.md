# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (Aug 5, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on bug fixes in the op-deployer, enhancing CI reliability, and significant internal refactoring across op-batcher, op-service, op-node, and op-dispute-mon for improved performance and maintainability.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### op-deployer Bug Fixes and Updates
Critical fixes were implemented in the `op-deployer` to correctly resolve `SuperchainConfig` in `InitLiveStrategy`, addressing issues with OPCMv2. Additionally, the `SP1PlonkAdapter` was integrated into OPCM releases, streamlining verifier deployment.

## CLOSED ISSUES

### op-geth Decoupling Milestones Achieved
The significant effort to decouple `op-geth` has reached a major milestone with the closure of [#20264](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20264). This issue focused on making `op-service/sources` the OP-aware eth client, a critical step in reducing reliance on upstream `go-ethereum` for L2-specific functionalities. The discussion highlighted the need for a payload-native approach for the batcher and careful handling of L2 transaction representations and receipt root derivations.
- [#20264](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20264)

### Fork Tooling Refinement
The `op-geth` decoupling initiative also led to the closure of [#21746](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21746), which involved checking and updating fork tools. This included deleting stale checkers and migrating remaining tools to `op-core` for better consistency and maintainability.
- [#21746](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21746)

### OPCM Contract Deployment
The deployment of the `SP1PlonkAdapter` contract by OPCM was addressed and closed in [#22176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22176). This ensures that the SP1 verifier is part of an OPCM release, following the pattern of other shared contracts like `MIPS.sol`.
- [#22176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22176)

### Kona Acceptance Test Flakiness Resolved
Several issues related to flaky acceptance tests when running against `kona-node` were closed. This includes resolving `TestCLELDivergence` and enforcing `TestMultiELSync` failures, improving the reliability and accuracy of the test suite for Kona.
- [#22247](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22247)
