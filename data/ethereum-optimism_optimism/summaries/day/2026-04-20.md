# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (Apr 20, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on enhancing interop functionality with log backfill and validation, alongside critical bug fixes in Kona for Interop activation and CI stability. Significant progress was also made in CI/CD improvements and test suite reliability.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Interoperability Enhancements and Supernode Improvements
A new feature was added to `op-supernode` to enable interop log backfill depth and validation gating, improving the pre-ingestion of initiating logs and enhancing the `ChainContainer` with `TimestampToBlockNumber` functionality ([#19973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19973)). This is a significant step towards more robust cross-chain interoperability within the Superchain.

### Kona Bug Fixes and CI/CD Stability
Several critical bug fixes were implemented in Kona, addressing state divergence issues between `kona-node` and `op-node` during single-chain Interop activation by conditionally deploying `CrossL2Inbox` ([#20147](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20147)). Another fix corrected an argument passed to `is_interop_active()` in the Kona proof driver, ensuring the correct block timestamp is used instead of the block number ([#20162](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20162)). CI stability was also improved by pinning `mise` to a specific version in the `docker-build-prep` action ([#20170](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20170)) and updating CI gate jobs in the CircleCI configuration ([#20190](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20190)).

### Test Suite Reliability and CI Provenance
Efforts were made to improve the reliability of the test suite, including unmarking flaky tests related to `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes` ([#20159](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20159)). Additionally, the CI process was strengthened by adding a verification step for `ci-base-clang` provenance attestation in GitHub Actions ([#20171](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20171)) and allowing overrides for `-count` and `-timeout` in `just test` for `op-acceptance-tests` ([#20161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20161)). The `ci-base-clang` image for Rust jobs was also pinned, and runtime `apt-install` was skipped ([#20135](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20135)).
