# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (Mar 23, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on enhancing testing infrastructure, fixing critical bugs in the Kona protocol and interop filter, and advancing interop capabilities with ETH bridge contract deployments.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Enhanced Testing and Flaky Test Tracking
Improvements were made to the testing framework, including the addition of `FLAKY_FAIL`/`FLAKY_PASS` annotations for better tracking of flaky tests. This helps in identifying and addressing intermittent test failures more effectively.
- [#19719](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19719)

### Critical Bug Fixes in Kona and Interop Filter
Several significant bugs were addressed, including a fix for the `action-tests-single` CI job in Kona that was not actually running tests. Another crucial fix prevents `uint64` underflow in the `op-interop-filter`'s `calculateStartingBlock` function, ensuring correct block processing. The Kona protocol also received a fix to continue processing logs even if some are malformed during receipt updates.
- [#19689](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19689)
- [#19688](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19688)
- [#19720](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19720)

### Interoperability and ETH Bridge Deployment
Work continued on interoperability, specifically with the deployment and funding of `SuperchainETHBridge` and `ETHLiquidity` contracts during interop network activation. This is a key step in enabling seamless ETH transfers within the Superchain.

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona Guest Panic Deserializing `chainList.json`
Discussion continues on the Kona guest program panicking during Cannon VM execution when deserializing the superchain registry chain list, specifically due to a missing `name` field. A link to a Notion page detailing "kona proofs panic: Failed to read chain list" was shared for further investigation.
- [#19654](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19654)

### Flaky Test `TestCLELDivergence` Investigation
Investigation into the flaky `TestCLELDivergence` continues. Recent comments suggest that the test's comment regarding EL-sync resetting the status tracker might be incorrect, and that the observed reset is part of the initial derivation pipeline lifecycle rather than an in-progress EL sync. The team is assessing whether the reset behavior is correct or if it indicates a bug in the reset logic.
- [#19681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19681)

### Interop Phase 3: Removing Chains From Shared Dispute Games
A new issue has been opened to discuss the ability to remove a chain from a shared dispute game. A question was raised regarding the necessity of this feature and if it implies chains being removed from the interop set.
- [#19692](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19692)

### CGT Chains and Shared Dispute Games
Discussion is ongoing regarding potential issues with custom gas token (CGT) chains forming interop sets. A comment highlighted that enabling CGT chains in the interop fork might require workarounds, specifically concerning the deployment and enabling of `SuperchainETHBridge`/`ETHLiquidity` contracts.
- [#19711](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19711)

### Review of `kona-interop` Block Hash Oracle
An update was provided on adding a fixture-based integration test for `OracleInteropProvider::header_by_number` in `kona-proof-interop` to address the review of the `kona-interop` block hash oracle. The new test uses real OP Mainnet proof data and runs significantly faster than a full acceptance test. The goal is to ensure block lookups prior to the current history window work correctly.
- [#18288](https://github.ism/optimism/issues/18288)

### Overlap Between CGT and ETHLiquidity Contracts
A comment noted the overlap between CGT and `ETHLiquidity` contracts, suggesting it's a worthy consideration when scoping the case for CGT chains joining interop.
