## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestFlashblocksTransfer` ([#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19934))
The `TestFlashblocksTransfer` continues to be a flaky test, with recent investigations revealing that `op-rbuilder` produced zero flashblocks for an entire test run, causing the test to hang. A new pull request ([#20600](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20600)) has been opened to restructure the test into two bounded phases with clearer diagnostics, aiming to distinguish between transaction confirmation issues and flashblock observation failures. This change will make failures faster and more unambiguous, though the underlying `op-rbuilder` flashblock-emission bug still needs to be addressed.

### Kona and op-node Brotli Channel Disagreement ([#19333](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19333))
Investigation into the divergence between Kona and op-node regarding brotli-compressed channels has revealed a critical bug in Kona's wrapper, where it silently accepts corrupted streams and produces incorrect output. This issue is consensus-relevant as a malicious batcher could exploit this. A fix for the Kona wrapper bug is being prepared, which is expected to eliminate 95% of the divergences. Further investigation is ongoing for the remaining 11 corruption-only divergences, and a library swap is deemed unnecessary.
