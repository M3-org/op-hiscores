## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Observations from Mirroring op-supernode in Lokahi [#22636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22636)
Discussions on this issue highlight several discrepancies and potential improvements found while mirroring `op-supernode`'s behavior in Lokahi. Key points from the latest comments include:
- **Missing `opstack` Block-Building Namespace in Kona-node:** `kona-node` lacks the experimental `opstack` block-building namespace (`opstack_openBlockV1`, `sealBlockV1`, etc.) that `op-node` registers. This leads to "Method not found" errors when the `op-test-sequencer` attempts to build blocks through `kona-node`'s CL, causing test failures. This is considered a significant feature gap for CL-side block building.
- **Missing `superroot` Namespace in Kona-node:** `kona-node`'s RPC does not register the `superroot` namespace, unlike `op-node`. This means that dispute infrastructure pointing to a standalone `kona-node` as a super root RPC will encounter "Method not found" errors.
- **Deep L1 Reorgs and Verification Halts:** A residual case after a stability gate fix shows that a deep post-commit L1 reorg that resets derivation below the first *stable* SafeDB entry can still wipe history and permanently halt verification. This behavior is similar to `op-supernode` and needs further investigation.
- **Misleading Job Naming in CI:** A detailed comment points out that the `memory-all-kona-lokahi-op-reth-<fork>` CI job has been running `supernode_kind: op-supernode` despite its name suggesting `lokahi`. This has led to false positives regarding Lokahi's acceptance test pass rates. Recommendations include renaming the job to accurately reflect the implementation being tested and ensuring the selected supernode kind is reported in test records. It was also noted that simply checking for the existence of a binary (`test -x`) does not confirm it was actually run.
