# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (May 23, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on enhancing the `op-supernode` with a new dependency-set flag and significant refactoring of interopgen configurations and `op-supervisor` components, streamlining the codebase.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Supernode Configuration Enhancements
A new top-level `--dependency-set` flag was added to `op-supernode`, simplifying the management of global dependency properties across chains. This improves configuration consistency for supernode operations. ([#20976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20976))

### Codebase Streamlining and Refactoring
The `op-supervisor` build and architecture references were removed, indicating a shift in its role within the ecosystem. Additionally, the `depset` package was relocated and renamed within `op-chain-ops/interopgen/config`, and unused `op-node` interop subcommands were removed to clean up the codebase. ([#20994](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20994), [#20993](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20993), [#20992](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20992))
