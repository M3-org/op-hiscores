# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (Aug 26, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
The day saw focused development on the `kona` component, with improvements to proof replay warnings and gossip subscription topic handling. Several new pull requests were opened, covering sequencer-defined fees, private interoperable chains, and documentation updates.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Kona Protocol Enhancements
Improvements were made to the `kona` protocol, specifically addressing proof replay warnings to make them more actionable and refining gossip subscription topic handling.
- Proof replay warnings in `kona-sp1-proposer` were made actionable by clarifying the expected behavior during L1 rewinding to recover complete channels ([#22623](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22623)).
- The `kona-gossip` component was updated to allowlist gossip subscription topics, moving away from the default `AllowAllSubscriptionFilter` to prevent acceptance of subscriptions for any announced topic ([#22661](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22661)).
