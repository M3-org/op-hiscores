## ACTIVE ISSUES

### High Intrinsic Gas on Optimism Sepolia after Karst Hard Fork
The issue [#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21457) regarding "intrinsic gas too high" on Optimism Sepolia after the Karst hard fork continues to be active. The latest comments confirm that the problem persists, with `eth_estimateGas` returning an error for `depositToken` calls, despite `staticCall` succeeding and explicit `gasLimit` allowing transactions to pass on-chain. The issue has been reproduced across multiple RPC providers (Alchemy, dRPC, Nodies, OnFinality), while Tenderly returns a valid gas estimate. A related pull request, [#21574](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21574), is being monitored for its progress in addressing this known issue, which is expected to be fixed in the next release.
