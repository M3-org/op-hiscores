# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (Jun 18, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
The day's development focused on enhancing the `op-reth` payload builder by integrating the supervisor interop failsafe, preventing interop transactions during failsafe windows.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### op-reth Payload Builder Enhancement
A new feature was added to the `op-reth` payload builder to gate its operation on the interop failsafe. This ensures that during a failsafe window, interop transactions are not sealed into unsafe blocks, addressing a previous oversight where the builder continued to process them. This improvement enhances the robustness and security of transaction processing within `op-reth`. ([#21448](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21448))
