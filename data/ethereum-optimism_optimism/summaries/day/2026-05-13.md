# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (May 13, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on critical bug fixes across the Superchain, including addressing buffer exhaustion in `kona::decompress_brotli` and stabilizing `superroot_atTimestamp` responses. A new metric for follow source errors was added, and significant refactoring occurred in `op-reth/proofs`.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Enhanced Stability and Error Handling in Supernode and Interop Components
Several bug fixes improved the robustness of the `op-supernode` and `op-interop-filter` components. This included preventing ingestion from continuing after fatal errors in `op-interop-filter` ([#20646](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20646)), stabilizing the `Data` field in `superroot_atTimestamp` responses ([#20658](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20658)), and guarding the executing-message verifier against interop activation block issues ([#20716](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20716)). Additionally, expected tip lookup misses in `op-supernode` were quieted to debug logs ([#20720](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20720)).

### Critical Fixes for Kona and FPVM Precompiles
Important bug fixes were implemented in the `kona` protocol, addressing a buffer-exhaustion bug in `decompress_brotli` ([#20598](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20598)). The FPVM precompile oracle gas for Glamsterdam (EIP-7904) was also updated to ensure correct L2 gas charges and oracle/L1 staticcall gas requirements ([#20689](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20689)).

### Improvements in Proofs and Developer Tooling
The `op-reth/proofs` component saw significant refactoring to introduce strict proof-window semantics and a `ProofWindowRange` API ([#20707](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20707)). A bug related to sharded key encoding for trie history was also fixed, ensuring correct byte-wise sorting ([#20713](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20713)). Furthermore, `op-e2e/config` was updated to return fully independent `DeployConfig` instances per call, preventing issues with shared configurations ([#20686](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20686)).

### New Metrics for Operational Visibility
A new Prometheus counter, `op_node_default_follow_source_errors_total`, was added to `op-node` to track errors during upstream following, providing better visibility into potential issues with `fetch_status`, `invalid_state`, `l1_lookup`, and `l1_mismatch` ([#20656](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20656)).
