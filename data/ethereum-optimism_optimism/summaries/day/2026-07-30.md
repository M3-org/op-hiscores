## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `kona-sp1-proposer`: Decompose `sync_games` [#22019](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22019)
The discussion on this issue highlights the need to refactor the `sync_games` function in `kona-sp1-proposer` due to its complexity (around 400 lines). A key point raised is the consolidation of creation-side state, which is currently tracked across multiple fields with separate reset logic. The suggestion is to unify these into a single lifecycle value, moving from `None` to `Unconfirmed(sent bytes)` to `Confirmed(sequence, address)` and back to `None` once the cache catches up. This aims to simplify the logic and reduce redundancy. Long-term, a proper transaction manager is envisioned to absorb the unconfirmed state out of the proposer entirely.
