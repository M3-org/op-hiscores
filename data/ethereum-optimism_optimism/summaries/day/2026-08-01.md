## ACTIVE ISSUES
### Light-Sequencer Oscillation During Recovery
The issue [#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21964) concerning the `op-node`'s light-sequencer configuration experiencing oscillation during recovery from a divergence between its unsafe head and the upstream safe chain remains active. A new occurrence was logged today, where the oscillation defeated a height-based gate, leading to a test failure. The `memory-all-opn-op-reth-prague` job on `develop` failed, with the light sequencer's EL block being its own locally-built fork, causing a transaction assertion to fail. The logs indicate a `Follow Source: Reorg onto upstream chain` followed by the sequencer sealing its own block, and then a `Failed to share forkchoice-updated signal` leading to a `Deriver system is resetting` due to an inconsistent forkchoice state. This suggests the oscillation continues to be a persistent problem, even with previous band-aid fixes.
