## ACTIVE ISSUES
- **Flaky Test: `TestReorgUnsafeHead` ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20198)):** Discussion continued on this persistent flaky test. A new CI occurrence was reported, highlighting a failure where the expected unsafe-head reorg did not trigger within the 30-attempt window. The comment noted that changes from PR [#21121](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21121) are unrelated to this specific flake, confirming it as a distinct issue. Previous comments had identified a root cause and proposed a fix in PR [#21112](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21112), which involved using `ReorgExact` DSL polls to ensure reorgs are confirmed and explicit parents are used. However, the latest comment indicates the flake persists, suggesting the previous fix may not cover all scenarios or new symptoms have emerged.
