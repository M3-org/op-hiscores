# ethereum-optimism/optimism Daily Update (Apr 28, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on significant advancements in Optimism's infrastructure, including the integration of NUT bundles for the Karst fork in Kona, enhanced support for ZK dispute games, and critical bug fixes across various components.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Kona Node Enhancements and Karst Fork Activation
The Kona node received a major feature enabling the execution of NUT bundles at the Karst fork activation, aligning its behavior with `op-node`. This involved adding a `build.rs` to `kona-hardforks` to process `karst_nut_bundle.json` at compile time, ensuring seamless hardfork transitions. Additionally, a bug fix addressed outdated Base references in Kona tests following an upstream registry removal.
- [#20157](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20157)
- [#20393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20393)

### Dispute Game and Deployer Improvements
Significant progress was made in supporting ZK dispute games, with the `op-deployer` now including `GameTypeZKDisputeGame` and `ZKDisputeGameConfig` for OPCM V2 upgrades. The deployer also gained the ability to output SuperFaultDispute Game implementations and hydrate `OpcmUtilsImpl` and `OpcmMigratorImpl` in its state, improving deployment accuracy. A critical fix addressed the default enabling of `cannon-kona` challenger runtimes in `op-acceptance-tests`.
- [#20219](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20219)
- [#20395](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20395)
- [#20403](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20403)
- [#20361](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20361)

### Protocol and Contract Management Refinements
The rollup configuration parsing was made non-strict to facilitate smoother transitions after the removal of the protocol versions field. A crucial fix was implemented in the OPCM to explicitly reject OZ v5 Initializable slot-clearing, enhancing contract security. Furthermore, the `implementations` struct in contracts was refactored to an array, streamlining contract management.
