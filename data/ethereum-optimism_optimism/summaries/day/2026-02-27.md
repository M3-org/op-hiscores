## ACTIVE ISSUES
### `op/kona-node lite`: Following `local_safe` and `safe` heads from source ([#19325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19325))
Discussion continues on how an `op-node` in lite mode (following a source) should handle `local_safe_l2` and `safe_l2` heads, especially in the context of interop. The core of the discussion revolves around distinguishing between "fully verified safe" (`safe_l2`) and "not yet interop-verified" (`local_safe_l2`). The current implementation in `op-node` for pre-interop scenarios sets `local_safe_l2` and `safe_l2` to be the same when `localSafeIsFullySafe()` is true. However, with interop, it's crucial to differentiate these two. The proposal is to ensure that a light CL's `local_safe_l2` and `safe_l2` accurately reflect those of its source, rather than conflating them. This would involve fine-grained injection of external information to maintain the distinction.
