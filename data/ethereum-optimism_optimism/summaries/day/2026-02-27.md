## ACTIVE ISSUES
### Op-Node Lite Mode and Safe Head Tracking ([#19325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19325))
Discussion continues on how an `op-node` in lite mode (following a source) should track both `local_safe` and `safe` heads. The core of the discussion revolves around distinguishing between "unqualified safe" (blocks derived from L1 without interop verification) and "qualified safe" (blocks derived from L1 and passed interop verification).

- **Clarification of Terminology**: The discussion clarified that `SyncStatus.safe_l2` represents the "fully verified safe" or "cross safe," while `SyncStatus.local_safe_l2` refers to a "qualified safe" that is not yet interop-verified.
- **Supernode vs. Light CL Behavior**: It was noted that in the supernode, virtual CLs track both `local_safe_l2` and `safe_l2`, which is crucial for interop. However, the current "follow source" protocol in lite CLs does not allow both heads to be synced independently, leading to the `safe_l2` of the source being used as the `local_safe_l2` of the destination.
- **Proposed Solution**: The proposal is to ensure that the `local_safe_l2` of the light CL aligns with the `local_safe_l2` of the source, and similarly for `safe_l2`, to accurately reflect the source's sync status. This would involve fine-grained injection of external information to distinguish between `local_safe_l2` and `safe_l2` during interop.
