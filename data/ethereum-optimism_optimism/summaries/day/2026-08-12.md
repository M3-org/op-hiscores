## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Interop Filter Performance and Reliability
- **[#21943](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21943) op-interop-filter: verdict RPC can block behind ingester write lock, turning slow ingestion into fail-closed timeouts:** A recent CI flake in `memory-all-opn-op-reth-fusaka` on `develop` was attributed to this defect. The `TestInteropFilter_IngressRejectsInvalid` test failed because `op-reth`'s `checkAccessList` query timed out, leading to a "interop quorum not reached" error. This behavior is consistent with the verdict path blocking behind the ingester write lock, causing a ~2.5s silence from the filter component. The issue highlights that a definitive rejection for an invalid access-list key should be immediate, rather than a timeout.
