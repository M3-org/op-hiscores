# ethereum-optimism/optimism Weekly Report (Jan 11 - 17, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the Optimism ecosystem focused on protocol simplification and the integration of the Rust-based `kona` stack into the monorepo. Significant progress was made in streamlining the `op-proposer` by removing legacy `L2OutputOracle` support and enhancing the `op-challenger` with improved VM stability and MIPSVM randomness. The project also reached a major milestone with the completion of L1 Pectra Defense upgrade tasks for both Sepolia and Mainnet, alongside the finalization of U18 releases. These efforts collectively advance the Superchain's security, interoperability, and developer experience.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol Simplification & Upgrades
The team continued to prune legacy logic and align with the latest Ethereum standards.
*   **Legacy Removal:** Support for the `L2OutputOracle` was removed from `op-proposer` to simplify the proposer's logic ([#18706](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18706)).
*   **Go 1.24 & MIPSVM:** The `SupportWorkingSysGetRandom` feature flag was removed, permanently enabling `SysGetRandom` support in the `cannon/mipsevm` ([#18624](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18624)).
*   **Core Updates:** The project saw an upgrade to `op-geth` ([#18767](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18767)) and the alignment of compiler restrictions for `StorageSetter` in `contracts-bedrock` ([#18715](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18715)).

### Interoperability & Superchain Infrastructure
Work accelerated on the "Interoperability" roadmap, focusing on the `op-supernode` and `op-interop-filter`.
*   **Security:** Added JWT authentication to the `op-interop-filter` admin API and introduced route-specific JWT support in `op-service` ([#18771](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18771), [#18769](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18769)).
*   **Metrics & Monitoring:** Multiplexed `op-supernode` metrics by `chain_id` to serve them at a single endpoint ([#18750](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18750)).
*   **Kona Integration:** Significant effort was directed toward upstreaming `op-rs/kona` to the monorepo, including adding a safe head DB and reviewing derivation logic ([#18754](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18754), [#18748](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18748)).

### Fault Proofs & Challenger Improvements
The `op-challenger` and related dispute monitoring tools received stability and feature updates.
*   **VM Stability:** Added a configurable timeout to the `vm-runner` to prevent indefinite hangs during trace generation ([#18594](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18594)).
*   **Dispute Monitoring:** Completed the integration of a supernode provider into the challenger and updated the `dispute-mon` dashboard with multi-node metrics ([#18524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/18524), [#18205](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/18205)).

### CI/CD & Developer Experience
*   **Testing Automation:** Implemented weekly updates for upgrade test blocks to ensure CI environments remain current ([#18634](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18634)).
*   **Fuzzing:** Converted `MintManager` tests to fuzz tests for broader edge-case coverage ([#18744](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18744)).
*   **Tooling:** Updated `golangci-lint` to v2 and introduced new semgrep rules for `OPContractsManagerV2` ([#18778](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18778), [#18679](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18679)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **L1 Pectra Defense:** Successfully closed tasks related to the DGF Upgrade for Sepolia and Mainnet ([#14065](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/14065), [#14068](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/14068)).
*   **Simulation Fixes:** Resolved a critical bug where `eth_simulateV1` returned errors regarding DA footprints after the Jovian fork; the fix was released in `op-geth v1.101605.0` ([#739](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/739)).
*   **Audit Completion:** The Dispute Game Creator Pattern audit was finalized as part of the U18 audit ([#17069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/17069)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Safe-Head Advancement:** Ongoing discussion regarding excessive Forkchoice Updated (FCU) calls in `op-node`. Current implementations may still be performing FCUs on every update rather than buffering them, causing latency issues ([#15393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/15393)).
*   **Sync Loops:** A new issue identifies a potential infinite loop for L1 nodes when `SkipSyncStartCheck` is enabled ([#18779](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/18779)).
*   **System Analysis:** Investigation is underway to determine if "zombie accounts" exist on OPM in light of retroactive EIP-7610 application ([#18757](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/18757)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by heavy collaboration on the integration of Rust components (`kona`) into the Go-centric monorepo. Discussions between contributors like `0x416e746f6e`, `teddyknox`, and `clabby` regarding FCU optimizations highlight a deep focus on node performance and network efficiency. Furthermore, the coordination between the `op-geth` and `op-node` teams was evident in the rapid resolution of the `eth_simulateV1` issue, which was critical for ecosystem participants utilizing backrunning strategies.
