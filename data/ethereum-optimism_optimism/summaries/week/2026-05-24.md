# ethereum-optimism/optimism Weekly Report (May 24 - 30, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on hardening the **Supernode** infrastructure and advancing the integration of the **Kona** Rust-based stack into the monorepo. Significant efforts were directed toward protocol stability, including critical fixes for engine controller initialization and verifier error propagation to prevent silent chain stalls. The project also made strides in developer experience by deprecating legacy components like `op-geth` in favor of `op-reth` and streamlining the codebase through the removal of stale `op-supervisor` packages and long-standing TODOs.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Supernode Stability & Interoperability
The Supernode received several enhancements to improve robustness during restarts and cross-chain operations.
*   **Initialization & Error Handling:** Fixed a bug ensuring the engine controller retries initialization during virtual node restarts ([#20991](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20991)) and ensured verifier read errors are propagated to the `super_authority` to prevent incorrect cold-start interpretations ([#21002](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21002)).
*   **Data Integrity:** Introduced Write-Ahead Log (WAL) canonical payloads for rewind and invalidate operations, ensuring data integrity during non-canonical state transitions ([#21015](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21015), [#21040](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21040)).
*   **Performance:** Implemented retries for `RewindEngine` on `DeadlineExceeded` errors to prevent stalls when interacting with slow Execution Layers ([#21023](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21023)).

### Kona Integration & CI/CD
Following the Kona monorepo merge, work continued to stabilize the Rust-based prestate environment.
*   **CI Workflows:** Added automated publishing of Kona prestates to GCS ([#20999](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20999)) and fixed Docker build issues to ensure `mise.toml` preservation for reproducible builds ([#21014](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21014)).
*   **Bug Fixes:** Resolved a critical issue in `op-chain-ops` where `check-prestate` for `cannon64-kona` was broken due to incorrect `superchain-registry` commit lookups ([#21036](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21036)).

### Execution Layer & Node Refinement
*   **op-reth Transition:** Documentation was updated to establish `op-reth` as the primary Execution Layer, including deprecation banners for `op-geth` ([#21001](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21001), [#21037](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21037)).
*   **Sync Improvements:** `op-reth` now clamps `sync_target` to mitigate race conditions ([#21012](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21012)), and `op-node` was updated to prevent stalls during EL sync when encountering `NotFound` errors ([#21004](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21004)).

### Smart Contracts & Tooling
*   **Resource Management:** Added functionality to scale `ResourceConfig` to fit smaller gas limits for the `v7.0.0` release ([#20925](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20925)).
*   **Dependency Updates:** Updated `op-deployer` to track the latest `op-contracts/v7.0.0-rc.4` tags ([#21045](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21045)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw a heavy emphasis on maintenance and technical debt reduction, evidenced by the mass removal of stale TODOs and the deprecation of legacy documentation. Collaboration was particularly visible in the **Supernode** and **Kona** workstreams, where cross-component fixes (linking `op-node`, `op-reth`, and `op-chain-ops`) were required to maintain protocol consistency. Discussions regarding `op-reth` RPC methods ([#21031](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21031)) and the SuperZKDG design ([#21038](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21038)) indicate active coordination on the next phase of the Superchain's security and dispute architecture.
