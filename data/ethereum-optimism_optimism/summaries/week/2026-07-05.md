# ethereum-optimism/optimism Weekly Report (Jul 5 - 11, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused heavily on the maturation of the **Super ZK Dispute Game (SZKDG)**, marking its readiness through successful migration testing and the integration of ZK mock verifiers. Significant stability improvements were made to the **Kona client** and **op-reth**, including a critical fix for a memory leak during backfills. The team also prioritized infrastructure reliability by transitioning scheduled tests to a daily cadence and refining the project's fuzzing strategy to focus on high-value, deterministic seed testing.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Super ZK & Dispute Game Evolution
The project reached a significant milestone in its ZK roadmap, completing the implementation and testing of migrations for isolated chains via `OPCMv2.upgrade()`.
*   **SZKDG Readiness:** Integrated a ZK mock verifier into `op-deployer` ([#21571](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21571)) and expanded integration test coverage for respected-type semantics and chain-set evolution ([#21128](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21128)).
*   **Tooling:** The `op-challenger` was enhanced with a native `game-proposal-outputs` subcommand, replacing legacy shell scripts to improve maintainability ([#21542](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21542)).
*   **Post-U19 Adjustments:** Updated `contracts-bedrock` to handle the disabled Cannon fork state and deprecated legacy permissionless games on OP/Ink ([#21621](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21621)).

### Node Infrastructure & Kona Client Stability
Efforts were concentrated on hardening the execution and rollup clients, particularly addressing resource management and protocol edge cases.
*   **Memory Management:** Resolved a critical memory leak in `op-reth` where Prometheus metric buckets were not being drained during CLI backfills, preventing unbounded RSS growth ([#21596](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21596)).
*   **Kona Client Fixes:** Addressed precompile handling in the fpvm provider, ensuring proper gas consumption during "out-of-gas" scenarios and halts ([#21582](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21582), [#21634](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21634)).
*   **Version Reporting:** Corrected version reporting for `op-reth` and `kona-node` to ensure metrics and CLI outputs align with build versions rather than upstream defaults ([#21627](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21627), [#21630](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21630)).

### CI/CD & Developer Experience
*   **Testing Cadence:** Moved scheduled tests from weekly to daily to accelerate the feedback loop ([#21595](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21595)).
*   **Documentation:** Introduced a new review guide and AI-assisted tooling for upstream dependency updates (`reth`, `revm`, `alloy`) to improve security during bumps ([#21581](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21581)).
*   **App Dev Support:** Added a comprehensive tutorial for deploying contracts to OP Sepolia ([#21607](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21607)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **ZK Scoping:** Successfully closed the primary epic for ZK Proofs research and scoping ([#21401](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21401)), alongside the bond sizing model ([#21417](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21417)).
*   **Protocol Fixes:** The Karst hard fork gas issue on OP Sepolia was resolved and included in the `op-reth/v2.3.3` release ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21457)).
*   **Spam Mitigation:** Several unsolicited security audit reports from "Lumi Security Audit" were identified as spam/advertisements and closed ([#21606](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21606)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Fuzzing Strategy:** A new discussion is underway regarding the retirement of low-value Go fuzz CI jobs in favor of deterministic seed tests, pending a more robust nightly fuzzing infrastructure ([#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21636)).
*   **Interoperability Flakiness:** A new issue ([#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21635)) identifies a race condition in the `SupernodeLightSequencer` where stale build work can halt the sequencer; a fix is currently being drafted.
*   **Dependency Bumps:** The update for `revm` to 2.4.0/2.3.1 remains blocked pending the upstream release ([#21524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21524)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw a high degree of internal coordination regarding the **op-geth decoupling effort**, specifically around the removal of `op-service/superutil` and the reorganization of EIP-1559 and fee imports ([#20271](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20271)). There is also an increasing focus on automated governance and review processes, evidenced by the introduction of AI agents to assist in dependency reviews, signaling a shift toward more scalable maintainer workflows.
