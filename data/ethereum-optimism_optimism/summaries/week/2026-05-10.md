# ethereum-optimism/optimism Weekly Report (May 10 - 16, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on hardening the **op-supernode** and **interoperability** stack, with significant progress made in stabilizing cross-chain message verification and recovery mechanisms. A major milestone was reached with the conclusion of interop stress testing, confirming the scalability of the 7-day message expiry window. Development also accelerated on the **op-reth** trie engine and **Kona** protocol, addressing critical consensus-level edge cases and improving diagnostic tooling for flashblocks. Despite resolving several long-standing flaky tests, new challenges emerged regarding Kona's parity with `op-node` and potential consensus splits in interop activation.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interoperability & Supernode Stability
The team implemented several critical fixes to ensure the `op-supernode` can reliably handle network disruptions and reorgs.
*   **Reorg Recovery:** Resolved a critical issue where `logsDB` failed to recover from L2 reorgs occurring while the supernode was offline ([#20628](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20628)).
*   **Sync Improvements:** Updated `op-proposer` to fail over to caught-up supernode sync status ([#20615](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20615)) and introduced a Raft-WAL-backed `LogsDB` for better persistence ([#20688](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20688)).
*   **Tooling:** Launched `check-interop`, a new CLI tool for sending and relaying interop transactions between L2 chains ([#20621](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20621)).

### op-reth & Kona Protocol
Significant work was dedicated to the trie engine and fault-proof components.
*   **Trie Engine:** Integrated the proofs ExEx to `EngineHandle` for background trie state mutations ([#20450](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20450)) and added a core engine layer combining the trie buffer and persistence service ([#20419](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20419)).
*   **Kona Refinements:** Fixed frame decoding to reject invalid `is_last` bytes ([#20590](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20590)) and corrected state trie handling for `DestroyedChanged` accounts ([#20640](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20640)).
*   **Gas & Standards:** Bumped FPVM precompile oracle gas for Glamsterdam (EIP-7904) in the Kona client ([#20689](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20689)).

### Infrastructure & Testing
*   **Flashblocks:** Refactored `TestFlashblocksTransfer` into bounded phases with parallel collection to prevent test hangs and improve diagnostics ([#20600](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20600)).
*   **Dependency Management:** Addressed a security advisory by bumping the `rkyv` Rust dependency to v0.8.16 ([#20653](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20653)).
*   **Contract Registry:** Improved contract flag coverage and OPCM prerelease filtering to ensure valid migration states across feature modes ([#20617](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20617)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Interop Stress Testing:** Closed [#15285](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/15285) after confirming that interop message processing costs are dominated by graph reduction rather than message age, validating the 7-day expiry window.
*   **Flaky Tests:** Successfully resolved several persistent test failures, including `TestUnsafeGapFillAfterSafeReorg` ([#19884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19884)) and `TestFollowL2_ReorgRecovery` ([#19967](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19967)), by addressing sequencer stalls and L1 EL transient issues.
*   **Governance/Deployment:** Resolved issues related to upgrading chains from permissioned to permissionless ([#20562](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20562)) and merging chains into shared dispute games ([#19703](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19703)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Consensus Risks:** A high-priority issue ([#20684](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20684)) was opened regarding a vulnerability where Kona accepts invalid interop activation-block transitions that the standard OP verification rejects, potentially causing a fault-proof validation split.
*   **Stability Concerns:** Reports emerged of `op-reth` v2.2.1 silently crashing when syncing with DB V2 ([#20679](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20679)).
*   **Kona Parity:** Kona may require a switch to a custom Brotli decompression implementation to maintain incremental-flush parity with `op-node` ([#20666](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20666)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by rapid response to infrastructure friction. The community actively engaged in resolving "bounty" issues, specifically regarding developer tooling for deployment factories ([#12](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/12)). There was also significant cross-project coordination, evidenced by the upstream PR in the Viem library to support timestamp-based withdrawal proofs ([#18614](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/18614)). The "Codex" team contributed several PRs to prevent P2P port collisions in supernode deployments, indicating a focus on improving the multi-chain developer experience.
