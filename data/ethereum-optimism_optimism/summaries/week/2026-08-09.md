# ethereum-optimism/optimism Weekly Report (Aug 9 - 15, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on the successful stabilization and verification of the **SDM (System Deposit Model) devnet**, which is now operating in a "hands-free" steady state with active fault proofs. Significant strides were made in the **Permissionless Chain Deployment (PCD)** pipeline, completing Milestone 4 and automating network creation workflows. Technical efforts also centered on hardening the **Kona engine** and gossip protocols, alongside expanding CI/CD infrastructure to support matrix testing for future L1 forks.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Kona Engine & Gossip Protocol Refinement
Developers implemented significant architectural improvements to Kona to enhance performance and security.
*   **Wire Type Abstraction:** Signed gossip framing, Snappy compression, and SSZ codecs were moved to private `kona-gossip` wire types to ensure payload authentication before decoding ([#22305](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22305)).
*   **Payload Optimization:** Introduced `OpExecutionPayloadEnvelope` to centralize block encoding and handle fork invariants across V1–V4 payloads ([#22304](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22304)).
*   **Logic Cleanup:** Optimized `L2BlockInfo` to eliminate redundant post-insertion decoding ([#22306](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22306)).

### PCD & Deployment Tooling
The Permissionless Chain Deployment (PCD) initiative reached a major milestone, focusing on automation and L1 compatibility.
*   **Milestone 4 Completion:** Finalized Netchef integration, artifact persistence, and prestate building with verification ([#20901](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20901), [#21375](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21375)).
*   **op-deployer Enhancements:** Updated the deployer to estimate gas against post-Glamsterdam L1s ([#21728](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21728)) and automatically select the release SP1 verifier for ZK-enabled operations ([#22367](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22367)).

### CI/CD & Build System Infrastructure
Efforts were made to reduce infrastructure failures and streamline the developer experience.
*   **Reliability Fixes:** Front-loaded `mise` tool installations to mitigate the project's most frequent CI failure class ([#22322](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22322), [#22223](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22223)).
*   **Build Automation:** Refined `justfile` configurations to automatically toggle between native and Docker-based Kona prestate builds ([#22341](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22341)).
*   **Testing Expansion:** Added matrix testing support for future L1 forks, including blob schedules and Engine API requirements ([#21920](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21920)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **SDM Devnet Rollout:** Successfully closed the primary rollout issue for `sdm-net-0`. The devnet is verified, dashboards are live, and a continuous workload is running ([#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21273), [#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22343)).
*   **Interop & ZK Support:** Resolved gaps in ZK game type deployment and proposer support for `ZKDisputeGame` ([#21463](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21463), [#22239](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22239)).
*   **Protocol Hardening:** Addressed a potential reset cycle in `op-supernode` during invalidation recovery ([#21948](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21948)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Flaky Tests:** A surge in CI instability led to the opening of several tracking issues for flaky tests, including `TestBatcherAutoDA` and `TestP2PFull` ([#22379](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22379), [#22381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22381)).
*   **Kona Stability:** New reports of `kona-node` crash loops during deep catch-up (SealTaskFailed) are under investigation ([#22342](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22342)).
*   **Lightnode Strategy:** A new initiative was launched to transition staging and production environments to be predominantly lightnode-based in preparation for Interop ([#22352](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22352)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was heavily centered on the **SDM and PCD workstreams**. The successful "weekend soak" of the SDM devnet indicates strong coordination between protocol engineers and DevOps. There is also an ongoing technical discussion regarding testing gaps in `kona/tests/proofs`; the team decided to rely on individual component testing for SDM payload validation until a full engine swap allows for integrated action-testing ([#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21344)). Documentation remains a priority, with updates for RPC providers (SwiftNodes) and new notices regarding subblocks and release docs for `op-deployer`.
