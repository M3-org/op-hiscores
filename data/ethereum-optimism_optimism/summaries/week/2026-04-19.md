# ethereum-optimism/optimism Weekly Report (Apr 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on hardening the Superchain infrastructure through critical bug fixes in **Kona** and **op-reth**, alongside a major initiative to decouple the monorepo from `op-geth` in favor of upstream `go-ethereum`. Significant progress was made in Superchain interoperability, specifically regarding log backfill features, cycle detection in message graphs, and the enforcement of EIP-7825 gas caps. The team also achieved a major milestone in CI stability, resolving several long-standing flaky tests and completing the migration of Rust crates into the monorepo.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Core Protocol & "op-geth" Decoupling
A significant architectural shift began this week with the initiative to decouple the monorepo from `op-geth` ([#20257](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20257)). 
- **New Core Packages:** Extracted `op-core/superchain` ([#20314](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20314)) and `op-core/eip1559` ([#20312](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20312)) to create OP-aware standalone libraries.
- **Hardfork Gating:** Introduced an L2CM feature toggle for the Karst NUT bundle in `op-node` ([#19888](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19888)) and enforced EIP-7825 gas caps in bundle validation ([#20205](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20205)).
- **RPC Enhancements:** Registered the `eth_config` (EIP-7910) endpoint in `op-reth` ([#20158](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20158)).

### Superchain Interoperability & Kona
Work continued on the "interop" milestone, focusing on state consistency and message validation.
- **Kona Stability:** Fixed critical state divergence issues in Kona by making `CrossL2Inbox` deployment conditional ([#20147](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20147)) and ensuring `chain_id` is correctly set for trie hints ([#20164](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20164)).
- **Supernode Enhancements:** Introduced interop log backfill depth and validation gating in `op-supernode` ([#19973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19973)) and added cycle detection to the `MessageGraph` ([#20303](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20303)).
- **Fault Proofs:** Added coverage for super dispute games created before rollup L1 genesis ([#20287](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20287)).

### ZK Proof Integration & Dispute Games
- **SP1 Integration:** Introduced the `SP1Adapter` to `contracts-bedrock`, providing interfaces for ZK verifiers (`ISP1Adapter`, `IZKVerifier`) to integrate zero-knowledge proofs into the dispute system ([#19987](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19987)).
- **ZK Quality Review:** Addressed code quality findings in `ZKDisputeGame.sol` ([#20236](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20236)).

### Infrastructure & CI/CD
- **Rust Toolchain:** Consolidated toolchain management into `mise.toml` ([#20207](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20207)) and updated the Rust OP stack to `revm 38` ([#20169](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20169)).
- **CI Reliability:** Improved supply chain security by verifying provenance for `ci-base-clang` images ([#20171](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20171)) and increased executor sizes to prevent OOM errors during compilation ([#20337](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20337)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
- **Flaky Tests:** Successfully resolved several persistent flakes, including `TestFlashblocksTransfer` ([#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19934)) and `TestUnsafeGapFillAfterUnsafeReorg` ([#19936](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19936)).
- **P2P Sync:** Closed the issue to remove deprecated req-resp p2p sync in `op-node` to improve sync reliability ([#14108](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/14108)).
- **Refactoring:** Completed the long-term tracking issue for migrating Rust crates to the monorepo ([#18927](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/18927)).

### New & Active Issues
- **Critical Bug:** A high-priority issue was reported where `op-reth v2.0.0` cannot import bedrock state snapshots when `storage.v2` is enabled ([#20160](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20160)).
- **SDM Integration:** A suite of new issues was opened to track **Sequencer Defined Messages (SDM)**, covering performance benchmarking, fault proof impact, and operator documentation ([#20184](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20184)).
- **Node Topology:** New discussions emerged regarding specialized `op-node` topologies for performance scaling ([#20192](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20192)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by heavy cross-team collaboration on the **Kona** and **Supernode** stacks. Significant review activity occurred around the "strong-consistency" interop follow-ups ([#19561](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19561)), with contributors ensuring that each fix was independently revertible. The developer experience was bolstered by the release of a comprehensive guide for writing acceptance tests ([#20225](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20225)), signaling a push for higher quality standards in community contributions. Clarifications were also provided regarding the `op-geth` deprecation timeline, confirming support until May 31st to assist node operators in their transition to `op-reth`.
