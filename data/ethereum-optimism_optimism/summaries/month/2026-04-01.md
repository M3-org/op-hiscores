# ethereum-optimism/optimism Monthly Report (April 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In April 2026, development focused heavily on the maturation of the Interop and Fault Proof systems, marked by the significant migration of interop tests to the acceptance framework and the unification of dispute game contracts. A major architectural shift occurred with the complete removal of legacy `OPContractsManager` (v1) and `OptimismPortalInterop` in favor of more streamlined, unified implementations. The Rust ecosystem saw critical upgrades, including the transition to `reth v2.0.0` and `alloy-evm 0.30.0`, while the "Kona" fault proof components received extensive stability fixes to resolve state divergence and improve consolidation logic.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Fault Proofs & Kona Enhancements
The project made substantial strides in the reliability of the fault proof pipeline, specifically focusing on the Kona executor and host.
*   **Game Type Migration:** Converted `op-e2e` fault proof tests to use `CANNON_KONA` and `SUPER_CANNON_KONA` game types, utilizing `kona-host` ([#19791](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19791), [#19983](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19983)).
*   **Consolidation Logic:** Fixed critical bugs in Kona's consolidation, including the removal of obsolete replacement deposits and handling missing L1 origin blocks in the sequencer ([#19894](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19894), [#19945](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19945)).
*   **Determinism:** Replaced `HashMap` with `BTreeMap` in the interop host to ensure deterministic JSON serialization and prevent VM state divergence ([#19906](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19906)).

### Interop & Superchain Infrastructure
Work continued on the "Supernode" and cross-chain interoperability features.
*   **Contract Unification:** Migrated shared dispute game functionalities to `OptimismPortal2` and deleted the deprecated `OptimismPortalInterop` ([#19840](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19840), [#19956](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19956)).
*   **Denylist Integration:** Updated the Supernode to utilize the Denylist for "OptimisticOutputRoot" verification ([#19880](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19880)).
*   **Rollup-Boost Removal:** Streamlined the architecture by removing native `rollup-boost` support from the Kona workspace, routing Engine API calls directly to the L2 execution engine ([#19900](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19900)).

### Rust Ecosystem & Core Protocol
Significant dependency management and protocol-level refinements were completed.
*   **Major Upgrades:** Upgraded `reth` to `v2.0.0`, `alloy-evm` to `0.30.0`, and updated `revm` to version 36 ([#19854](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19854), [#19989](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19989)).
*   **Derivation Pipeline:** Fixed a pipeline initialization bug where incorrect system configurations were used during resets ([#19792](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19792)).
*   **Kona Genesis:** Added a `rollup_config_override` feature to allow for custom sequencer drift settings ([#19994](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19994)).

### Testing & CI Reliability
Extensive efforts were made to stabilize the CI pipeline and reduce test flakiness.
*   **Flashblocks Stability:** Resolved persistent flakes in `TestFlashblocksTransfer` and `TestFlashblocksStream` by switching to the `op-rbuilder` stream and removing wall-clock assertions ([#19985](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19985), [#19959](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19959)).
*   **Synchronization:** Added `WaitForBlockNumber` synchronization to `op-reth` tests to prevent race conditions between the sequencer and validator ([#19899](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19899)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Interop Phase 3:** Successfully closed several issues related to removing chains from shared dispute games and merging SuperDisputeGame chains in OPCM ([#19692](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19692), [#19011](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19011)).
*   **L1 Reorgs:** Resolved issues concerning L1 reorg handling within the Supernode via the Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) implementation ([#19245](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19245)).
*   **Contract Enforcement:** Closed the investigation into automated Semgrep rules to prevent storage variable collisions in `L2ContractsManager` ([#19315](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19315)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Receipt Root Mismatch:** A high-priority issue ([#19930](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19930)) was identified where the receipt trie was incorrectly built for pre-Canyon rules, affecting sync for older chains like Aevo and Manta.
*   **Kona DoS Vulnerability:** Discussions intensified around a critical DoS vulnerability in `kona-protocol` ([#19334](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19334)) where unknown batch types could cause panics; a fix involving proper error propagation is underway.
*   **Resource Contention:** Several new issues highlight CI flakiness due to OOM errors during Rust compilation and resource pressure causing timing failures in fault proof tests ([#19995](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19995)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month was characterized by a high volume of "mechanical" cleanup and audit-driven refactoring. Collaboration between the protocol and tooling teams was evident in the rapid response to flaky tests, with contributors like `wwared` and `sebastianst` providing deep-dive investigations into CI resource contention. The project also saw active engagement from security researchers, notably `zhuzhushiwojia`, who proposed a fix for the Kona batch-type panic. The transition to a monorepo structure continued to drive "chore" activity, including the removal of over 100 unused workspace and crate dependencies to improve build hygiene.
