# Overall Project Monthly Summary (August 2026)

Optimism is dedicated to building a scalable and secure home for the decentralized web. This month, our efforts focused on reinforcing the "foundations" of the Superchain—ensuring that the software running our network is more resilient to errors, expanding the variety of assets available to users, and hardening our infrastructure against security threats.

## Executive Summary
In August, the team prioritized protocol stability by tackling complex synchronization issues and improving how nodes recover from data gaps. We also continued to grow the Base ecosystem by integrating new tokens, ensuring the Superchain remains a versatile and secure environment for developers and users alike.

### Key Strategic Initiatives & Outcomes

**Strengthening Network Reliability and Stability**
*Goal: We want to ensure that the nodes powering the network stay online and synchronized, even when unexpected data errors occur.*
*   We began improving how nodes recover missing proof history during startup in [ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism), which prevents synchronization stalls and ensures a verified network state ([#22177](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22177)).
*   Engineers are actively diagnosing a complex "oscillation" bug in [ethereum-optimism/infra](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra) and [ethereum-optimism.github.io](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io) that affects how sequencers recover from chain forks ([#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21964)). Resolving this is a high priority for maintaining consistent block finalization.

**Expanding the Superchain Ecosystem**
*Goal: To make the Superchain the best place for decentralized finance, we are constantly adding support for new and diverse assets.*
*   The Base network expanded its utility by adding support for **Wrapped Ratatoskr (wRATR)** ([#1346](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1346)) and the **WAKU token** ([#1345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1345)).

**Hardening Infrastructure Security**
*Goal: We proactively update our underlying tools to protect the network from potential vulnerabilities.*
*   We updated critical networking libraries in [ethereum-optimism/infra](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra) to address known security vulnerabilities, ensuring the communication layer remains robust ([#696](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/696), [#695](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/695)).

### Cross-Repository Coordination
This month required significant coordination between our protocol and infrastructure teams to address **Sequencer Recovery Stability**.
*   **The Goal:** To fix a bug where a node acting as both a sequencer and a follower would get "confused" during a network recovery, leading to a loop of resets.
*   **The Result:** By sharing logs and data across [ethereum-optimism/infra](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra) and [ethereum-optimism.github.io](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io), developers identified that current height-based checks aren't enough to handle specific fork scenarios. This cross-team investigation is paving the way for a more robust fork-choice logic that will benefit the entire Superchain.

*   **Proof History Recovery:** Initiated work to allow `op-reth` nodes to fill in gaps in proof history during startup ([#22177](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22177)), reducing the risk of data inconsistencies.
