# Overall Project Weekly Summary (Jun 28 - Jul 4, 2026)

This week, the Optimism ecosystem focused on strengthening the foundation for launching new networks and ensuring the stability of existing ones following recent upgrades. The team made significant strides in automating how new chains are built while actively collaborating with the community to smooth out technical ripples caused by the Karst hard fork.

## Executive Summary
The primary focus was on streamlining the "Superchain" launch process, moving toward a future where new Layer 2 networks can be deployed with greater security and less manual effort. At the same time, developers worked quickly to address gas estimation and node synchronization issues to ensure a reliable experience for current operators and users.

### Key Strategic Initiatives & Outcomes

**Simplifying and Securing Chain Deployments**
*Goal: We want to make it easier and safer for anyone to launch a new blockchain within the Optimism ecosystem.*
*   The team completed the design for a new deployment pipeline in [ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism) ([#20897](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20897)). This allows new networks to start with advanced security features, like permissionless disputes, active from the very first block.
*   By centralizing the "blueprints" for new chains through a new Manager tool ([#20903](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20903)), we have reduced the risk of errors during the setup of new networks.

**Improving Network Reliability and Node Performance**
*Goal: We are working to ensure that the software running our networks is efficient and easy for community members to operate.*
*   In [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry), the team is addressing a synchronization hurdle ([#21465](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21465)) that affects "pruned" nodes—those designed to save disk space. This ensures that even users with limited hardware can reliably participate in the network.
*   We are finalizing a fix for a gas estimation bug on the Sepolia test network ([#21574](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21574)) that appeared after the Karst upgrade, which will make transaction costs predictable again for developers.

**Automated Maintenance and Health Monitoring**
*Goal: We use automation to keep our systems up-to-date and catch potential problems before they affect users.*
*   The [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry) successfully processed several automated updates ([#1270](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1270), [#1271](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1271)) to keep the list of official Superchain networks accurate.
*   Testing schedules in [ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism) were moved to a daily frequency ([#21595](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21595)) to provide faster feedback on the health of the protocol.

### Cross-Repository Coordination
This week required tight coordination between the core protocol and the registry teams to manage the aftermath of the Karst hard fork.
*   **Hard Fork Stabilization**: While the [superchain-registry](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry) served as the hub for reporting gas estimation errors ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21457)), the actual technical fix is being coordinated and implemented within the [optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism) repository ([#21574](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21574)). This ensures that data integrity and software fixes move in lockstep across the ecosystem.

## Repository Spotlights

### ethereum-optimism/optimism
*   Finalized the architecture for the **New Deployment Pipeline** ([#20897](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20897)), enabling permissionless dispute games from block zero.
*   Centralized L2 setup logic via the **L2ContractsManager** ([#20903](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20903)), simplifying how new chains are initialized.
*   Improved the **op-deployer** tool with new features for generating network states ([#21601](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21601)) and selecting anchor blocks ([#21600](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21600)).
*   Enhanced the **proof system** with batching capabilities ([#21602](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21602)) and better error handling for data streams ([#21597](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21597)).

### ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry
*   Maintained the accuracy of the Superchain ecosystem through automated **codegen updates** ([#1270](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1270), [#1271](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1271), [#1272](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1272)).
*   Investigated and triaged **intrinsic gas anomalies** on Optimism Sepolia ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21457)) to restore accurate gas estimation for developers.
*   Initiated a critical fix for **node synchronization** ([#21465](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21465)) to prevent failures for operators using pruned execution layers.
