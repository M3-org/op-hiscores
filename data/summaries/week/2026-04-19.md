# Overall Project Weekly Summary (Apr 19 - 25, 2026)

## Executive Summary
This week, Optimism focused on hardening the infrastructure for the Superchain, specifically by stabilizing interoperability features and streamlining the core codebase. The team made significant progress in transitioning to more modern execution clients and resolving technical hurdles that ensure different chains in the ecosystem can communicate reliably.

### Key Strategic Initiatives & Outcomes

**Strengthening Cross-Chain Interoperability**
*Goal: To ensure that different blockchains within the Superchain can talk to each other seamlessly and securely.*
*   Finalized the technical rules (specifications) for how chains interact, moving from the design phase to stable implementation in [ethereum-optimism/specs](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/905).
*   Fixed a critical "mismatch" error in the Kona system that could have caused chains to disagree on the state of shared messages ([ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20147)).
*   Added "cycle detection" to message graphs to prevent infinite loops in cross-chain communications ([ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20303)).

**Modernizing Core Infrastructure**
*Goal: To make the platform easier to maintain and more compatible with the broader Ethereum ecosystem.*
*   Began a major effort to decouple Optimism from custom versions of Ethereum software, moving toward standard "upstream" libraries to simplify future updates ([ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20257)).
*   Clarified the transition timeline for node operators, confirming support for older software through May 31st to ensure a smooth migration to the new `op-reth` standard ([ethereum-optimism/infra](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20203)).
*   Integrated zero-knowledge (ZK) proof interfaces, laying the groundwork for much faster and more secure dispute resolutions in the future ([ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19987)).

**Improving Developer Experience and System Reliability**
*Goal: To create a more stable environment for builders by fixing "flaky" tests and automating manual tasks.*
*   Resolved several long-standing "flaky" tests that were causing unpredictable failures in the automated build system, improving the speed and reliability of new code reviews ([ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19934)).
*   Introduced new automation templates for managing Superchain operations, reducing the risk of human error during network upgrades ([ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1397)).

### Cross-Repository Coordination
*   **Kona & Interop Stabilization**: Protocol, infrastructure, and ecosystem teams coordinated across [specs](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs), [optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism), and [infra](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra) to resolve output root mismatches and finalize the `CrossL2Inbox` deployment logic. This ensures that the "Interop" milestone remains on track for upcoming devnet launches.
*   **L2CM Milestone Migration**: The [superchain-registry](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry) and [superchain-ops](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops) teams synchronized the transition of Layer 2 Chain Manager milestones into the broader "Upgrade 19" process, aligning technical work with community governance schedules.
