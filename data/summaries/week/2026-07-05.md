# Overall Project Weekly Summary (Jul 5 - 11, 2026)

## Executive Summary
This week, the Optimism ecosystem focused on stabilizing the network following the Karst upgrade and advancing the roadmap for Zero-Knowledge (ZK) security. By resolving critical gas calculation issues and streamlining testing infrastructure, the team ensured a smoother experience for developers and users across the Superchain.

### Key Strategic Initiatives & Outcomes

**Strengthening Network Stability and Reliability**
*Goal: To ensure the network remains predictable and cost-effective for users after major protocol upgrades.*
*   Resolved a critical bug on the Sepolia testnet that was causing "intrinsic gas" errors, restoring normal operations for app developers in [ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21457).
*   Corrected gas limit discrepancies on OP Mainnet and Soneium to ensure block space remains consistent with intended system settings in [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1480).
*   Fixed a memory leak in the node software that could have caused system slowdowns during data synchronization in [ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21596).

**Advancing the Zero-Knowledge (ZK) Roadmap**
*Goal: To move toward a more secure and decentralized "Dispute Game" system using ZK technology.*
*   Reached a major milestone by completing the research and scoping phase for ZK Proofs in [ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21401).
*   Integrated "mock verifiers" into the deployment tools, allowing the team to test how ZK security will work on live chains in [ethereum-optimism/optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21571).

**Optimizing Developer and Contributor Experience**
*Goal: To make the development process faster and more efficient by removing "noisy" or low-value automated tests.*
*   Conducted a strategic audit of the testing suite, deciding to replace slow, low-value "fuzzing" tests with faster, more reliable checks in [ethereum-optimism/infra](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21636).
*   Improved the speed of cross-chain interaction checks, making it more efficient for apps to communicate across the Superchain in [ethereum-optimism/op-geth](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/633).

### Cross-Repository Coordination
The week was defined by a highly synchronized response to the Karst hard fork.
*   **Unified Bug Response**: The "intrinsic gas" fix required coordinated updates across the execution client ([op-geth](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth)), the analytics engine ([op-analytics](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics)), and the core protocol ([optimism](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism)) to ensure all parts of the stack were aligned.
*   **Superchain Expansion**: Operational teams worked across the [superchain-ops](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops) and [superchain-registry](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry) repositories to migrate Ink Mainnet and adjust Osaka L2s, ensuring new chains inherit the latest security features.
