On Jan 8, 2026, Optimism focused on critical bug fixes, particularly resolving an `eth_simulateV1` failure post-Jovian fork in `op-geth` and addressing an `op-batcher` stability issue in `optimism`. Significant progress was also made in RetroPGF M11 results and weights, alongside ongoing infrastructure updates and preparations for the U18 deployment on Sepolia across multiple Superchain ecosystem chains.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Core Protocol Stability & Interoperability
*   **`op-geth`**: Fixed `eth_simulateV1` calls failing after the Jovian fork due to missing deposit transactions, ensuring continued functionality of the simulation API ([#732](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/732)).
*   **`optimism`**: Resolved a critical bug in `op-batcher` where empty batches were submitted in a loop when using shadow compressor and Brotli, improving batch submission stability ([#18485](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18485)).
*   **`optimism`**: Migrated `op-dispute-mon` to use `op-supernode` instead of `op-supervisor` for super roots, enhancing cross-chain interoperability and dispute game mechanisms ([#18711](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18711)).
*   **`optimism`**: Closed the issue concerning the migration of the `dispute-mon` to `supernode` APIs, aligning it with the latest Superchain architecture ([#18523](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/18523)).
