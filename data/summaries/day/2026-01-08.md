Today, the project saw critical bug fixes in `op-geth` related to `eth_simulateV1` calls post-Jovian fork and in `op-batcher` addressing empty batch submissions. Significant progress was also made on M11 RetroPGF results and infrastructure modernization in the `optimism` repository, alongside preparations for the U18 deployment on Sepolia across multiple Superchain ecosystem chains.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Core Protocol Stability & Infrastructure
- **ethereum-optimism/op-geth**: A critical bug affecting `eth_simulateV1` calls after the Jovian fork was resolved, fixing errors related to DA footprint calculation due to missing deposit transactions ([#732](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/732)).
- **ethereum-optimism/optimism**: A critical bug in the `op-batcher` was fixed, preventing it from submitting empty batches in a loop when using shadow compressor and Brotli, improving stability and efficiency ([#18485](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18485)).
- **ethereum-optimism/optimism**: Unused Asterisc contracts and associated deployment scripts were removed, and git submodules were standardized to use HTTPS ([#18689](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18689), [#18690](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18690)).
- **ethereum-optimism/optimism**: The `op-dispute-mon` component was updated to utilize `op-supernode` instead of `op-supervisor` for super roots, aligning with architectural improvements ([#18711](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18711)).

### RetroPGF M11 Results & Documentation
- **ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding**: Significant progress was made in adding and refining M11 results, including final results, updated weights for onchain builders, and a draft of M11 results with updated queries and data files ([#95](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/pull/95), [#94](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/pull/94), [#93](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/pull/93)).
- **ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding**: The changelog for M11 was updated to reflect the latest changes and results ([#96](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/pull/96)).
- **ethereum-optimism/docs**: The `gov-faq` document was updated to reflect S9 Citizenship selections ([#1939](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/pull/1939)).

### Infrastructure Maintenance & Updates
- **ethereum-optimism/infra**: The `urllib3` dependency was bumped from version 2.5.0 to 2.6.3 within the `/op-conductor-ops` directory ([#535](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/535)).
- **ethereum-optimism/op-analytics**: A minor update was made to the Dagster configuration, affecting the `Makefile` and `helm/dagster/values.yaml` files ([#1781](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1781)).
