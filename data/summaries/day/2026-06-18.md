Today, Optimism significantly enhanced `op-reth` by integrating the `superchain-registry` for chain specifications and improving the payload builder with interop failsafe gating. Critical bug fixes were deployed across interop L2 contracts and SDM transaction handling, while ongoing discussions focused on `kona-node` synchronization and standardizing the SDM operator flag.

## 🚨 Needs Attention
- **Urgent Discussions**:
    - `op-node` and `op-reth` Syncmode Challenges: The default `syncmode.offset-el-safe` to 12h fails for `--minimal` nodes when running `op-node` v1.19.0 + `op-reth` v2.3.1 on various Superchain registry chains, leading to warnings about failing to insert unsafe payloads for EL sync. Input is needed to address this critical sync issue. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21465](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21465))
    - Superchain Registry State Management: The `superchain-registry` commit/submodule needs to be coupled between `op-core`, `op-reth`, and `kona` as it currently requires manual synchronization in three places without checks. A solution is needed to ensure these are in sync. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21467](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21467))
    - `op-challenger` and Permissioned Games: Permissioned games in `op-challenger` still require `cannon-prestate` in some cases, causing errors if the placeholder prestate cannot be found when `--prestate-url` is passed. This needs to be resolved to prevent errors in `op-challenger`. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21470](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21470))
    - Interop Security Narrative: A security narrative for interop needs to be created. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21475](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21475))
- **Active Discussions**:
    - `kona-node` SafeDB and Syncmode Parity: Discussions around `kona-node`'s `SafeDB` ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20278), [ethereum-optimism/infra#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20278), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20278), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20278), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20278)) and the need for `syncmode.offset-el-safe` parity with `op-node` ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21243), [ethereum-optimism/infra#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21243), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21243), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21243), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21243)) are ongoing, with a recommendation to fix the `syncmode` issue soon and prioritize `SafeDB` implementation.
