## ✅ Completed Work
### ZK Dispute Game Infrastructure
- Initial scaffolding for SP1 super interop proof shapes for ZK dispute games was added, introducing shared public IO types for unified super-range and super-aggregation programs ([#21578](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21578)).
- The implementation of the super range program and the super aggregation program for the ZK super STF has been completed, crucial for recursive verification and emission of public values ([#21576](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21576), [#21577](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21577)).

### Superchain Configuration and Rollup Logic Refinement
- The `055-gas-limit-op` task was marked as executed in `superchain-ops`, resolving a pending item related to gas limit operations ([#1494](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1494)).
- Superchain configurations were improved by rejecting unknown keys during registry decoding, preventing silent dropping of critical fields ([#21650](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21650)).
- Rollup configuration loading was moved to a new `op-node/superchain` package to better organize dependencies ([#21648](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21648)).

### Testing Enhancements and CI Optimization
- A flaky fuzz test related to blob encoding was fixed by addressing how non-encoded bits in `blob[0]` are handled during decoding ([#21647](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21647)).
- Go fuzz CI jobs were retired to optimize CI resources, as their effectiveness was limited due to lack of persistent corpus and sparse seeds ([#21639](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21639), [#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21636)).
- Tests for L2 fork upgrades were enhanced to skip past already applied NUT bundles on forked chains, preventing `CreateCollision` errors ([#21652](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21652)).
- The overarching task of full integration testing, internal review, and advanced testing fixes has been completed ([#20760](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20760)).

### Specification Clarification
- The Ecotone blob-encoding specification was clarified to align with reference implementations, specifically regarding the top two bits of the first field element not being enforced during decoding ([#923](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/923)).
- The issue regarding the technical specifications for breaking cyclic dependencies in the `op-deployer` pipeline was closed, with each stage and assumptions defined ([#912](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/912)).
