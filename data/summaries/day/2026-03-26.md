On Mar 26, 2026, Optimism's development focused heavily on refining L2 Contract Manager (L2CM) upgrade processes, with multiple related issues closed across repositories, alongside critical discussions and fixes for persistent flaky tests. A significant breaking change in `op-geth`'s `StorageKeys` value was identified, requiring immediate attention due to its impact on `op-geth` v1.101701.0.

## 🚨 Needs Attention 
- **Urgent Discussion: `op-geth` Breaking Change**: The `op-geth` v1.101701.0 introduces a breaking change where `StorageKeys` values are `null` instead of `[]`, impacting INK Mainnet and potentially other systems due to `SortedFunc` returning `nil` for empty slices. This requires immediate investigation and resolution. ([ethereum-optimism/infra#780](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/780), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#780](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/780), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#780](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/780), [ethereum-optimism/specs#780](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/780), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#19764](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19764), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#780](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/780), [ethereum-optimism/op-geth#780](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/780), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#780](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/780))
