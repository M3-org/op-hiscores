On March 15, 2026, Optimism's development efforts focused on critical bug fixes and infrastructure improvements, particularly in reorg handling and CI pipelines within the `optimism` repository, alongside significant smart contract refactoring. Concurrently, a new token was proposed for addition to the Base List in the `ethereum-optimism.github.io` repository, indicating ongoing ecosystem expansion.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Core Protocol Stability & Refinements
- **Reorg Handling Improvements**: Bug fixes were implemented in `op-devstack` to refine reorg detection, splitting `ReorgTriggered` into exact and relaxed variants to prevent failures when reorgs occur earlier than expected ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19549](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19549)).
- **Acceptance Test Enhancements**: Acceptance tests were improved to ensure the Execution Layer (EL) advances before calling `BlockRefByLabel` ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19550](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19550)).
- **op-geth Upgrade**: An upgrade to `op-geth` was completed, incorporating changes from a related pull request in the `op-geth` repository ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19433](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19433)).

### Smart Contract Architecture
- **Contract Refactoring for Reusability**: The `ProxyAdminOwnedBase` contract and its interface were refactored and moved from `src/L1/` to `src/universal/`, allowing both L1 and L2 contracts to inherit from it and improving codebase organization ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19478](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19478)).

### CI/CD Pipeline & Tooling Enhancements
- **CI Pipeline Optimization**: The `large-kona-sequencer` variant was removed from the rust-e2e CI pipeline due to redundancy ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19553](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19553)).
- **Code Quality Checks**: A new `deny` just target was added to `rust/justfile` for `cargo-deny` checks, and the CI was updated to use this new target ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19520](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19520)).
