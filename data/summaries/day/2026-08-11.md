Overall Project Summary:

Today, the project made significant strides in State-Dependent Mining (SDM) development, successfully rolling out and benchmarking `sdm-net-0` and verifying its dashboards, alongside substantial enhancements to Kona's engine and `op-deployer` capabilities. However, investigations into several newly identified flaky tests, particularly `TestP2PFull`, are ongoing and require further attention to maintain CI stability.

## 🚨 Needs Attention

- **Urgent Discussions**:
    - [ethereum-optimism/infra#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21344): A secure channel was requested by a contributor to discuss technical concerns related to SDM/Lagoon/PostExec/Kona privately.
    - [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#22344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22344): `debug_traceBlockByNumber` failed on 100% of 0x7d blocks with SDM-on, and throughput at 30 TPS was lower with SDM-on, suggesting a potential confirmation-latency increase, requiring further investigation.
    - [ethereum-optimism/infra#22381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22381): Investigation into the flaky `TestP2PFull` test continues, with insufficient evidence for a root-cause fix for "no addresses" and "noise: message is too short" errors.

## ✅ Completed Work

### State-Dependent Mining (SDM) Rollout & Benchmarking
- Established a standing workload on `sdm-net-0` to continuously exercise the SDM network, meeting acceptance criteria including continuous rebate signals ([ethereum-optimism/infra#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22343), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22343), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22343), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22343), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22343)).
- Performed baseline benchmarks on `sdm-net-0` to evaluate performance, showing significant gas refunds with SDM enabled and negligible overhead for payload building and execution ([ethereum-optimism/infra#22344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22344), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#22344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22344), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#22344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22344), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#22344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22344)).
- Verified SDM dashboards in OPE Grafana for `sdm-net-0`, with two fixes in flight to address empty panels ([ethereum-optimism/infra#22345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22345), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#22345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/22345), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#22345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22345), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#22345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22345), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#22345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/22345)).

### Core Protocol Enhancements
- Refined Kona's engine and gossip protocols by moving signed gossip framing and SSZ codecs to private wire types, authenticating payload bytes before decoding, and introducing `OpExecutionPayloadEnvelope` ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#22305](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22305), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#22306](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22306), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#22304](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22304)).
- Improved `op-deployer` for ZK deployments and the PCD process, including selecting the release SP1 verifier and implementing PCD record implementations ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#22367](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22367), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#22337](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22337)).
- Expanded acceptance tests for future L1 forks and hardened deposit gas accounting against upstream changes in `alloy-op-evm` and `kona` ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21920](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21920), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#22372](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22372)).

### Node Synchronization & Configuration Fixes
- Resolved an issue where invalidation recovery could repeatedly reset when unsafe sync resumed before EL convergence ([ethereum-optimism/infra#21948](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21948), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#21948](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21948), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#21948](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21948), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#21948](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21948), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#21948](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21948)).
- Closed a bug regarding `op-reth` missing hardfork override flags, which prevented p2p connections ([ethereum-optimism/infra#20224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20224), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#20224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20224), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#20224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20224), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#20224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20224), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#20224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20224)).

### Documentation & Data Updates
