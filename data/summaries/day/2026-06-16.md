Jun 16, 2026

Today, the Optimism project significantly advanced its ZK Proofs initiative by opening numerous new issues covering research, infrastructure, and rollout, while also making critical progress on protocol security with new L2 genesis allocation validation and bug fixes. Discussions solidified the strategy for ZK proofs in CI and local development, largely favoring mock provers to manage costs and complexity.

    - `ethereum-optimism/infra` [#21409](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21409): Active discussion on the strategy for ZK proofs in CI and local development, recommending a mock prover due to cost and benefit considerations.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Protocol Security & Stability
- **Enhanced L2 Genesis Validation**: Implemented a structural validator for L2 genesis allocations, ensuring expected accounts and EIP-1967 admin/impl slot coherence. ([#21345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21345))
- **Critical Bug Fixes**: Addressed a panic guard in `kona/mpt`, corrected block base fee recording in `op-wheel`, fixed live Sepolia upgrade tests in `op-deployer`, and improved a panic guard in `op-chain-ops`. ([#19728](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19728), [#21127](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21127), [#21400](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21400), [#21396](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21396))
- **`proxyd` Logging Improvement**: Fixed `proxyd` to log unexpected block tags as decimal integers for better readability. ([#661](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/661))

### Code Refactoring & Hardfork Management
- **Improved Hardfork Logic**: Refactored code to track predeploy initialization, gate SDM on the Lagoon hardfork, and move `Predeploys` to `kona-genesis` to reduce hardfork dependencies. ([#21346](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21346), [#21379](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21379), [#21442](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21442))
- **Lagoon SDM Specification Clarification**: Added clarification to the Lagoon SDM specification regarding block-level warming exclusions for intrinsically-warm accesses. ([#919](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/919))

### ZK Dispute Game Resolution & Strategy
- **ZK Dispute Game Bond Claiming**: Closed multiple issues related to `address(0)` bond credit claiming in `ZKDisputeGame`, with the work now tracked in a new issue in the `optimism` repository. ([#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21420), [#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21420), [#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/21420), [#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21420), [#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21420), [#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21420), [#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21420))
- **ZK Proofing Strategy Decision**: Closed the issue regarding ZK proofing in CI and local development, with the consensus to use a mock prover. ([#21409](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21409))
