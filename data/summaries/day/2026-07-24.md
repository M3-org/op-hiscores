On July 24, 2026, the Optimism project saw significant activity across multiple repositories, with a strong focus on resolving a critical `op-reth` TLS connection issue and advancing the EIP-8130 specification. ## ✅ Completed Work
### Core Protocol Stability & Enhancements
- **`op-reth` TLS Connection Fix**: The critical `op-reth` v1.11.0 TLS connection issue, which prevented connection to the flashblocks WebSocket endpoint, was resolved across `specs`, `op-geth`, `op-analytics`, `infra`, and `optimism` repositories. ([#19322](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19322), [#19322](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/19322), [#19322](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/19322), [#19322](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19322), [#19322](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19322))
- **`op-reth` Transaction Pool Improvements**: The `op-reth` transaction pool was enhanced to accurately reserve Isthmus operator fees and L1-info deposit gas, preventing under-funded transactions and achieving parity with `op-geth`. ([#21609](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21609), [#21548](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21548), [#21608](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21608), [#21546](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21546))
- **`op-challenger` Updates**: The `op-challenger` was updated to align with the new contracts API/ABI for the ZK game type. ([#21415](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21415))
