On Feb 9, 2026, Optimism's development focused on enhancing Supernode performance and stability, particularly in preventing rewinds over finalized heads, alongside critical bug fixes in data analytics. A significant new discussion emerged regarding improving sequencer reliability in `op-geth` by supporting multiple RPC endpoints, while `superchain-ops` streamlined multi-signature management.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Supernode Performance and Stability
- **Optimized Supernode Processing**: The Supernode's processing speed was improved by lowering the interop activity ticker rate and introducing a backoff period to prevent busy-waiting when chains are not ready. ([#19037](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19037))
- **Prevented Rewinds Over Finalized Heads**: Implemented a critical error condition to prevent the Supernode from rewinding over finalized heads, enhancing stability and addressing a previous issue where `op-supervisor` could panic or allow rewinds affecting finalized states. ([#19109](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19109), [#19066](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19066))

### Infrastructure and Testing Enhancements
- **Enhanced Test Logging for `op-acceptor`**: Improved the `op-acceptor` test runner to ensure consistent log capture, even during unexpected process terminations, by directing stdout to a temporary file. ([#548](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/548))

### Secure Contract Management
- **Streamlined Multi-Signature Management**: Added a new template to `superchain-ops` for swapping multiple owners for the same multisig in a single task, simplifying secure contract operations. ([#1368](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1368))
- **Expanded Testnet Capabilities**: Created a new task (065) for U18 on the Soneium Minato testnet to expand testing for specific network configurations. ([#1370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1370))
- **Task Execution Update**: Task 040-U18-op-ink-mmz-arena-swell was marked as executed in the ETH environment. ([#1372](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1372))

### Data Quality and Analytics
- **Fixed Data Quality Bug**: Addressed a bug in the `other_chains_tracking` module within `op-analytics` that impacted several scripts related to chain TVL trends, transaction retrieval, and contract labeling. ([#1790](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1790))
