A new discussion was initiated regarding `op-geth` sequencer endpoint redundancy to improve resilience.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Supernode Performance and Stability
- Supernode processing speed was improved and busy-waiting prevented by lowering the interop activity ticker rate and introducing a backoff period. ([#19037](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19037))
- A critical fix was implemented to prevent the Supernode from rewinding over finalized heads, addressing a potential crash scenario. ([#19109](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19109))

### Infrastructure and Test Enhancements
- Improvements were made to the `op-acceptor` test runner to ensure logs are consistently captured, even if a process is unexpectedly terminated. ([#548](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/548))

### Multi-Signature Management and Testnet Operations
- A new template was added to `superchain-ops` to facilitate the swapping of multiple owners for the same multisig in a single task. ([#1368](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1368))
- A new task (065) was created for U18 on the Soneium Minato testnet, expanding testing capabilities. ([#1370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1370))
- Task 040-U18-op-ink-mmz-arena-swell was marked as executed in the ETH environment. ([#1372](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1372))

### Analytics Data Quality
- A bug related to data quality within the `other_chains_tracking` module was fixed, impacting several scripts for tracking chain TVL trends and other EVM data. ([#1790](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/pull/1790))
