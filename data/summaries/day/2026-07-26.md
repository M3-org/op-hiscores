On Jul 26, 2026, the Optimism project focused heavily on enhancing the robustness of deployment processes and refining contract upgrade mechanisms within the Bedrock and Superchain ecosystems. Key efforts included hardening RPC and gas handling for the deployer and improving L1 RPC handling tolerance across various services, alongside addressing the complexities of contract upgrades, particularly for shared contracts and proxy administration.
