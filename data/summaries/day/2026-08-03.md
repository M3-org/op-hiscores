### Core Protocol & Deployment Enhancements
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism` introduced new metrics and logging to improve observability of reorg events within `op-node`, tracking "follow source" and "super authority" reorgs ([#22106](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22106)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism` updated `op-deployer` to support continuing prepared chain deployments by snapshotting canonical intent and predicted addresses ([#21974](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21974)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism` improved `op-deployer` to revert on game configuration mismatches instead of silently promoting them to Super Games ([#22128](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22128)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism` updated `op-reth` to recover proof history gaps on startup by seeding the historical-proofs engine's catch-up target from the durable execution head ([#22177](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22177)).

### CI/CD & Infrastructure Optimizations
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism` implemented scheduled triggers for weekly Rust nightly bumps, daily L2 fork tests, and Kona link checks, including Slack notifications ([#21567](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21567)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism` enabled parallel execution of ZK dispute game tests in `op-acceptance-tests` to reduce execution time ([#22208](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22208)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism` optimized the `kona-proof` suite's `test_roots_of_unity` by sampling field elements, reducing CI wall-clock time ([#22201](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22201)).
- `ethereum-optimism/infra` switched `replica-healthcheck` to `StaticJsonRpcProvider` to investigate a potential memory leak and fixed a null-dereference bug ([#694](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/694)).
