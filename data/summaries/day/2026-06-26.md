On Jun 26, 2026, Optimism made significant strides in enhancing Superchain stability and developer tooling, with critical fixes for `op-reth` and `op-node` addressing consensus-layer sync issues and `eth_estimateGas` functionality. The Superchain registry expanded with new chain configurations and OPCM v2 support, while CI infrastructure saw improvements with parallel Apko image builds. A key challenge remains the persistent flakiness of `TestReorgUnsafeHead` and ongoing discussions around `op-node` compatibility with EIP-4444 enabled L1 nodes.
