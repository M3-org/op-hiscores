Overall Project Summary for Aug 24, 2026:

Today, significant progress was made on clarifying and documenting the State-Dependent Mining (SDM) operator, particularly for `op-reth`, with environment variable support and hardfork linkage now resolved. Concurrently, `op-deployer` saw improvements with a reduced default genesis time offset and a new minimum, while a regression test was added for `alloy-op-evm` to address a specific SDM producer policy leak.

*   Resolved documentation for the SDM operator, confirming `op-reth` as the sole component with an SDM operator option, documenting all relevant environment variables, and linking the SDM guide's Lagoon mention to the hardfork registry page.
