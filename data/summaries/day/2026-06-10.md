Today, significant progress was made on the Interop NUT bundle activation across multiple repositories, with follow-up tasks completed and specifications reorganized to clarify activation alongside other features like SDM. Key infrastructure updates included the shift to `op-reth` as the default for `op-node` and enhanced interoperability monitoring, while discussions continue on ecosystem integration for the new SDM transaction type.

## ✅ Completed Work

### Interop NUT Bundle Activation & Specification Refinement
- The interop specifications have been moved from the "experimental" directory to "lagoon" within the protocol specs, clarifying that the interop hardfork will activate with other features like SDM. ([#917](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/917))
- All follow-up items related to the Interop NUT bundle activation, stemming from previous review feedback, have been completed and the issue is now closed across `specs`, `superchain-registry`, `superchain-ops`, and `optimism`. ([#21114](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21114), [#21114](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21114), [#21114](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21114), [#21114](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21114))

### Infrastructure & Documentation Enhancements
- Ownership of the Osaki Sepolia chain (chainId `9111973`) was successfully transferred from the standard Sepolia L1PAO Safe to a new owner Safe, including its L1-to-L2 alias on L2. ([#1455](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1455), [#1457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1457))
- A new `AGENTS.md` file was added to `superchain-ops` to provide guidance for AI agents and human contributors, specifically focusing on safety constraints for governance operations. ([#1454](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1454))
- The `op-interop-mon` tool has been updated to align with the current interop design, focusing on supernodes, light nodes, and `op-interop-filter`, making it strictly observe-only. ([#21283](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21283))
- `op-reth` interop filter metrics were consolidated into a single labeled decision counter for improved observability. ([#21302](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21302))
- Significant documentation updates were made, including defaulting chain operator guides to `op-reth` and archiving snap-sync pages as `op-reth` does not support it. ([#21179](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21179), [#21318](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21318))
- New AI agent guidance for derivation, execution, and fault proofs was added to `optimism`. ([#21293](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21293))
- The CI configuration was improved by restoring the Go module cache in all Go-building jobs to prevent slow downloads and flakes. ([#21313](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21313))
- The default `--l2.enginekind` for `op-node` was changed to `reth` following `op-geth`'s end-of-support. ([#21295](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21295))

### Kona Executor & Prestate Updates
- The Kona executor received updates to make `StatelessL2Builder` and `KonaExecutor` generic over receipt builders, improving flexibility. ([#21275](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21275))
- A critical bug fix was implemented to pin the blob base fee to 1 on the OP Stack within the Kona executor. ([#21328](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21328))
- Kona client v1.6.0-rc.2 absolute prestate hashes, including both non-interop and interop versions, were added to `standard-prestates.toml`. ([#1256](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1256))
