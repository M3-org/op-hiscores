On Feb 5, 2026, Optimism's development focused on critical bug fixes in contract deployments and Superchain configuration, alongside significant enhancements in testing and interop validation for the Supernode and fault proof systems. A recurring theme across multiple repositories was the ongoing discussion and identification of inconsistencies in the `op-reth` snapshot setup, particularly concerning naming conventions and outdated symlinks for data directories.

## 🚨 Needs Attention
- **Urgent Discussions**:
    - **`op-reth` Snapshot Setup Inconsistencies**: Multiple repositories (specs, Retro-Funding, infra, docs, optimism) are actively discussing inconsistencies in the `op-reth` snapshot setup at `https://datadirs.optimism.io/`. This includes inconsistent naming patterns for reth archival files, discrepancies between `mainnet-reth` files and `latest-mainnet-reth` symlinks, and outdated `latest-reth` symlinks. Clarification is needed on whether certain files are archival or full, and improvements to file naming and symlink updates are suggested. ([ethereum-optimism/specs#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/536), [ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/Retro-Funding/issues/536), [ethereum-optimism/infra#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/536), [ethereum-optimism/docs#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/536), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/536))
