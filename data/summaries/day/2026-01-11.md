## ✅ Completed Work
### Protocol & VM Enhancements
- **Cannon MIPSVM Streamlining**: The `SupportWorkingSysGetRandom` feature flag was removed from `cannon/mipsevm` in `ethereum-optimism/optimism`, permanently enabling `SysGetRandom` support and simplifying the MIPSVM's interaction with system randomness. ([#18624](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18624))
- **VM Runner Stability**: A configurable timeout was added to the `vm-runner` within `op-challenger` in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` to prevent indefinite hanging during VM execution, improving trace generation stability. ([#18594](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18594))
