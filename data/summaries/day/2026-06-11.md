Optimism Daily Project Digest (Jun 11, 2026)

Today, the Optimism project made significant strides in refining core infrastructure and developer tooling, particularly through extensive deprecation of the `op-program` and `op-geth` components in favor of `kona-proof` and `op-reth`. Key efforts included enhancing Tenderly simulation support, improving CI performance, and updating `proxyd` for greater stability, while also focusing on critical contract validation and fee calculation improvements within the `optimism` repository.
