### Supernode Interoperability & L2 Contract Enhancements
- **Supernode Script Updates**: The `check-super-root` script was updated to utilize a single Supernode RPC for fetching verified super roots ([#19454](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19454), [#19177](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19177)).

- **Standardized L2 Contract Ownership**: L2 proxied contracts with initialization functions now inherit from `ProxyAdminOwnedBase`, standardizing contract ownership and management ([#19564](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19564)).
- **Supernode Release Process**: The issue tracking official releases for Supernode and interop filter binaries was closed ([#19568](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19568)).
