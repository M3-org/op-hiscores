On June 1, 2026, the Optimism project made significant strides in L2 upgrade testing with critical bug fixes and enhanced dispute monitoring for super permissioned games. A major achievement was the successful completion and verification of Interop Proofs Devnet, while ongoing efforts are focused on resolving persistent `op-supernode`/`op-reth` synchronization stalls and addressing the "engine client not initialized" error.

## ✅ Completed Work
### L2 Upgrade & Dispute System Enhancements
- A critical bug fix was implemented to resolve issues identified during betanet testing for L2 upgrades, including removing leading zeros and resolving an unused function parameter. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21091](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21091))
- Support for simplified super permissioned games was added to `op-dispute-mon`, introducing a lightweight caller and routing these games through existing super-root agreement checks. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21106](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21106))
- The necessary work to make `dispute-mon` compatible with the simplified super permissioned game has been completed. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21098](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21098))

### Interop Proofs & Security Audit Closure
- The audit of Kona Proof Interop logic and the development of Interop Proofs Devnet have been successfully completed, including deploying a network with SuperRootMigrations and InteropPortal contract feature flags. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19920](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19920))
- The Interop Proofs Devnet issue was closed after a withdrawal was successfully proven and confirmed to work on an Anvil fork of Sepolia, verifying the withdrawal path. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20489), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20489), [ethereum-optimism/op-analytics#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/20489), [ethereum-optimism/op-geth#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20489), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20489))
- Feedback related to the Lumi Security Audit for `doc.go` has been addressed and the issue closed. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21124), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21124), [ethereum-optimism/op-analytics#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21124), [ethereum-optimism/op-geth#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/21124), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#21124](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21124))

### CI/CD & Developer Tooling Improvements
- Continuous Integration was enhanced to enable manual contract artifact publishing and to add a `rust-review` just recipe for AI-assisted code reviews. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21140](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21140), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#21118](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21118))
- New documentation was added for the `op-interop-filter` chain-operator tool, providing essential information for chain operators running an interop chain. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21109](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21109))
- Diff publishing to op-geth.optimism.io was removed in CI due to `op-geth`'s deprecation, streamlining the CI/CD process. ([ethereum-optimism/op-geth#801](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/801))
- A routine automated codegen update was performed, modifying the `addresses.json` file to ensure consistency and up-to-date configurations. ([ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#1250](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1250))
