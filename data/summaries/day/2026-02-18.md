Optimism's development today saw significant enhancements to `op-reth` with historical proof porting and dependency updates, alongside critical bug fixes for interoperability and CI workflows. The ecosystem also expanded with new documentation for the Stork oracle and the addition of GNET by Galactica.com, while a persistent memory leak in `op-reth` across multiple nodes remains a key concern.

## 🚨 Needs Attention
- **Urgent Discussion**: The memory leak in `op-reth` ([ethereum-optimism/docs#19212](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/19211), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#19212](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19212), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#19212](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19212)) is a recurring and widespread problem across all nodes, requiring immediate attention to prevent high memory consumption and crashes.
