On May 12, 2026, Optimism's development focused heavily on improving interoperability and stability across the Superchain, particularly addressing flaky withdrawal tests and advancing the Interop Proofs Devnet. Significant progress was made in enhancing the `op-reth` trie engine and resolving critical bugs in `op-supernode` and `op-proposer` to ensure robust L2 reorg recovery and overall system reliability.

## ✅ Completed Work

### Interoperability & Stability Enhancements
*   **`op-reth` Trie Engine Enhancements**: Major progress on the `op-reth` trie engine, wiring up the engine in Exex and introducing core components for background trie state mutations and typed message exposure, improving performance and reliability ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20450](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20450), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#20419](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20419)).
*   **Interop Tooling & Fixes**: Added new CLI tools for interop transaction testing and resolved issues in `op-supernode` and `op-interop-filter` related to L2 reorg recovery, earliest block validation, and L1 head processing ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20621](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20621), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#20585](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20585), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#20628](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20628), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#20648](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20648), [ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20647](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20647)).
*   **`cci-stats` Service Stability**: Fixed a critical bug in the `cci-stats` service preventing nil-pointer dereferences when processing pipelines lacking VCS information, improving robustness ([ethereum-optimism/infra#621](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/621)).

### Critical Bug Fixes & Test Improvements
*   **Flaky Withdrawal Test Resolution**: Investigated and created a draft fix for the persistent `TestWithdrawal_Cannon` flaky test, addressing `OptimismPortal_InvalidProofTimestamp()` errors by explicitly waiting for the L1 head time to be greater than the game creation time before retrying the prove transaction ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20677](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20677)). This issue was also closed in [ethereum-optimism/infra#19963](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19963), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#19963](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19963), and [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#19963](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19963).
*   **`op-supernode` `RequiredL1` Clarification**: Closed the issue regarding `op-supernode`'s `superroot_atTimestamp` reporting `RequiredL1` as cross-safe L1, confirming correct reporting of local-safe-derivation L1 after extensive testing and rewriting acceptance tests ([ethereum-optimism/infra#20657](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20657), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#20657](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20657), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#20657](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20657), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#20657](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20657)).
*   **Kona Protocol & Supernode Stability Fixes**: Resolved a bug in Kona's frame parsing where invalid `is_last` bytes were not rejected ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19335](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19335)) and fixed a critical issue preventing `op-supernode`'s `logs.db` from recovering after L2 reorgs while offline ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20627](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20627)).
*   **Deployment Tooling & Upgrade Support**: Closed issues related to deployment tooling for merging chains into a shared dispute game ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19703](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19703)), implemented past upgrade handling for L2 forks ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20524)), and added an upgrade template supporting transitions from permissioned to permissionless chains ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20562](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20562)).

### V800 Upgrade & OPCM Enhancements
*   **V800 Upgrade and Migration Enhancements**: Improved V800 upgrade and migration processes, including adding fallback JSON fixtures for devnets and supporting upgrades from permissioned to permissionless configurations ([ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#1423](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1423), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#1418](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1418)).
*   **OPCM Behavior and Validation Fixes**: Addressed specific behaviors and validation issues related to the OPCM, including supporting odd OPCM behavior during state diff checks and aligning migration validation ABIs ([ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#1424](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1424), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#1422](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1422)).
