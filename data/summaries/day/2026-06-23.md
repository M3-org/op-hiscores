## ✅ Completed Work

### Core Protocol & Infrastructure Enhancements
- **`op-txverify` Link Compression**: Implemented compression for `op-txverify` JSON payloads using Solady `LibZip.flzCompress` and switched to URL-safe Base64 links. ([ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#1483](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1483))
- **Karst Upgrade Gas Handling**: Introduced `keep_karst_upgrade_gas` flag for Sepolia and Mainnet superchains to manage upgrade gas behavior post-Karst hardfork. ([ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#1259](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1259))
- **`op-reth` Stability & Features**: Made chainspec reth-codec opt-in ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21483](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21483)), enabled `op-reth-premium` for devstack acceptance tests ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21515](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21515)), and fixed invalid interop transaction eviction ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21447](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21447)).
- **SDM PostExec Consensus Stabilization**: Stabilized the PostExec consensus surface with a `0x7D` guard and opt-in TOCTOU. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21502](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21502))
- **`op-rbuilder` Improvements**: Gated interop transactions during failsafe operations. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21494](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21494))
- **Karst Upgrade Gas Fix**: Ensured Karst upgrade gas applies only to the activation block in the derive component. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21441](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21441))
- **`op-geth` Decoupling Progress**: `op-node` is now wired onto `op-core/eip1559` and `op-service/txinclude` onto `op-core/fees`. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21500](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21500))
