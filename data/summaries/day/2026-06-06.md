On Jun 6, 2026, Optimism's development focused on enhancing testing infrastructure by transitioning `optimism`'s acceptance tests to `op-reth` for sequencing, deprecating `op-geth` for Karst+ blocks, and updating related documentation. Concurrently, the `superchain-registry` saw a routine automated update to its `addresses.json` file, ensuring configuration currency.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Testing Infrastructure & Documentation Updates
- The acceptance test suite in `optimism` was updated to sequence on `op-reth` for Karst+ blocks, deprecating `op-geth` for these blocks to ensure robust Superchain testing. ([#21188](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21188))
- Documentation in `optimism` was updated to remove outdated references to `--proofs-history.prune-interval` in `op-reth`, improving accuracy for node operators. ([#20937](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20937))
- The issue concerning the switch to `op-reth`-only sequencing for Karst+ in `op-acceptance-tests` was closed, reflecting the deprecation of `op-geth` for these blocks. ([#21182](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21182))

### Superchain Registry Maintenance
- An automated code generation update was performed in `superchain-registry` to update the `addresses.json` file, maintaining current configurations. ([#1253](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/pull/1253))
