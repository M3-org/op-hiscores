## ✅ Completed Work
### Core Infrastructure Refinements
- **Optimism Genesis Block Validation**: `op-geth` implemented a skip for `extraData` validation in Optimism genesis blocks to accommodate their non-empty `extraData` field, which was previously causing rejections ([#770](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/770)).
- **Genesis Interop Activation Refactoring**: `op-devstack` in `optimism` was refactored to streamline genesis interop activation by adding a `UseGenesisInterop` flag and deferring genesis timestamp resolution ([#19302](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19302)).
