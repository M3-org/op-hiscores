- **`proxyd` Interoperability Fix**: A bug in `proxyd` was addressed, tightening interop access-list descriptor timestamp validation to ensure correct validation of executing-message transactions. ([ethereum-optimism/infra#662](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/662))
- **Block Processing & L2 Genesis Upgrade Fixes**: Fixed a bug in `alloy-op-evm` by rolling back SDM block-warming for declined `op-rbuilder` candidates and resolved issues with interop L2 genesis upgrade tests under custom gas tokens. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#21359](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21359), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#21444](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21444))

- **Ink Sepolia Migration Preparation**: New tasks were added and documentation updated to facilitate the Ink Sepolia rollup-operator migration to OP Enterprise, including setting batcher unsafe signer, proposer rotation, and fee vault updates.
