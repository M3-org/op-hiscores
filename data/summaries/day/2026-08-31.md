## ✅ Completed Work
### Super ZK Dispute Game & Challenger Enhancements
- Internal review findings for the Super ZK dispute game were remediated, adding a solvency invariant and migration boundary test for `ZKDisputeGame`, and bounding ZK durations within `OPContractsManagerStandardValidator` and `op-deploy` ([#22328](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22328)).
- `op-challenger` was updated to support deriving super-root game inputs directly from `op-node` when a supernode is not set, simplifying super-root prestate exercises ([#22642](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22642)).
- `op-deployer` was updated to reflect `op-contracts/v8.0.0-rc.3` standard versions by bumping the `superchain-registry` dependency ([#22703](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22703)).

### Infrastructure Security & Monitoring Improvements
- High-severity transitive dependencies were patched in `replica-healthcheck` by refreshing Mocha and pinning `js-yaml` and `minimatch` to patched releases ([#729](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/729)).
- The `golang.org/x/crypto` module was updated to v0.55.0 across `op-txproxy` and `proxyd` to address security concerns ([#728](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/728)).
- `proxyd`'s `tx_monitor` inclusion-latency clock was updated to start at the request receipt for more accurate measurement ([#727](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/727)).

### Governance & Auction Adjustments
- The default price ceiling for `kona-sp1` proof requests was raised to ensure bids are drawn, addressing unfulfilled production proof requests ([#22640](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22640)).
- New tasks were added to transition the Unichain Mainnet ProxyAdmin Owner to the standard OP-governed model, aligning with a recent governance proposal ([#1527](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1527)).
