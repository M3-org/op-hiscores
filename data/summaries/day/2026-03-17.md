Today, the Optimism project made significant strides in stabilizing its CI/CD pipelines by resolving numerous flaky tests across multiple repositories, addressing issues ranging from nonce races to resource contention. Concurrently, discussions advanced on refining the `op-node`'s initial EL Sync behavior and enhancing Supernode test coverage, while documentation was streamlined by removing outdated SuperchainERC20 references.
