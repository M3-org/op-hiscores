Apr 20, 2026

Today, significant progress was made on Superchain interoperability and stability, with key bug fixes in Kona for Interop activation and enhancements to `op-supernode` for log backfill and validation. Critical L2CM milestones were closed across repositories, now owned by the Upgrade 19 process, while discussions continued on Supernode functionality and a resurfacing flaky test.

## 🚨 Needs Attention 
- **Urgent Discussions**:
    - `ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops`: The flaky `TestFlashblocksStream` test has resurfaced with a new failure mode, indicating an assertion error where "0" is not greater than "1". A fix has been proposed in a related `optimism` pull request ([#19883](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19883)).
    - `ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops`: Discussion continues on whether `op-supernode` should return super roots for unsafe data, with low urgency noted and potential deferral ([#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19059)).
    - `ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops`: The fix for conditionally deploying `CrossL2Inbox` needs to ensure `kona-derive` (and `kona-proof`) do not generate spurious deployments, rather than focusing solely on `kona-node` ([#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19311)).

## ✅ Completed Work
### Superchain Operations & Upgrade Preparation
- A new operations template for the Superroot upgrade was introduced, including related Solidity files and test configurations ([#1397](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1397)).
- The `sep/074` Deputy Pause Module key rotation task was re-signed and migrated off `FoundationOperationsSafe` to fix a regression-test fixture ([#1402](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1402)).
- The `DeputyPauseModule` address in `addresses.toml` was updated to reflect the latest DPM addresses ([#1401](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1401)).

### Interoperability & Supernode Enhancements
- `op-supernode` was enhanced with interop log backfill depth and validation gating, improving pre-ingestion of initiating logs and adding `TimestampToBlockNumber` functionality to `ChainContainer` ([#19973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19973)).
- Kona bug fixes addressed state divergence during single-chain Interop activation by conditionally deploying `CrossL2Inbox` ([#20147](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20147)).
- An argument passed to `is_interop_active()` in the Kona proof driver was corrected to use the block timestamp instead of the block number ([#20162](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20162)).

### CI/CD & Test Suite Reliability
- CI stability was improved by pinning `mise` to a specific version in the `docker-build-prep` action ([#20170](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20170)).
- CI gate jobs in the CircleCI configuration were updated ([#20190](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20190)).
- Flaky tests related to `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes` were unmarked, indicating improved stability ([#20159](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20159)).
- A verification step for `ci-base-clang` provenance attestation was added to GitHub Actions ([#20171](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20171)).
- Overrides for `-count` and `-timeout` were allowed in `just test` for `op-acceptance-tests` ([#20161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20161)).
- The `ci-base-clang` image for Rust jobs was pinned, and runtime `apt-install` was skipped ([#20135](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20135)).
