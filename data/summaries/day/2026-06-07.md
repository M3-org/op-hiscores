Today, the Optimism project focused on critical stability improvements, specifically enhancing the Kona host's witness fetching mechanism for more resilient optimistic-block re-execution and fixing a bug in the `op-challenger` to ensure reliable VM process termination.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Protocol Stability & Reliability
- Improved the Kona host's witness fetching to retry via the `get_preimage` loop, addressing issues with high-level hints not reliably re-fetching preimages upon initial errors ([#21269](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21269)).
- Fixed a bug in the `op-challenger` to ensure the VM process group is reliably torn down on timeout, improving the stability of trace execution ([#21268](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21268)).
