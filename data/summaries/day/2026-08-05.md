Aug 5, 2026 - Overall Project Daily Digest

Today's focus across the Optimism ecosystem involved significant progress on the `opgeth-decoupling` initiative, with multiple related issues closed and core components refactored. Key technical updates included an ownership transfer for Swell Mainnet L2 and updates to the interop derivation specification, while persistent flakiness in the `TestInteropHappyTx` test remains an active concern.

## 🚨 Needs Attention
- **Urgent Discussions**: The `TestInteropHappyTx` test continues to be flaky across `ethereum-optimism/specs`, `ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops`, and `ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry` due to sequencer action starvation, consistent with the driver event-loop starvation fingerprint identified in [#21952](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21952). This requires immediate investigation and resolution. ([#21940](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21940), [#21940](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21940), [#21940](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21940))
- **New Issues**: A regression in `op-service/sources` regarding transaction hash and decode caching was reported in `ethereum-optimism/optimism`, potentially impacting performance. ([#22255](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22255))

## ✅ Completed Work
### Core Protocol & Interoperability Enhancements
- The interop derivation specification was updated to incorporate the new Network Upgrade Transactions (NUT) bundle design, replacing the superseded Lagoon upgrade-transaction spec. ([#927](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/927))
- `op-deployer` received critical fixes to correctly resolve `SuperchainConfig` in `InitLiveStrategy` and integrated `SP1PlonkAdapter` into OPCM releases for streamlined verifier deployment. ([#22258](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22258), [#22252](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22252), [#22218](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22218), [#22245](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22245))

### Infrastructure & Superchain Operations
- A maintenance upgrade proposal was executed to transfer the L2 ProxyAdmin for Swell Mainnet to an aliased AltLayer Safe. ([#1512](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1512))
- CI stability was improved by retrying transient failures, unskipping/enforcing several acceptance tests for `kona-node`, and streamlining image building processes. ([#22200](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22200), [#22249](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22249), [#22246](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22246), [#22224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22224), [#22257](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22257))

### Component Refactoring & Decoupling
- Significant internal refactoring was completed across `op-batcher` (loading L2 blocks as execution payloads), `op-service` (becoming the OP-aware eth client), `op-node` (gating `PostExec` transaction structure validation), and `op-dispute-mon` (typed monitor lanes). ([#21911](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21911), [#21908](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21908), [#22101](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22101), [#22238](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22238), [#22225](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22225), [#22261](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22261), [#22260](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22260), [#22256](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22256))
