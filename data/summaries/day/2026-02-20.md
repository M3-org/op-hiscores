([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19262](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19262), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#19256](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19256), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#19261](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19261))
- **Supernode & Devstack Refinements**: `op-devstack` now uses a unified `Registry` for component storage, and `op-supernode` defers to `superAuthority` for the finalized L2 head, addressing finalization issues. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#18873](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/18873), [ethereum-optimism/optimism#19189](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19189))
