On Jun 15, 2026, the Optimism project saw significant progress in preparing for the Lagoon hardfork, including renaming "Interop" to "Lagoon" across the OP Stack and addressing critical `op-geth` decoupling challenges related to L2 transaction representation and test migration. Concurrently, a major cleanup of stale issues occurred across multiple repositories, alongside crucial bug fixes in `proxyd` and `op-reth` to enhance stability and correctness.

## ✅ Completed Work
- **Lagoon Hardfork Preparation & Configuration**:
    - The "Interop" hardfork was officially renamed to "Lagoon" across the OP Stack, involving updates to `params.ChainConfig` and JSON tags in `core/genesis.go`, `params/config.go`, and `superchain/chain.go`. ([#803](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/pull/803))
    - The `proxyd` v4.28.1 release was added to the release notes. ([#21373](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21373))
    - The SDM (Superchain Data Model) was directly gated on the Lagoon hardfork. ([#21379](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21379))
- **Core Infrastructure Stability & Bug Fixes**:
    - A critical bug in `proxyd` was fixed to correctly route websocket transaction RPCs through pre-forward validation and backend-group forwarding. ([#655](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/655))
    - `op-reth` fixed a bug reproducing trailing SDM PostExec transactions during derived block rebuilding, ensuring consensus requirements. ([#21321](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21321))
    - A critical bug in contracts was addressed by clearing residual proxy admin owner nonces in L2 genesis, preventing unintended nonce bumps. ([#21343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21343))
    - An upstream fix in Reth resolved a critical `op-reth` bug causing SIGBUS crashes during deep FCU rewinds. ([#20934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20934), [#20934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-geth/issues/20934), [#20934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20934), [#20934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20934), [#20934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20934))
- **Developer Experience & Documentation Improvements**:
    - The Rust stack centralized the OP fork to implied L1 fork mapping, improving consistency. ([#21368](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21368))
    - A new upstream trait for `ValidatingPool` was implemented in `OpPool` for `op-reth`, adding validation capabilities. ([#21378](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21378))
    - Documentation was enhanced with a warning for `op-reth` operators regarding a Superchain Registry loading bug on OP Sepolia. ([#21381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21381))
    - A new section was added to the Rust development guide noting the canonical OP to L1 fork mapping. ([#21384](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21384))
    - A task was executed in `superchain-ops` related to `sep/099-U19-op-ink-mmz-soneium`. ([#1465](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1465))
