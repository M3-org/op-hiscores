Overall Project Summary:

Today, the Optimism project saw significant activity across multiple repositories, with a strong focus on addressing critical `op-reth` stability issues like memory leaks and state root mismatches, alongside substantial progress in `optimism` core development including bug fixes, CI/CD enhancements, and supernode refactoring for stronger consistency.
