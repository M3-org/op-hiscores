On May 4, 2026, the Optimism project made significant strides in interoperability and Superchain infrastructure, with extensive stress testing validating interop mechanisms and a critical OPCM upgrade integrating the ZK_DISPUTE_GAME slot. Key efforts focused on refining supernode functionality, optimizing prestate builds for faster development, and addressing several critical bugs across core components like `op-reth` and `op-challenger`.

## 🚨 Needs Attention
- **Urgent Discussions**:
    - **Runbook for Super Root Chain Update**: Discussion on [#20473](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20473) suggests deferring the runbook update for moving a super root chain from permissioned to permissionless until after interop has shipped. Guidance is needed on how to track this as follow-up work without blocking the current release.
    - **Fault Proof Divergence**: A potential fault-proof divergence in `MessageGraph::check_single_dependency` was identified in [#20528](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20528), where the executing-chain interop activation guard is missing for the Kona client versus `op-program`. This requires immediate investigation.
- **Pull Requests to Review**:
    - **Refactor SetBatcherAndOrSigner**: [#1413](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1413) in `superchain-ops` refactors `SetBatcherAndOrSigner` to make `safeAddressString` configurable via `config.toml`.
    - **Add ET10 on Base**: [#1312](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1312) in `ethereum-optimism.github.io` adds the ET10 (EventTrader 10 Index) on Base.
    - **Migrate op-conductor-ops to apko and melange**: [#615](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/615) in `infra` proposes migrating `op-conductor-ops` to use `apko` and `melange` for streamlined build processes.
    - **Post-Execution transactions and SDM specs**: [#908](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/908) in `specs` introduces specifications for Post-Execution transactions and the Superchain Data Model (SDM).
    - **SDM specs at specs.optimism.io (WIP)**: [#20517](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20517) in `optimism` is a work-in-progress PR to add SDM specs.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Interoperability and Supernode Enhancements
- **Interop Chain Creation**: Ensured new chains can be created post-interop, with new OPCM deploying chains into their own dependency set of 1. ([#15884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/15884), [#15884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/15884), [#15884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/15884), [#15884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/15884), [#15884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/15884), [#15884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/15884), [#15884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/15884))
- **Supernode Functionality**: Addressed supernode behavior with unsafe data by opting for independent construction of expected super roots in tests. ([#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19059), [#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19059), [#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19059), [#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19059), [#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19059), [#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19059), [#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19059))
- **Supernode Bug Fix**: Fixed a bug in `op-supernode` where `VerifiedRequiredL1` used the MIN instead of MAX of per-chain L1s. ([#20481](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20481))
- **Supernode L1s Update**: `op-supernode` was updated to correctly use the MAX of per-chain L1s for `VerifiedRequiredL1`. ([#20484](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20484))

### Prestate Generation and Challenger Fixes
- **Fast Kona Prestate Build**: Implemented a faster Kona prestate build process, now available outside of Docker. ([#19057](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19057), [#19057](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/19057), [#19057](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19057), [#19057](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19057), [#19057](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19057), [#19057](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19057), [#19057](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19057))
- **`op-challenger` Prestate URL Fix**: Resolved duplicate issues regarding `op-challenger` using the incorrect prestate URL for `SuperPermissionedGameType`. ([#20500](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20500), [#20500](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/20500), [#20500](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20500), [#20500](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20500), [#20500](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20500), [#20500](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20500), [#20500](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20500))
- **`op-challenger` Routing**: `op-challenger` now correctly routes `SuperCannonKonaGameType` to `KonaSuperExecutor` ([#20512](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20512)) and `SuperPermissionedGameType` through `cannon-kona` ([#20501](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20501)).
- **Kona Proof Decoding**: Fixed `kona-proof` to correctly decode EIP-2935 storage slot values as U256. ([#20518](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20518))
- **`op-program` Configuration**: Made configuration arguments for the permissioned dispute game in `op-program` optional. ([#20404](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20404))

### Testing and Infrastructure Improvements

([#20370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20370), [#20370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/20370), [#20370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20370), [#20370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20370), [#20370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20370), [#20370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20370), [#20370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20370))

- **`op-reth` Fixes**: Fixed `op-reth` to wait for proofs ExEx store before `eth_getProof` ([#20487](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20487)) and resolved a stall issue during `init-state` by committing RW transactions earlier ([#20485](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20485)).

- **OPCM Migration**: Completed migration of interop activation out of `OPContractsManagerV2.upgrade()` into `OPContractsManagerMigrator`. ([#20368](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20368))
