Today, the Optimism project significantly advanced its infrastructure by completing the migration of Docker builds to GitHub Actions, enhancing CI/CD efficiency across repositories. - Improvements were made to the CI/CD pipeline in `optimism`, including avoiding unnecessary artifact downloads and adding a new CI check for `op-geth` versions.
