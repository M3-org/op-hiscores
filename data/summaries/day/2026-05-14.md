Today, the Optimism project made significant strides in core infrastructure stability and future-proofing with the integration of `rollup-boost` into the monorepo and critical bug fixes addressing sequencer stalls and L1 reorg resilience. Concurrently, development is actively progressing on enhancing the `ZKDisputeGame` and improving `proxyd`'s consensus tracking, while several persistent flakiness issues in acceptance tests and `op-batcher` synchronization challenges require further investigation.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Core Infrastructure Stability & Observability
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism`: The `rollup-boost` library has been fully vendored into the monorepo, streamlining development workflows for `op-rbuilder` ([#20669](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20669)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism`: `op-node` now includes a new Prometheus counter to track successful follow source updates, enhancing observability ([#20763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20763)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism`: Fixed a deterministic deadlock in `--sequencer.max-safe-lag` that caused permanent sequencer stalls and a batcher-side deadlock ([#19758](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19758)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism`: Improved `op-devstack` for progress-aware initial sync checks in ELSync verifiers ([#20652](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20652)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism`: Made `op-node` more resilient to deep L1 reorgs beyond the cross-safe head ([#20769](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20769)).

### Consensus Tracking & Proof Interoperability
- `ethereum-optimism/infra`: Refactored `proxyd` to bundle `CLSyncBody` and `CLL1Num` into `ConsensusTrackerState` for improved error handling and consistency ([#622](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/622)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism`: Kona client fixes include rejecting future-timestamped prestate in `BootInfo::load` for honest actor behavior ([#20727](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20727)).
- `ethereum-optimism/optimism`: Validated the output-root version word in `fetch_safe_head_hash` and `fetch_output_block_hash` to prevent incorrect L2 block hash slicing ([#20724](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20724)).
