Today, a critical intrinsic gas bug on Optimism Sepolia was resolved with the `op-reth/v2.3.3` release, while significant progress was made on the Super ZK Dispute Game, including new integration tests and a ZK mock verifier. Concurrently, the team initiated a comprehensive review of Go fuzzing strategies across multiple repositories, largely recommending retirement of current PR-time fuzzing jobs due to limited value.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Core Protocol Stability & Performance
- **Optimism Sepolia Intrinsic Gas Fix**: The issue causing high intrinsic gas on Optimism Sepolia after the Karst hard fork was resolved and included in the `op-reth/v2.3.3` release, improving transaction estimation reliability ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21457), [#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/op-analytics/issues/21457), [#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21457), [#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/21457), [#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21457)).
- **Kona Precompile Gas Handling Fixes**: Two critical bugs in Kona related to precompile gas handling were fixed, preventing panics in out-of-gas scenarios and ensuring correct gas expenditure when precompiles halt ([#21582](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21582), [#21634](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21634)).

### Super ZK Dispute Game Advancements
- **Super ZK Dispute Game Enhancements**: Significant progress was made on the Super ZK dispute game, including adding a ZK mock verifier to `op-deployer` for easier testing ([#21571](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21571)), applying QA fixes to contract behavior ([#21615](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21615)), and introducing four new integration test scenarios to improve coverage ([#21128](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21128)).
- **Super ZK Dispute Game Readiness**: Several issues related to the Super ZK Dispute Game were closed, indicating readiness, including migration path implementation ([#20758](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20758)), integration tests and deployment scripts ([#20761](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20761)), and a comprehensive QA review ([#21603](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21603)).

### Developer Experience & CI/CD Improvements
- **Documentation Updates**: New default feature guides for Flashblocks and Alt-DA modes were added for chain operators, and a getting-started tutorial for app developers deploying contracts to OP Sepolia was introduced ([#21145](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21145), [#21607](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21607)).
- **CI Process Refinements**: Go fuzz jobs were configured to run as seed-only checks ([#21547](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21547)), and CircleCI workflow routing was extracted into a dedicated script for better organization ([#21579](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21579)).
- **Op-Geth Decoupling Progress**: Progress was made on the `opgeth-decoupling` initiative, specifically addressing the swapping of `eip1559`, `superchain`, and `fees` imports, and the deletion of `op-service/superutil` ([#20271](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20271)).
