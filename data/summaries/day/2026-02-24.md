On Feb 24, 2026, Optimism focused on critical RPC performance vulnerabilities related to `eth_getLogs` memory exhaustion, with new issues opened and duplicates closed across multiple repositories. Significant progress was also made in enhancing fault proof testing, integrating the Karst hard fork, and developing a new policy engine for stake-aware transaction ordering within the `optimism` repository. Discussions continued on `op-batcher` ordering and `op-supernode` SLA updates, highlighting ongoing efforts to refine core infrastructure and improve developer experience.

- **Blocked Issues/PRs**:
    - `op-reth` P2P Sync Test Failure ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19298](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19298)): The `p2p::can_sync` e2e test in `reth-optimism-node` is crashing due to an incorrect `debug_assert!` in upstream reth v1.11.1 and is currently ignored, requiring a fix to enable this test.
    - `op-proposer` Sepolia Devnet Timestamp Discrepancy ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19301](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19301)): A bug in `op-proposer` on the Sepolia devnet is causing unexpected root claim checks due to a timestamp-like value being used instead of a block number for `anchorStateRegistry`.
