- Increased the timeout for `TestSupernodeInteropActivationAfterGenesis` to 5 minutes to address flakiness in the merge queue in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` ([#19297](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19297)).
- Integrated Karst hard fork activation logic, though pending `op-geth HardforkConfig` update in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` ([#19250](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19250)).

- Introduced a new `PolicyEngineStaking` periphery contract to enable stake-aware transaction ordering in `op-rbuilder` in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` ([#19192](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19192)).

### CI/CD and Supernode Stability Improvements
- Disabled incremental compilation for all Rust CI jobs by setting `CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0` and bumped the Rust build cache version from 15 to 16 in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` ([#19278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19278)).
- Implemented a critical fix to prevent supernode hangs on shutdown by ensuring the metrics service always decrements in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` ([#19293](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19293)).

### Documentation & Compatibility Enhancements
- Archived an outdated upgrade notice for Upgrade 18 and removed related callouts, ensuring documentation remains current in `ethereum-optimism/docs` ([#1986](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/pull/1986)).
- Added `linux/arm64` release binaries to `op-acceptor`, improving compatibility for Apple M series users in `ethereum-optimism/infra` ([#555](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/555)).
