Optimism's development today saw significant progress in resolving long-standing interoperability issues, particularly concerning Kona's output root mismatches and conditional `CrossL2Inbox` deployments, alongside the completion of `OPSuccinct` feature flag integrations. Concurrently, efforts intensified to stabilize CI/acceptance tests, with multiple fixes for flaky tests and the migration of interop tests, while new discussions emerged around `op-reth` hardfork overrides and `op-conductor` reorg support.

## 🚨 Needs Attention
- **Urgent Discussions**:
    - `ethereum-optimism/optimism` [#20224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20224): `op-reth` is missing hardfork override flags, causing superchain nodes to fail p2p connections when the registry lacks timestamps.
    - `ethereum-optimism/design-docs` [#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/19266): Discussion continues on `kona-interop`'s handling of internally created blocks, specifically the non-storage of receipts for replacement blocks, which could lead to consolidation failures.
    - `ethereum-optimism/specs` [#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19266): Discussion revolves around how Kona-proofs handles receipts for internally generated replacement blocks, with concerns about consolidation failures if deposit-only blocks are referenced without stored receipts.
