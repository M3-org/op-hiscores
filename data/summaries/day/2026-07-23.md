Today, Optimism saw extensive documentation improvements, including new curriculum hubs and restructuring, alongside critical bug fixes in `op-reth` for transaction receipts and enhanced SuperAuthority deny-list checks in `op-node`. Significant progress was also made in dispute game classification and ZKVM development, while `op-node` synchronization issues with `op-reth` backends continue to be an active challenge.

### Dispute Game and ZKVM Development

- `op-dispute-mon` was updated to correctly classify `ZKDisputeGame` as a super-root game ([#21979](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21979)).
- The epic for the Kona-based SP1 super-root state-transition guest has been closed ([#21402](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21402)).
