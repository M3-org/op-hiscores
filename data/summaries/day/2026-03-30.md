- Kona host tests were improved for isolation and deduplication of KV store creation. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19807](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19807))
- The issue regarding Kona's `MessageGraph` using a hardcoded `MESSAGE_EXPIRY_WINDOW` was resolved, with the fix allowing for configurable message expiry. ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#19636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19636))
