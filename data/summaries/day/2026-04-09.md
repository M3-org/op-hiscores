On Apr 9, 2026, Optimism's development focused on refining Superchain interoperability specifications, significant infrastructure improvements including CircleCI enhancements and contract refactoring in the core `optimism` repository, and routine dependency updates across the ecosystem. Key efforts were directed towards streamlining the codebase and enhancing CI/CD capabilities, alongside addressing new challenges in CI stability and interop filter design.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Superchain Interoperability Specification Refinements
- Updates were made to the interoperability specifications, including the removal of "Supervisor / Managed Node" concepts, simplification of replacement logic, and clarification of Superroot API behavior to streamline interop documentation within `ethereum-optimism/specs`. [#900](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/900)

### Infrastructure and CI/CD Enhancements
- The CircleCI workflow in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` was updated to include a `circleci-api-token` context for specific jobs, enhancing CI/CD capabilities. [#19960](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19960)

### Contract Refactoring and Cleanup
- The `OptimismPortalInterop` contract and all its associated references were deleted from `ethereum-optimism/optimism`, streamlining the codebase. [#19956](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19956)

### Dependency Updates
- The `op-geth` dependency in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` was bumped to version `v1.101702.1-rc.1`. [#19992](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19992)
- The `fast-xml-parser` dependency in `ethereum-optimism/ecosystem` was updated from version 4.5.3 to 4.5.6. [#990](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/pull/990)
