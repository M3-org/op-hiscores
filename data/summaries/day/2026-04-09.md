On Apr 9, 2026, Optimism saw significant progress in refining Superchain interoperability specifications and cleaning up core contracts by removing deprecated components. Concurrently, infrastructure improvements were made to CI/CD processes and dependencies, while new issues emerged concerning CI flakiness and further interop enhancements.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Superchain Interoperability Refinements
*   Updates to the interoperability specifications removed "Supervisor / Managed Node" concepts, simplified replacement logic, and clarified Superroot API behavior to streamline documentation and reflect current architectural understanding in `ethereum-optimism/specs` ([#900](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/900)).

### Core Contract Cleanup and Infrastructure Updates
*   The `OptimismPortalInterop` contract and all its references were deleted, streamlining the codebase and removing deprecated components across `contracts-bedrock` and `op-deployer/pkg` in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` ([#19956](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19956)).
*   A CircleCI workflow was updated to include a necessary API token for specific jobs, enhancing CI/CD processes in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` ([#19960](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19960)).
*   The `op-geth` dependency was bumped to `v1.101702.1-rc.1` in `ethereum-optimism/optimism` ([#19992](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19992)).
*   The `fast-xml-parser` dependency was updated from version 4.5.3 to 4.5.6 in `ethereum-optimism/ecosystem` ([#990](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/pull/990)).
