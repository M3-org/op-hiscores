On Jan 13, 2026, Optimism focused on significant infrastructure updates, including the redeployment of Betanet to v3 and comprehensive documentation enhancements for Custom Gas Token v2 and Kona proofs. Key progress was made in CI/CD improvements, protocol simplification by removing L2OutputOracle support, and security enhancements with JWT authentication for `op-interop-filter`.
