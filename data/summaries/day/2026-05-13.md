On May 13, 2026, Optimism's development focused heavily on enhancing the stability and reliability of the Superchain, particularly addressing critical vulnerabilities in Kona, resolving several long-standing flaky tests, and making significant progress on the L2CM retroactive project. Key efforts included stabilizing `superroot_atTimestamp` responses, fixing a buffer exhaustion bug in `kona::decompress_brotli`, and refining interop activation block handling across core components, alongside substantial refactoring in `op-reth/proofs` for improved proof-window semantics.

## 🚨 Needs Attention

This requires investigation into the `op-node` / `op-reth` interaction. ([ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19821), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19821), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19821))

([ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19934), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19934), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19934))

([ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19944), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/19944), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/19944))

([ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops#20134](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20134), [ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io#20134](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/issues/20134), [ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry#20134](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-registry/issues/20134))
