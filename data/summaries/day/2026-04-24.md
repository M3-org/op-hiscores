Today's focus across the Optimism project was heavily on interoperability, with significant progress in stabilizing the SuperDisputeGame implementation and addressing critical CI flakiness. ([ethereum-optimism/specs#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/905), [ethereum-optimism/infra#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/905), [ethereum-optimism/supersim#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/supersim/issues/905), [ethereum-optimism/ecosystem#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/issues/905))
