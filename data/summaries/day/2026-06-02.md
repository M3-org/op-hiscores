Optimism's development today significantly advanced Superchain capabilities with flashblocks support for SDM and improved `op-reth` proofs, alongside critical `op-node` and `op-supernode` stability fixes. A major focus emerged on addressing interop transaction pool resilience and developing recovery runbooks for invalid interop messages, while documentation was substantially updated with new feature pages and release information.

## 🚨 Needs Attention
- **Urgent Discussions**:
    - [ethereum-optimism/design-docs#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21149): The `op-reth` interop transaction pool does not resume after the failsafe is disabled, requiring a restart of both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` on sequencers; a failsafe-state gauge metric is proposed for observability.
    - [ethereum-optimism/design-docs#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21161): A runbook is needed for recovering a chain after an invalid interop message triggers a reorg, covering existing issues and ensuring failsafe disablement.
    - [ethereum-optimism/design-docs#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/20691): A local devnet is running, and the next step is to establish a long-running devnet.
    - [ethereum-optimism/design-docs#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/issues/21069): The team aims to stay in sync with new `revm` releases, with particular interest in v111 for Amsterdam hardfork support and shared type definitions.

## ✅ Completed Work
- **Superchain Interoperability & Data Management**:
    - Flashblocks support was integrated into the SDM system across `op-acceptance-tests`, `op-chain-ops`, `op-devstack`, `op-node`, `op-service`, and `op-rbuilder` ([#20738](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20738), [#21115](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21115), [#21162](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21162)).
    - `op-interop-filter` now includes correct and per-reason failsafe metrics and logs, improving observability ([#21162](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21162)).
    - `op-supernode` was updated to pre-initialize interop decision metric series for comprehensive tracking ([#21162](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21162)).
- **Node Stability & Proofs**:
    - `op-reth` proofs system now supports backfilling the proofs window backward for robust proof generation ([#20793](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20793)).
    - `op-node` preserves safedb across EL-sync restarts, preventing data loss ([#21111](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21111)).
    - `op-node` correctly reorgs follow-mode sequencers onto the upstream chain upon divergence ([#21155](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21155)).
    - `op-supernode` reliability was improved by blocking on Virtual Node readiness and lazily dialing the interop engine controller ([#21163](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21163), [#21152](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21152)).
- **Documentation & Specifications**:
    - New feature pages for L2 Contract Manager (L2CM) and Osaka on L2 were added to documentation ([#20971](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20971), [#21035](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21035)).
    - Release pages on docs.optimism.io were expanded with per-component release information ([#21160](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21160)).
    - Documentation for Karst Osaka changes was added to specifications ([#904](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/904)).
    - The draft specifications for Osaka on L2 were officially closed ([#903](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/903)).
- **Smart Contract & Codebase Refinements**:
    - Several issues related to EVM engineering and smart contract best practices were closed, including fixing `InitializableOZv5` tests and removing `Ownable` usage ([#11691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/11691), [#11766](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/11766), [#11767](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/11767), [#12139](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/12139), [#12232](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/12232), [#14609](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/14609), [#15113](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/15113), [#15114](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/15114), [#15115](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/15115), [#15248](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/15248), [#17332](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/17332)).
    - Extensive codebase cleanup was performed, removing unused dependencies, scripts, configs, and deprecated elements across the monorepo ([#19996](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/19996), [#20015](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20015), [#20018](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20018), [#20019](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20019), [#20042](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20042), [#20043](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20043), [#20036](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20036), [#20037](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20037), [#20038](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20038), [#20039](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20039), [#20041](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20041)).
- **Build Process Improvements**:
    - Docker builds now ignore lifecycle scripts during dependency installation, improving reliability ([#1007](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ecosystem/pull/1007), [#1324](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/ethereum-optimism.github.io/pull/1324)).
- **Superchain Operations**:
    - A bug in `MultisigTask` was fixed to tolerate negative gas refunds, improving robustness ([#1443](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1443)).
    - Several U19 betanet tasks (086-089) were executed on Sepolia, related to permissioned/permissionless settings and dispute game implementation ([#1444](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1444)).
