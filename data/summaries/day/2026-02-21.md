On Feb 21, 2026, the Optimism project significantly enhanced `op-reth` performance by implementing a transaction caching mechanism to optimize flashblock processing, aiming to reduce execution overhead. Concurrently, the team is addressing emerging concerns regarding L1 batch derivation causing block building delays and ensuring the availability of the latest `op-reth` Docker images.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Flashblock Performance Optimization
*   A `TransactionCache` was introduced in `op-reth` to store execution results, allowing the system to resume from a cached state for flashblocks with matching transaction prefixes, thereby avoiding re-execution of unchanged transactions and improving overall performance ([#19030](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19030)).
