Today, significant progress was made on integrating ZK-based dispute games into the OP Stack, with a new design document outlining its architecture and initial feature flag integration in the `optimism` repository. Additionally, critical stability improvements were implemented in `op-supernode` and `proxyd`, alongside enhancements to e2e testing for output games.

## ✅ Completed Work
### ZK Dispute Game Foundations
- A new design document was added detailing the integration of a ZK-based dispute game into the OP Stack, outlining its contract architecture, lifecycle, and OPCM. ([#368](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/design-docs/pull/368))
### Stability and Interoperability Fixes
- A bug was fixed in `proxyd` to ensure the correct chain ID is passed during interop access list validation, improving interoperability. ([#564](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/564))
- A bug in `op-supernode` was fixed where `VerifiedBlockAtL1` would incorrectly iterate with a zero `FinalizedL1` value during startup, improving stability. ([#19405](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19405))
### Developer Experience Improvements
- The process of creating output games in end-to-end tests was simplified by automatically deriving the `rootClaim` parameter, streamlining testing. ([#19294](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19294))
