# ethereum-optimism/specs Monthly Report (April 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In April 2026, development focused on streamlining the Interoperability (Interop) framework and resolving critical consensus discrepancies in older chains. Significant progress was made in simplifying the Superchain architecture by removing the "Supervisor/Managed Node" concepts in favor of more robust replacement logic and Superroot API definitions. While the team successfully addressed technical debt through dependency audits and L2 contract management enforcement, a major focus remained on triaging complex "receipt root mismatch" errors and persistent CI flakiness in flashblock and reorganization tests.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interoperability & Superchain Architecture
The primary focus this month was refining the interop specifications to ensure they reflect the current architectural direction.
*   **Simplification of Node Concepts:** A major update was initiated to remove the "Supervisor / Managed Node" concept, simplifying the replacement logic and clarifying how the Superroot API should behave ([#900](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/900)).
*   **L1Block Scalar Parsing:** Introduced a new guide for Jovian L1Block Scalar Parsing to improve protocol documentation ([#901](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/901)).

### Protocol Specification & Execution
Refinements were made to the execution layer and upgrade procedures to ensure clarity for developers and node operators.
*   **Upgrade Transaction Requirements:** The L2 upgrades execution document was updated to remove confusing notes regarding "one-time only" transactions, providing clearer requirements for protocol upgrades ([#902](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/902)).
*   **L2ContractsManager Enforcement:** Resolved an investigation into automated enforcement to prevent the introduction of storage variables in `L2ContractsManager`, ensuring long-term maintainability ([#19315](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19315)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Infrastructure & Dependency Cleanup:** The team successfully closed a broad issue regarding the removal of unused npm dependencies and dead Docker images. This work was strategically split into smaller, more manageable tasks to optimize the repository's footprint ([#20016](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20016)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Critical Consensus Errors:** A high-priority investigation is underway regarding `receipt root mismatch` errors on older chains. The issue involves incorrect trie encoding for pre-Canyon rules in `op-reth`, where `deposit_nonce` was erroneously included. This has been replicated across multiple OP Stack rollups ([#19930](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19930)).
*   **CI Stability & Flaky Tests:** Two significant flaky tests are under analysis. `TestFlashblocksTransfer` is experiencing timing races where transaction block times exceed flashblock times ([#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19934)), and `TestUnsafeGapFillAfterUnsafeReorg_RestartL2CL` has failed repeatedly with permanent operation failures during head matching ([#19936](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19936)).
*   **Go Dependency Audit:** An ongoing audit of direct Go dependencies has identified several candidates for removal or replacement with standard library equivalents to reduce the project's attack surface and complexity ([#19996](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19996)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month was marked by deep technical collaboration on consensus-level bugs. Contributors like **wwared** and **maurelian** were instrumental in providing CI run contexts to debug race conditions in the flashblock testing suite. There is also a strong emphasis on cross-project coordination, as evidenced by the replication of receipt root issues across various OP Stack chains. Finally, the community reached a symbolic milestone with the "Monorepo Celebration" issue ([#10000](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/10000)), reflecting a positive sentiment regarding the project's structural evolution.
