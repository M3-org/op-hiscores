# ethereum-optimism/specs Monthly Report (August 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In August 2026, development focused heavily on refining the interoperability framework and optimizing the data availability (DA) footprint for the upcoming Lagoon upgrade. A major milestone was reached with the transition from the superseded Lagoon upgrade-transaction specification to the more robust Network Upgrade Transactions (NUT) bundle design. The team also made significant strides in protocol stability, addressing critical CI bottlenecks and progressing on the `sdm-net-0` Sepolia alphanet rollout. Despite these advancements, the month was characterized by an intensive focus on debugging complex integration test flakiness and synchronization issues within the execution layer.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interoperability and Protocol Upgrades
The project underwent a significant shift in its interoperability strategy by replacing outdated components with the NUT bundle design.
*   **NUT Bundle Integration:** The interop derivation specification was overhauled to replace the superseded Lagoon upgrade-transaction spec. This involved comprehensive updates across `derivation.md` and `l2-upgrades-1-execution.md` to enhance Superchain interop capabilities ([#927](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/927)).
*   **Lagoon DA Optimization:** A new refinement to the Lagoon protocol excludes post-execution transactions (`0x7D`) from Jovian DA footprint accounting. By specifying zero contribution to `daFootprint` and `blobGasUsed`, the team clarified late-construction tradeoffs and addressed linear underestimation issues ([#926](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/926)).

### Infrastructure and Performance
*   **SDM Rollout:** Significant progress was made on the `sdm-net-0` Sepolia alphanet. Terraform configurations and L2 contract deployments are complete, with current efforts shifting toward transaction load generation and porting the SDM Grafana dashboard ([#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21273)).
*   **Decoupling Efforts:** The `opgeth-decoupling` initiative successfully concluded its phase of checking and swapping fork tools, streamlining the codebase for future execution layer flexibility ([#21746](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21746)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **CI Optimization:** The team resolved issues regarding CI makespan by adopting a strategy of merging smaller, individual PRs rather than large batches, effectively reducing wait times ([#21186](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21186)).
*   **Proofs Consistency:** A bug causing `op-reth` proofs history to lag after clean shutdowns was addressed. The resolution focuses on ensuring database consistency at startup rather than modifying shutdown logic, which remains vulnerable to unclean exits ([#22089](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22089)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sync Failures:** Users reported significant peering difficulties when running `op-node` with `--syncmode=execution-layer`, encountering frequent `Ecies` errors and stream unreadability ([#20974](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20974)).
*   **Test Flakiness:** Two major flaky tests are under investigation. `reth-optimism-node` integration tests are experiencing SIGSEGVs on teardown, potentially linked to unclosed MDBX environments or detached threads ([#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20973)). Additionally, `TestInteropHappyTx` is failing due to sequencer event-loop starvation, preventing timely transaction inclusion ([#21940](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21940)).
*   **Metrics DSL:** A proposal is active to introduce a high-level Domain Specific Language (DSL) for metrics usage in acceptance tests to improve developer experience and test readability ([#22207](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22207)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month saw deep technical collaboration between maintainers and contributors, particularly in the diagnostic efforts surrounding the `reth` integration tests. The discussion on issue [#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20973) showcases a high degree of coordination, with the team instrumenting CI to capture core dumps and stack traces to resolve low-level memory and threading issues. Furthermore, the transition to the NUT bundle ([#927](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/927)) involved cross-component updates, indicating tight synchronization between protocol researchers and specification maintainers. The community is also actively engaged in benchmarking, with suggestions to utilize `op-benchmark` for the upcoming SDM performance evaluations.
