# ethereum-optimism/specs Monthly Report (May 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In May 2026, development focused heavily on hardening the Interop infrastructure and optimizing the Supernode environment. Significant progress was made in stress-testing message validation, which confirmed linear cost scaling and bolstered confidence in the 7-day expiry window for cross-chain messages. The project also achieved major milestones in developer experience by streamlining Kona prestate builds and stabilizing `op-node` synchronization with Reth. These efforts, combined with the introduction of the Superchain Data Model (SDM) specifications, signal a transition toward a more robust and interoperable Superchain ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interop & Superchain Architecture
The foundation for cross-chain interoperability saw significant specification and testing activity.
*   **Superchain Data Model (SDM):** A major new pull request ([#908](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/908)) was introduced to define specifications for Post-Execution transactions and the SDM, providing a standardized framework for data handling across the Superchain.
*   **Interop Stress Testing:** Extensive testing on the `interop-jnt-v1` devnet confirmed that message validation scales linearly. A critical fix for Kona EIP-2935 proof decoding was implemented during this process, ensuring performance stability within the expiry window.
*   **Supernode Enhancements:** The `op-supernode` was refined by migrating "after head" tests to the acceptance test framework and resolving issues related to fetching super roots for unsafe data ([#19059](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19059)).

### Developer Experience & Tooling
Efforts to reduce friction for contributors and maintainers resulted in faster local development cycles.
*   **Kona Prestate Optimization:** The build process for Kona prestates was overhauled to remove Docker dependencies, significantly increasing local development velocity ([#19057](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19057)).
*   **Protocol Simplification:** The `ProtocolVersions` contract was removed to streamline the contract landscape, as it is no longer required for `op-node` monitoring ([#20309](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20309)).

### Specification Refinement
*   **Derivation Specs:** Minor documentation errors were corrected in the `derivation.md` specification to maintain high-quality technical standards ([#909](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/909)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **`op-node` Stability:** A long-standing issue where `op-node` would get stuck in Execution Layer (EL) sync mode when paired with Reth was resolved, improving the reliability of the node stack ([#18468](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18468)).
*   **Infrastructure Deployment:** The `proxyd-CL` was successfully deployed to Mainnet production to serve Light nodes ([#20573](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20573)).
*   **Healthcheck Support:** `op-supernode` charts now correctly support healthchecks on chain-id subdomains ([#18451](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18451)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Flaky Test Mitigation:** Work is ongoing to address flakiness in fault proof testing. A fix was submitted to reorder `freezeChains` to ensure `LocalSafe` is drained to `LocalUnsafe` before stopping batchers ([#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19804)).
*   **Challenger Configuration:** Discussions are underway regarding the consolidation of per-VM flags in `op-challenger`. This is a complex task as different game types may require distinct configuration formats ([#20547](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20547)).
*   **Gas Accounting:** Progress is being made on reconciling Go vs. EVM gas accounting, with new documentation and experimental Foundry configurations being tested to handle edge cases ([#20388](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20388)).
*   **Superroot Support:** A new requirement was identified to add `superroot_atTimestamp` support in `op-node` for non-interop chains to facilitate future fault dispute games ([#20552](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20552)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month was characterized by high-intensity technical triage and collaborative problem-solving. Maintainers like `sebastianst`, `geoknee`, and `ajsutton` were central to resolving sync issues and debating the merits of configuration refactors. The collaboration between the protocol and tooling teams was evident in the rapid identification and fixing of the Kona EIP-2935 decoding bug. While some interop-related batcher tasks are currently queued behind sync priorities, the community remains focused on the end-of-month timelines for Betanet milestones.
