# ethereum-optimism/specs Weekly Report (Aug 9 - 15, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the team focused on refining the Holocene upgrade specifications and streamlining dispute game deployments. Significant progress was made in hardening span batch processing through new overlap requirements, while the project simplified its approach to fault proofs on the `sdm-net-0` environment by opting for permissioned models. The closure of several long-standing issues related to `op-batcher` and `op-deployer` signals a shift toward stabilizing core protocol behavior and ZK dispute game configurations.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Span Batch Hardening
Efforts to improve the robustness of batching logic were central to this week's activity. A new pull request was introduced to mandate specific checks for span batch overlaps ([#928](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/928)). This change is aimed at ensuring the correctness of span batch processing within the Holocene upgrade, preventing potential data inconsistencies during sequencing.

### Dispute Game & Deployment Infrastructure
The team finalized updates to the `op-deployer` and Optimism Composition Manager (OPCM) to support new options for ZK dispute games. This work ensures that the deployment tooling is aligned with the latest protocol capabilities for zero-knowledge proofs. Additionally, the strategy for fault proofs on the `sdm-net-0` network was pivoted; rather than pursuing permissionless proofs, the team resolved to use permissioned fault proofs to simplify implementation while maintaining the necessary security properties for the environment ([#22348](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22348)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Protocol Simplification:** The team decided to close the issue regarding `op-batcher` behavior during sequencing-window expiry for span batches as "wontfix" ([#16036](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/16036)). The complexity of supporting recovery mode for span batches was deemed unnecessary, and the protocol will continue to support recovery mode exclusively in singular-batch mode.
*   **Tooling & Deployment:** Issues related to `op-deployer` changes for ZK dispute games were successfully resolved, marking the completion of these configuration updates ([#21429](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21429)).
*   **Network Stability:** The resolution of fault proof implementation on `sdm-net-0` ([#22348](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22348)) removes a potential blocker for that environment by utilizing existing, simpler permissioned models.

### New & Active Issues
*   The primary focus for new work is currently centered on the Holocene specification, specifically the implementation and review of the span batch overlap checks introduced in [#928](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/928).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by decisive triage and a focus on implementation efficiency. The collaboration between contributors led to the resolution of several complex architectural questions, such as the trade-offs between permissioned and permissionless proofs for specific test environments and the feasibility of recovery modes in batching. The move to close "wontfix" issues indicates a disciplined approach to maintaining the specification's scope and avoiding unnecessary technical debt.
