# ethereum-optimism/specs Weekly Report (May 24 - 30, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on formalizing core protocol definitions and resolving complex synchronization bottlenecks within the OP Stack. The introduction of the Karst derivation specification marks a significant step in refining the foundational technology of the Superchain. Meanwhile, engineering efforts were heavily directed toward diagnosing critical Consensus Layer (CL) sync stalls and stabilizing the testing infrastructure to ensure protocol reliability. These developments underscore a dual priority of advancing long-term protocol architecture while maintaining the stability of current interoperability and proof systems.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Karst Protocol Specification
The primary technical milestone this week was the introduction and expansion of the Karst derivation specification. This work defines the protocol's behavior regarding bundle storage and derivation effects within the monorepo.
*   **Derivation Framework:** Added a comprehensive overview and detailed derivation documents to establish the foundational logic for Karst ([#913](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/913)).

### Protocol Reliability & Testing
Significant effort was dedicated to hardening the codebase against non-deterministic failures and ensuring smooth interoperability activation.
*   **Interop Activation:** Resolved a bug where `op-supernode` would incorrectly resolve `FinalizedL2` to the genesis state during interop activation ([#20365](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20365)).
*   **Test Stabilization:** Identified the root cause of flakiness in `TestReorgUnsafeHead`. The issue stemmed from the test reading mutable Execution Layer (EL) labels that could lag; a fix was developed using the `ReorgExact` DSL to ensure explicit parent selection and proper polling budgets ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20198)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Critical Sync & Performance Issues
*   **CL Sync Stalls:** A high-priority investigation is underway regarding `op-supernode` and `op-reth` stalling during recovery. The issue ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21076)) appears to be a complex interaction where `op-node` advances the local safe head without reconciliation after FCU timeouts, potentially exacerbated by backpressure in `op-reth`.
*   **Proof System Optimization:** Discussions are ongoing regarding adjusting `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and air gap constants. These changes are intended to support faster withdrawals via ZK proofs while maintaining the standard 7-day finality window ([#20774](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20774)).

### Blockers & Infrastructure
*   **L2CM FeeVaults:** Updates to `setWithdrawalRoute` are currently blocked by deficiencies in the `TestActivationBlockNUTBundle`. The test's reliance on L2Genesis state conflicts with semver enforcement in the L2CM code, preventing contract upgrades ([#20806](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20806)).
*   **Interop Proofs Devnet:** Finalization of withdrawals on the `inphi-devnet-0` is pending a fix for the `op-chain-ops` proving script ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20489)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by deep technical coordination between core maintainers like **karlfloersch**, **wwared**, and **sebastianst**. Collaboration was particularly evident in the debugging of the CL sync stall, where contributors utilized advanced tooling like `tokio-console` and detailed logging to trace cross-client interactions.
