# ethereum-optimism/specs Weekly Report (Apr 12 - 18, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on long-term protocol planning and addressing synchronization edge cases within the OP Stack. The most significant development was the initiation of the Osaka L2 specification process, signaling the next phase of protocol evolution. Meanwhile, technical discussions centered on refining the CrossL2Inbox gas mechanics and troubleshooting persistent execution layer (EL) synchronization issues in `op-node`.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no new pull requests were merged during this period, the groundwork for upcoming protocol upgrades was established through specification planning.

### Protocol Evolution & Future Upgrades
The team has begun laying the foundation for the next major milestones in the Optimism roadmap.
*   **Osaka L2 Planning:** Work has officially commenced on drafting the specifications for the Osaka upgrade on Layer 2 ([#903](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/903)). This represents a shift toward future-proofing the stack beyond the current development cycle.
*   **Cross-Chain Messaging Refinement:** Discussions regarding the removal of gas introspection from the `CrossL2Inbox` ([#19374](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19374)) have clarified the implementation timeline. The team determined that this change is not critical for the immediate "Glamsterdam Defense" phase and will instead be prioritized for the Glamsterdam L2 upgrade.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The week's issue activity was characterized by deep-dive troubleshooting of node stability and the formalization of new feature requests.

### New & Active Issues
*   **`op-node` Sync Stalls:** A critical investigation is underway regarding `op-node` getting stuck in EL sync mode upon restart, specifically when paired with a Reth engine that has already completed its data sync ([#18468](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18468)). Current mitigation strategies involve manually switching the sync mode from EL to CL (Consensus Layer) sync, but a permanent fix is being actively researched.
*   **Specification Drafting:** Issue [#903](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/903) was opened to track the creation of the Osaka L2 specs, serving as the primary hub for upcoming protocol changes.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was primarily internal and focused on technical triage. The dialogue surrounding the `op-node` synchronization issue ([#18468](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18468)) demonstrates active coordination between protocol engineers to resolve complex interactions between the rollup node and execution clients like Reth. Additionally, the clarification on the `CrossL2Inbox` timeline indicates a disciplined approach to scope management, ensuring that immediate security and stability goals (Glamsterdam Defense) are prioritized over non-critical feature changes.
