# ethereum-optimism/specs Weekly Report (Jun 7 - 13, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on refining the technical specifications for the OP Stack, with a heavy emphasis on protocol decoupling and cross-layer consistency. Significant progress was made on the `opgeth-decoupling` initiative, specifically regarding the porting of fee calculation logic to `op-core/fees`. Developers also worked to align the `CrossL2Inbox` specification with its actual contract implementation to ensure protocol integrity. These efforts represent a continued push toward modularity and precision in how the Superchain handles transaction costs and cross-chain communication.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol Decoupling & Fee Logic
The primary technical focus this week involved the creation of `op-core/fees` as part of the broader `opgeth-decoupling` effort. This work ensures that L1 cost and operator-fee calculations are abstracted correctly for the OP Stack.
*   **DA Cost Accuracy:** Developers identified and corrected a requirement for `TxRollupCostData` to use `tx.MarshalBinary()` instead of `tx.Data()`. This ensures Data Availability (DA) costs are computed over the full binary-encoded transaction rather than just the data payload.
*   **Legacy Function Porting:** Pure-math legacy functions, including `L1CostBedrock` and `NewL1CostFuncEcotone`, have been successfully ported. State-reading dispatchers have been deferred to a later phase as they are primarily used for testing post-decoupling.
*   **Fork-Specific Operator Costs:** To support explicit fork differentiation, `OperatorCost` was renamed to `OperatorCostIsthmus`, and a new `OperatorCostJovian` variant was introduced to reflect updated calculation requirements.

### Specification Alignment
*   **CrossL2Inbox Consistency:** A new pull request, [#916](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/916), was opened to update the `CrossL2Inbox` specification. This fix ensures the documentation accurately reflects the underlying contract implementation, preventing discrepancies in cross-L2 messaging logic.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Active Issues & Ongoing Discussions
*   **Fee Estimation Fixes:** Significant progress was reported on issue [#17817](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/17817) regarding `txinclude`. By consistently applying the `Jovian` operator-fee formula for both estimation and actual-cost refunds, the team is ensuring more accurate budget reservations for transactions.
*   **Decoupling Roadmap:** Discussion continues on [#20261](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20261) regarding the transition to `op-core/fees`. The current strategy involves deferring complex state-reading dispatchers to Phase 4 of the decoupling roadmap to maintain focus on core mathematical porting.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The activity this week highlights a high level of coordination regarding the internal architecture of the OP Stack. The detailed technical discussions surrounding `op-core/fees` demonstrate a collaborative effort to ensure that the decoupling of `op-geth` does not introduce regressions in fee logic. The synchronization between specification writers and contract implementers is evident in the proactive fix for the `CrossL2Inbox`, ensuring that the "source of truth" remains consistent across the repository and deployed code.
