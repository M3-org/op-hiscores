# ethereum-optimism/specs Weekly Report (Jan 18 - 24, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on advancing core protocol infrastructure and refining transaction execution mechanics within the OP Stack. Significant progress was made in formalizing the next iteration of the Optimism Portal and Manager (OPCMv2) and establishing a structured framework for L2 contract upgrades. Additionally, technical refinements were introduced to optimize transaction inclusion and gas limit management for flashblocks, ensuring more robust and predictable block production.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol Infrastructure & Upgrades
The repository saw the introduction of foundational specifications aimed at evolving the Superchain's management and upgradeability.
*   **OPCMv2 Specification:** A major feature PR was opened to introduce the OPCMv2 specification, marking a significant step in the evolution of the Optimism Portal and Contract Management systems ([#875](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/875)).
*   **L2 Contract Upgrades:** Work began on formalizing the process for upgrading Layer 2 contracts. The first part of this specification was introduced to provide a clear roadmap for secure and standardized contract migrations ([#874](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/874)).

### Transaction Execution & Flashblocks
Efforts were made to refine how transactions are handled and included in blocks, specifically focusing on performance and reliability.
*   **Flashblock Gas Limits:** A new specification was proposed to address gas limits and transaction inclusion logic within flashblocks. This work is critical for maintaining network stability and optimizing the user experience during high-frequency transaction periods ([#873](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/873)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
During this period, the focus remained primarily on the submission of new feature specifications. No new issues were opened or closed, indicating a week dedicated to proactive protocol design and the formalization of upcoming technical milestones.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The activity this week reflects a high level of coordination among core contributors regarding the future architecture of the OP Stack. The simultaneous introduction of OPCMv2 and L2 upgrade specifications suggests a synchronized effort to enhance the governance and technical flexibility of the Superchain. These pull requests serve as the primary venues for technical review and collaborative refinement of the protocol's security and decentralization standards.
