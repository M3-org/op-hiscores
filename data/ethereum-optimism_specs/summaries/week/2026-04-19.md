# ethereum-optimism/specs Weekly Report (Apr 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on stabilizing the Interop and Fault Proof infrastructure as the project nears code completion for major milestones. Significant progress was made in resolving output root mismatches during message expiry consolidation and refining Kona’s interop validation logic. While several long-standing issues regarding contract deployments and specification updates were closed, the team continues to battle persistent CI flakiness and race conditions in core sequencer tests. Efforts are now shifting toward finalizing the SuperDisputeGame adoption and ensuring the batcher and op-conductor can gracefully handle interop-driven reorgs.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interop & Fault Proof Infrastructure
The team resolved critical blockers for Interop Fault Proofs, specifically addressing output root mismatches in Kona during message expiry consolidation.
*   **Message Expiry Consolidation:** Integrated configurable message-expiry windows and fixed re-execution hangs, ensuring tests pass across both `op-geth` and `op-reth` fault-proof environments ([#19848](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19848)).
*   **Kona Validation:** Refined Kona’s behavior to conditionally deploy the `CrossL2Inbox` based on chain dependency sets, aligning it with Go-based implementations ([#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19311)).
*   **Internal Block Access:** Concluded a review of how Kona handles internally created replacement blocks, confirming that interop validation is handled correctly ([#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19266)).

### Protocol Specifications & Contract Deployments
Work continued on aligning the written specifications with the evolving codebase and deploying necessary feature flags.
*   **Spec Updates:** Integrated Karst Osaka's changes ([#904](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/904)) and updated interop semantics to reflect the removal of same-timestamp requirements and simplified replacement blocks ([#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/905)).
*   **OPSuccinct Integration:** Successfully integrated the `OPSuccinct` feature flag into Optimism contracts and deployment scripts (`DeployImplementations.s.sol`) ([#18419](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18419), [#18420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18420)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Interop Application Behaviors:** Closed a legacy issue regarding chain readiness checks and metadata recording as the work is now fully implemented ([#18742](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18742)).
*   **Op-geth Deprecation:** Clarified that `op-geth` remains supported through May 31st, providing a clear timeline for node operators ([#20203](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20203)).
*   **Dependency Cleanup:** Removed unused `toml` dependencies from the documentation package to reduce bloat ([#20017](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20017)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Persistent Test Flakiness:** Several tests remain unstable. `TestFlashblocksTransfer` ([#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19934)) saw a new panic related to a nil channel despite previous fixes. `TestReorgUnsafeHead` ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20198)) was traced to an asynchronous update race condition between the op-node and sequencer.
*   **Sync & Reorg Risks:** Work on "Verification-Aware CurrentL1" for the batcher ([#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19944)) and graceful reorg recovery for `op-conductor` ([#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20006)) are currently queued behind immediate devnet sync issues, posing a risk to the end-of-month timeline.
*   **SuperDisputeGame Adoption:** Tracking continues for Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the SuperDisputeGame. While main contract PRs are merged, Kona proof completion remains the primary focus for the current sprint ([#19690](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19690), [#19691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19691)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by deep technical troubleshooting between maintainers, particularly regarding the Kona host and sequencer stability. Detailed analysis by contributors like **ajsutton** on flaky tests provided a roadmap for resolving race conditions that affect both `op-geth` and `op-reth` implementations. The collaboration between the protocol and tooling teams is evident in the synchronized updates to the interop specifications and the corresponding implementation in Kona and the Optimism contracts.
