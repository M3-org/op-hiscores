# ethereum-optimism/specs Weekly Report (May 10 - 16, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
The week of May 10 - 16 was characterized by a period of maintenance and minor technical refinement for the Optimism specifications. Activity focused primarily on improving the clarity of documentation surrounding flashblock RPC endpoints and addressing network configuration queries within the `op-node` environment. While no major protocol shifts were introduced, the work ensured that existing specifications remain accurate and that community members are finding resolutions to version-specific deployment hurdles.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Documentation & RPC Specification Refinement
Efforts this week were directed toward ensuring the technical accuracy of the Superchain's developer-facing specifications.
*   **Flashblock Specification Cleanup:** A pull request was initiated to resolve redundancies in the documentation of flashblocks. Specifically, PR [#910](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/910) corrects duplicate "state" descriptions across five distinct RPC endpoint specifications. This ensures that developers implementing or interacting with flashblock technology have clear, non-ambiguous references.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Network Configuration & Versioning:** The community resolved a notable configuration hurdle regarding `op-node` network presets. In issue [#20810](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20810), a user reported that the `base-mainnet` identifier was missing from the predefined networks in version v1.18.0. The issue was closed after the contributor confirmed a successful workaround by reverting to version v1.17.0, highlighting a potential regression or change in network handling in the latest release that may require further internal monitoring.

### New & Active Issues
The repository saw low volume in new issue reporting this week, with the primary focus remaining on the resolution of the aforementioned network configuration discrepancies. No significant new blockers or protocol-level bugs were introduced during this period.
