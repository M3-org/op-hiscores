# ethereum-optimism/specs Weekly Report (May 17 - 23, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on refining the technical foundations of the Interop project and resolving critical discrepancies between execution clients. Significant progress was made in aligning `kona-node` and `op-node` regarding Brotli channel handling, effectively eliminating the majority of divergence issues through targeted fixes. The team also streamlined the Interop architecture by migrating RPC namespaces and finalizing the L2CM upgrade mechanism for the Interop hard fork. Additionally, infrastructure stability was addressed through the migration of dispute game tests and the resolution of timing-related flaky tests in the challenger suite.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no major pull requests were marked as "completed" in the technical developments section this week, the project saw the introduction of new feature work and the resolution of several architectural investigations.

*   **Feature Development**
    *   A new feature pull request, [#911](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/911) ("feat: szkdg"), was opened to introduce new functionality to the specification repository.
*   **Performance & Optimization Research**
    *   An investigation into the `debug_executePayload` and related crates ([#20879](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20879)) concluded that these components are essential for performance. They serve as an active, opt-in fast-path for fault-proof witness prefetching in `op-program` and `kona`, with a maintained implementation in `op-reth`.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
The team successfully closed several high-priority issues related to the Interop roadmap and protocol consistency:
*   **Interop & Supervisor Infrastructure:** The migration of the supervisor RPC namespace to an interop filter namespace was completed ([#20005](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20005)). Furthermore, the implementation details for the Interop hard fork via the L2CM upgrade mechanism were finalized and moved to protocol-specific tracking ([#20082](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20082)).
*   **Client Consistency:** Discrepancies between Kona and `op-node` regarding Brotli channels were resolved ([#19333](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19333)). Investigations revealed a wrapper bug in Kona and non-spec-compliant behavior in the Rust `brotli` crate on corrupted inputs; a 4-line fix addressed 95% of the divergences.
*   **Test Suite Maintenance:** Remaining dispute game tests were successfully migrated from `op-e2e` to `op-acceptance-tests` ([#20814](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20814)), improving the organization of the project's end-to-end testing framework.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Interop Proofs Devnet:** Work continues on the Interop Proofs Devnet ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20489)), which remains active for long-term internal testing. Key milestones remaining include the Superdispute game, Flashblocks integration, and Conductor Re-org testing.
*   **Flaky Test Resolution:** A new issue ([#20869](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20869)) was opened to address flakiness in `TestChallengerCountersPreGenesisGame`. The root cause was identified as a timing collision between the migration process and the rollup start block; a fix involving increased delays and post-migration L1-head re-reads has been proposed.
