# ethereum-optimism/specs Daily Update (Jan 29, 2026)

The day saw advancements in flashblock specifications with new gas limit features and a fix for math rendering. An issue related to CCI Stats Cronjob Failures was resolved, and new issues regarding historical proofs and op-reth snapshots were opened.
