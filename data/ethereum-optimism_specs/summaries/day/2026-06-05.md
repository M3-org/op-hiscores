## ACTIVE ISSUES

### op-supernode/op-reth: CL sync recovery can stall when SafeDB/bookkeeping references EL-missing block [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21076)
The discussion on this issue continues to focus on identifying the root cause of CL sync recovery stalls. Recent updates indicate that a candidate fix from reth ([paradigmxyz/reth#24875](https://github.com/paradigmxyz/reth/pull/24875)) did not resolve the wedge, and in some cases, reproduced the issue more reliably. The problem appears to stem from a cyclic deadlock in reth's `StateTrieOverlayManager` / `DeferredTrieData` machinery, specifically due to locks being held across `rayon` computes. Two distinct sites for this anti-pattern have been identified, and two patches (eager and lazy + sequential sort) have been developed and are being tested. Initial soak tests with these patches show promising results, with continuous CL-sync for several hours without wedges. The issue is being flagged for the reth team as it involves reth's `chain-state` code.

### op-supervisor/op-supernode cross-safe cannot drain a backlog faster than real-time after a stall [#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21117)
The latest comment suggests that the observed 12.06-hour window for the cross-safe head to catch up after a multi-hour interruption is consistent with sequencer window expiry behavior. If the local safe head did indeed catch up to unsafe, and the sequencer window expired, manual intervention would be required. The current understanding is that if this behavior is expected, no further action is needed on this ticket.
