# ethereum-optimism/specs Daily Update (Jun 10, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on organizing specifications, specifically moving interop specs out of "experimental" and into "lagoon" to clarify their activation alongside other features. Several issues related to Interop NUT bundle activation were also closed.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Specification Reorganization
The interop specifications have been moved from the "experimental" directory to "lagoon" within the protocol specs. This change clarifies that the interop hardfork will activate with other features like SDM. ([#917](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/917))

### SDM: Review Ecosystem Integration
Discussion continues regarding the ecosystem integration for the new SDM transaction type (0x7d). A key point raised is the need to work with ecosystem teams (e.g., Blockscout, Viem) to ensure proper visualization and processing of this new transaction type, and to communicate that `gasUsed` semantics will differ unless `opGasRefund` is also considered.
