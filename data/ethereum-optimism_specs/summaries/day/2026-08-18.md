## CLOSED ISSUES

- **Kona-node L1 Chain Config Handling**: Issue [#22491](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22491), concerning `kona-node`'s acceptance of base64-encoded custom L1 chain configurations, was closed. The resolution indicates that inline base64 handling should be managed at the deployment layer, with the chart decoding the value into a mounted file.

- **Subblocks Spec Integration**: A new issue, [#929](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/929), was created to replace the existing flashblocks spec with the subblocks spec, focusing on public-facing aspects as detailed in the Optimism documentation.

## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **SDM Performance Benchmarking**: Issue [#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20176) was closed today after extensive performance benchmarking. The latest comments from einar-oplabs detail the final conclusions, indicating no measurable CPU or RSS impact from SDM at tested loads. Tracing on refund blocks was confirmed to work end-to-end in production, and A/B testing confirmed earlier findings regarding gas accounting. A comprehensive SOP run entry for the 2026-08-17 benchmark was provided, including write-up, config/results matrix, and artifacts.

- **Flake: TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement**: Issue [#22234](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22234) remains active with significant discussion. The latest comment from einar-oplabs consolidates the issue/PR chain, outlining the history of the flake report, prior de-flake attempts, and the current fix for signature A ([#22321](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22321)). It also highlights signature B ([#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22325)) as a separate investigation and points to [#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21964) as the live owner of the oscillation causing the race window. A correction was made regarding the misattribution of the oscillation behavior to a previously fixed issue. The analysis confirms the flake is a real race in the test's final assertions, not an environmental failure.
