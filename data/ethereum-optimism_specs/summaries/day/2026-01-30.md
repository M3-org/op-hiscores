## ACTIVE ISSUES
### op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/17631))
Discussion continued regarding the behavior of `op-node` marking a reached target as finalized after initial EL Sync, even if the block is not actually finalized. A comment from `trianglesphere` clarified that this was the intended design behavior, though it could be considered a bug now. The discussion also touched upon the implications for re-running derivation on snap-synced nodes.

### chore(kona/proofs): remove outdated just recipes once kona's migration is complete ([#18654](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18654))
The issue was reopened by `ajsutton` due to a remaining TODO in the code, indicating that the removal of outdated `just` recipes for building `kona-host/client` is not yet complete, despite a related PR being merged.

### op-reth: batcher throttling ([#18764](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18764))
The conversation focused on the implementation of batcher throttling in `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder`. `einar-oplabs` clarified that the throttling mechanism is controlled via JSON-RPC, not a CLI interface, and provided an update on the progress of throttle logic, tests, and RPC implementation for both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder`.

### op-supernode: Safe Label Adjusting ([#18976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18976))
Extensive discussion took place on how to adjust the "Safe" label in `op-supernode` to better reflect the verified superchain state. `axelKingsley` praised the proposed diagram and terminology, highlighting the distinction between "Local Safe" and "Super Node" safety. `geoknee` further elaborated on the challenges of reading data from CL and EL, proposing options to avoid deadlocks when the supernode controls the EL's safe and finalized heads. The discussion emphasized the need for clear identification of local vs. supernode-verified safety.
