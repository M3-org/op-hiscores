### op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/17631))

A comment from `trianglesphere` clarified that this was the intended design behavior, though it could be considered a bug now, and noted that re-running derivation on a snap-synced node doesn't work.

### op-reth: batcher throttling ([#18764](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18764))
The discussion focused on the implementation of batcher throttling in `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder`. `einar-oplabs` clarified that the throttling mechanism is controlled via JSON-RPC, not a CLI interface, and provided an update on the progress of throttle logic, tests, and RPC implementation for both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder`.

### op-supernode: Safe Label Adjusting ([#18976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18976))
The conversation revolved around the proposed design for adjusting the "Safe" label in `op-supernode`. `axelKingsley` praised the provided diagram and terminology, expressing optimism about the design's potential. `geoknee` further elaborated on reading and writing data between the CL and EL, suggesting that the supernode should control the EL's Safe and Finalized labels directly and proposing options for handling the interop activity's reliance on the EL's safe block at a given timestamp to avoid deadlocks.
