## CLOSED ISSUES

### Interop Validation and Reorg Handling
Two issues related to interop validation and reorg support were closed. [#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19266) confirmed that Kona is correctly handling interop validation, specifically regarding the access of internally created blocks. [#20214](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20214) addressed the need for `op-conductor` to gracefully recover from protocol-caused chain regressions, preventing chain stalls.

The issue [#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/905) concerning Interop Specs Updates was closed, indicating that the necessary updates to the latest semantics of the spec, including the removal of same-timestamp and simplification of replacement blocks, have been completed.

A flaky CI issue, [#19935](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19935), related to `rust-tests` experiencing Out Of Memory (OOM) errors during `reth-optimism-node` compilation, was closed. However, a new occurrence of this flake was reported, suggesting further investigation or mitigation is needed.

## NEW ISSUES

### EL Restart Syncing Recovery
A new issue, [#20307](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20307), was opened to add a real regression guard for EL restart SYNCING recovery in `op-acceptance-tests`. This aims to prevent stalls when an EL returns `SYNCING` after an `op-reth` restart.
