## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona Node Service: EngineActor becomes ChainController ([#22527](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22527))
Discussions focused on the renaming of `EngineActor` to `ChainController` and its extended responsibilities. Key points from the latest comments include:
- The read-only query handler for "atomic local-safe-at-timestamp + L1-at-safe snapshot" was opened as #22597.
- Clarification that the single read for `EngineQueries::handle` was already available, and #22596 made it meaningful by including the L1 origin in the state.
- The Go side's `ChainContainer.OptimisticAt` still has a structural gap, performing multiple reads that can lead to inconsistent views.
- The handler for timestamp queries should provide four distinct verdicts (ahead, head's timestamp, behind, below L2 genesis) to avoid collapsing different scenarios into a single "absent" answer.
- An incidental fix was made to `engine.mdx` to update `InsertTask` snippet to reflect the origin instead of `is_payload_local_safe`.
- The `ChainController` cannot reliably form the L2→L1 pairing due to queue order, potential partial drains, and the existence of writers that do not hold an L1 key. The conclusion is that the write hook for this pairing belongs in `kona-engine`'s sync state.

### Kona Node Service: shared single-chain composition entry point ([#22530](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22530))
Notes from the implementation of this issue (#22598) were shared:
- The seam was identified as composition vs. running, leading to a split into `compose` and `run_actors` for reusability.
- `spawn_and_wait!` was refactored from a macro to allow for a `Vec<BoxedNodeActor>` to hold actors of unrelated types.
- The L1 watcher is deliberately not part of a chain's composition, returning `L1WatcherPorts` for the host to decide how many watchers to attach.
- `RollupNode` remains the per-chain unit to avoid duplicating configuration fields.
- Spawn order changed but should not matter.
- A sequencing note for #22532 highlights that it will be the first merge point for parallel branches.
- A gap was identified: a dying actor cannot say which chain it belonged to, as `NodeActor::Error` is only `Debug`. This is left for #22532.

### Lokahi-interop: stores (verifiedDB + WAL, logsDBs, invalidated-output archive) ([#22535](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22535))
This issue was landed as **PR #22603**. Key points from the discussion:
- Two follow-ups were deliberately left out of the PR to avoid a second base-merge round: preallocation from an on-disk count before validation in `decode_outputs`, and reporting an unrecognised WAL decision tag as data corruption instead of a downgrade.
- The PR implemented verified store, log store (one per chain), and invalidated-output archive.
- Decisions were made regarding checksum computation (living in `lokahi-interop` for now), different chain-id types (u64 for internal, U256 for external references), binary encoding, WAL decision tags, and dropping the deny-list filter half.
- The "O(1) `Contains`" property is not fully replicated due to RocksDB's behavior.
- A decision from @ajsutton confirmed that superroots must be returnable over the full fault dispute window, with an option for unbounded retention, leading to the use of RocksDB for stores and no pruning initially.

### Lokahi-interop: verification round loop ([#22536](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22536))
This issue was landed as **#22605**. Key points from the discussion:
- The verification round loop is now implemented, observing chains and L1, deciding from a snapshot, writing to WAL, and applying.
- `Invalidate` and `Rewind` do not write to WAL, instead holding the verified frontier.
- `step` returns a pace and does not sleep, keeping tests deterministic.
- The four verdicts of `local_safe_snapshot_at` are carried through, not flattened.
- A crash during the very first advance would have re-run cold start with a WAL slot pending; `Verifier::new` now treats a slot as a resume.
- Kona's `ExecutingMessage` parser is wider than Go's; the port narrows to Go's behavior.
- The published `current_l1` and activation-timestamp override are deferred.
- A decision from @ajsutton confirmed that the activation-timestamp override is deferred for the initial release.
- The activation source divergence between Kona and op-supernode was discussed, with the conclusion that for the initial release, activation is uniform across the cluster and block-aligned.
