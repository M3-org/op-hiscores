# ethereum-optimism/specs Daily Update (Jun 23, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's activity focused on the initial stages of integrating PCD (Proof-Carrying Data) specifications into the Optimism ecosystem, with two new pull requests opened to introduce these docs. Additionally, two significant issues related to SDM test hardening and PCD infrastructure design were closed.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

## NEWLY OPENED PULL REQUESTS
- Two new pull requests were opened today: [#921](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/921) introduces PCD specifications, and [#920](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/920) adds PCD documentation. Both are in the initial stages of review.

## CLOSED ISSUES

### SDM Test Hardening
- The issue [#21324](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21324) regarding SDM (State-Derived Metrics) test hardening has been closed. Key areas covered include ETH supply conservation, the PostExecSettlementUnderflow path, and handling of 0x7D mid-block/out-of-order index payloads.

### PCD Infrastructure Design
- The design document for PCD (Proof-Carrying Data) infrastructure support, tracked in [#21327](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21327), has been approved and the issue closed.

## NEW ISSUES

### Reth Slot-Preimage Seeding
- A new feature request, [#21523](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21523), proposes reusing upstream slot-preimage seeding helpers in `op-reth` to remove duplication and improve efficiency. This is anticipated to be implemented with the next reth release.

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Kona-Node: SafeDB
- In [#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20278), `einar-oplabs` has started implementing `SafeDBv1` based on the design document, with a branch available for review. The goal is to establish a baseline before experimenting with `SafeDBv2` for interface and implementation improvements. `geoknee` previously emphasized the need for `SafeDB` to work well with interop and proofs, while also considering better designs than a direct adoption from `op-node`.

### Optimism Sepolia Intrinsic Gas Too High
- Issue [#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21457) reports that `eth_estimateGas` returns an "intrinsic gas too high" error on Optimism Sepolia after the Karst hard fork for a specific contract call. `yuercl` confirmed encountering this problem, noting that `staticCall` succeeds and sending with an explicit `gasLimit` also works on-chain. `geoknee` previously acknowledged this as a known issue that will be fixed in the next release.
