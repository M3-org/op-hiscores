### op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness
Discussion on [#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/17631) continues to revolve around the behavior of op-node marking the initial EL Sync target as finalized, even if the block is not truly finalized.
- **trianglesphere** clarified that while making Snap Sync follow the finalized L2 as a maximum target could work with op-reth in EL sync, it's not feasible for op-geth in snap sync due to how state snapshots are stored.
- **axelKingsley** emphasized that EL sync should only treat content as finalized if it is actually finalized, and any Snap Sync targets after finalization are subject to being incorrect. They suggested Snap Sync should follow the finalized L2 as a maximum target.
- **sebastianst** agreed that the current behavior is buggy and proposed running derivation from a prior L1 block and then consolidation to verify alignment with the EL-synced unsafe chain. They also suggested fixing the code to only advance the unsafe ref while keeping safe and finalized at genesis, with a plan for the transition.
