## ACTIVE ISSUES

**op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness** [#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/17631)
Discussions continued regarding the `op-node`'s behavior during initial EL Sync, specifically concerning how it marks the reached target as finalized, potentially leading to a block being considered finalized even if it isn't. The conversation explored the limitations of snap sync with `op-geth` versus `op-reth` and the implications of treating EL-synced tips as derived results. Suggestions include making Snap Sync follow the finalized L2 as a maximum target and then resuming full sync. There's also a recognized bug where the code might incorrectly advance safe and finalized heads during initial EL sync.
