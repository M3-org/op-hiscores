### Flaky Test Investigation
The `TestInteropHappyTx` test continues to be flaky, with a new occurrence reported on August 5th. The failure involved the test setup failing to deploy `EventLogger` due to sequencer actions starving for an extended period, preventing transaction inclusion. This behavior is consistent with the driver event-loop starvation fingerprint identified in #21952. ([#21940](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21940))
