The day's activity focused on refining the Lagoon protocol by excluding post-execution transactions from data availability footprint accounting. Discussions continued on critical issues including SDM rollout, op-node sync failures, and integration test flakiness.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

- **Lagoon Protocol Refinement**
    - Excluded Lagoon post-execution transactions (`0x7D`) from Jovian Data Availability (DA) footprint accounting, specifying zero contribution to `daFootprint` and `blobGasUsed`. This change clarifies the late-construction tradeoff and the bounded, linear underestimation. ([#926](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/pull/926))

- **SDM Devnet Rollout and Benchmarking**
    - Discussions on [#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21273) and [#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20176) highlighted progress on the `sdm-net-0` Sepolia alphanet rollout, with terraform and L2 contract deployment completed. - **op-node Sync Issues with `execution-layer`**
    - On [#20974](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20974), a user reported that their op-node, when started with `--syncmode=execution-layer`, struggled to establish peers, experiencing numerous `Ecies(ECIESError(UnreadableStream))` and `TooManyPeers` disconnects. After 5 days, the node managed to establish two peers, with logs showing continuous errors and retries before successful peering. The user noted that `--syncmode=consensus-layer` was more successful but resulted in a lower reported block height.

- **Flaky `reth-optimism-node` Integration Test**
    - The discussion on [#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20973) regarding the `peers_negotiate_eth_69` test SIGSEGVing on teardown was paused. The team has instrumented CI to capture stack traces upon the next flake, hoping to identify the root cause. Previous extensive analysis refuted the initial hypothesis of "detached payload thread polls a torn-down runtime" and suggested other potential causes like unclosed MDBX environments, detached engine/persistence threads, or RocksDB background threads. The recommended next step is to capture a core dump/faulting stack in CI to definitively diagnose the issue.

- **High-level DSL for Metrics Usage in Tests**
    - Issue [#22207](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22207) proposes adding a high-level DSL for metrics usage in acceptance tests, following up on a discussion from a pull request. The `op-dispute-mon` acceptance tests were suggested as a good starting point for this implementation.
