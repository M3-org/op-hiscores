## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Proofs: Faster Withdrawals with ZK
Discussion on [#20774](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20774) revolves around increasing `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and reducing air gap constants to enable faster withdrawals with ZK, while maintaining a 7-day total for fault proofs. A key point of discussion is the "max history needed" for proofs. Inphi commented that the max history is technically unbounded but practically limited by the frequency of proposals (e.g., 1 day for 1-day withdrawals). They also noted that challenging does not require history as the withdrawal root in isthmus allows reconstruction of the correct output root.

### Centralizing OP Fork to L1 Fork Mapping
Issue [#21338](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21338) addresses the duplication of the OP fork to implied L1 (Ethereum) fork mapping across the Rust stack. Sebastianst provided a detailed analysis, identifying seven instances of this duplication and even uncovering a latent bug where `Karst → Osaka` was missing in one mapping. A comprehensive plan was outlined to centralize this mapping in `alloy-op-hardforks` as a canonical table, with derived views for different use cases. The plan also includes rewiring direct consumers, eliminating OP-fork knowledge from `kona-engine`, and implementing consistency tests for `op-revm` instead of direct dependency. Alternatives like runtime dependency or codegen were considered and rejected.
