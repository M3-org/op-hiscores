*   **Receipt Root Mismatch Investigation** ([#19930](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19930))
    *   Discussion centered on a `receipt root mismatch` error encountered during syncing older chains with hard forks.
    *   It was noted that the issue is likely rooted in `op-reth/crates/consensus/src/proof.rs`, where the receipt trie was incorrectly built with `encode_2718` for pre-Canyon op-geth rules.
    *   A secondary issue was identified in `validation/mod.rs` where `receipt_root_bloom` was unconditionally trusted for consensus validation.
    *   The proposed fix involves correcting trie encoding with Canyon-awareness and gating the `receipt_root_bloom` shortcut for block validation.
    *   The problem was replicated on other op-stack rollups, suggesting a broader upstream issue.
