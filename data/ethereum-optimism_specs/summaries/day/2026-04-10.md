## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **Receipt Root Mismatch in Older Chains** ([#19930](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19930))
    *   Discussion centered on a critical `receipt root mismatch` error encountered when syncing older chains with hard forks.
    *   The issue appears to stem from `op-reth/crates/consensus/src/proof.rs` where the receipt trie was incorrectly built with `encode_2718` for pre-Canyon op-geth rules, leading to the inclusion of `deposit_nonce` when it should have been omitted.
    *   A secondary problem was identified in `validation/mod.rs`, where the `receipt_root_bloom` was unconditionally trusted for consensus validation.
    *   The proposed fix involves correcting the trie encoding with Canyon-awareness in `proof.rs` and gating the `receipt_root_bloom` shortcut to prevent its use in block validation.
    *   The problem was replicated on other op-stack rollups, confirming it's not isolated.

*   **Go Dependency Cleanup** ([#19996](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19996))
