### `op-node` Synchronization Issues
Discussion continues on an issue where `op-node` gets stuck in EL sync mode after a restart when Reth has already synced data [#18468](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18468). A team member is actively investigating the problem, and a temporary workaround involving changing the sync mode to CL sync was previously suggested. The issue has been observed to block Base Mainnet nodes.

### CrossL2Inbox Gas Introspection
A comment was added to the issue regarding the removal of gas introspection from CrossL2Inbox [#19374](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19374). The comment clarifies that this change is not critical until Glamsterdam on L2, suggesting it does not need to be addressed for Glamsterdam Defense.
