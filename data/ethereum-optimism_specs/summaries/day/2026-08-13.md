## CLOSED ISSUES

### `op-batcher` Sequencing Window Expiry
The issue [#16036](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/16036), concerning `op-batcher`'s behavior during sequencing-window expiry with span batches, was closed as "wontfix." The decision was made because recovery mode with span batches is deemed too complex or impossible to support, and it's not a critical requirement. Recovery mode will continue to be supported only in singular-batch mode.

### `op-deployer` and Dispute Game Options
The issue [#21429](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21429), related to `op-deployer` changes and new OPCM options for ZK dispute games, was closed as completed.

### Fault Proofs on `sdm-net-0`
The issue [#22348](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/22348), regarding the restoration of permissionless fault proofs on `sdm-net-0`, was closed. The resolution involves using permissioned fault proofs instead, as they are simpler and already implemented, and the permission model is orthogonal to SDM execution.
