## ACTIVE ISSUES

### ZKDisputeGame: Handle address(0) bond credit claiming via monitoring or contract change ([#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19743))
The issue was closed, with the work now tracked in a separate Optimism repository. Discussions highlighted that leaving the bond in `delayedweth` (effectively soft-burning it) was considered acceptable due to the unlikelihood of the scenario and the expectation that honest challengers would prevent such situations. It was also noted that the burn acts as a disincentive for proposers.

### vm-runner: Support ZK execution ([#18325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18325))
Recent comments suggest that extracting relevant parts of the proposer might be a good approach to minimize new code. Earlier discussions indicated that actual ZK execution would be in Rust due to `sp1` dependencies, but the `vm-runner` could drive this executor.

### Implement address(0) bond-credit claiming fix ([#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21420))
This issue was closed, with the work being tracked in a separate Optimism repository. The decision was made to close it in favor of the external tracking issue, as explained in the comments of issue [#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19743).

### ZK: Decide what to use for proofs in CI and local development ([#21409](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21409))
The consensus is to use a mock prover for CI and local development. Proving on op-sepolia via the regular proposer fast-finality path could be considered later if production infrastructure for local proving is established. Proving in CI is deemed too expensive for minimal benefit.
