### Progress on `op-geth` Decoupling: Fee Calculations
Discussion on [#20261](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20261) (opgeth-decoupling: create op-core/fees) indicates that the `op-core/fees` component has been implemented in a separate PR (#21314). Key points from the latest comment include:
- **`TxRollupCostData` Correction**: It was clarified that `TxRollupCostData` must use `tx.MarshalBinary()` for accurate DA cost calculation, not `tx.Data()`, to avoid undercounting.
- **Pre-Fjord Cost Functions**: Pure-math legacy cost functions (`L1CostBedrock`, `NewL1CostFuncEcotone`) were ported, while state-reading dispatchers were deferred to a later phase.
- **Operator Cost Renaming**: `OperatorCost` was renamed to `OperatorCostIsthmus`, and a new `OperatorCostJovian` variant was added for clarity and consistency with fork names.
- **Jovian Operator-Fee Formula**: Issue #17817 was addressed by consistently using the Jovian operator-fee formula in `txinclude` for both estimates and actual-cost refunds, ensuring it works correctly on Jovian chains and over-reserves budget on pre-Jovian chains.
