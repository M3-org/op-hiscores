## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Flaky Test: `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes`** ([#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19804)): This flaky test was re-opened after a previous fix proved insufficient. The new root cause was identified as an issue with `freezeChains` not ensuring `LocalSafe == LocalUnsafe` before stopping batchers, leading to `startTimestamp` being less than the unsafe head time. A fix has been submitted in `ethereum-optimism/optimism#20580` to reorder `freezeChains` to drain `LocalSafe` up to `LocalUnsafe` before stopping batchers.

- **Interop + Batcher: Use Verification-Aware CurrentL1** ([#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19944)): This issue, concerning the batcher's reliance on `CurrentL1` and potential reverts when fetching from the Virtual Node, is currently queued behind more immediate sync issues on devnets. It is considered at risk for its notional end-of-month timeline. A related ticket is `https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/20134`.

- **[L2CM retro] Go vs. EVM and Gas Accounting issues** ([#20388](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20388)): Progress has been made on both items. A draft PR (`#20570`) adds documentation on "Gas Accounting in Tests" to address edge cases. Another draft PR (`#20572`) experimentally flips `isolate = true` globally in Foundry to assess its impact, with the goal of informing a decision on global vs. per-test configuration.

- **OPCM.migrate: Ensure SystemConfig is updated with new addresses** ([#20511](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20511)): A fix has been put up to ensure that `SystemConfig` is updated with new shared addresses during migration, specifically checking that `delayedWeth()` returns the correct shared address on chain B.

- **Add unsafe head executing message validation** ([#20553](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20553)): Ongoing work includes updating `proxyd` to point at the interop filter for querying `cross-unsafe` and limiting the head to `cross-unsafe`. Additionally, there's a plan to make failsafe optional by adding reorg support to the interop filter, which would involve dropping `cross-unsafe` back to safe, rewinding `logsdb`, and progressing on reorgs.
