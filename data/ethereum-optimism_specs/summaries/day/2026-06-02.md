## NEW ISSUES

### Interoperability Transaction Pool Issues
A new issue, [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21149), was opened to report that `op-reth`'s interop transaction pool does not resume after a failsafe is disabled, requiring a restart.

### Invalid Interop Message Recovery
A new issue, [#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21161), was created to address the need for a runbook to recover a chain after an invalid interop message triggers a reorg.

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `op-revm` Support
Discussion continued on [#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21069) regarding `op-revm` support. The latest comments indicate that the team aims to stay in sync with new `revm` releases and will update guides to more eagerly update dependencies. There's particular interest in REVM's latest release (v111) for its WIP support for the Amsterdam hardfork.

### `op-reth`: Interop Tx Pool Does Not Resume After Failsafe Disabled
On [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21149), the latest update clarifies that restarting `op-reth` alone was insufficient to restore interop after clearing the failsafe. It was discovered that `op-rbuilder` (the block builder) was holding the evicted state. The remediation now involves restarting both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` on the affected chain's sequencers. A failsafe-state gauge metric is proposed to make this state observable.

### Invalid Interop Message Recovery Runbook
On [#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21161), the discussion focused on the scope of the runbook. It was suggested that the runbook should cover issues such as [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21149), [#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20006), and [#21125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21125), in addition to ensuring the failsafe is disabled again.
