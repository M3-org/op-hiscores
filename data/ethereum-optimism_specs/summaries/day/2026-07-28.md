## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Post-Migration OPCM Upgrades
Discussions continued regarding how to handle post-migration OPCM upgrades for all chains within an interop set, particularly concerning shared components. The latest comments indicate a preference for "Option 1: Resolve the ProxyAdmin during upgrade," despite concerns about potential inconsistencies during the upgrade process. The alternative, "Option 2: Skip updating shared components if they don't have our ProxyAdmin," was deemed less favorable due to the risk of silently unapplied configurations. The consensus leans towards always upgrading shared components to avoid operational complexity and ensure consistency.
- [#21731](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21731) Make post-migration OPCM upgrades work for every chain in an interop set
