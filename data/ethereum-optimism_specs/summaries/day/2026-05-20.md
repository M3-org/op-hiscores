## ACTIVE ISSUES

**Flaky Test: `TestFollowL2_ReorgRecovery`**
*   The flaky `TestFollowL2_ReorgRecovery` test has resurfaced, with a new failure signature involving a sequencer RPC deadline rather than the previous `expected head to match: local-safe` polling exhaustion. This suggests the same underlying timing/sync issue manifesting differently. ([#19967](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19967))

**Flaky Test: `FuzzEncodeDecodeBlob`**
*   A flaky failure in `FuzzEncodeDecodeBlob` was identified. The root cause was found to be `Blob.FromData` formatting the entire payload into its error message, leading to large error strings that caused the fuzzing process to hang during minimization. A fix has been drafted to format `len(data)` instead of the full payload and to early-return on `ErrBlobInputTooLarge`. ([#20935](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20935))

**Flaky Test: `FuzzDetectNonBijectivity`**
*   A flaky failure in `FuzzDetectNonBijectivity` was reported. The root cause was identified as the fuzz target capturing `*rand.Rand` outside the `f.Fuzz` closure, leading to non-determinism during fuzzing and causing the engine to stall. A fix has been drafted to derive `byteToFlip`/`bitToFlip` from `sha256.Sum256(d)` to ensure determinism. ([#20936](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20936))

**`op-reth` Bedrock Bootstrap Incompatibility**
*   An issue regarding the documented minimal Bedrock bootstrap for `op-reth` being incompatible with storage v2 (hot/cold RocksDB) is being investigated. A user attempted to reproduce the issue and provided logs showing a successful `init-state` and node launch without panicking, indicating further investigation is needed to understand the specific conditions under which the panic occurs. ([#20821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20821))

**`[L2CM] DevFeatures` Removal PR for Auditor Review**
*   The merge of the PR to remove `DevFeatures` and make L2CM the default codepath is being delayed until all audit fixes are in. A comment was added suggesting to insert a comment in the code with a flag to track future "to-do" or "close" actions and to standardize this process for the Engineering Organization. ([#20084](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20084))
