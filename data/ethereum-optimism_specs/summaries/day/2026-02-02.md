## ACTIVE ISSUES
### op-supernode: Safe Label Adjusting
Discussions on [#18976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18976) focused on the control of the Execution Layer (EL) and the implications of different safe label representations. A key update suggests that the Consensus Layer (CL) should maintain the full set of pointers and remain the sole controller of the engine, except for interop resets. This approach aims to prevent conflicts arising from multiple stateful controllers and aligns with existing interop-aware data structures in the op-node engine controller. The potential downside is that the op-node would have some awareness of interop, breaking a desirable invariant.

### Flashblocks support within Geth
Comments on [#756](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/756) highlighted the existing `op-geth` version with Firehose Live Tracer and Flashblock(s) support, available at https://github.com/streamingfast/go-ethereum/tree/release/optimism-1.x-fh3.0. The community expressed interest in merging this functionality into the main `op-geth` to simplify operations and reduce disk space consumption compared to archive versions of reth.

### Feedback: `BlockNotFoundError` in Step 5 (Prove the withdrawal on L1)
The discussion on [#1956](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/1956) centered on the `BlockNotFoundError` encountered during the ETH bridge tutorial. A key point raised is the absence of output root validation in Viem's withdrawal guide and actions, unlike the Optimism SDK. This lack of validation can lead to issues when a withdrawal is initiated, and the `waitToProve` action is executed before the state root is published to L1.
