## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `op-supernode` Safe Label Adjusting Discussion
Discussion on [#18976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/18976) focused on the control of the Execution Layer (EL) and Consensus Layer (CL) safe labels within the `op-supernode`. A key update from `geoknee` suggests that the CL should maintain the full set of pointers and remain the sole controller of the engine, with the supernode controlling the EL's safe and finalized states directly. This approach aims to address potential synchronization issues if multiple stateful controllers interact with the same engine. `axelKingsley` praised the detailed diagram and terminology, noting that if the CL believes local safe payloads are applying correctly, it should maintain the SafeDB as a "Local Safe" representation. The conversation also touched upon the need for RPC router considerations for virtual nodes to distinguish between "Local Safe" and "Super Node" safe states.

### Flashblocks Support within Geth
The discussion on [#756](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/756) highlighted the existing `op-geth` version with Firehose Live Tracer and Flashblock(s) support, as shared by `maoueh`. `sunjiahui` expressed enthusiasm for merging this functionality into `op-geth`, citing the benefits over running archive versions of Reth, which consume significant disk space and are less straightforward to operate than Geth.

### Feedback: `BlockNotFoundError` in Step 5 (Prove the withdrawal on L1)
On [#1956](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/1956), `opfocus` noted that while the Optimism SDK includes a validation step for the output root, Viem's withdrawal guide and actions do not appear to perform equivalent validation. This lack of validation could contribute to the `BlockNotFoundError` experienced during the withdrawal proving step. The previous comments from `opfocus` also detailed a temporary workaround involving manually retrieving and overriding output root data from Etherscan to resolve the issue when the state root has not yet been published to L1.
