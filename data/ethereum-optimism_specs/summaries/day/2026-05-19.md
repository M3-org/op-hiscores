## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Brotli Channel Divergence ([#19333](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/19333))
The issue, now closed, saw extensive discussion regarding the divergence between Kona and op-node for brotli-compressed channels. A detailed analysis by `sebastianst` on May 7th, 2026, identified a "kona wrapper bug" as the dominant cause, eliminating 95% of divergences with a 4-line fix. Further investigation on May 8th revealed that the Rust `brotli` crate used by Kona was not RFC 7932 compliant on corrupted inputs, silently accepting them and producing incorrect output. The issue was closed on May 19th by `sebastianst`, confirming that all concerns were fixed, with follow-up work and an upstream brotli fix in progress.

### Flaky Test: `TestChallengerCountersPreGenesisGame` ([#20869](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20869))
`maurelian` provided a detailed root cause analysis and proposed fix on May 19th, 2026. The flake is due to a timing issue where `plannedRollupStartBlockNumber` is computed from a one-shot L1 head read, and on some CI runs, the migration process consumes the entire allocated budget, causing the transaction to land in the same block as the `plannedRollupStartBlockNumber`, tripping a strict-less-than assertion. The proposed fix involves increasing `preGenesisRollupStartBlockDelay` from 6 to 20 and adding a post-migration L1-head re-read with a fast-fail invariant.
