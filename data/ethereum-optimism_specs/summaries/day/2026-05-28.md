## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **CL Sync Recovery Stalling in `op-supernode`/`op-reth`:** Issue [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21076) details a critical problem where CL sync recovery can stall if `SafeDB`/bookkeeping references an EL-missing block. Extensive discussions by karlfloersch and wwared highlight a complex interaction between `op-node` and `op-reth`. Observations from SN3 testing indicate that `latest/unsafe` can advance, but `safe/finalized` remain stuck due to FCU timeouts and `op-supernode` refusing to apply a stale super-authority head. The core problem appears to be a combination of `op-node` advancing `localSafeHead` without proper reconciliation after FCU timeouts and `op-reth`'s engine actor stalling between `CanonicalBlockAdded` and `CanonicalChainCommitted` events. Potential causes for the `op-reth` stall include ExEx backpressure. Further debugging with detailed logging and `tokio-console` is suggested to pinpoint the exact mechanism.

- **`op-revm` Support and Synchronization:** Issue [#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/21069) discusses the ongoing support for `op-revm` following its transfer to the `ethereum-optimism` organization. sebastianst clarified that the team aims to stay in sync with new `revm` releases and periodically updates `reth` dependencies, which often includes shared dependencies like `revm` and `alloy` crates. The discussion also noted that `cargo` already resolves `alloy` crates to those specified by `reth`, and updates to the manifest will reflect the versions in use.

- **Proof System Constants for Faster Withdrawals:** Issue [#20774](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/specs/issues/20774) addresses the need to increase `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and reduce air gap constants in preparation for faster withdrawals with ZK proofs, while maintaining a 7-day total. wwared commented on the importance of running numbers to assess the effects of these changes on the maximum history needed.
