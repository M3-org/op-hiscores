### Persistent Flakiness in `TestPreserveDatabaseOnCLResync`
The issue [#19661](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/19661) continues to track flaky tests related to database preservation and truncation during CL/EL resync. While `TestPreserveDatabaseOnCLResync` and `TestCLUnsafeNotRewoundOnInvalidDuringELSync` appear fixed with 113 consecutive passes, `TestTruncateDatabaseOnELResync` still shows recent failures, requiring further monitoring.

### `TestCLELDivergence` Flakiness Investigation
Discussion on [#19681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/19681) regarding the flaky `TestCLELDivergence` test indicates a potential mitigation with PR [#19733](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/19733), but it needs time to confirm. Earlier investigation revealed that the test's expectation of CL-EL divergence persistence might be incorrect if the derivation pipeline resetting the unsafe head is expected behavior. The current assessment suggests that the observed reset is part of the initial derivation pipeline lifecycle and not necessarily a bug during EL sync, challenging the test's original assumptions.

### Interop Phase 3: Removing Chains From Shared Dispute Games
Issue [#19692](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/19692) discusses the ability to remove a chain from a shared dispute game. Clarification was provided that this refers to supporting the removal of chains from the interop set within the proofs system, not solving all interop-related problems.

### Monitoring for ZK Games in `dispute-mon`
Issue [#18424](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/18424) highlights a specific monitoring detail for ZK games: when `ZKDisputeGame` resolves with `CHALLENGER_WINS` and no challenger exists, bond credit is assigned to `address(0)`. This can lead to ETH being stranded in DelayedWETH as `claimCredit(address(0))` is not called. `dispute-mon` will likely need to detect this scenario and automate the claim and withdrawal process, as tracked in [#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/19743).

### `op-reth` Interop Transaction Pool Features
Discussion on [#19411](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/19411) focuses on implementing interop-related transaction pool features in `op-reth`, specifically the interop ingress filter and reorg eviction, which are currently present in `op-geth`. An upstream reth issue ([https://github.com/paradigmxyz/reth/issues/23205](https://github.com/paradigmxyz/reth/issues/23205)) has been identified to enable supernode's rewind operations to work correctly under `op-reth`.
