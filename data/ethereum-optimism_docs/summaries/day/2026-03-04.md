### op-node: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/17631))
Discussions continued regarding the `op-node`'s behavior of marking the reached target as finalized after initial EL Sync, even if not truly finalized. Karlfloersch raised questions about whether this bug is specific to light CL/interop or also present without it, how it's currently mitigated, and why light CL might exacerbate it. Earlier comments from trianglesphere and axelKingsley debated the feasibility of using Snap Sync with finalized L2 as a maximum target and the implications for `op-reth` versus `op-geth`. Sebastianst agreed on the buggy behavior and suggested running derivation from a prior L1 block with consolidation.

### Investigate Glamsterdam gas pricing impacts on precompile acceleration ([#19375](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/19375))
Discussions focused on the potential impact of Glamsterdam's increased gas costs for precompiles on fault proofs. Ajsutton provided links to internal documentation and noted that `requiredGas` will need to be bumped to match L1 costs, but it's not an issue if it differs from L2 gas cost. The only affected precompile, `bn256Pairing`, has a minor variable cost increase. Call data costs, especially with Glamsterdam's increases, also need consideration.
