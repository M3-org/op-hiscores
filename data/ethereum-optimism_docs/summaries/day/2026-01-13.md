### Simplification of Kona Proofs CI
Discussion on [#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/18773) revolves around simplifying Kona proof's CI by leveraging information from Kona's monorepo. The latest comments highlight the dual use of `kona/version.json` for both acceptance tests and the `op-challenger` docker image, raising questions about how `kona-host` releases should be managed in relation to `op-challenger` to avoid confusion. The ideal scenario suggests building `kona-host` from the same commit as `op-challenger`.
