## ACTIVE ISSUES
- **Optimizing Safe-Head Advancement FCU Messages**
  Discussion on [#15393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/15393) continues regarding the problem of excessive FCU messages when the `batcher` runs with a considerable `--max-channel-duration`. The latest comment from 0x416e746f6e on Jan 12, 2026, indicates that a previous attempt to fix this issue by replacing `e.needFCUCall` was likely misapplied, and the system still performs an FCU on every safe head update instead of buffering them. This follows earlier discussions about the impact of FCU spam on execution layer performance, especially with non-local ELs, and potential solutions like debouncing FCU calls or more eagerly disabling the transaction pool.
