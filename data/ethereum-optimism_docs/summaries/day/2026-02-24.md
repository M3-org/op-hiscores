Discussion on [#18507](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/18507) highlights a bug where `op-batcher` fails to account for post-Holocene ordering in concurrent mode. ### op-supernode: Superroot SLA Updates
The discussion on [#19180](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/19180) revolves around the ill-defined and currently suboptimal SLA of the Superroot object. The latest comment from axelKingsley suggests that Optimistic Roots should be a best-attempt effort, with any unobtainable roots either excluded or represented by empty values.
