## CLOSED ISSUES

### Flashblocks and Rollup-Boost Acceptance Tests Closed
Several acceptance tests related to flashblocks, sequencer failover, and rollup-boost functionality were closed. These issues, including tests for kill-switch activation, sequencer failover when rbuilder fails, HA failover, rbuilder failure scenarios, and sequencer failover when all rollup-boost instances are unhealthy, were closed as they would require infrastructure manipulation, which was scoped out of op-acceptance-tests. [#316](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/316), [#314](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/314), [#318](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/318), [#317](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/317), [#350](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/350)
