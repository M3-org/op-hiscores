# ethereum-optimism/docs Monthly Report (February 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
February focused on operational transparency and infrastructure optimization for the Optimism documentation repository. The month began with a strong emphasis on performance tracking through the initiation of comprehensive metrics reporting for the previous month. Additionally, community discussions highlighted a strategic interest in refining the Superchain's execution layer support, specifically regarding Flashblocks integration to alleviate operational overhead for node operators.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While the initial reports for the month did not detail completed pull requests, the technical focus remained on infrastructure planning and performance monitoring.

### Performance Monitoring & Metrics
The team prioritized data-driven insights by initiating the generation of January 2026 metrics. This work aims to track key performance indicators (KPIs) such as time to first response, time to close, and time to answer for both issues and pull requests. This effort ensures the maintainer team can identify bottlenecks in the documentation workflow and improve the contributor experience.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The month's issue activity centered on administrative reporting and technical enhancements for the OP Stack's execution clients.

*   **New Issues:** Two primary issues were opened to facilitate monthly reporting: [#1967](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/1967) for tracking closed issues and [#1968](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/1968) for tracking opened pull requests.
*   **Active Discussions:** Significant attention was directed toward Flashblocks support in Geth ([#756](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/docs/issues/756)). The community is actively advocating for the integration of Flashblocks into `op-geth`. This discussion is driven by a need for more resource-efficient alternatives to current implementations, specifically citing the high disk space consumption and operational complexity associated with other clients like `reth`.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was characterized by a proactive feedback loop between users and maintainers regarding node operations. The discussion surrounding `op-geth` and Flashblocks demonstrates a highly engaged community that is not only using the documentation but also providing critical feedback on the underlying infrastructure's usability. The emphasis on metrics reporting further signals a commitment from the maintainers to maintain a healthy, responsive environment for open-source contributors.
