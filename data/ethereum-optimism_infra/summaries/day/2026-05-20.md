    *   The test has resurfaced as flaky, with a different failure signature (sequencer RPC deadline exceeded) but the same underlying timing/sync issue. The issue was reopened after a previous fix attempt.
*   **Flaky test: FuzzEncodeDecodeBlob (op-service/eth)** [#20935](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20935)
    *   A root cause and fix have been identified and a draft PR created. The issue was due to `Blob.FromData` formatting an excessively large error string for `ErrBlobInputTooLarge`, causing the fuzzing process to hang. The fix involves formatting `len(data)` instead of the full payload and early-returning on `ErrBlobInputTooLarge`.
*   **Flaky test: FuzzDetectNonBijectivity (op-service/eth)** [#20936](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20936)
    *   A root cause and fix have been identified and a draft PR created. The flakiness was caused by the fuzz target capturing `*rand.Rand` outside the `f.Fuzz` closure, leading to non-deterministic behavior during minimization. The fix involves deriving `byteToFlip`/`bitToFlip` from `sha256.Sum256(d)` to ensure determinism.
*   **op-reth: minimal Bedrock bootstrap (init-state --without-ovm) incompatible with storage v2 — node panics on launch (unwind to block 0)** [#20821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20821)
    *   An attempt to reproduce the issue was made, and the provided logs show `op-reth` starting successfully with storage v2 enabled, indicating the issue might not be consistently reproducible or depends on specific environmental factors.
