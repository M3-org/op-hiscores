## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `op-core/superchain` Module Consumability
Discussions continued regarding the unconsumable nature of `op-core/superchain` by downstream Go modules due to `superchain-configs.zip` being gitignored. Key points from the latest comments include:
- **Git LFS is not an option**: `cmd/go` builds module zips from raw git data, and proxy.golang.org does not run lfs smudge, meaning consumers would get pointer files instead of the actual zip.
- **Import-graph measurement**: Analysis shows that 206 of 560 monorepo packages currently reach the bundle, with nearly half being accidental due to specific dependency edges.
- **Proposed cleanup**: Several PRs are incoming to split `depset`, delete dead code, and extend `depguard` tests to prevent new accidental dependencies, aiming to reduce the number of packages genuinely needing the bundle to around 109.
- **Cost of committing the zip**: Historical data from `op-geth` suggests that committing the zip would cost approximately 3 MB/year in `.git` growth, which is significantly less than previously estimated.
- **Loose files vs. zip**: While loose files would offer better reviewable diffs, the storage cost is barely distinguishable from a zip bundle.
- **Submodule limitations**: Embedding directly from the `superchain-registry` submodule is not viable as `//go:embed` cannot escape its package directory, and submodule content is absent from published modules.
[#22678](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22678)
