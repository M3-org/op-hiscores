# ethereum-optimism/infra Daily Update (Jan 21, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's focus was on improving the robustness of the `op-acceptor` by ensuring log persistence during unexpected terminations. Additionally, two long-standing issues related to `OpChainConfig` and `SystemConfig` overrides were closed.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Enhanced Log Persistence for `op-acceptor`
A critical bug fix was implemented to prevent data loss in `op-acceptor` logs when the process terminates unexpectedly (e.g., due to timeouts or SIGKILL). This ensures that buffered writes are flushed to disk, improving debugging capabilities. ([#540](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/540))
