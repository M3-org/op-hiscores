### Conditional Deployment of CrossL2Inbox in `kona-node` and `interop`
Discussion continued on [#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19311) regarding the conditional deployment of CrossL2Inbox. The latest comments confirm that the concern is not with `kona-node` itself, but rather ensuring that `kona-derive` and `kona-proof` do not generate spurious CrossL2Inbox deployments. It was noted that the core crates used by `kona-proof` and `kona-node` for post-interop derivation need to be adjusted to resolve this.
