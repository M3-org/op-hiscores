## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on enhancing CI infrastructure with parallel Apko image builds. Additionally, a critical issue regarding consensus-layer sync failures after an op-reth upgrade was identified, analyzed, and a fix proposed and validated.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

A new parallel Apko image build workflow was introduced, running alongside the existing Docker-based image workflow. This enhancement aims to optimize the CI process by adding Melange and Apko configurations for various images and publishing Apko images to a separate namespace to prevent conflicts. ### ZK Proving Spike Completed
The spike to generate a real super-root state-transition proof to baseline latency and cost has been completed, with an initial benchmarking report posted to Notion. ([#21411](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21411))

### Consensus-Layer Sync Failure Resolved
An issue where consensus-layer sync failed with "pruned history unavailable" after upgrading to op-reth v2.3.2 was identified, analyzed, and a fix was proposed and validated. The root cause was traced to an upstream reth dependency bump that introduced a block expiry guard, affecting `eth_getBlockByNumber` for genesis blocks. A proposed fix involves exempting genesis in reth's guard. ([#21584](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21584))

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestReorgUnsafeHead` ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20198))
The `TestReorgUnsafeHead` continues to exhibit flakiness. The latest comment from @geoknee on June 26, 2026, highlights a specific failure where the test expected a reorg of the unsafe head that never occurred, leading to a timeout. The error message indicates that the expected and actual head hashes were identical, suggesting the reorg did not trigger as anticipated. This occurred while working on a separate branch, confirming it as an unrelated flake. Previous discussions by @wwared and @ajsutton in May 2026 and April 2026, respectively, also detailed similar symptoms, including the reorg not happening or issues with faucet funding during setup. A proposed fix in April 2026 by @ajsutton aimed to replace sleeps with a poll on `SyncStatus().UnsafeL2.Hash` to address stale data issues. However, the recurrence of the flake indicates the issue persists.

### `op-node`: Fix compatibility with EIP-4444 enabled L1 node ([#13570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/13570))
The issue regarding `op-node`'s compatibility with EIP-4444 enabled L1 nodes, specifically concerning "pruned history unavailable" when the Rollup L1 Genesis Blockhash is not accessible, received a new comment from @Creamers158 on June 26, 2026. The comment questions whether the fix applied to `op-reth v2.3.2` should also be extended to `opnode v1.19.1` for `CheckL2GenesisBlockHash`, providing a log snippet showing `op-node` failing to initialize due to an inability to determine the L2BlockRef of height 0, citing "pruned history unavailable." This suggests that the problem of accessing genesis block information from a pruned L1 history remains a concern for `op-node` in certain configurations.
