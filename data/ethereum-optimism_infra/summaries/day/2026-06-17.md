A bug in `proxyd` was addressed, tightening the interop access-list descriptor timestamp validation. ## ACTIVE ISSUES

### ZK Game Acceptance Tests and Local Proving
Discussions on [#18326](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18326) and [#21418](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21418) (which was identified as a duplicate of [#18326](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18326)) revolved around implementing local/mock proving for CI and debugging. ### Kona Host Game Input and Witness Generation Review
Issue [#18430](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18430) focuses on reviewing the Kona host game input and witness generation for soundness. A recent comment highlighted the necessity for `kona-host` data fetchers to retry indefinitely, similar to existing fetchers, to ensure sufficient reliability.

### SDM Opt-in Operator Flag Consistency
Issue [#21360](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21360) addresses improving the consistency of the SDM opt-in flag across `op-node`, `op-reth`, and `op-rbuilder`. The scope will be narrowed to `op-reth` and `op-node`. There's also a discussion about standardizing the naming of the "protocol active" gate, which currently has inconsistent names like `post_exec_opt_in`, `operator_opted_in`, `opt_in`, and `sdm_post_exec_opt_in`.
