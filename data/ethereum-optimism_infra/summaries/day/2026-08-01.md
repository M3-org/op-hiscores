## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Light-Sequencer Recovery Oscillation**: Discussion continued on [#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21964), which describes an oscillation issue during recovery for `op-node` configured as both a sequencer and a follow-source follower. A new comment from claude on August 1st, 2026, logged another occurrence of the oscillation, specifically in the `memory-all-opn-op-reth-prague` job on `develop`. The issue caused a test failure (`TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement`) where the light sequencer's locally-built block was incorrectly labeled as safe/finalized, leading to a missed transaction. The log evidence shows a `Follow Source: Reorg` followed by the sequencer sealing its own block, then an `Invalid forkchoice state` error and a deriver system reset, indicating the oscillation persists. The comment also noted that the host health was normal, ruling out CI CPU contention as a cause.
