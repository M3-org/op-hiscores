## CLOSED ISSUES

### Flaky Test Resolution
A long-standing flaky test, `TestWithdrawal_Cannon`, was investigated and a draft fix PR was created. The issue was traced to an `OptimismPortal_InvalidProofTimestamp()` error caused by `estimateGas` being evaluated against an L1 head that was too old, leading to insufficient budget for the prove transaction. The fix involves explicitly waiting for the L1 head time to be greater than the game creation time before retrying the prove transaction.
- [#19963](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19963)

### `op-supernode` `RequiredL1` Clarification
The issue regarding `op-supernode`'s `superroot_atTimestamp` reporting `RequiredL1` as cross-safe L1 instead of local-safe L1 was closed. After extensive testing and rewriting acceptance tests for deterministic sequencing, it was confirmed that `op-supernode` correctly reports the local-safe-derivation L1. The observed divergence was attributed to a test-setup artifact.
- [#20657](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20657)
