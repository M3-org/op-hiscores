A specific monitoring detail for ZK games was highlighted [#18424]: when a `ZKDisputeGame` resolves with `CHALLENGER_WINS` and no challenger exists, bond credit is assigned to `address(0)`. This can lead to stranded ETH in DelayedWETH, suggesting that `dispute-mon` will likely need to detect and automatically claim/withdraw such funds.
