### Supernode Reset Handling Discussions
Discussions continued on [#18740](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18740) regarding the handling of resets in op-supernode. The latest comments from axelKingsley and geoknee outline potential strategies. axelKingsley supports option #1 as an initial enablement, dismisses option #2 as unfeasible, and prefers option #3 for the long term. geoknee confirmed the current plan is to proceed with option #1 for now, with a revisit to other options later. geoknee also provided a detailed note explaining that the failure of an FCU to reset the unsafe head, when the target hash is canonical, aligns with specs, and outlined four potential options for addressing the issue, including hacking around FCU behavior, making supernode-driven resets similar to regular reorgs, adding a new engine API method, or modifying EL FCU behavior.
