### Simplifying Permissioned Dispute Game Handling
On [#21678](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21678), the discussion revolved around simplifying `op-challenger`'s handling of `SuperPermissionedDisputeGame` after the transition is complete. A key point raised was the need for the challenger to `close()` the game after finalization to update the anchor state registry, which is further tracked in [#21708](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21708). A suggestion was made to add `closeGame()` to the generic `IDisputeGame` interface.

### Advancing Anchor for Finalized SuperPermissionedDisputeGame Games
Related to the above, [#21708](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21708) discusses the need for `op-challenger` to advance the anchor for finalized `SuperPermissionedDisputeGame` games, as these games resolve to `DEFENDER_WINS` but lack a `closeGame()` method. A suggestion was made to add `closeGame()` to the generic `IDisputeGame` interface to address this.
