## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SDM: standing workload on sdm-net-0 ([#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22343))
The standing workload for the SDM devnet is now live and acceptance criteria have been met. The rollout involved merging k8s and devnets-private PRs. Verification shows that every block since ~1192040 carries rebates, with detailed anatomy provided for block 1192060. The next step is to verify the dashboard against this continuous rebate signal.

### SDM: rollout an alpha/beta devnet for SDM ([#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21273))
The SDM devnet rollout is complete, with `sdm-net-0` running since July 30th and Lagoon activated on August 7th. SDM is live and verified with rebates on-chain. Kona validators are consolidating PostExec-carrying blocks without issue. Fault proofs are now functioning for the first time after a fix to align `fp-permissionless` with deployed contracts. The operator opt-in is now armed at boot, making the devnet hands-free. Remaining work is organized into sub-issues, with standing workload (#22343) being the next priority.
