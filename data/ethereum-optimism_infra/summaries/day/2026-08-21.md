# ethereum-optimism/infra Daily Update (Aug 21, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused heavily on advancing the Lokahi multi-chain interop node, with significant progress on its core components, including persistent storage, the verification round loop, and RPC services. Key discussions also revolved around the Lokahi's integration into the devstack and addressing a flaky test.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Lokahi Interop Node Core Development
The Lokahi interop node saw substantial progress with the implementation of its persistent state, including the verified store, per-chain log stores, and the invalidated-output archive [#22603](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22603). The verification round loop, which observes chains, makes decisions, and applies them, was also landed [#22605](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22605).

### Lokahi RPC and Sequencer Integration
Work continued on integrating Lokahi with existing RPC services and enabling its sequencer mode. The `supernode_syncStatus` and `superroot_atTimestamp` RPCs were implemented, with careful consideration for differences from the Go op-supernode [#22615](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22615). Additionally, the sequencer mode was enabled for Lokahi, allowing per-chain sequencing with configurable depths and key management [#22618](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22618).

### Devstack Integration and Testing Improvements
Efforts were made to abstract the `SupernodeTestControl` in the devstack to accommodate out-of-process implementations like Lokahi [#22606](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22606). A flaky test, `TestSDMActivatesAtLagoonBoundary`, was identified and a fix was confirmed to address timing-dependent failures during sequencer startup [#22638](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/22638).

## NEW ISSUES

### Flaky Test Identification
A timing-dependent acceptance test failure was reported for `TestSDMActivatesAtLagoonBoundary`, where the sequencer started too early, causing the test to fail its pre-Lagoon assertion [#22630](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22630).

### Observations from Lokahi Mirroring
New observations were recorded from mirroring op-supernode's behavior in Lokahi, highlighting areas where the copied behavior might not align with original intentions. This includes a job name that incorrectly advertises a supernode it doesn't run, leading to false positives in CI [#22636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22636).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### [#22527] kona-node-service: EngineActor becomes ChainController
Discussion continued on renaming `EngineActor` to `ChainController` and extending its functionality. A key comment clarified that the read-only query handler was already available and that the atomicity of L2 block and L1 key pairing is crucial. It was noted that the Go side's `OptimisticAt` still has a structural gap in atomicity. The comment also detailed the four possible verdicts for timestamp queries and an incidental fix to `engine.mdx` documentation. The read query and SafeDB write are straightforward consumers once the pairing is in the sync state.

### [#22521] lokahi: N-chain scaffolding without interop (phase 2)
The status of Phase 2 items was updated, noting that #22531 (multi-chain L1 watcher) is done, and #22530 (shared single-chain composition entry point) is now open as #22598. It was highlighted that #22532 (lokahi N-chain composition and config) will be the first item to merge different development lines, and nothing in this phase demonstrates N>1 until #22532. The decision that chains are fixed at construction was reaffirmed, simplifying the composition API.

### [#22530] kona-node-service: shared single-chain composition entry point
Notes from the implementation of this issue (#22598) were shared. The key insight was that the seam is between composition and running, not just extracting a function. The `spawn_and_wait!` macro was replaced with a more flexible approach to handle N chains. The L1 watcher was deliberately kept separate from a chain's composition. It was also noted that a dying actor cannot currently specify which chain it belonged to, which will become an issue with multiple chains.

### [#22532] lokahi: N-chain service composition and config
The issue was landed as #22599, enabling `lokahi node --config <file>` to run N chains in one process. This involved merging #22567 and #22569 into the composition line of #22598, resolving disagreements regarding the L1 head watch and where a watcher is attached. A latent test bug was exposed and fixed. A loose end from #22530 regarding actor error reporting was addressed by labeling chain actors.

### [#22533] op-devstack: sysgo component and admin/test RPC for lokahi
The sysgo component and the first slice of the admin/test RPC were landed as #22602. The acceptance gate is a 2-chain smoke test in `op-acceptance-tests/tests/supernode/lokahi`. Two blocking issues were found and handled: `NetworkConfig::new` causing `GossipsubCreationFailed` and `lokahi` not being a workspace default member. Two gaps were identified: shared-supernode presets still turning lokahi away, and lokahi lacking a way to resolve a chain's unsafe block signer.

### [#22537] lokahi: cross-safe promotion lifecycle and finality
The issue was implemented in [#22610](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22610). The main points were that the gap was wiring, not logic, and promotions now route through Kona's external promoter entry point. `kona-safedb` gained a writer, and interop activation is all-or-nothing across the hosted set. A decision was deliberately left to the team regarding clamping internal actor writes. A suspected finality gap was found not to exist.

### [#22535] lokahi-interop: stores (verifiedDB + WAL, logsDBs, invalidated-output archive)
Two follow-ups were deliberately left out of [#22603](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22603) to avoid a second base-merge round. These include a preallocation issue in `decode_outputs` that could lead to excessive memory allocation with corrupt data, and an unrecognised WAL decision tag being reported as data corruption instead of a downgrade. Earlier discussions confirmed the use of RocksDB for stores and no custom segmented log.

### [#22536] lokahi-interop: verification round loop
The issue was landed as #22605. `lokahi-interop` now contains the verification round loop. Decisions made include `Invalidate` and `Rewind` not writing to the WAL, `step` returning a pace instead of sleeping, and the four verdicts of `local_safe_snapshot_at` being carried through. A crash during the first advance and a difference in Kona's `ExecutingMessage` parser compared to Go's were found and addressed. The activation-timestamp override was deferred.

### [#22544] lokahi: supernode_syncStatus and superroot_atTimestamp RPCs
The issue was implemented in [#22615](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22615). Three deliberate differences from Go op-supernode were highlighted: how `current_l1` is aggregated, the absence of an invalidated-output archive in Lokahi's optimistic branch, and how reorgs below a verified block are handled. A bug in Lokahi's own Go devstack wrapper was caught. Earlier discussions confirmed that `superroot_atTimestamp` must be answerable over the full fault-dispute window, making archive durability a gate for this RPC's parity claim.

### [#22545] lokahi: sequencer mode
The issue was implemented in [#22618](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22618). The sequencer mode is now derived from the presence of settings, not a separate flag. No new sequencing logic was added, but configuration and signer handling were implemented. Two confirmation depths were set. A key decision was made to generate a missing key to match op-supernode behavior, with a warning for differing keys. The `sequencer-stopped` config is accepted but not yet plumbed in the devstack.

### [#22546] op-devstack: abstract SupernodeTestControl and add lokahi to the acceptance matrix
The abstraction half of this issue is done, with #22606 making `stack.SupernodeTestControl` RPC-shaped and #22607 adding `RestartWithFreshDataDir`. However, the acceptance-matrix half is blocked on #22537, #22544, and #22545. The gate remains until Lokahi implements the necessary interop verifier, `QueryAPI`, and sequencer capabilities.

### [#22547] lokahi: Rust multi-chain interop node (tracking)
The devstack supernode test control is now implementable out of process via #22606. The `InteropActivity()` method was changed to `InteropTestAPI()`, exposing four methods for commands and reads. This change makes the harness RPC-shaped, but Lokahi still needs to serve these calls for the preset gate to be removed.

### [#22630] flaky test: `TestSDMActivatesAtLagoonBoundary`
The root cause of the flaky test was confirmed and a fix is available in #22638. The issue stemmed from the sequencer starting during setup and catching up L2 time to wall clock before the test's initial pre-Lagoon assertion, leading to block 15 being sealed at the Lagoon timestamp. The fix involves starting the future-Lagoon test sequencer paused and queuing transactions deterministically.
