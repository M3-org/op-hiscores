## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes` [#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19804)
The root cause of this flaky test was identified as `l1BlockWithLocalSafeBlocks()` polling the supernode's `RequiredL1` values, leading to race conditions due to sequencer block production. A fix has been submitted in #19805, which replaces supernode polling with direct L1 head reads at each batcher choreography stage.

### Flaky Test: `TestSuperCannonRootBeyondProposedBlock_ValidRoot/mt-cannon-next` [#19806](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19806)
This issue, reported as a flaky test, was determined to be a false positive. A comment indicated that the reporting tool mistakenly flagged a local test failure as a flaky test.

### Flaky Test: `TestFollowSource_HeadsDivergeThenConverge` [#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19821)
The root cause of this flaky test was identified: during `Supernode.Stop()`, `Interop.Stop()` cancels its context but doesn't wait for the `Start()` goroutine to exit, leading to panics when `SafeHeadAtL1()` is called on a closed SafeDB. A fix has been proposed in #19824, which adds `ErrClosed` guards to SafeDB read/write methods to convert panics into graceful error returns.
