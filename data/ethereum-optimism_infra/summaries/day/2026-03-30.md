## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Flaky Test: `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes`** [#19804](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19804)
  - The issue reports a flaky test `TestPreinteropFaultProofs_VariedBlockTimes` (and `_FasterChainB` variant) in `github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/op-acceptance-tests/tests/isthmus/preinterop`.
  - The root cause has been identified: `l1BlockWithLocalSafeBlocks()` polls the supernode's `RequiredL1` values, which can shift due to sequencer block production during batcher choreography.
  - A fix has been submitted in #19805, replacing supernode polling with direct L1 head reads at each batcher choreography stage to maintain invariants.
