# ethereum-optimism/infra Daily Update (Jun 5, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on enhancing `proxyd`'s consensus layer routing with output-root split protection and adding external peer support to the `peer-mgmt-service`. Significant progress was also made in addressing critical issues related to CL sync recovery and devstack configurations.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Enhanced Consensus Layer Routing and Peer Management
`proxyd` now includes output-root split protection for its consensus layer routing, ensuring more robust and reliable operation by comparing output roots across candidate backends [#646](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/646). Concurrently, the `peer-mgmt-service` has been updated to support external peers, allowing nodes to omit `rpc_address` and be reachable only from internal nodes [#647](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/647).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### CL Sync Recovery Stalls in op-supernode/op-reth
Discussion on [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21076) continues to focus on the `op-supernode/op-reth` CL sync recovery stalling. Recent updates confirm that a candidate fix from reth ([paradigmxyz/reth#24875](https://github.com/paradigmxyz/reth/pull/24875)) did not resolve the wedge, with all tested cold-start arms still wedging due to libmdbx read-transaction timeouts. The root cause has been identified as a cyclic deadlock in reth's `StateTrieOverlayManager` / `DeferredTrieData` machinery, specifically due to locks being held across blocking `rayon` computes. Two validated fixes, which remove the guard from across the compute, have shown success in testing.

### op-supervisor/op-supernode Cross-Safe Backlog
The discussion on [#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21117) revolves around `op-supervisor/op-supernode`'s inability to drain a backlog faster than real-time after a stall. The latest comment suggests that the observed 12-hour delay in cross-safe recovery might be expected behavior if the sequencer window expires, requiring manual intervention. If this is indeed the intended behavior, no further action may be needed on this ticket.
