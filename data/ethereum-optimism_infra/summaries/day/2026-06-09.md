# ethereum-optimism/infra Daily Update (Jun 9, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on enhancing `proxyd`'s robustness with fail-closed interop filtering and critical bug fixes, alongside a significant cleanup by removing the `op-acceptor` component.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Enhanced `proxyd` Interoperability and Reliability
The `proxyd` service received significant updates to improve its interoperability filtering and overall reliability. A new feature makes interop filtering fail-closed by default, with the safest `agreement` strategy now being the default for unset strategies ([#649](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/649)). Additionally, a critical race condition in `AvgSlidingWindow` access was fixed, ensuring proper synchronization during concurrent operations ([#653](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/653)).

### `proxyd` Terminology Refinement and Test Stability
Terminology within `proxyd` was refactored, renaming "supervisor" to "interop-filter" across code, metrics, and documentation for clarity and consistency ([#648](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/648)). A race condition in the `TestMulticall` assertion was also addressed, improving the stability and reliability of integration tests ([#652](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/652)).

### Removal of `op-acceptor`
The `op-acceptor` component, which was no longer used for running acceptance tests due to `op-devstack` dropping sysext support, has been entirely removed from the repository ([#651](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/651)). This streamlines the codebase and removes deprecated infrastructure.

## ACTIVE ISSUES
### Decoupling `op-geth` and Fee Calculation Refinements
Discussion on [#20261](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20261), "opgeth-decoupling: create op-core/fees (L1 cost + operator-fee math)", highlights the implementation details and decisions made during the creation of `op-core/fees`. Key points from the latest comment by sebastianst include:
- **`TxRollupCostData` must use `tx.MarshalBinary()`**: It was clarified that the DA cost should be computed over the full binary-encoded transaction, not just the calldata, to accurately reflect `op-geth`'s behavior.
- **Pre-Fjord cost functions**: While not used in production, pure-math legacy functions for L1 cost (Bedrock) and Ecotone were ported for e2e tests, deferring state-reading dispatchers to a later phase.
- **OperatorCost Renaming**: `OperatorCost` was renamed to `OperatorCostIsthmus`, and a new `OperatorCostJovian` was introduced to explicitly align with fork versions.
- **Inclusion of #17817**: A fix related to always using the Jovian operator-fee formula in `txinclude` was incorporated, ensuring consistent budget estimation.

### Deletion of `op-simulate` and `op-run-block`
Issue [#21282](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21282), "opgeth-decoupling: delete op-simulate and op-run-block", received a spam comment regarding an airdrop. The core goal of this issue remains to delete `op-chain-ops/cmd/op-simulate` and `op-chain-ops/cmd/op-run-block` as part of the `op-geth` decoupling effort.
