## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Flaky Test: TestRelayWithInvalidMessagesSteady**
  A flaky test, `TestRelayWithInvalidMessagesSteady`, which fails due to transient EL dial timeouts, was identified and a draft fix has been opened in a related repository. The root cause was confirmed to be the sampler goroutine's limited error handling, which did not catch `*net.OpError` timeouts. The fix involves allowing the sampler to warn and continue on non-context errors, aligning with existing test tolerance patterns ([#20254](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20254)).

- **Kona-Interop: Review Access of Internally Created Blocks**
  Discussion continued on an issue concerning Kona-proofs interop validation. A related issue in the kona-interop consolidation logic was identified. Specifically, when a block replacement occurs, the generated receipts are not stored, leading to consolidation failures if the deposit-only block is referenced by other messages. A test was confirmed to reproduce this behavior, demonstrating that replacement block receipts are accessed during consolidation even when the original block was invalid ([#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19266)).

- **op-node: Remove Req-Resp P2P Sync**
  This issue, aiming to remove the req-resp p2p sync due to observed sync problems, was closed in favor of a new issue in the `protocol-team` repository. Previous discussions highlighted the slowness and instability of req-resp, especially in cloud environments and with multiple nodes, and suggested an alternative centralized driver for gossiping blocks ([#14108](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/14108)).
