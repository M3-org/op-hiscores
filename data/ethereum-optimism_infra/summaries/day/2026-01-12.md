### `op-node`: Single Aggregate Safe-Head Advancement FCU Message Per Channel
Discussions on issue [#15393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/15393) continue to focus on optimizing the frequency of `FCU` (Fork Choice Update) messages in `op-node`. The latest comment from 0x416e746f6e on January 12, 2026, indicates that a previous attempt to buffer `FCU` calls might have been incorrectly applied, leading to the system still performing an `FCU` on every safe head update instead of buffering them. This suggests that the problem of frequent `FCU` calls, which can impact performance, particularly with higher network latency, persists. Earlier discussions highlighted the potential for "FCU spam" and explored solutions like debouncing `FCU` calls and conditionally disabling the transaction pool.
