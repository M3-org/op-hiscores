### Support for Multiple Sequencer RPC Endpoints
Discussion on [#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/763) revolves around supporting multiple sequencer RPC endpoints for `op-geth` with automatic failover. While the proposed change is backward compatible and aims to prevent outages caused by single endpoint failures, concerns were raised about the user experience of configuring multiple URLs. An alternative suggestion was to focus on building redundancy into a single URL, similar to standard web2 practices, rather than requiring users to manage multiple endpoints.
