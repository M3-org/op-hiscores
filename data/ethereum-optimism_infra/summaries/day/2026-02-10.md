### op-conductor: SEGFAULT during leader election
Discussion continues on the `op-conductor` SEGFAULT issue [#13363](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/13363). The latest comment confirms the problem is still present in the current version, specifically when sequencers are paused during migration from single to HA setups, causing snapshot attempts that lead to SEGFAULTs. Previous discussions highlighted potential causes related to invalid data in raft logs or snapshots, and the importance of proper cluster setup and unpaused conductor states.

### Support for Multiple Sequencer RPC Endpoints
The discussion around [#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/763) focuses on supporting multiple sequencer RPC endpoints for transaction forwarding. The latest comments debate the approach to achieving reliability. While the change is backward compatible and offers optional support for multiple comma-separated URLs to prevent outages due to single endpoint failures, concerns were raised about user-friendliness. An alternative suggestion is to leverage existing web2 reliability solutions for a single URL rather than requiring users to configure multiple endpoints.
