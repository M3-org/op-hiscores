### Kona Proofs and Recipes
Discussion continues on [#18654] regarding the removal of outdated `just` recipes for Kona. A recent comment from Inphi highlights the continued need for these recipes for developer convenience in building prestates during local testing, despite the monorepo now being used for prestate building.

### L2CM Upgrades Risk Modeling
On [#18869], maurelian provided templates for performing risk modeling (FMA or threat model) for the L2CM design, referencing existing FMA documents for OPCMv2.

### Kona Hint Serialization
Issue [#19125] discusses changing Kona hints from string serialization to a binary format. While kien-rise suggests this change for correctness and potential performance improvements, ajsutton raises concerns about backward compatibility requirements for the host program and questions the significant performance benefits, suggesting that the string encoding works well and simplifies debugging.

### Devfeatures Cyclic Dependency
On [#19151], maurelian suggested that `op-core` would be an appropriate location for shared OP protocol-specific code, such as `devfeatures`, to resolve a cyclic dependency issue. This would involve creating a new package `op-core/devfeatures` and shortening function names.
