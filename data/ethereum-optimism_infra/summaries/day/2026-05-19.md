# ethereum-optimism/infra Daily Update (May 19, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
The day saw significant progress in closing out several long-standing issues, particularly around interop hard fork implementation, RPC namespace refactoring, and brotli channel divergence. A new pull request was opened to fix a proxyd interop access-list namespace issue.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

- **Interop Hard Fork Implementation & RPC Refactoring:** The interop hard fork activation using the L2CM upgrade mechanism was completed for both `kona-node` and `op-node`, marking a significant step for the Interop project. Concurrently, the `supervisor` RPC namespace was successfully replaced with a dedicated interop filter namespace, improving clarity and removing a dependency on the soon-to-be-removed supervisor.
- **Brotli Channel Divergence Resolution:** Extensive investigation and fixes were implemented to address divergence issues between Kona and op-node concerning brotli-compressed channels. This included identifying and resolving a Kona wrapper bug and addressing an upstream brotli crate bug, ensuring consistent decompression behavior.
- **Fault Proof Witness Prefetching Clarification:** An investigation confirmed that `debug_executePayload` and related crates are essential for fault-proof witness prefetching, serving as an opt-in optimization with a canonical fallback. This clarifies its role and prevents its removal.

## NEWLY OPENED PULL REQUESTS
- A new pull request, [#623](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/623), was opened to address a fix for the `proxyd` interop access-list namespace.

## CLOSED ISSUES

- **Interop Hard Fork and RPC Namespace Finalization:** The implementation of the interop hard fork using L2CM in `kona-node` and `op-node` was completed and tracked under the Interop project, leading to the closure of [#20082](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20082). Additionally, the replacement of the `supervisor` RPC namespace with an interop filter namespace was finalized, closing [#20005](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20005).
- **Brotli Channel Divergence Resolved:** All identified issues and concerns regarding Kona and op-node disagreeing on brotli channels, including a Kona wrapper bug and an upstream brotli crate bug, have been fixed, resulting in the closure of [#19333](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19333).
- **Dispute Game Test Migration Completed:** The migration of remaining dispute game tests from `op-e2e/super_test.go` to `op-acceptance-tests` was completed, closing [#20814](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20814).
- **`debug_executePayload` Necessity Confirmed:** An investigation into the necessity of `debug_executePayload` and related crates concluded that they are required for fault-proof witness prefetching, leading to the closure of [#20879](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20879) as "wontfix."

## NEW ISSUES

In the interim, efforts are focused on establishing a local devnet using `bal-devnet-6` images.
- **Flaky Test: `TestChallengerCountersPreGenesisGame`** ([#20869](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20869)): Investigation into this flaky test is complete, and a proposed fix has been submitted in [#20874](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20874). The root cause was identified as a timing issue during migration, where the `plannedRollupStartBlockNumber` could collide with the receipt landing block due to varying CI load. The fix involves increasing the delay budget and adding a pre-submission fast-fail invariant.
