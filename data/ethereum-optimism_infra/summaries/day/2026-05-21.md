## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestFlashblocksStream` ([#19883](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19883))
The issue continues to be a source of flakes, with the latest comment from hdcesario-op on May 21, 2026, at 09:10:18Z, indicating a root cause related to port collision (`Address already in use`) in `rollup-boost`. A fix has been proposed in PR #20938, which aims to eliminate the pre-allocation of RPC ports. Previous comments highlighted different failure modes, including `rollup-boost` crashes, assertion failures in flashblocks stream, and sequencer stop failures due to context cancellation. The proposed fix is expected to resolve the `EADDRINUSE` issue.

### Flaky Test: `TestTruncateDatabaseOnELResync` ([#19897](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19897))
This test remains flaky, with maurelian providing a detailed analysis on May 21, 2026, at 15:24:36Z and 15:19:57Z. The new failure mode involves the verifier's `LocalSafe` head remaining at 0 after an EL database wipe/restart, leading to a `nodes not in sync` error. The root cause is identified as insufficient time for `op-geth` to snap-sync blocks under loaded CI conditions, similar to a previously fixed issue but occurring in a post-restart sync phase. A fix direction is recommended, suggesting the use of a progress-aware pattern like `MatchedWithProgressFn` to monitor `LocalUnsafe` progress and `LocalSafe` matching, with a longer max wait and a stall timeout. This issue is not related to PR #19847.

### Flake: `TestSequenceWindowExpired` — 'must send tx: already known' ([#20941](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20941))
This flaky test, where transactions fail with `txpool.ErrAlreadyKnown`, was closed on May 21, 2026, at 10:49:15Z. ajsutton on May 21, 2026, at 00:36:35Z, opened PR #20942 with a fix. The root cause was identified as Bob's nonce being derived from `eth_getTransactionCount(pending)`, which could lead to identical transaction hashes when the on-chain nonce remains at 0 and the txpool's `pendingNonces` table is not reliably advanced. The fix involves tracking Bob's nonce locally in the test to ensure unique nonces.
