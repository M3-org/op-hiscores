## ACTIVE ISSUES
### Flaky Test in `op-service/eth`
Discussion continued on [#21539](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21539) regarding the flaky `FuzzDetectNonBijectivity` test. A detailed comment from wwared clarified that the test's premise is flawed because the blob encoding/decoding functions in both `op-node` and `kona` silently mask out the top two bits of the first field element. This means that flipping these specific bits in the fuzz test does not result in a decoding error or a change in the decoded data, causing the test to fail. The comment highlighted that changing this behavior to return an error would require a hardfork due to potential backward compatibility issues with existing on-chain blobs. It was also noted that well-behaved batchers would not post malformed blobs, suggesting that the current behavior might not pose a practical problem. The core challenge is whether to fix the test harness or modify the encoding/decoding functions, with the latter having significant implications.
