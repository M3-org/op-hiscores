## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract` ([#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20376))
This flaky test has resurfaced with a different symptom but the same underlying race condition. The `WaitForProofsStoreBlock` function, which was added to gate proof fetches, is now timing out after 30 seconds, indicating that the proofs store is not indexing blocks in time. The validator's proofs ExEx store never advanced past genesis for the entire 30-second window, while the validator EL imported blocks normally. The sequencer's proofs indexing engine also showed no progress during the test, flushing blocks only during teardown. The issue is believed to be related to proofs-ExEx indexing latency under concurrent-devnet CI load rather than a correctness bug. Suggestions include raising the `require.Eventually` timeout and investigating why the proofs indexing worker makes no progress for extended periods.

### Default-`X` Benchmarking for L2 Genesis Deployment ([#20916](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20916))
This issue, concerning the benchmarking of the default genesis-time-offset (`X`) for L2 deployments, was reopened today. It was previously closed but still has two TODOs in `op-deployer` referencing it. These TODOs indicate that a reasonable minimum for `X` needs to be enforced and that the current value should be replaced with a benchmark based on the time it takes to perform a full L2 genesis deployment. The issue will continue to be reopened by the `todo-issue-checks` CI job until these TODO comments are updated or removed.
