## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract` [#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20376)
The flaky `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract` test has resurfaced with a different symptom but the same underlying race condition. The `WaitForProofsStoreBlock` gate, introduced to address previous failures, now times out after 30 seconds. The validator's proofs ExEx store never advanced past genesis, while the validator EL imported blocks normally. The sequencer's proofs indexing engine also showed no activity during the test, flushing all blocks in a single burst at teardown. This suggests the proofs indexing worker is being starved rather than broken. The issue is considered a flake, not a regression, as the PR diff is unrelated to ExEx indexing, and the job passed previously. The suggested direction is to increase the `require.Eventually` timeout in `WaitForProofsStoreBlock` and investigate why the proofs indexing worker makes no progress for extended periods.

### [PCD] Default-`X` benchmarking [#20916](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20916)
This issue was reopened because there are still TODOs in `op-deployer` referencing it, specifically regarding benchmarking the default genesis-time-offset (`X`). The TODOs indicate that a reasonable minimum for `X` will be enforced and that the current value should be replaced with a benchmark based on the time it takes to perform a full L2 genesis deployment. The issue will continue to be reopened by the `todo-issue-checks` CI job until these TODO comments are updated or removed.

### `chore(sysgo/p2p): fix op-reth <-> op-geth connection flakiness` [#18570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18570)
