Today's development focused on significant refactoring within the `proxyd` component, streamlining its consensus tracking interface and preparing for enhanced block hash tracking. ## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### `proxyd` Consensus Tracker Refactoring
The `proxyd` component saw a significant refactoring of its `ConsensusTracker` interface. This change replaced a multi-method interface with a simpler `GetState()`/`SetState()` approach, improving code clarity and maintainability. This work is part of a broader effort to enhance `proxyd`'s functionality. ([#588](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/588))
