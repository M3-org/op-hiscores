## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SDM Performance Benchmarking
Significant progress has been made on the SDM performance benchmarking ([#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20176)). Latest updates confirm that tracing on refund blocks now works end-to-end in production, with `debug_traceBlockByNumber` succeeding on sampled sequencer blocks, including those with a trailing 0x7d. Latency measurements show no measurable 0x7d penalty. A/B testing confirmed earlier findings regarding gas savings, with per-transaction canonical gas at 26,190 + opGasRefund 40,791 = 66,981, representing a 60.9% saving on the repeated-slot workload. All five benchmark cells passed post-flight. A caveat remains that stock `op-reth` replicas still fail due to an outdated release, requiring tracing against premium sequencers until the next `op-reth` release. The benchmark recipe has been refined to survive devnet constraints.
