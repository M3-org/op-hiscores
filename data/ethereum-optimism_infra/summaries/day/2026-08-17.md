## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SDM Performance Benchmarking [#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20176)
The issue, focused on measuring the performance overhead of the SDM (State Diff Merkle) feature, saw significant progress and detailed analysis on August 17th. Key updates include:
- **Tracing on Refund Blocks:** Reruns confirmed that `debug_traceBlockByNumber` now successfully traces 40/40 sampled sequencer blocks, including those with a trailing `0x7d`, with no measurable latency penalty. `debug_traceTransaction` also succeeds on the `0x7d` hash itself. A caveat notes that stock `op-reth` replicas still fail due to an outdated release, requiring tracing against premium sequencers for now.
- **A/B Rerun Confirmation:** An A/B rerun on `sdm-net-0` confirmed earlier findings regarding gas accounting, with per-transaction canonical gas plus `opGasRefund` equaling the SDM-off per-transaction gas, demonstrating a 60.9% saving on the repeated-slot workload. Post-execution validation stage p99 remained low at 3–17µs.
- **CPU/RSS Impact:** Measurements over A/B windows showed no measurable CPU or RSS impact from SDM at 10-30 TPS loads.
- **Overall Conclusion:** Across various metrics including payload building, execution, RPC, throughput, and resource utilization, SDM shows no measurable overhead beyond a minor p99 PostExec delta under load. The issue is considered ready to close.
