## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement
The discussion on [#22234](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22234) continues to be active, with a detailed analysis provided by einar-oplabs. The issue, initially reported as a flake, has been identified as a real race in the test's final assertions. The root cause involves the `Reached(eth.Safe, N)` check not stabilizing by-number reads, leading to `AssertTxInBlock` failing due to transient disagreements between the light CL and the upstream branch. A proposed fix involves replacing the single-shot assertions with retrying checks. Another signature of the flake, where the resend budget is exhausted, is also being investigated. The discussion also clarified that a previously cited issue, [#21119](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21119), was misattributed, and the current oscillation behavior is tracked in [#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21964). Related PRs [#22321](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22321) (the current fix) and issue [#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22325) (for signature B) are open and under review/investigation.
