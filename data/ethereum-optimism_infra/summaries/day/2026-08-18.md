Today's development focused on infrastructure improvements, specifically addressing CircleCI runner image issues and refining CI processes. Two new pull requests were opened, and two issues were closed, including a detailed analysis of a flake in acceptance tests.

Efforts were made to get the `circleci-runner-wolfi` base image working, which is intended to replace `ci-base-clang`. This involved addressing `sudo` command failures within the `checkout-with-mise` orb. Additionally, the `ci-base-clang` image, previously orphaned, is now being built within the `infra` repository to ensure its continued availability and updates [#720](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/720), [#719](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/719).

## CLOSED ISSUES

### SDM Performance Benchmarking Concluded
The issue tracking SDM performance benchmarking has been closed after extensive measurements. The final conclusion indicates no measurable CPU or RSS impact from SDM at tested loads, and no significant overhead beyond a minor PostExec delta. Tracing on refund blocks now works end-to-end in production, with no measurable 0x7d penalty [#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20176).

### Kona-node L1 Chain Config Handling
An issue regarding `kona-node`'s acceptance of base64-encoded custom L1 chain configurations was closed. The decision was made not to implement inline base64 handling in `kona-node`, suggesting that the deployment layer should decode the value into a mounted file instead [#22491](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22491).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flake in Supernode Light Sequencer Interop Test
Discussion on [#22234](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22234) continues regarding the `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` flake. A detailed analysis confirmed a real race in the test's final assertions, not an environmental failure. The issue has been consolidated with related PRs and issues, including a prior test-side de-flake ([#21806](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21806)), a rejected `MarkFlaky` annotation ([#22235](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22235)), and the current fix for signature A ([#22321](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22321)). Signature B, related to cross-safe wedges, has been split into a new issue ([#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22325)). The underlying node-side behavior causing the oscillation is tracked in [#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21964). The latest comment clarifies that the previous misattribution of the oscillation to [#21119](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21119) was incorrect, and the mechanism described in the analysis remains valid.
