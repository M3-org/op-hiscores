### `op-supernode`: Safe Label Adjusting
Discussions continued regarding the `op-supernode`'s approach to safe label adjustments ([#18976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18976)). The latest comment from `geoknee` at 2026-02-02T14:27:30Z suggests leaning towards the CL having a full set of pointers and remaining the sole controller of the engine, except for interop resets. This approach aims to avoid issues with multiple stateful controllers and leverages the existing interop-aware data structures within the `op-node` engine controller. It also addresses concerns about managing RPC responses from virtual nodes.
