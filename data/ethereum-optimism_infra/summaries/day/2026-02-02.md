## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `op-supernode`: Safe Label Adjusting
Discussions continued regarding the `op-supernode`'s safe label adjusting mechanism ([#18976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18976)). The latest comment from `geoknee` suggests leaning towards the CL maintaining a full set of pointers and remaining the sole controller of the engine, except for interop resets. This approach aims to avoid multiple stateful controllers getting out of sync and aligns with the existing `op-node` engine controller's interop-aware data structures. It also addresses concerns about managing RPC responses from virtual nodes.

### Flashblocks Support within Geth
The discussion around integrating Flashblocks support into Geth ([#756](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/756)) saw an update from `maoueh`, who provided a link to their `op-geth` version which includes Firehose Live Tracer and Flashblock(s) support. This highlights ongoing efforts to bring this functionality to `op-geth`.

### Feedback: `BlockNotFoundError` in Step 5 (Prove the withdrawal on L1)
Further analysis was provided for the `BlockNotFoundError` encountered during L1 withdrawal proving ([#1956](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/1956)). `opfocus` noted that while the Optimism SDK includes `isValidOutputRoot` validation, Viem's `Withdrawals guide` and related actions do not appear to perform equivalent output-root validation. This suggests a potential discrepancy in validation practices between the SDK and Viem, which could lead to issues when the state root has not yet been published to L1.
