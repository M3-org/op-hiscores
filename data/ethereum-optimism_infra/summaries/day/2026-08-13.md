## CLOSED ISSUES

### op-batcher Sequencing Window Recovery
The issue concerning the `op-batcher`'s sequencing-window expiry recovery with span-batches has been closed as "wontfix" ([#16036](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/16036)). It was determined that recovery mode with span batches is either impossible or too complex to support, and not a critical requirement. Recovery is currently supported by switching to singular batches.

### op-deployer and OPCM Options
The issue tracking `op-deployer` changes and new OPCM options for ZK dispute games has been closed ([#21429](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21429)), indicating that the necessary work has been completed.

### SDM-Net-0 Fault Proofs
The issue regarding restoring permissionless fault proofs on `sdm-net-0` has been closed ([#22348](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22348)). The decision was made to use permissioned fault proofs instead, as they are simpler and already implemented on `sdm-net-0`.
