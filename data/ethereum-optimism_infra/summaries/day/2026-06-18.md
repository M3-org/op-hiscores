# ethereum-optimism/infra Daily Update (Jun 18, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's development focused on a critical bug fix in `proxyd` to prevent deadlocks in multi-call strategies, alongside ongoing discussions on `kona-node` synchronization and SDM flag consistency.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### `proxyd` Stability Improvements
A race condition in the `proxyd` multi-call strategy was fixed, preventing potential deadlocks when reading results and draining discarded ones. This ensures more robust and reliable operation of the proxy service. ([#663](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/663))

### `kona-node` SafeDB and Syncmode Parity
Discussions continued on implementing `SafeDB` for `kona-node` ([#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20278)) and achieving behavior parity with `op-node` regarding `syncmode.offset-el-safe` ([#21243](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21243)). The need for a `SafeDB` in `kona-node` was reiterated, with requirements to be gathered from Interop and Proofs. A suggestion was made to fix the `syncmode` issue soon and add a TODO to prioritize the `SafeDB` when available.

### SDM Operator Flag Consistency
The discussion around improving the consistency of the SDM opt-in operator flag across `op-node`, `op-reth`, and `op-rbuilder` continued ([#21360](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21360)). The scope was narrowed to `op-reth` and `op-node`. Agreement was reached on naming the flag `operator_sdm_opt_in` to clearly convey its purpose as a choice for chain operators to enable or disable SDM.
