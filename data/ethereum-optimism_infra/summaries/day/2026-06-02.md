## NEW ISSUES

### Interop Transaction Pool Recovery
A new issue was opened regarding the `op-reth` interop tx pool not resuming after a failsafe is disabled, requiring a restart. This highlights a critical operational challenge for interop transactions within the Superchain ecosystem. ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21149))

### Invalid Interop Message Recovery Runbook
A new issue proposes the creation and testing of a runbook for recovering a chain after an invalid interop message triggers a reorg. This is crucial for protocol security and decentralization, as current systems don't smoothly handle large reorgs. ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21161))

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Spin up devnet ([#20691](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20691))
The latest update indicates that a local devnet is now running, with the next step being to establish a long-running devnet. This is a step towards improving developer experience by providing a stable testing environment.

### `op-revm` support ([#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21069))
Discussion continues regarding the synchronization of `op-revm` with new `revm` releases. The team aims to stay in sync and will update guides to more eagerly update dependencies, especially when `reth` dependencies are bumped. There's particular interest in REVM's latest release (v111) for its Amsterdam hardfork support and shared type definitions.

### op-reth: interop tx pool does not resume after failsafe is disabled (requires restart) ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21149))
Further investigation revealed that restarting `op-reth` alone was insufficient to restore interop after clearing a failsafe. The `op-rbuilder` (also reth-based) was holding evicted state. The remediation now requires restarting both `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder` on the affected chain's sequencers. A new issue ([#21151](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21151)) has been proposed to add a failsafe-state gauge metric for better observability.

### Invalid interop message recovery runbook ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21161))
The discussion on this newly opened issue immediately highlighted the scope of the runbook, which should cover several existing issues related to interop message handling and reorgs, including [#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21149), [#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20006), and [#21125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21125), in addition to ensuring the failsafe is disabled. This emphasizes the complexity of recovering from invalid interop messages and the need for comprehensive documentation.
