# ethereum-optimism/infra Daily Update (Aug 3, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's focus was on addressing a potential memory leak in the replica-healthcheck service by switching to `StaticJsonRpcProvider` and fixing a null-dereference bug. Additionally, two issues were closed, one related to CI makespan and another concerning op-reth proofs history.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Replica Healthcheck Improvements
A change was implemented in the `replica-healthcheck` service to switch from `validators.provider` to `validators.staticJsonRpcProvider` for both `referenceRpcProvider` and `targetRpcProvider` in `HealthcheckService`. This aims to investigate a potential memory leak. A latent null-dereference in the `getBlock(minBlock)` null-check branches was also fixed. ([#694](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/694))

## CLOSED ISSUES

### CI Optimization
An issue related to lowering the makespan of CI was closed. The resolution involved merging smaller, individual pull requests instead of a larger one. ([#21186](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21186))

### op-reth Proofs History Lag
An issue concerning op-reth proofs history lagging permanently after a clean shutdown was closed. The discussion highlighted that the proofs exex should catch up if the chain produces blocks, and that consistency should be ensured on startup rather than by changing shutdown logic. ([#22089](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22089))
