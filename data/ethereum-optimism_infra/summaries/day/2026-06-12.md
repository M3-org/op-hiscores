## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Centralizing OP Fork to L1 Fork Mapping
Discussion on [#21338](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21338) revealed that the OP fork to L1 (Ethereum) fork mapping is duplicated in seven places within the Rust stack, not just three as initially thought. A latent bug was identified where `Karst → Osaka` mapping was missing in `RollupConfig::ethereum_fork_activation`. A detailed plan was proposed to centralize this mapping in `alloy-op-hardforks` as a single source of truth, derive views from it, rewire direct consumers, eliminate OP-fork knowledge from `kona-engine`, and implement a consistency test for `op-revm` without adding new dependencies.

### Proofs: Adjusting `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and Air Gap Constants
On [#20774](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20774), the discussion revolved around the implications of reducing finality and proof maturity delays for ZK and 1-day withdrawals while maintaining compatibility with fault proofs. A key point raised was that the maximum history needed for proofs might be unbounded, depending on proposal frequency. However, for 1-day withdrawals, the max history would be no longer than one day, as a chain of proposals can be built by linking to the parent game. It was also noted that challenging does not require history, as the withdrawal root in Isthmus allows reconstruction of the correct output root.
