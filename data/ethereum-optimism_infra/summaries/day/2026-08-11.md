## ACTIVE ISSUES
### SDM Test Coverage
Issue [#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21344) discusses adding coverage in `rust/kona/tests/proofs` for SDM. The latest comments suggest that existing coverage for the scenario (invalid PostExec tx leading to a deposit-only block) is already present in separate components. It's proposed to close the issue and related PR as the combined scenario is not currently testable in `rust/kona/tests/proofs` with the current `op-geth` engine and would require a swap to `op-reth`. A secure channel was also requested by a contributor to discuss technical concerns related to SDM/Lagoon/PostExec/Kona privately.
### Flaky Test: `TestP2PFull`
Issue [#22381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/22381) is investigating a flaky `TestP2PFull` test. An investigation summary indicates that the `no addresses` failures are likely due to a race condition where disconnect cleanup races an immediate reconnect. While a deterministic delayed-cleanup model reproduced the `no addresses` symptom, a proposed fix did not eliminate failures in a secure real-host reconnect loop. The Noise error (`message is too short`) is also observed but its cause is unknown. The current conclusion is that there is insufficient evidence for a root-cause fix, and the issue remains open.
