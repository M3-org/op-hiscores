## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `op-supernode`/`op-reth` CL Sync Recovery Stalling
Discussions continued on the issue of CL sync recovery stalling when `SafeDB`/bookkeeping references an EL-missing block. A detailed observation from SN3 indicated that `latest/unsafe` progress was made, but `safe/finalized` did not advance due to FCU timeouts. Further analysis suggests that wiping only the supernode DBs makes the verified DB empty, leading to the SuperAuthority returning the activation anchor. The `op-supernode`'s aggregate sync status is also affected by uninitialized dependency-set chains, preventing cross-safe/finalized advancement. The current working hypothesis points to a combination of `op-node` advancing `localSafeHead` without proper EL reconciliation on FCU timeouts and `op-reth`'s engine actor stalling mid-canonicalization. The next steps involve testing a supernode-only DB reset/restart with per-chain sync mode set to `execution-layer` to bypass the long CL startup walkback. [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21076)

### `op-revm` Support and Sync
The discussion around `op-revm` support and synchronization with `revm` releases continued.
