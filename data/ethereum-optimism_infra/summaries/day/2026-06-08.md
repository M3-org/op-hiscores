## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Enhanced Interoperability Metrics
A new feature was added to `proxyd` to track interop validation outcomes. This introduces a Prometheus metric, `proxyd_rpc_interop_validations_total`, which counts transactions that are passed through, filtered, or errored during interop access list validation, improving monitoring capabilities.
