# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (Apr 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused heavily on stabilizing the **Kona** and **Interop** stacks, resolving critical output root mismatches and finalizing interop specification updates. Significant effort was directed toward improving system reliability by triaging a surge of flaky tests related to execution engine (EL) synchronization and race conditions in the sequencer. While progress on Interop Phase 1 and 2 remains largely on track, some secondary tasks like `op-conductor` reorg support have been flagged as "at risk" as developers pivot to address immediate synchronization issues observed on devnets.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Kona & Interoperability Refinement
The team resolved several technical hurdles regarding Kona’s interoperability and block replacement logic.
*   **Output Root Mismatch:** Fixed a critical issue where deposit-only block replacement during message expiry consolidation caused root mismatches ([#19848](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19848)).
*   **Kona-Interop Validation:** Concluded a deep dive into how `kona-interop` accesses internally created blocks, confirming that the current validation logic is correct ([#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19266)).
*   **Conditional Deployment:** Completed work on the conditional deployment of `CrossL2Inbox` within `kona-node` ([#18742](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18742), [#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19311)).

### Infrastructure & Tooling Updates
Updates to core infrastructure components focused on dependency management and networking efficiency.
*   **Proxyd Enhancements:** A new PR was opened to allow `proxyd` to skip consensus polling for forced candidate backends, streamlining backend management ([#608](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/608)).
*   **Dependency Maintenance:** Initiated several version bumps, including `pgx/v5` for `/cci-stats` ([#609](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/609)), OpenTelemetry (`otel`) for `op-signer` ([#610](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/610)), and the removal of the unused `toml` dependency from documentation ([#20017](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20017)).
*   **P2P Sync Deprecation:** The legacy req-resp P2P sync in `op-node` was officially closed in favor of a new centralized driver approach to address long-standing instability in cloud environments ([#14108](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/14108)).

### Contract & Protocol Governance
*   **Feature Flags:** Integrated new feature flags for **OPSuccinct** into the Optimism Portal Contract Manager (OPCM) `_devFeatureBitmap` ([#18419](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18419), [#18420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18420)).
*   **Op-Geth Lifecycle:** Clarified that `op-geth` remains supported for use with `op-node` until May 31st, providing a clear migration window for users moving to `op-reth` ([#20203](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20203)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Interop Specs:** Formally closed the Interop Specs update task ([#905](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/905)), signaling that the protocol definitions are now stable.
*   **CI Stability:** Resolved a CI bottleneck where `rust-tests` were failing due to Out-of-Memory (OOM) errors during `reth-optimism-node` compilation ([#19935](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19935)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sequencer & Sync Bugs:** A new issue was identified where `op-node` (configured as a sequencer LightCL) performs unnecessary engine resets on restart, potentially rewinding EL heads ([#20294](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20294)). Additionally, a regression guard is being developed for EL restart `SYNCING` recovery to prevent engine stalls ([#20307](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20307)).
*   **Flaky Test Surge:** Multiple tests are under investigation for flakiness:
    *   `TestReorgUnsafeHead`: Root cause identified as asynchronous updates in `op-node`; a fix is in draft ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20198)).
    *   `TestRelayWithInvalidMessagesSteady`: Caused by transient EL dial timeouts; a fix to improve error handling in the sampler goroutine is underway ([#20254](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20254)).
    *   `TestTruncateDatabaseOnELResync`: Reopened due to failures in `CrossSafe` promotion during EL sync ([#19897](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19897)).
*   **Project Risks:** Work on `op-conductor` reorg recovery ([#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20006)) and verification-aware batching ([#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19944)) are currently at risk as resources are diverted to high-priority devnet sync issues.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week demonstrated strong cross-team coordination between the infrastructure and protocol teams, particularly in the transition of the P2P sync issue to a more specialized repository. Discussions around Kona interop consolidation logic showed a high level of technical scrutiny, with contributors using reproduction tests to validate complex block-replacement behaviors. The clarification on `op-geth` deprecation also highlights an active effort to maintain transparent communication with node operators during the transition to the Superchain-ready stack.
