# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (Jun 7 - 13, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week’s development focused on hardening the `proxyd` service and streamlining the repository by removing deprecated components. Significant progress was made in interoperability infrastructure, specifically through the introduction of fail-closed security defaults and enhanced Prometheus observability for transaction validation. The team also executed a major terminology refactor to align the codebase with the "interop-filter" naming convention and continued high-level architectural discussions regarding the decoupling of `op-geth` fee calculations.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### `proxyd` Interoperability & Security
The `proxyd` service received several updates aimed at making cross-chain interoperability more robust and observable.
*   **Security Hardening:** Interop filtering now defaults to a "fail-closed" state, ensuring that transactions are not processed if validation cannot be confirmed. The default strategy has been updated to `agreement` for maximum safety ([#649](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/649)).
*   **Enhanced Observability:** A new Prometheus metric, `proxyd_rpc_interop_validations_total`, was introduced to track whether transactions are passed, filtered, or errored during access list validation ([#643](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/643)).
*   **Refactoring:** To improve clarity, all "supervisor" terminology was renamed to "interop-filter" across code, configs, and metrics ([#648](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/648)). Work also began on unifying transaction filters on the submission path ([#654](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/654)).

### Stability & Maintenance
Efforts were made to resolve concurrency issues and clean up the codebase.
*   **Bug Fixes:** Critical fixes addressed concurrent access issues in `AvgSlidingWindow` ([#653](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/653)) and resolved a race condition in `TestMulticall` to stabilize CI ([#652](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/652)).
*   **Component Removal:** The `op-acceptor` component was entirely removed from the repository. It was deemed obsolete following the removal of its underlying system extensions in `op-devstack` ([#651](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/651)).
*   **Tooling:** The `make lint` target was updated to include `golangci-lint`, ensuring local development environments match CI requirements ([#650](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/650)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
*   **`op-geth` Decoupling (Fees):** Significant progress was reported on issue [#20261](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20261) regarding the creation of `op-core/fees`. Key technical decisions include using `tx.MarshalBinary()` for accurate DA cost calculations and the introduction of `OperatorCostJovian`. This work also addresses legacy issues by standardizing the Jovian operator-fee formula.
*   **`op-geth` Decoupling (Cleanup):** Discussion continues on the deletion of `op-simulate` and `op-run-block` ([#21282](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21282)) as part of the broader effort to decouple OP-Stack components from Geth.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by high-impact internal refactoring and architectural alignment. The transition from "supervisor" to "interop-filter" terminology suggests a concerted effort to standardize language across the Superchain ecosystem. Technical discussions involving `sebastianst` on fee calculations indicate deep collaboration on protocol-level math and state-reading dispatchers, ensuring that the decoupling of core components does not compromise the accuracy of L1 cost and operator fee logic.
