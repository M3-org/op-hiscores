# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (Apr 5 - 11, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on hardening the `op-supernode` infrastructure and refining the Consensus Layer (CL) architecture within `proxyd`. Significant progress was made in L1 reorg handling through the implementation of Write-Ahead Logging (WAL), which resolved several long-standing data pruning issues. While the team successfully defined Superroot SLAs and updated core dependencies, the week was also characterized by an intensive effort to triage and debug a series of persistent flaky tests in the CI pipeline, particularly those affecting `rollup-boost` and interop action tests.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Proxyd & Consensus Layer (CL) Architecture
Work began on a significant rebase and architectural overhaul of the `proxyd` CL consensus mechanism.
*   **Consensus Rebase:** Initiated a rebase for CL consensus to align with recent upstream changes ([#596](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/596)).
*   **Architecture Refinement:** Introduced a new feature PR for the "arch4" main consensus architecture in `proxyd`, aimed at improving how the service handles consensus-related requests ([#597](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/597)).

### Infrastructure Hardening & Dependency Management
The team performed routine but critical maintenance on service dependencies and protocol specifications.
*   **OpenTelemetry Updates:** The `op-acceptor` service received a suite of updates, bumping OpenTelemetry SDK and exporter dependencies from v1.34.0 to v1.43.0 to leverage improved observability features ([#599](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/599), [#600](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/600), [#601](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/601)).
*   **Security & Utility Bumps:** Updated `lodash` in the `replica-healthcheck` service to address potential vulnerabilities and improve utility performance ([#598](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/598)).
*   **Superroot SLA:** Finalized the Superroot Service Level Agreement (SLA) updates, specifically integrating "Full Outputs in Denylist" and "OptimisticOutput" logic ([#19180](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19180)).

### Protocol & Runtime Configuration
*   **Reactive Config Reloading:** Discussions are underway regarding how `op-node` handles unsafe block signer mismatches. A proposal is being vetted to implement a "grace period" for sequencer key rotations, allowing both old and new keys to be accepted briefly to prevent derivation UX degradation ([#19981](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19981), [#19982](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/19982)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **L1 Reorg Handling:** A major milestone was reached in `op-supernode` data consistency. By implementing Write-Ahead Logging (WAL), the team resolved issues related to pruning the interop database ([#19287](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19287)), clearing denied blocks ([#19245](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19245)), and managing the denylist ([#19289](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19289)) during L1 reorgs.
*   **Test Stability:** Resolved `TestExecutePayloadSuccess` ([#19898](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19898)) by implementing a polling mechanism for `debug_proofsSyncStatus`, effectively eliminating a race condition in the ExEx store.

### New & Active Issues
*   **CI Flakiness:** A significant portion of the week's triage was dedicated to recurring test failures. `TestFlashblocksStream` ([#19883](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19883)) and `TestFlashblocksTransfer` ([#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19934)) are showing timing-related failures and "address already in use" errors.
*   **Interop Testing:** The migration of `op-e2e` interop action tests to `op-acceptance-tests` remains an active priority to streamline the testing suite ([#18974](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/18974)).
*   **Superchain Regressions:** `TestSuperchain` ([#19586](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19586)) has resurfaced as a flake, with suspected panics in the `sepolia` subtest causing Anvil processes to hang.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week showed heavy collaboration between maintainers (notably `wwared` and `maurelian`) on CI diagnostics. The detailed trace analysis provided in the flaky test issues indicates a high level of technical rigor in identifying race conditions within the `rollup-boost` and `op-node` components. The transition of interop tests also suggests a cross-team effort to consolidate testing infrastructure for better maintainability.
