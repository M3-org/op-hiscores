# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (May 10 - 16, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused heavily on stabilizing the `op-acceptance-tests` suite by identifying root causes for several persistent flaky tests related to reorg recovery and database management. Significant progress was made in the Interop Proofs Devnet initiative, with development moving toward a comprehensive "Definition of Done" that includes superdispute game play and flashblock integration. Additionally, the team addressed critical service stability issues in `cci-stats` and continued refining cross-chain interoperability logic within `proxyd`.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Test Suite Stability & Reliability
A major effort was directed at resolving flakiness in the acceptance testing framework, ensuring more reliable CI/CD pipelines.
*   **Reorg & Sync Fixes:** Root causes were identified and fixes proposed for `TestUnsafeGapFillAfterSafeReorg` and `TestFollowL2_ReorgRecovery` ([#20651](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/20651)). These tests had previously suffered from `context deadline exceeded` errors during memory-intensive operations.
*   **Database Integrity:** A fix was identified for `TestNotTruncateDatabaseOnRestartWithExistingDatabase` ([#20652](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/20652)) to prevent improper database truncation during node restarts.
*   **Withdrawal Proofs:** Investigation into `TestWithdrawal_Cannon` led to a draft fix addressing `OptimismPortal_InvalidProofTimestamp` errors by ensuring the L1 head time surpasses the game creation time before attempting proofs ([#19963](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/19963)).

### Infrastructure & Service Improvements
*   **`cci-stats` Stability:** Fixed a critical nil-pointer dereference bug in the `cci-stats` service. The service now correctly handles API-triggered or scheduled pipelines that lack Version Control System (VCS) information ([#621](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/621)).
*   **`proxyd` Refactoring:** Initiated a refactor to bundle Consensus Layer (CL) sync bodies into the `ConsensusTrackerState` to streamline state management ([#622](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/622)).
*   **Dependency Management:** Updated the `urllib3` library from 2.6.3 to 2.7.0 within the `op-conductor-ops` module to maintain security and performance standards ([#620](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/620)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **`op-supernode` Investigation:** Issue [#20657](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20657) regarding `superroot_atTimestamp` was closed. Investigation confirmed the `RequiredL1` field functions correctly, and previously reported divergences were artifacts of the test environment rather than protocol bugs.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Persistent Sync Flakiness:** `TestELSyncSafeRetractedByOffset` has resurfaced as a high-priority flaky test ([#20334](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20334)). Despite increasing retries to 120, the test continues to fail, suggesting a fundamental timing issue in the CrossSafe/LocalUnsafe sync check.
*   **Interop Proofs Devnet:** Work continues on the Interop Proofs roadmap ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20489)), focusing on achieving "superdispute" game functionality and ensuring the conductor handles re-orgs correctly within the new interop framework.
*   **Message Validation:** Discussions are ongoing regarding adding unsafe head executing message validation ([#20553](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20553)). A proposal is on the table to update `proxyd` to query interop filters for "cross-unsafe" status.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw active technical coordination between contributors like `pauldowman`, who led the charge on debugging the acceptance test suite, and `sebastianst`, who provided deep-dive analysis into sync-related failures. Collaboration also extended beyond the internal repository, with contributors working upstream on the `viem` library ([viem#4597](https://github.com/wevm/viem/pull/4597)) to transition withdrawal proving from block-number-based logic to timestamp-based logic, aligning the broader Ethereum ecosystem with Optimism's technical requirements.
