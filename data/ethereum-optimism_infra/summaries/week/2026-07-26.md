# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (Jul 26 - Aug 1, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on hardening infrastructure security and improving observability for core services. A significant milestone was reached in the security posture of the `bailiff` service through its migration to a Wolfi-based image, aligning it with the project's broader security standards. Simultaneously, `proxyd` received critical telemetry updates to support Service Level Objective (SLO) monitoring, while the team continued to investigate complex regressions in consensus layer synchronization and gas reserve requirements for the upcoming Glamsterdam upgrade.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Infrastructure Security & CI Maintenance
The team prioritized supply chain security and workflow reliability by updating base images and CI dependencies.
*   **Bailiff Security Migration:** The `bailiff` Dockerfile was migrated from Alpine to `chainguard/wolfi-base:latest` for both build and runtime stages. This move reduces the attack surface and aligns the service with the security practices used across the Optimism ecosystem ([#675](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/675)).
*   **CI Factory Updates:** CI factory pins were updated to the latest `main` commit, ensuring that `build-images.yaml` and `build-images.apko.yaml` workflows utilize the most recent stable versions of internal factory actions ([#677](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/677)).

### Observability & Metrics
Enhancements to `proxyd` provide deeper insights into RPC traffic patterns and reliability.
*   **Per-RPC Metrics:** New metrics (`proxyd_rpc_calls_total`) were introduced to track usage and error rates at a granular level. These metrics capture metadata including backend names, methods, status codes, and transport types, which are essential for maintaining and alerting on service SLOs ([#676](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/676)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Active Technical Discussions
*   **Glamsterdam Upgrade Gas Reserves:** Discussion continues regarding `PreimageOracle.PRECOMPILE_CALL_RESERVED_GAS` ([#19380](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19380)). Current analysis suggests that the proposed increase to `200_000` gas may be insufficient if the goal is to guarantee whole-function completion under EIP-8037/8038. The team is weighing whether to close the issue as unnecessary for core correctness or to significantly increase the reserve toward `400_000`.
*   **Consensus Layer Sync Regressions:** The `TestUnsafeChainNotStalling_CLSync` test suite is experiencing recurring failures ([#21041](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21041)). Recent logs indicate an Execution Layer (EL) peer-management defect where `op-reth` fails to re-establish connections after a sequencer disconnects, suggesting a stall in the Engine API rather than a test configuration issue.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week's activity reflects a strong focus on internal infrastructure standards, particularly in the transition toward Wolfi-based images and standardized CI workflows. Technical collaboration is currently centered on protocol-level stability, as evidenced by the deep-dive discussions between contributors regarding gas estimation for the Amsterdam upgrade and the debugging of `op-reth` synchronization behavior. These efforts highlight a coordinated approach between infrastructure maintainers and protocol engineers to ensure network reliability ahead of upcoming upgrades.
