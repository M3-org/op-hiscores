# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (Apr 26 - May 2, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused on hardening the infrastructure layer through significant enhancements to `proxyd` and the resolution of critical testing and upgrade bottlenecks. Key efforts were directed toward improving Consensus Layer (CL) reliability via new tiebreaking and sync status mechanisms. Additionally, the team made progress on build system modernization with a new Proof of Concept for `ko/apko` integration and successfully closed long-standing issues related to Cannon test stability and L2CM upgrade canonicalization.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### `proxyd` Enhancements for Consensus Reliability
Significant work was initiated to improve how `proxyd` handles Consensus Layer data, ensuring higher availability and data integrity.
*   **CL Output Root Tiebreaking:** Introduced a ranked priority system for CL output root tiebreaking to resolve potential discrepancies between nodes ([#612](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/612)).
*   **Consensus-Aware Sync:** Integrated Redis with CL consensus awareness to provide a uniform and accurate synchronization status across the infrastructure ([#611](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/611)).

### Build System & Tooling
The team is exploring more efficient containerization and deployment workflows.
*   **`ko/apko` Integration:** A new Proof of Concept was opened to explore the integration of `ko` and `apko` for building OCI images, potentially streamlining the development-to-deployment pipeline ([#613](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/613)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Cannon Test Stability:** Resolved a critical failure in the `kona-host-client-offline-cannon` test. The fix involved correcting target specifications in `cannon-builder:v1.0.0`, bind-mounting the missing `mips64-unknown-none.json`, and refreshing witness tars to ensure successful verification ([#19895](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19895)).
*   **L2CM Upgrades:** Successfully implemented the Bundle Canonicalization process for L2CM Upgrades. This automates and validates Network Upgrade Transactions (NUT) on a per-hardfork basis, improving the reliability of protocol upgrades ([#19307](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19307)).

### Active Issues & Blockers
*   **`op-alloy` Trait Conflict:** There is an ongoing discussion regarding a trait implementation conflict between `Optimism` and `Ethereum` wallets in `op-alloy`. While a fix exists in the main `optimism` repository, it has not yet been released to crates.io, leading to community questions regarding the maintenance status of the `op-alloy` crate ([#20281](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20281)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week's activity reflects a strong focus on cross-repository coordination, as several infrastructure improvements were unblocked by or merged into the primary `optimism` repository. Community engagement is currently centered on the `op-alloy` maintenance discussion, where users are seeking clarification on release schedules for critical upstream fixes. The introduction of the `ko/apko` PoC suggests an ongoing internal effort to refine the developer experience and build tooling for contributors.
