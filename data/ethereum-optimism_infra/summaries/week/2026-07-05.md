# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (Jul 5 - 11, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the infrastructure team focused on stabilizing the Optimism Sepolia network following the Karst hard fork and optimizing core service performance. A critical fix for high intrinsic gas costs was successfully deployed via a new `op-reth` release, while significant progress was made in identifying the root causes of interop-related flaky tests. Additionally, the team conducted a strategic audit of the project's fuzzing infrastructure, leading to a streamlined testing approach that prioritizes deterministic unit tests over low-value PR-time fuzzing jobs.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Proxyd Performance & Scalability
Efforts to enhance the performance of `proxyd` are underway with a focus on reducing latency in backend updates.
*   **Parallelized RPC Calls**: A new pull request, [#665](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/665), was introduced to parallelize Execution Layer (EL) consensus-poller RPCs within the `UpdateBackend` function. This change aims to improve the efficiency of backend health checks and state synchronization.

### Protocol Stability & Dependencies
The team continues to manage the evolution of the OP Stack through critical dependency updates and protocol-level fixes.
*   **Op-reth Updates**: Work is ongoing to update the `revm` dependency in `op-reth` to version 2.4.0 or 2.3.1 ([#21524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21524)). The team is currently monitoring for the official release of these versions to proceed with the integration.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Optimism Sepolia Gas Fix**: The team resolved a critical issue ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21457)) where intrinsic gas calculations were incorrectly high on Sepolia post-Karst hard fork. The fix was bundled into the `op-reth/v2.3.3` release, restoring expected gas behavior for the testnet.
*   **Fuzzing Strategy Overhaul**: Following a comprehensive audit ([#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21636)), the team decided to retire six existing Go fuzzing jobs that provided minimal value due to sparse seeds and lack of a committed corpus. These have been converted into deterministic seed tests within standard unit-test jobs to maintain coverage without the CI overhead.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Interoperability Testing Flakiness**: A new investigation ([#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21635)) is targeting `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement`. The root cause was identified as a race condition between `FollowSource` processing and sequencer build events. A proposed fix involving force-resetting active sequencers is currently being tracked in a draft PR.
*   **Nightly Fuzzing Infrastructure**: While PR-time fuzzing was retired, a new requirement has been identified for a scoped nightly job that includes crash-artifact persistence and a committed seed corpus to provide more robust security guarantees.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by deep technical audits and cross-component troubleshooting. The investigation into issue [#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21635) demonstrated significant coordination between developers working on `op-reth` and the sequencer logic, resulting in a sophisticated diagnosis of ordering issues in the interop stack. The decision-making process regarding the fuzzing infrastructure ([#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21636)) also highlighted a pragmatic approach to developer experience, opting to remove "noisy" CI jobs in favor of more reliable testing patterns.
