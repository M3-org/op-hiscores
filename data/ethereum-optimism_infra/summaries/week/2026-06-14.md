# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (Jun 14 - 20, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on critical infrastructure stability and the ongoing architectural decoupling of `op-geth`. Key technical achievements included a significant routing fix for `proxyd` to ensure WebSocket transaction parity with HTTP requests and the resolution of several long-standing issues related to proofs and the Superchain ecosystem. Development also centered on defining the future of ZK execution within the `vm-runner` and establishing a sustainable proving strategy for CI environments.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### `proxyd` Enhancements
Significant effort was directed toward improving the reliability and observability of the `proxyd` service.
*   **WebSocket Transaction Routing:** A critical fix was implemented to ensure WebSocket transaction RPCs follow the same pre-forward validation and backend-group forwarding paths as HTTP requests. This ensures consistency across transport layers while maintaining pinned upstream connections for subscriptions ([#655](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/655)).
*   **Logging Improvements:** To improve developer experience and troubleshooting, `proxyd` was updated to log unexpected block tags as decimal integers rather than hexadecimal ([#661](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/661)).

### `op-ufm` Reliability
*   **Transaction Tracking:** A new pull request was introduced to fix a bug in `op-ufm` that incorrectly marked missing transactions as "seen," which will improve the accuracy of user-facing monitoring ([#660](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/660)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### `op-geth` Decoupling & Test Migration
Extensive technical discussions occurred regarding the migration of tests and client implementations as the project moves toward upstream `go-ethereum` compatibility.
*   **Transaction Representation:** The team resolved to avoid a generic `optypes.Transaction` wrapper. Instead, L2 transaction accessors will be partitioned by class (e.g., `InfoAndUserTxs`, `InfoAndDeposits`).
*   **Lagoon Support:** Specific handling for Lagoon+ chains was identified, particularly regarding the `0x7D` PostExecTx, which must be treated as a canonical block transaction during decoding and test filtering.
*   **Transition Scaffolding:** It was clarified that `optypes` helpers are temporary "scaffolding" to facilitate the cutover and should not be used for long-lived APIs ([#20265](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20265), [#20264](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20264), [#20262](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20262)).

### ZK Proofs & Dispute Games
*   **Proving Strategy:** Discussions on [#21409](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21409) lean toward using a mock prover for CI and local development to avoid the high costs of full ZK proving, while potentially utilizing `op-sepolia` for production-grade validation.
*   **Bond Credit Resolution:** Issues regarding `address(0)` bond credit claiming in `ZKDisputeGame` were closed in favor of centralized tracking in the main `optimism` repository ([#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/19743), [#21420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21420)).

### General Cleanup
A major triage effort resulted in the closure of several stale issues:
*   **Superchain-Ops:** Resolved discussions on Ledger UX, nested Safe simulations, and environment variable management ([#360](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/360), [#674](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/674)).
*   **Protocol & Explorer:** Closed issues related to `OPCMUpgradeV700` prestate corrections and L2 block explorer migrations ([#1412](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/1412), [#1131](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/1131)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by deep technical coordination between maintainers regarding the `op-geth` decoupling strategy. The collaboration highlights a disciplined approach to "transition scaffolding," ensuring that temporary fixes do not become permanent technical debt. Additionally, the shift of ZK-related bond issues to the primary `optimism` repository indicates active cross-repo coordination to streamline the development of the ZK Stack.
