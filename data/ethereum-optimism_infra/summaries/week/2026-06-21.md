# ethereum-optimism/infra Weekly Report (Jun 21 - 27, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on optimizing CI infrastructure and resolving critical synchronization issues within the consensus layer. A major milestone was reached with the conclusion of the ZK proving spike, providing essential latency and cost benchmarks for super-root state transitions. Development also prioritized CI efficiency through the introduction of parallel image builds, while the team actively triaged complex flaky tests and compatibility challenges related to EIP-4444 and upstream dependency updates.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### CI Infrastructure & Build Optimization
Efforts were concentrated on reducing build times and modernizing the containerization workflow.
*   **Parallel Apko Builds:** The team introduced parallel Apko image builds to the CI process, integrating Melange and Apko configurations for various images. This transition aims to optimize build speeds while maintaining compatibility with existing Docker-based workflows. To avoid environment pollution, these new images are published to a dedicated namespace ([#664](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/664)).

### Protocol & Node Stability
Critical fixes were addressed to ensure network stability following version upgrades.
*   **Consensus-Layer Sync Fix:** A critical failure in consensus-layer synchronization was identified in `op-reth v2.3.2`. The root cause was traced to an upstream `reth` dependency update that introduced a block expiry guard, which inadvertently prevented the resolution of genesis blocks. A validated fix was proposed to restore sync functionality ([#21584](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21584)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **ZK Proving Benchmarks:** The spike to generate a real super-root state-transition proof has concluded. The resulting data, which baselines the latency and cost of these proofs, has been documented in a benchmarking report ([#21411](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21411)).
*   **Sync Resolution:** As noted in the developments, the `op-reth` sync failure was successfully analyzed and resolved this week.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Flaky Test Triage:** Two significant flaky tests remain under investigation:
    *   **`FuzzDetectNonBijectivity` ([#21539](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21539)):** Discussion revealed that the test's premise is flawed because `op-node` and `kona` mask the top two bits of field elements during blob encoding. Fixing this would require a hardfork for backward compatibility, leading to a debate on whether to modify the protocol or simply update the test harness.
    *   **`TestReorgUnsafeHead` ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20198)):** Despite previous fixes, this test continues to fail due to reorgs not occurring as expected and intermittent faucet funding failures during setup.
*   **EIP-4444 Compatibility:** Ongoing discussions are exploring whether the `op-reth` genesis blockhash fix should be ported to `op-node v1.19.1` to resolve errors when running against L1 nodes with EIP-4444 history pruning enabled ([#13570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/13570)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by deep technical collaboration on protocol-level edge cases. Significant dialogue occurred between contributors regarding the trade-offs of hardforks versus test-harness adjustments for blob encoding. The rapid identification and resolution of the `op-reth` sync issue demonstrate a high level of responsiveness to upstream dependency changes. Furthermore, the cross-pollination of ideas is evident as contributors are actively evaluating if fixes for one component (reth) should be applied to others (op-node) to maintain ecosystem-wide compatibility with EIP-4444.
