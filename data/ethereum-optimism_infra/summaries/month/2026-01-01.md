# ethereum-optimism/infra Monthly Report (January 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In January 2026, the `infra` repository focused on refining the scope of its testing infrastructure and maintaining dependency security. A significant effort was made to triage the `op-acceptance-tests` backlog, resulting in the closure of several long-standing issues related to flashblocks and sequencer failover that were deemed outside the current architectural scope. This re-scoping ensures that the project's testing suite remains focused on achievable validation while moving benchmarking efforts toward dedicated automation milestones.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Dependency Management & Security
Maintenance efforts this month included routine security and version updates for internal tooling.
*   **OP-Conductor Updates:** The `op-conductor-ops` directory received a dependency bump for `urllib3`, moving from version 2.5.0 to 2.6.3 to ensure the stability and security of the conductor's operational scripts [#535](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/535).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Testing Suite Re-scoping
A major cleanup of the issue tracker took place this month, specifically targeting the `op-acceptance-tests` suite. The team determined that certain tests required infrastructure manipulation capabilities that the current framework is not designed to handle.
*   **Sequencer & Failover Tests:** Several issues were closed as they fell outside the current scope of `op-acceptance-tests`, including work on sequencer failover ([#350](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/350)), rollup-boost kill-switches ([#314](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/314)), and specific flashblock acceptance criteria ([#316](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/316), [#318](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/318), [#317](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/317)).
*   **Benchmarking Migration:** The flashblocks load test ([#379](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/379)) was closed and re-categorized under the Benchmarking Milestone, intended for future integration into nightly benchmarking automations.

### Bug Fixes
*   **Validation Checks:** A bug that caused errors when running validation checks across all chains ([#668](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/668)) was resolved and closed.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Activity this month was characterized by administrative maintenance and strategic triage by the maintainers. By re-evaluating the scope of the acceptance testing suite, the team has clarified the boundaries for future contributors regarding what types of tests should be integrated into `op-acceptance-tests` versus what belongs in benchmarking or infrastructure-level manipulation tools. The focus remains on streamlining the codebase and reducing technical debt within the testing infrastructure.
