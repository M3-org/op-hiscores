## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### New & Active Issues
The most significant technical hurdle currently facing the team is the **Light-Sequencer Recovery Oscillation** ([#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21964)). This issue represents a complex edge case in `op-node` where a node configured as both a sequencer and a follow-source follower experiences instability during recovery.

*   **Technical Impact:** Recent logs from the `memory-all-opn-op-reth-prague` job indicate that this oscillation leads to test failures, specifically in `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement`. 
*   **Root Cause Analysis:** The failure occurs when a light sequencer's locally-built block is incorrectly labeled as safe or finalized following a reorg. This causes the sequencer to seal its own-fork block, triggering an inconsistent-forkchoice deriver reset. 
*   **Current Status:** The investigation has revealed that current height-based safety-label checks are insufficient for distinguishing between forks once a local fork has been relabeled during recovery. This remains a high-priority blocker for CI stability and protocol consistency.
