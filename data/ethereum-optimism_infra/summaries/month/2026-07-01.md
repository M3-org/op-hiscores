# ethereum-optimism/infra Monthly Report (July 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In July 2026, the infrastructure team focused on hardening the OP Stack's reliability and performance, specifically addressing critical gas calculation issues following the Karst hard fork. Significant progress was made in synchronizing the Superchain Registry across core components like `op-reth` and `kona`, streamlining the interoperability of the ecosystem. The month was also characterized by a strategic audit of testing infrastructure, leading to a refined approach to fuzzing and the resolution of complex race conditions in sequencer synchronization.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Proxyd Performance and Reliability
Efforts were directed toward optimizing `proxyd` to handle high-load environments more efficiently.
*   **Parallelized RPCs:** A new initiative ([#665](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/pull/665)) was launched to parallelize Execution Layer (EL) consensus-poller RPC calls within the `UpdateBackend` function, aiming to reduce latency in backend updates.
*   **Behavioral Consistency:** The team began investigating a regression in `eth_call` behavior ([#666](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/666)) where out-of-range blocks were returning null results instead of errors, ensuring the proxy remains spec-compliant.

### Protocol Stability and Node Operations
Critical fixes were deployed to ensure the stability of Optimism Sepolia and the broader OP Stack node configurations.
*   **Intrinsic Gas Fix:** A high-priority issue ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21457)) regarding incorrect intrinsic gas calculations on Optimism Sepolia was resolved and shipped in `op-reth/v2.3.3`.
*   **Pruning Standards:** Technical analysis of `op-node` failures ([#21465](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21465)) led to a new architectural standard: OP Stack nodes should never prune block bodies, as they are essential for reading L1-info deposit transactions during EL sync. Documentation is being updated to reflect these supported pruning configurations.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Superchain Registry Synchronization:** The team successfully unified configuration loading between `kona`, `op-reth`, and `op-node` ([#20420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20420)). This ensures consistent chain data across the Superchain ecosystem, with further configuration refactoring planned as non-blocking follow-up work.
*   **CI Reproducibility:** Duplicate issues regarding SP1 guest ELF verification key reproducibility were consolidated to streamline CI maintenance.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Flaky Test Remediation:** A deep-dive into `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` ([#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21635)) revealed race conditions between `FollowSource` processing and sequencer build events. A fix is currently in progress to force active sequencer resets on local-safe divergence.
*   **Fuzzing Strategy Pivot:** Following an audit ([#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21636)), the team decided to retire low-value PR-time fuzzing jobs in favor of deterministic seed tests. Future fuzzing efforts will be reserved for high-value differential targets via nightly jobs with committed corpora.
*   **Dependency Management:** The update of the `revm` dependency to v2.4.0/2.3.1 for `op-reth` ([#21524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/21524)) remains blocked pending an upstream release.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month saw high-quality technical coordination between contributors, particularly in the root cause analysis of sequencer flakiness and the fuzzing audit. The detailed investigation provided by @ajsutton on sequencer resets and the strategic recommendations by @geoknee regarding testing infrastructure demonstrate a strong emphasis on long-term maintainability. Collaboration between the `op-reth` and `kona` workstreams has also matured, as evidenced by the successful synchronization of the Superchain Registry.
