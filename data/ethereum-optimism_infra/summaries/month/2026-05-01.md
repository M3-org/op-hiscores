# ethereum-optimism/infra Monthly Report (May 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In May 2026, development efforts focused heavily on refining the stability and logic of the Superchain infrastructure, specifically targeting `op-supernode` and `op-reth` compatibility. A significant portion of the month's activity involved triaging a critical upgrade panic in `op-reth` and clarifying the configuration requirements for cross-chain dispute monitoring. The team is currently addressing fundamental logic discrepancies in how the Supernode calculates L1 verification requirements, which is essential for the integrity of the Superchain's shared security model.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While the month began with a focus on triage and architectural clarification rather than new feature merges, the following technical areas saw significant progress:

### Supernode & Dispute Monitoring Integration
Work focused on streamlining how monitoring tools interact with the Superchain's node infrastructure.
*   **Dispute Monitor Versatility:** It was established that `op-dispute-mon` can simultaneously monitor both standard CANNON and SUPER_CANNON_KONA games. This is achieved by passing both a rollup-rpc and a supernode-rpc, with the `op-supernode` itself capable of serving as the rollup-rpc. This clarification ensures that infrastructure operators can maintain comprehensive visibility across different game types without redundant deployments.

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The month was characterized by deep-dive investigations into protocol-level bugs and state transition logic.

### Closed Issues
*   **Configuration Clarification:** Issue [#20476](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20476) was resolved by clarifying the interoperability between `op-dispute-mon` and `op-supernode` regarding Virtual Nodes (VNs) and output roots.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Supernode L1 Verification Logic:** A high-priority issue ([#20481](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20481)) was opened regarding the `VerifiedRequiredL1` calculation in `op-supernode`. The current implementation uses the minimum of per-chain L1 values, whereas the protocol may require the maximum. This discrepancy in `Superroot.atTimestamp` could impact how the Supernode tracks and publishes verified state.
*   **op-reth Upgrade Panic:** Investigation continues into a fatal panic occurring during the transition from `op-reth` 1.x to 2.x ([#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/infra/issues/20462)). Findings suggest a non-deterministic bug in the `alloy-trie-0.9.5` branch. Notably, the `--engine.legacy-state-root` flag does not resolve the issue, as the panic persists across both `StateRootTask` and parallel trie computation paths when interacting with 1.x database states.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The activity this month highlights a strong collaborative effort between infrastructure maintainers and protocol engineers to debug complex state-root issues. The detailed technical exchange regarding the `op-reth` panic indicates a high level of coordination in testing various flags and trie computation strategies. Furthermore, the resolution of dispute monitor configurations demonstrates an ongoing commitment to improving the developer and operator experience by providing clear architectural guidance for the Superchain ecosystem.
