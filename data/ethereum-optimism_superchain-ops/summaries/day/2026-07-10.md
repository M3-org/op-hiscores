## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test Investigation
Discussion continued on the flaky `TestReorgUnsafeHead` ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20198)). A new CI occurrence was added, with analysis pointing to an in-flight sequencing/forkchoice action by the supernode's op-node sequencer reasserting the original head, thereby undoing the reorg. A suggested fix involves applying a quiescence guard before the first `ReorgExact` to prevent op-node from reasserting the original head.

### Uniswap Joint Devnet 4 Status
The Uniswap Joint Devnet 4 issue ([#21260](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21260)) is being put on hold. The reason is an ongoing effort to migrate Unichain into OPE, which Uniswap engineers may not be aware of. An action item was taken to flag this to partnerships for careful navigation.
