## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Supernode/Reth CL Sync Recovery Stalls
- **Problem**: The `op-supernode/op-reth` CL sync recovery can stall when SafeDB/bookkeeping references an EL-missing block. ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21076))
- **Latest Update**: Testing of a candidate fix ([paradigmxyz/reth#24875](https://github.com/paradigmxyz/reth/pull/24875)) revealed it does not resolve the wedge, with 5 out of 5 cold-start arms still wedging due to libmdbx 300s read-tx timeout. The issue is identified as a cyclic deadlock in reth's `StateTrieOverlayManager` / `DeferredTrieData` machinery, specifically due to locks being held across blocking `rayon` computes. Two proposed patches (eager compute-before-insert + eager sort, and lazy + sequential sort) have shown 0/5 wedges in testing, indicating the fix lies in removing the guard from across the compute.

### Supernode Cross-Safe Backlog Drainage
- **Problem**: `op-supervisor/op-supernode` cross-safe cannot drain a backlog faster than real-time after a stall, particularly when the sequencing window expires. ([#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21117))
- **Latest Update**: Discussion suggests that the observed 12-hour delay in cross-safe recovery is consistent with the sequencer window expiry behavior, which requires manual intervention. If the sequencer pauses and manual steps are taken, this behavior is considered expected, and no further action on this ticket may be needed.
