## CLOSED ISSUES

*   **SDM Performance Benchmarking Concluded**
    *   The issue tracking performance benchmarking for SDM was closed after extensive measurements confirmed no measurable CPU, RSS, or latency impact from SDM at various loads. Tracing on refund blocks was also verified to work end-to-end in production ([#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20176)).
*   **Kona-Node L1 Chain Config Handling**
    *   An issue regarding `kona-node`'s inability to accept base64-encoded custom L1 chain configurations directly was closed. The resolution indicates that base64 decoding should be handled at the deployment layer, not within `kona-node` itself ([#22491](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22491)).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **Flaky Test: TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement ([#22234](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22234))**
    *   The discussion on this flaky test continued with a detailed consolidation of the issue and related PRs. The root cause was identified as a real race in the test's final assertions, specifically due to the `Reached(eth.Safe, N)` gate checking the wrong postcondition, leading to transient disagreements between the safe label number and by-number reads.
    *   A proposed fix for "Signature A" (the `AssertTxInBlock` failure) is in draft PR [#22321](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22321), which replaces single-shot assertions with retrying checks.
    *   "Signature B" (resend budget exhaustion) was split into a new issue ([#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/22325)) for further investigation, as it appears to be a genuine stall in cross-safety after invalid-message replacement, rather than just a budget issue.
    *   The underlying node-side behavior causing oscillation during light-sequencer recovery is tracked in [#21964](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/issues/21964).
    *   A previous fix ([#21806](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/21806)) for this test was noted, but the current failures postdate it, indicating a different underlying race.
    *   The idea of marking the test as flaky ([#22235](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22325)) was rejected as it would only suppress the issue without fixing it.
