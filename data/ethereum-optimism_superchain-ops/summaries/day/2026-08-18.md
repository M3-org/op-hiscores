# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Daily Update (Aug 18, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
The day saw a cleanup of FOS > FUS transfer comments and the closure of two issues, one related to SDM performance benchmarking and another concerning `kona-node` configuration.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

*   **Codebase Cleanup and Readability**
    *   Streamlined and clarified comments related to FOS > FUS transfer tasks, improving code readability and maintainability without altering functionality or inputs ([#1522](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1522)).

## NEWLY OPENED PULL REQUESTS
No new pull requests were opened today.

## CLOSED ISSUES

*   **SDM Performance Benchmarking Concluded**
    *   The issue tracking performance benchmarking for SDM was closed after extensive testing confirmed no measurable CPU, RSS, or latency impact from SDM at various loads, beyond a minor p99 PostExec delta ([#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20176)).
*   **Kona-node L1 Chain Config Handling**
    *   An issue regarding `kona-node`'s inability to accept base64-encoded custom L1 chain configurations was closed, with the decision that base64 decoding should be handled at the deployment layer rather than within `kona-node` itself ([#22491](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22491)).

## NEW ISSUES
No new issues were reported today.
