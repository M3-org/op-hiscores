## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/17631))
Discussion continues on the correctness and user experience (UX) of `op-node`'s initial EL sync. The current implementation is deemed incorrect as it sets safe and finalized heads to the unsafe head after sync, rather than respecting L1 finalization. Three options for improvement were discussed:
1.  **Set safe/finalized to genesis:** Simplest but requires long derivation time.
2.  **Set safe/finalized to (unsafe head minus sequencer window):** Balances correctness and UX by reducing derivation time to approximately 12 hours. This option is currently favored.
3.  **Allow safe/finalized to be provided externally:** Best UX but adds complexity and relies on trusted external values.
The conversation highlights the need to ensure safe and finalized heads accurately reflect L1's justified/finalized state.

### Non-terminating CI job: memory-all-opn-op-geth ([#19563](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19563))
This issue, concerning a CI job that frequently hangs, was reopened today after another occurrence where `TestChallengerPlaysGame` and `TestProposer` timed out. The root cause was identified as RPC calls within polling loops blocking indefinitely when the in-memory L1 geth instance experiences resource contention under CI load. A draft fix has been opened to create context-bound copies of `FaultDisputeGame` bindings for polling loops to respect per-wait timeouts.

### Flaky test: `TestSupernodeSameTimestampInvalidTransitive` ([#19570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19570))
This test was reopened today due to continued flakiness. A detailed root cause analysis was provided, identifying a race condition in multi-chain transitive invalidation. The test consistently times out because the `RewindEngine` gets stuck in an infinite retry loop when a synthetic payload is rejected due to linking to a previously rejected block. This occurs because `ErrRewindSyntheticPayloadRejected` is not treated as a critical error, leading to futile retries. Potential fixes include treating the error as critical, clearing geth's block rejection cache, or coordinating multi-chain rewinds differently.
