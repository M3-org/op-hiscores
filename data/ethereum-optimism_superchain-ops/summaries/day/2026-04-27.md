**LightCL Genesis Boot Observation**
The issue [#20295](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20295), concerning `safe_l2`, `local_safe_l2`, and `finalized_l2` being zero on a fresh-genesis boot with LightCL, saw significant discussion. On April 27, 2026, karlfloersch identified the likely root cause: genesis `L2BlockRef`s are being treated as unset state due to comparisons relying solely on `Number`, which is zero for both unset and genesis refs. This prevents valid upstream genesis refs from passing "is this ahead?" checks. A recommended fix involves distinguishing "unset" from "genesis" using a full zero-value check or block identity, not just the block number, and adding a regression test for this specific fresh-genesis boot scenario. karlfloersch also inquired if a related bug fix in `optimism#20352` might address this issue.
