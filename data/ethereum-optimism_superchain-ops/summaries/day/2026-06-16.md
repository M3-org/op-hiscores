## ACTIVE ISSUES

### ZK Execution Support in vm-runner
- **[#18325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18325) vm-runner: Support ZK execution**: The discussion revolves around updating `vm-runner` to support running ZK proof programs for verification without actual proof generation. A recent comment suggests extracting relevant parts of the proposer to minimize new code. Earlier, it was noted that actual ZK execution would require Rust due to its dependency on `sp1`, with options to integrate it into `vm-runner` or its Docker image.

### ZK Proofs in CI and Local Development
- **[#21409](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21409) ZK: Decide what to use for proofs in CI and local development**: The primary focus is on deciding between local proving and a mock prover for ZK proofs in CI and local development. The latest comment strongly recommends using a mock prover, citing the high cost and limited benefit of proving in CI. It also suggests that if production infrastructure for local proving is established, proving on op-sepolia via the regular proposer fast-finality path could be considered at a set cadence.
