## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SDM Test Coverage and Benchmarking
- **SDM: add coverage in `rust/kona/tests/proofs`** [#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21344): Discussion indicates that existing coverage for invalid PostExec txs and deposits-only fallback is already present in other areas. The combined scenario is not currently testable in `rust/kona/tests/proofs` due to the `op-geth` engine not supporting SDM execution. The suggestion is to close this issue as the components are individually tested, and the full integration test is blocked by a future engine swap. A core developer also requested a secure channel to discuss technical concerns related to SDM/Lagoon/PostExec/Kona.
- **SDM: baseline benchmarks on `sdm-net-0`** [#22344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22344): Baseline benchmarks were executed, comparing SDM-on vs SDM-off on `sdm-net-0` with an identical StateBloat workload. Key findings include: SDM-on used significantly less gas (395M vs 849M at 10 TPS), payload building and execution overhead were negligible, and RPC `eth_getBlockReceipts` showed no penalty. However, `debug_traceBlockByNumber` failed on 100% of 0x7d blocks with SDM-on, and throughput at 30 TPS was lower with SDM-on, suggesting a potential confirmation-latency increase. Tooling findings also highlighted issues with `op-benchmark` arm64 image and `--remote` targeting.
- **flaky test: `TestP2PFull`** [#22381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22381): An investigation into the flaky `TestP2PFull` test revealed two main failure modes: `no addresses` and `noise: message is too short`. The `no addresses` error is likely due to a race condition during disconnect cleanup and immediate reconnect. While a deterministic model reproduced this, a proposed fix did not eliminate failures in a secure real-host reconnect loop. The Noise error's root cause is still unknown. The current conclusion is that there is insufficient evidence for a root-cause fix, and the issue remains open.
