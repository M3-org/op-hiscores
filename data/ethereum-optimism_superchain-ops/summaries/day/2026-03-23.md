### Flake: TestCLELDivergence
Comments on [#19681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19681) included an investigation update suggesting it might be a real bug rather than just a flaky test. The core issue appears to be a race condition where the derivation pipeline resets the `UnsafeL2` status after an `UnsafeUpdateEvent`, making an intermediate state unobservable. A potential fix is being explored in a separate PR.
