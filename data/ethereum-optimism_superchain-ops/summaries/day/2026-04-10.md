## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **Receipt Root Mismatch on Older Chains ([#19930](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19930)):**
    *   Discussion today focused on identifying the root cause of the receipt root mismatch. It was determined that the problem lies in `op-reth/crates/consensus/src/proof.rs` where the receipt trie was incorrectly built with `encode_2718` for pre-Canyon op-geth rules.
    *   A secondary issue was identified in `validation/mod.rs` where the precomputed `receipt_root_bloom` was unconditionally trusted.
    *   The proposed fix requires correcting the trie encoding with Canyon-awareness and gating the `receipt_root_bloom` shortcut for block validation.
    *   Testing on other old op-stack rollups confirmed the same issue, indicating a broader problem.

*   **Cleanup of Barely-Used Go Dependencies ([#19996](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19996)):**
    *   The discussion highlighted "High Priority" dependencies for removal or consolidation, with plans to address them.
