### chore(ci/kona): simplify kona-proof's ci using kona's monorepo ([#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18773))
Discussion revolves around the implications of simplifying `kona-proof`'s CI by using Kona's monorepo. A key point raised is the dual use of `kona/version.json` for both acceptance tests in `cannon+kona` and the release of `kona-host` in the `op-challenger` Docker image. The desire is to use the latest Kona from the monorepo for acceptance tests. For `kona-host`, the ideal scenario is for it to be released as part of `op-challenger` to ensure consistency, similar to how `op-program`'s host program is managed. The concern is to avoid confusion if `Kona-host` had separate releases that weren't aligned with what's included in `op-challenger`.
