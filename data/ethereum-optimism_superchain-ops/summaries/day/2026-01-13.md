## ACTIVE ISSUES

### chore(ci/kona): simplify kona-proof's ci using kona's monorepo ([#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18773))
Discussion revolves around simplifying the `kona-proof` CI once a related PR in the main Optimism repository is merged. A key point of discussion is the dual use of `kona/version.json` for both acceptance tests (which should use the latest Kona from the monorepo) and for the `op-challenger` docker image's `kona-host` release. The ideal scenario for `kona-host` is to be released by tagging `op-challenger` to ensure consistency, as standalone `kona-host` releases might be confusing if they don't align with what's included in `op-challenger`.
