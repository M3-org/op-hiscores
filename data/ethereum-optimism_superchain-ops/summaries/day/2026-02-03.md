## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **`op-node`: On the Initial EL Sync UX and Correctness** ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/17631))
    *   Discussion continued regarding `op-node`'s behavior of marking the target of initial EL Sync as finalized, even if not truly finalized.
    *   `trianglesphere` noted that this was the intended design but acknowledged it's now considered a bug, highlighting that re-running derivation on a snap-synced node doesn't work for blocks before snap syncing is complete.
    *   `sebastianst` suggested that while execution from the EL unsafe head is necessary, derivation from a prior L1 block with consolidation could verify alignment. They also proposed fixing the code to only advance the unsafe ref while keeping safe and finalized at genesis, with a plan for the transition.
    *   `axelKingsley` argued that EL sync should only drive to finalized content and that Snap Sync should follow the finalized L2 as a maximum target, then resume full sync for the range from finalization to tip.
    *   `trianglesphere` countered that Snap Sync in `op-geth` only works if the state snapshot is available, which is limited to 128 blocks from its tip, making it unsuitable for syncing to the unsafe head.
