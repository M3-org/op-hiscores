## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **`op-conductor`: SEGFAULT during leader election** ([#13363](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/13363))
    The discussion on this issue continues, with a recent comment from 0xVasconcelos confirming the problem persists in the current version. The issue describes a SEGFAULT in `op-conductor` when a peer is killed during leader re-election, potentially due to invalid data being written to raft logs. Previous comments from 0x00101010 and dpulitano explored potential causes such as uncommitted payloads, raft cluster formation, and snapshotting behavior. The problem seems to be exacerbated when conductors are paused during initial sync, leading to snapshotting issues.

*   **Follow-up to activate blob tip oracle** ([#18618](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18618))
    This issue was reopened by ajsutton because a `TODO` referencing it still exists in the codebase, despite the issue being previously closed. The `TODO` indicates that before activating the blob tip oracle, confirmation is needed that `newBlobSavings` is mostly true in production to justify its use.

*   **Support multiple sequencer RPC endpoints for transaction forwarding** ([#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/763))
    The discussion revolves around the best approach to achieve reliability for sequencer RPC endpoints. ajsutton expressed concern that supporting multiple URLs might be user-unfriendly, suggesting that building redundancy into a single URL (similar to web2 practices) would be a better solution. brendontan03 countered that the proposed change is backward compatible and provides optional support for multiple comma-separated URLs, which would have prevented past production incidents caused by single endpoint failures.
