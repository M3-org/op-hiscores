### Blob Tip Oracle Activation Follow-up
The issue [#18618](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18618) was reopened due to a remaining TODO in the codebase referencing it. The TODO indicates that before activating the blob tip oracle, confirmation is needed in production that `newBlobSavings == true` most of the time to justify its use.

### Support for Multiple Sequencer RPC Endpoints
Discussion on [#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/763) revolves around supporting multiple sequencer RPC endpoints for transaction forwarding. @ajsutton expressed concern about the user-friendliness of requiring multiple URLs for reliability, suggesting that built-in redundancy for a single URL might be a better approach. @brendontan03 clarified that the proposed change is fully backward compatible and provides optional support for multiple comma-separated URLs, which would prevent outages caused by single endpoint failures.
