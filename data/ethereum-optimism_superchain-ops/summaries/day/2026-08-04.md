## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SDM Performance Benchmarking and Devnet Rollout
Discussions on [#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20176) and [#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21273) continued, focusing on performance benchmarking for SDM and the rollout of an alpha/beta devnet. A recent comment on [#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20176) suggested pointing the `op-benchmark` tool at the SSD devnet with SDM for performance measurement. For [#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21273), a comment highlighted the availability of rudimentary scripts for driving traffic to an SDM network, which could be used for transaction generation.

### op-node Synchronization Issues with op-reth Backend
The issue of `op-node` failing to sync when `--syncmode=execution-layer` is enabled with the `op-reth` backend ([#20974](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20974)) saw new activity. A user reported that their fullnode pod eventually established two peers after 5 days of continuous errors/retries, noting a consistent 45-second retry cadence for `Ecies(ECIESError(UnreadableStream))` errors.

### reth-optimism-node Integration Test Flakiness
Discussion on the flaky `reth-optimism-node` integration test `peers_negotiate_eth_69` ([#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20973)) was paused. The team has instrumented CI to be notified with a stack trace the next time this flake occurs, hoping to identify the root cause.
