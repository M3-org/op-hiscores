# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Daily Update (Jun 23, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
The day saw a significant update to `op-txverify` links with compression and registry bumps. Several pull requests were opened, including gas-limit resets and a registry submodule bump. Two issues related to testing and design documentation were closed, while discussions continued on intrinsic gas issues and a new feature for `op-reth`.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### `op-txverify` Link Compression and Registry Update
A significant update was implemented to compress `op-txverify` JSON payloads using Solady `LibZip.flzCompress`. This change also involved emitting URL-safe Base64 without padding and switching printed links from legacy `tx=` to compressed `txz=`. The `lib/superchain-registry` was bumped from `8bc6125b` to `3d2`. ([#1483](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1483))

## ACTIVE ISSUES

### `kona-node`: SafeDB ([#20278](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20278))
Discussion continues on the `kona-node` SafeDB implementation. A recent comment from einar-oplabs indicates a preliminary implementation based on the design document, starting with a `SafeDBv1` trait for a 1-to-1 copy, with plans to introduce `SafeDBv2` for further experimentation. geoknee previously emphasized the importance of this working well with interop and proofs, while also encouraging better design considerations beyond simply adopting `op-node`'s approach.

### Optimism Sepolia Intrinsic Gas Too High After Karst Hard Fork ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21457))
The issue of high intrinsic gas on Optimism Sepolia after the Karst hard fork is actively being discussed. yuercl provided a detailed report of the problem, noting that `eth_estimateGas` returns "-32000 intrinsic gas too high" on various RPC providers, while `staticCall` succeeds and explicit `gasLimit` allows transactions to pass. geoknee confirmed this is a known issue and will be addressed in the next release, referencing the Optimism upgrade notices.
