## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **Supernode Derivation Fork and Interop Stall Analysis**: Issue [#22138](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22138) details a critical problem where a supernode's derivation silently forked after an invalidation replacement, leading to a logsDB recovery failure and a stalled interop safety frontier for the entire devnet.
    - Initial analysis by jelias2 corrected the scope, indicating that the cross-safe head was frozen across all CL nodes on both chains, not just a single supernode's logsDB fault. The `safe_l2` was found to be stale for over 4 hours, pinned to replacement blocks from a previous invalidation.
    - Further investigation by jelias2 revised the root cause, eliminating conductor failover and network-wide reorgs as explanations. It was determined that the supernode had been on a private fork since block 259687, silently verifying against a non-existent chain.
    - The issue highlights a detection gap, as standard metrics like `unsafe_l2` and block production appeared normal. An alert on cross-safe lag or `safe_l2` age was suggested as a necessary improvement.
    - The revised framing of the fix suggests that the supernode pins its logsDB frontier at `lastValidParentBlock`, but derivation rebuilds can include and re-derive that block, leading to a deadlock as logsDB cannot rewind below its own rewind target.

- **Kona SP1 Proposer Game Synchronization Refactoring**: Issue [#22019](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22019) discusses the need to decompose and refactor the `sync_games` function in the Kona SP1 proposer, which is currently around 400 lines long.
    - digorithm suggested consolidating the creation-side state, noting that the proposer currently tracks its latest creation across three fields with separate reset logic. The proposal is to merge these into a single lifecycle value (None -> Unconfirmed -> Confirmed -> None) to simplify state management and resets.
    - A longer-term goal is to integrate a proper transaction manager to handle aspects like fee bumps, nonce management, and deduplication, which would further abstract the unconfirmed state from the proposer.

- **Flaky Test: `TestSequencerFailover_ConductorRPC`**: Issue [#22094](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22094) addresses a flaky test that hangs and panics the `op-e2e/system/conductor` package.
    - ajsutton provided an update, stating that a fix is in a draft PR ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#22143](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22143)).
    - The fix addresses two main problems: the `ensureOnlyOneLeader` 30s deadline, which was root-caused to `op-conductor` and caused the cluster to wedge with zero active sequencers, and the 20-minute hang due to `RaftConsensus.Shutdown` blocking indefinitely.
    - The proposed solution for the leadership issue involves the FSM replaying missing ranges, waiting for observed adoption, and escalating to a leader transfer instead of endless retries. The hang is resolved by bounding `raft.Shutdown()`.
    - The test's flake rate was high (247 flakes in `go-tests-short`, 62 in `go-tests-full`), and all failures originated from the same call site, indicating a genuine `op-conductor` availability bug rather than just test flakiness.
