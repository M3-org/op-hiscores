### Flaky Test Investigation: `TestChallengerCountersPreGenesisGame`
An investigation into the flaky test `TestChallengerCountersPreGenesisGame` [#20869](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20869) concluded that the root cause was a timing issue where the `plannedRollupStartBlockNumber` was calculated too early, leading to a collision with the migration transaction's receipt block on some CI runs. A fix has been proposed in #20874, which involves increasing the `preGenesisRollupStartBlockDelay` and adding a post-migration L1-head re-read with a fast-fail invariant.

### `debug_executePayload` Usage Clarification
An inquiry into the necessity of `debug_executePayload` and related crates [#20879](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20879) determined that these components are not removable. They serve as an active, opt-in fast-path for fault-proof witness prefetching in both `op-program` (Go) and `kona` (Rust), with a maintained server implementation in `op-reth`. The "experimental" label signifies an opt-in optimization with a canonical fallback, not a lack of maturity. Removing it would result in a loss of performance optimization for fault-proof workloads and require significant deprecation and removal of related code. The issue was closed as "wontfix."
