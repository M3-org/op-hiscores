### Missing Events on `eth_getLogs` Calls
Discussion on missing events for `eth_getLogs` calls immediately after `newHeads` [#667](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/667) received a new comment. The comment suggested that the issue might be related to backend freshness or propagation lag, especially if `newHeads` and `eth_getLogs` can land on different backends behind a load balancer. It recommended verifying that websocket subscriptions and RPC calls are pinned to the same node and comparing `eth_blockNumber`, `eth_getBlockByNumber`, and `eth_getLogs` before and after a delay.
