# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Daily Update (Aug 28, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
Today's focus was on enhancing multi-chain support for ownership transfers and executing key ownership handovers for Metal, Mode, Zora, and Dust to Conduit. Significant progress was also made in testing a new L1 chain deployment on Plataberget.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Enhanced Multi-Chain Ownership Transfer Capabilities
The `TransferOwners` and `TransferL2PAOFromL1` templates now support transferring ownership across multiple chains simultaneously to a single new owner within a single Safe transaction, improving efficiency and flexibility for Superchain operations. ([#1519](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1519))

### Ownership Handover to Conduit for Metal, Mode, Zora, and Dust
Key ownership of L1 ProxyAdmin, DisputeGameFactory, and L2 ProxyAdmin for Metal, Mode, Zora, and Dust chains has been transferred to Conduit's designated Safes on both mainnet and Sepolia. ([#1521](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1521))

## NEWLY OPENED PULL REQUESTS

## CLOSED ISSUES

### Plataberget Test Deployment and Withdrawal Testing
The Plataberget test deployment was successfully completed, including bootstrapping the OPCM, deploying a new L1 chain, and testing a deposit and withdrawal path. The withdrawal test involved impersonating the proposer, creating a permissioned game, fast-forwarding the chain, and finalizing the withdrawal. ([#22696](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22696))
