### Persistent Flaky Test: `TestInteropHappyTx`
The `TestInteropHappyTx` continues to exhibit flakiness, with a new occurrence reported today. The test failed due to a `failed to deploy EventLogger` error, where the deploy transaction was never included because both chains' sequencer actions starved simultaneously. This behavior is consistent with the driver event-loop starvation fingerprint identified in [#21952](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21952).
