## ACTIVE ISSUES
### Flaky Flashblocks Transfer Test Investigation
Discussions continued on the flaky `TestFlashblocksTransfer` ([#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19934)). The latest comments indicate that the test's failure mode has been identified: `op-rbuilder` is failing to produce flashblocks, causing the test to hang. A draft PR ([ethereum-optimism/optimism#20600](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20600)) has been opened to restructure the test for clearer diagnostics, distinguishing between transaction confirmation issues and flashblock generation failures. The underlying `op-rbuilder` bug remains to be addressed.

### Kona and op-node Brotli Divergence
Significant progress was made in investigating the `brotli` channel divergence between Kona and `op-node` ([#19333](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19333)). It was discovered that the Rust `brotli` crate used by Kona is non-spec-compliant, silently accepting corrupted streams and producing incorrect output, which could lead to consensus divergence. A critical Kona wrapper bug was also identified, accounting for 95% of observed divergences. The recommended path forward includes landing the Kona wrapper fix, investigating the remaining corruption-only divergences, and ensuring strict-mode parity for brotli termination. A library swap is deemed unnecessary.

### Safedb Proof Requirements
Discussion continued regarding the `safedb` proof requirements ([#20593](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20593)). A key point raised is the need to review how `op-supernode` utilizes the safe head db, as it is currently the primary use case. It was also noted that the `safedb` might become redundant once proofs transition to ZK, as its original purpose of challenging early proposals before L1 batch data would no longer be relevant if output roots are from the canonical chain.
