# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Daily Update (Jul 16, 2026)
## OVERVIEW 
The day's development focused on documentation updates for executed governance tasks and the removal of prematurely merged OPCM V800 templates.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Documentation of Executed Governance Tasks
Four OP Mainnet governance tasks related to signer rotations were marked as `EXECUTED` in their respective READMEs, with links to their on-chain execution transactions. This was a documentation-only change. ([#1503](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1503))

### Removal of Premature OPCM V800 Templates
The `OPCMUpgradeV800` and `OPCMMigrateV800` templates, along with their integration tests and example tasks, were removed. These templates were merged before the V800 OPCM was deployed on-chain. ([#1501](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1501))

### Resolution of op-proposer Revert Issue
An issue where `op-proposer`'s `HasProposedSince` function reverted on `SuperPermissionedDisputeGame` (type 5) was closed. The problem stemmed from `op-proposer` attempting to read `claimData(0)` using the `FaultDisputeGameABI` on every recent game. This issue has been fixed.
