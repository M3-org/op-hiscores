## CLOSED ISSUES

### `op-node` Sync Mode Resolution
The issue where `op-node` was getting stuck in EL sync mode after restart when reth already had synced data has been closed. Fixes implemented to stabilize `op-node` driving `op-reth` are cited as the reason for closure. ([#18468](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18468))

### ProtocolVersions Contract Removal
The task to remove the `ProtocolVersions` contract and its deployment paths has been completed and the issue closed, following the `op-node` no longer watching it. ([#20309](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20309))
