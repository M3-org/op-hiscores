## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **Precompile Gas Cost for Glamsterdam (EIP-8037)** ([#19380](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19380))
    *   A recent comment suggests the issue might be stale as written, after testing the current oracle bytecode against an Amsterdam-capable EVM.
    *   The `PRECOMPILE_CALL_RESERVED_GAS` is primarily for precompile call setup, not guaranteeing whole-function completion.
    *   EIP-8037/8038 drafts indicate that even a `200_000` value might be insufficient if the goal is whole-function completion, potentially requiring closer to `400_000`.
    *   The recommendation is to either close the issue as unnecessary for correctness (while adding Amsterdam gas-estimation/regression coverage) or redefine it to explicitly guarantee whole-function success at the guard threshold with an updated value.

*   **Flaky Test: TestUnsafeChainNotStalling_CLSync Family** ([#21041](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21041))
    *   A new occurrence of the flake was observed, showing a total EL-backfill stall rather than a slow catch-up.
    *   The issue appears to be an EL peer-management defect, where the follower `op-reth`'s only EL peer disconnected at the start of backfill and never re-established, leading to a stall.
    *   Previous analysis indicated the root cause was an `op-reth` Engine API stall, not a test budget bug, with `context deadline exceeded` errors during payload execution.
    *   The investigation is pivoting to focus on the EL peer-management defect and potential `op-reth` issues.
