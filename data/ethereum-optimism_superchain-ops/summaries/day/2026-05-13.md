## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Flaky Test: `TestFollowSource_HeadsDivergeThenConverge`
The flaky test [#19821](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19821) has resurfaced with a different signature. The new failure is a convergence timeout on `memory-all-opn-op-reth`, where `local-safe` never catches up to `cross-safe` on the follower within the test deadline. The `op-node` repeatedly resets due to the EL (op-reth) being stuck in `SYNCING`. This is considered a recurrent flake.

### Flaky Test: `TestFlashblocksTransfer`
The flaky test [#19934](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19934) flaked again, with the error message indicating that "Alice's transfer never confirmed on the sequencer EL within 1m0s". A previous draft PR [#20600](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20600) was noted to make the test fail faster and provide clearer diagnostics, but the underlying `op-rbuilder` flashblock-emission bug remains.

### Interop + Batcher: Use Verification-Aware CurrentL1
Discussion on [#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19944) suggests that the batcher's current logic, designed for sequencer restart regressions, doesn't fully account for L1 reorgs. The `currentL1` is observed to get stuck at a zero block reference, causing the batcher to spin. It's argued that the problem lies with `op-node` / `op-supernode` not correctly progressing `CurrentL1` after a reorg, rather than the batcher needing to handle `CurrentL1` going backwards. A related PR [#20694](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/20694) was linked.

### op-supernode: cross-safe head stalls after deep L1 reorg
This issue [#20134](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20134) is related to [#19944](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19944), with a comment suggesting that the fix should be in `op-node` / `op-supernode` rather than the batcher.

### Flaky Test: `TestWithdrawal_Permissioned`
The flaky test [#20345](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20345) continues to fail, with another instance reported where the test ran for over 20 minutes before failing.
