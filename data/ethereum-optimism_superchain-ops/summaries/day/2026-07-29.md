## ACTIVE ISSUES

### SDM Devnet Rollout and Benchmarking ([#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21273))
The `sdm-net-0` spec has been merged, and the `netchef CreateNetwork` workflow has completed terraform and L2 contract deployment. The team is currently awaiting approval of generated artifacts and k8s manifests. Key gaps identified against the `kupcake` devnet include the need for contract and transaction load, an SDM Grafana dashboard, and a policy hot-enable/disable feature (which requires protocol code). The plan involves finishing the rollout, verifying SDM live, standing up load, porting the SDM dashboard, reviving the hot-toggle with the Core team, and conducting baseline benchmarks.

### Audit `op-reth` CLI flags ([#21687](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21687))
Discussions continue regarding the auditing of `op-reth` CLI flags to either remove unsupported combinations or add support for them. There's a debate on whether to remove the "minimal" pruning option or adjust its defaults and clarify its risks, as it's a preset of different pruning settings. The recent update to documentation about supported pruning configs was also noted.

### `kona-sp1-proposer`: which games should the proposer defend when challenged ([#22111](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22111))
A comment suggests that no changes should be made at this point regarding which games the proposer defends. The current strategy of keeping the challenger's bond for every valid game proven is seen as sufficient, as it's higher than the proving cost. The "proof of whale" attack is acknowledged as a known weakness, with blacklisting or retiring games being the current defense until bond economics are improved.

### `op-reth`: update reth dependency to 2.4.2 / 2.5.0 ([#22115](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22115))
The `op-reth-premium` team is waiting for an upstream `reth` change to be pulled in, which will allow for a cleaner solution to the state-root wait issue. This upstream fix would expose the result receiver, enabling direct `recv_timeout` calls and removing the need for a proxy channel and detached OS thread, thereby eliminating a potential unbounded resource leak in the current workaround.
