## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Fatal Panic in StateRootTask
Issue [#20462](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20462) describes a fatal panic in `StateRootTask` when upgrading `op-reth` from 1.x to 2.x. The latest update confirms that the `--engine.legacy-state-root` flag does not resolve the panic. The panic, an assertion failure in `alloy-trie-0.9.5/src/nodes/branch.rs`, occurs non-deterministically during parallel trie computation against 1.x database states, affecting both `StateRootTask` and `Parallel` strategies.

### op-supernode: VerifiedRequiredL1 Calculation Error
Issue [#20481](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20481) highlights a bug in `op-supernode` where `Superroot.atTimestamp` incorrectly tracks `minVerifiedRequiredL1` as the minimum of per-chain `verifiedL1` values instead of the maximum. This leads to an incorrect `SuperRootResponseData.VerifiedRequiredL1` being published. The issue notes that relevant acceptance tests are pending migration.
