## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **Flaky Test: `TestStorageProofUsingSimpleStorageContract`** ([#20376](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20376)): The issue was reopened due to a recurrence of the flake, presenting a different symptom but the same underlying race condition. The `WaitForProofsStoreBlock` gate is now failing with a 30s timeout, indicating the proofs ExEx store on the validator never advanced past genesis. The proofs indexing engine on the sequencer appeared starved, with all `trie::engine::*` logs appearing in a single burst at teardown. The root cause is suspected to be proofs-ExEx indexing latency under concurrent-devnet CI load. Suggested directions include raising the `require.Eventually` timeout and investigating why the proofs indexing worker makes no progress for extended periods.
