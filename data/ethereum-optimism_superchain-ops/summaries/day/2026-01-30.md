### op-supernode: Safe Label Adjusting ([#18976](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18976))
The conversation revolved around the complex interaction of "safe" labels across EL, CL, and Superchain layers and how to adjust them for better clarity and correctness.
- **axelKingsley** praised the proposed diagram and terminology, noting the potential for a great design if the CL can correctly apply Local Safe payloads. They also suggested adding a mechanism to mark RPC router responses to distinguish between "Local Safe" and "Super Node" safe states.
- **geoknee** proposed options for handling data reading from CL and EL, suggesting either adorning ELs with a "local safe" label or having the supernode communicate with the CL to determine which block to retrieve from the EL, similar to the batcher's approach. They also outlined a strategy for the supernode to control EL-[S] and EL-[F] directly by intercepting and making its own engine calls.
