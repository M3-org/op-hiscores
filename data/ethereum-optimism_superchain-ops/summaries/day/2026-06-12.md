## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Proofs: Increasing `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and Reducing Air Gap Constants
Discussion continued on increasing `MAX_CLOCK_DURATION` and reducing air gap constants for faster ZK withdrawals while maintaining a 7-day total. A recent comment by Inphi questioned the assumption of an unbounded max history, suggesting that the max history for a 1-day withdrawal proof would not exceed one day, as a chain of proposals can be built by linking to the parent game. They also noted that challenging does not require history, as the withdrawal root in isthmus allows for reconstruction of the correct output root. ([#20774](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20774))

### Centralizing OP Fork to L1 Fork Mapping in the Rust Stack
A detailed analysis by sebastianst revealed that the OP fork to implied L1 (Ethereum) fork mapping is duplicated in seven places within the codebase, not just three as initially thought. A latent bug was identified where `Karst → Osaka` mapping was missing in `RollupConfig::ethereum_fork_activation`, which could lead to issues if queried. A comprehensive plan was proposed to centralize this mapping in `alloy-op-hardforks` as a canonical, sparse table, with derived views for cumulative and inverse lookups. The plan also includes rewiring direct consumers, eliminating OP-fork knowledge from `kona-engine`, and implementing a consistency test for `op-revm` instead of adding a direct dependency. ([#21338](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21338))
