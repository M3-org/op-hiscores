## ACTIVE ISSUES

### Addressing `op-core/superchain` Go Module Consumability
Discussions are ongoing regarding the issue where `op-core/superchain` is unconsumable by downstream Go modules due to `superchain-configs.zip` being gitignored ([#22678](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22678)). The latest comments from sebastianst provide a detailed analysis:
- **Git-LFS is not an option**: It was confirmed that `git-lfs` cannot be used for module zips as `cmd/go` builds from raw git data and `proxy.golang.org` does not run `lfs smudge`.
- **Import-graph measurement**: An analysis revealed that 206 of 560 monorepo packages currently reach the bundle. Several accidental dependencies were identified, and strategies to reduce this count to ~109 genuine data consumers were outlined.
- **Cleanup PRs incoming**: Two PRs ([#22709](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22709), [#22712](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22712)) are planned to split the `depset` loader, delete dead code, and extend `depguard` tests to prevent new accidental dependencies.
- **Cost of committing the zip**: geoknee previously quantified the cost of committing the zip, noting that `op-geth` already tracks its copy without significant issues. The marginal cost of one bump is about ~245 KB, leading to roughly 3 MB/year, which is much lower than initial estimates of "`.git` growth."
- **Loose files vs. zip**: It was noted that loose files offer better reviewability for diffs but provide minimal storage benefits over a zip due to the nature of the compressed genesis files.
- **Submodule limitations**: Embedding directly from the `superchain-registry` submodule is not feasible as `//go:embed` cannot escape its package directory and submodule content is absent from published modules.
- **Reducing package reach**: A fourth direction was proposed to reduce the number of packages that need to reach the bundle at all, by fixing accidental dependencies like `op-service/opprof` and `op-core/interop/depset`. This includes a `depguard` test to prevent future accidental edges.
