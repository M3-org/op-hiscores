## ACTIVE ISSUES
- **Withdrawal Proofs and Upgrade Strategy**: Discussion on [#18517](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18517) (contracts: Prove withdrawals using chain-specific root claim) indicates a decision to move this to the backlog. The `OptimismPortalInterop` changes are complete, but updating `OptimismPortal2` must wait until U19 is executed. This is to ensure withdrawals continue to function immediately post-upgrade, requiring the `OptimismPortal2` upgrade to occur separately and well after `FaultDisputeGames` support the new `rootClaimByChainId(uint256)` method.
- **op-supernode SyncStatus API**: On [#18947](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18947) (op-supernode: Introduce SyncStatus API), the discussion with @axelKingsley led to a refined interface for the SyncStatus API. It should include the `op-node` SyncStatus for every chain, the sync status of every verification activity, and for interop activity, the latest verified timestamp and the current L1. Additionally, the SyncStatus should report the latest verified L2 to simplify acceptance tests.
- **EIP-1559 Parameter Decoding Fix**: Issue [#18951](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18951) (op-alloy: Fix EIP-1559 params decoding) is being actively discussed, with a community member expressing interest in taking on the task to properly catch zero elasticities during EIP-1559 parameter decoding at the `op-alloy` level.
