### op-geth Decoupling Progress
Issue [#20263](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20263) regarding the decoupling of `opgeth` by swapping call-sites to `op-core/*` across `op-node`, `op-service`, and `op-batcher` was closed. The latest comment from sebastianst confirms that all sub-issues are closed and the remaining scope was landed via PRs #21791 and #21904. This means `op-node/op-service/op-batcher` no longer reference `op-geth-fork` transaction/params symbols outside the `op-core` differential tests, marking a significant step in the `op-geth` decoupling plan.

### Persistent Flake in reth-optimism-node Integration Tests
Discussion continued on issue [#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20973) concerning a `SIGSEGV` during teardown in the `reth-optimism-node` integration test `peers_negotiate_eth_69`. einar-oplabs noted that a different approach offered in PR #20983 might solve the problem without directly addressing the identified race condition in upstream reth, presenting it as an attractive workaround. This follows a detailed analysis by sebastianst on July 20th, which refuted the initial hypothesis of "detached payload thread polls a torn-down runtime" and suggested that the actual mechanism of the segfault remains unidentified. The analysis also recommended capturing a core dump/faulting stack in CI as the next crucial step to diagnose the issue.
