## ACTIVE ISSUES

- **CL Sync Recovery Stalls**: Discussion on [#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21076) continues, with a new A/B test reproducing the issue without `op-supernode`, indicating the problem lies within the `op-node`/`op-reth` CL derive/build Engine API path. The latest update details a half-completed transition shape where `Block added to canonical chain` appears but `Received new payload` and `Canonical chain committed` do not, leading to timeouts. The next steps involve capturing traces and focused regression testing.
- **Cross-Safe Backlog Recovery**: Issue [#21117](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21117) discusses the `op-supervisor`/`op-supernode` cross-safe's inability to drain a backlog faster than real-time after a stall. The latest comment suggests that the observed behavior, where the cross-safe head is 12 hours behind, strongly resembles a scenario where the sequencing window has expired, requiring manual intervention and sequencer recovery mode.
