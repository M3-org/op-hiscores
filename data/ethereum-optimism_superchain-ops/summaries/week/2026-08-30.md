# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Aug 30 - Sep 5, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on critical governance transitions for the Unichain ecosystem and resolving architectural bottlenecks within the OP Stack's Go module structure. A primary achievement was the initiation of the ProxyAdmin Owner (PAO) transition for Unichain Mainnet and Sepolia, aligning them with the standard OP-governed model. Significant progress was also made in addressing the consumability of `op-core/superchain` by downstream modules, with a clear technical roadmap established to reduce package dependencies and manage configuration data. Additionally, protocol stability was improved through the resolution of span batch validation issues in Holocene.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Unichain Governance Integration
Work is underway to bring Unichain into alignment with standard Superchain governance models. This involves transitioning ownership configurations for both L1 and L2 components.
*   **Mainnet Transition:** New tasks were added to facilitate the Unichain Mainnet ProxyAdmin Owner transition ([#1527](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1527)).
*   **Testnet Alignment:** Parallel tasks were introduced for the Unichain Sepolia testnet transition (tasks sep/107-108) via [#1533](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1533).

### Tooling & Validation Enhancements
The team continues to harden the deployment and configuration templates used across the Superchain.
*   **Task Validation:** Enforcement for V800 task validation was introduced within project templates to ensure higher consistency and error checking during operations ([#1534](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1534)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Holocene Span Batch Validation:** A critical issue regarding the validation of span batch overlap content against the safe chain in Holocene was resolved. The fix ensures that batch elements are properly validated, maintaining protocol integrity ([#22351](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22351)).

### Active Issues & Technical Debt
*   **Go Module Consumability (`op-core/superchain`):** Extensive discussion occurred regarding the inability of downstream modules to consume `op-core/superchain` because `superchain-configs.zip` is currently gitignored ([#22678](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22678)). 
    *   **Analysis:** The team determined that Git-LFS is not a viable solution due to how `cmd/go` and the Go proxy handle raw git data. 
    *   **Strategy:** The current plan involves reducing the "package reach" from 206 to approximately 109 genuine data consumers by fixing accidental dependencies. 
    *   **Upcoming Work:** Two PRs ([#22709](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22709), [#22712](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/optimism/pull/22712)) are planned to split the `depset` loader and implement `depguard` tests to prevent future dependency bloat.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by deep technical coordination regarding the monorepo's architecture. Contributors **sebastianst** and **geoknee** provided critical data-driven insights into the Go module issue, specifically quantifying the storage costs of committing configuration zips (~3 MB/year) versus the benefits of loose files for reviewability. This collaborative analysis has moved the project toward a concrete resolution for a long-standing downstream developer experience bottleneck.
