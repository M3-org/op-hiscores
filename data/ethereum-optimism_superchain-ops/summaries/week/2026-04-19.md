# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Apr 19 - 25, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on streamlining Superchain operations through the introduction of new automation templates and the resolution of critical interoperability milestones. Significant progress was made in refining the Layer 2 Chain Manager (L2CM) and Superroot upgrade paths, alongside essential maintenance of the Deputy Pause Module. While the team successfully closed several long-standing issues related to feature flags and batcher configurations, efforts continue to stabilize the testing suite against persistent race conditions and flaky assertions in the sequencing and flashblock modules.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Operational Templates & Configuration
The team introduced several templates to standardize and automate recurring administrative tasks within the Superchain ecosystem.
*   **Superroot & Migration Scaffolding:** Added a new operations template for Superroot upgrades ([#1397](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1397)) and scaffolded the `OPCMMigrateV700` task template ([#1404](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1404)) to prepare for upcoming protocol migrations.
*   **Batcher & Signer Management:** A new template was implemented for configuring batchers and signers, enhancing the Superchain-ops Ownership Tracker workflow ([#1393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1393)).
*   **SystemConfig:** Initiated a template for SystemConfig Owner transfers ([#1406](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1406)).

### Infrastructure & Security Maintenance
*   **Deputy Pause Module (DPM):** Addressed a critical bug in the Sepolia DPM key rotation, involving re-signing rotations and migrating off the FoundationOperationsSafe ([#1402](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1402)). Subsequent chores ensured accurate on-chain tracking by updating `addresses.toml` ([#1401](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1401)) and managing execution states for rotations 074 and 075 ([#1403](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1403), [#1405](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1405)).

### Protocol & Interoperability Refinement
*   **Kona & Interop Logic:** Resolved a critical output root mismatch in Kona caused by deposit-only block replacements ([#19848](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19848)). The team also refined `CrossL2Inbox` deployment logic to ensure transactions are only generated when necessary, matching Go-based implementations ([#19311](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19311)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **L2CM Milestones:** Several issues regarding Interop Betanet deployment and Sepolia activation were closed as they are now integrated into the broader "Upgrade 19" process ([#20088](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20088), [#20090](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20090)).
*   **Feature Flags:** Successfully integrated feature flags for OPSuccinct into the OPCM `_devFeatureBitmap` ([#18419](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18419)).
*   **Batcher Throttling:** Confirmed existing support for batcher throttling in `op-reth` and `op-rbuilder`, closing redundant requests ([#18764](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18764)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Flaky Tests:** Testing stability remains a primary focus. `TestFlashblocksStream` ([#19883](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19883)) and `TestReorgUnsafeHead` ([#20198](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20198)) were identified as having race conditions or incorrect assertions. A fix for `TestSequencingWindowExpiry` is currently in draft to address a write lock issue in `OpProofsExEx` ([#20212](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20212)).
*   **Sequencer Behavior:** A new issue was opened regarding "LightCL" sequencers performing unnecessary engine resets on restart, which may cause EL head rewinds ([#20294](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20294)).
*   **Kona Receipt Storage:** Ongoing discussion highlights a potential risk where block replacements in `kona-interop` do not store generated receipts, potentially leading to consolidation failures ([#19266](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19266)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw active coordination between protocol developers regarding the deprecation of `op-geth` in favor of `op-reth`, with maintainers providing clear timelines (May 31st) and documentation to users ([#20203](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20203)). There was also significant cross-repository collaboration to resolve Docker image tag failures for recent releases, with the team providing immediate workarounds via `-rc.1` tags while permanent fixes are implemented ([#20228](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20228)).
