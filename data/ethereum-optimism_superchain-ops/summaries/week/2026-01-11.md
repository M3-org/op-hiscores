# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Jan 11 - 17, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on significant infrastructure upgrades across the Superchain ecosystem, headlined by the redeployment of Betanet to v3 and the broad enablement of the SaferSafes module. Development efforts prioritized network security and liveness through enhanced monitoring tools for dispute resolution and the introduction of a supernode provider for the challenger. While progress was made in transaction simulation with fixes to `eth_simulateV1`, the team continues to investigate persistent issues regarding `op-node` safe-head advancement efficiency.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Betanet Infrastructure & Upgrades
The team executed a major transition for the Betanet environment, focusing on versioning and prestate updates.
*   **Betanet v3 Redeployment:** The network was successfully redeployed to v3, necessitating comprehensive updates to configuration files and `addresses.json` ([#1338](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1338)).
*   **Prestate & Task Management:** Work was completed on the u18 betanet prestate update task and general status tracking for the environment ([#1337](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1337), [#1339](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1339)).

### Security & Network Liveness (SaferSafes)
A concerted effort was made to roll out the SaferSafes module across multiple networks to improve security and operational reliability.
*   **Multi-Network Enablement:** New features and configurations were deployed to enable SaferSafes on Betanet/Sepolia, FUS, and FOS ([#1340](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1340), [#1343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1343)).
*   **Ongoing Tasks:** Additional tasks related to SaferSafes implementation are currently being processed under [#1342](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1342).

### Challenger & Monitoring Enhancements
Improvements were made to the fault proof and monitoring stack to support multi-node operations.
*   **Dispute Monitoring:** The `dispute-mon` dashboard was updated to include new multi-node metrics, providing better visibility into the state of network disputes ([#18205](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18205)).
*   **Supernode Provider:** A supernode provider was introduced for the `challenger`, intended to eventually replace the existing supervisor provider for more robust performance ([#18524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18524)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Transaction Simulation:** Two critical issues regarding `eth_simulateV1` were resolved. Notably, a fix for the "missing deposit transaction" error during DA footprint calculation was released in `op-geth` v1.101605.0 ([#739](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/739), [#14690](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/14690)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **`op-node` Optimization:** Discussion persists on [#15393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/15393) regarding safe-head advancement. Current data suggests that `op-node` is still performing Fork Choice Updates (FCU) on every safe head update rather than buffering them into a single aggregate message per channel, indicating that previous fix attempts may have been unsuccessful.
*   **CI Simplification:** A new initiative was launched to simplify the `kona-proof` CI by leveraging the Kona monorepo ([#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18773)). This involves coordinating the use of `kona/version.json` between acceptance tests and `op-challenger` docker releases to ensure version consistency.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week's activity showed strong coordination between protocol developers and DevOps, particularly regarding the Betanet v3 migration and the SaferSafes rollout. Technical discussions on issue [#18773](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18773) highlight a focus on long-term maintainability and release consistency for the Kona stack. Meanwhile, the ongoing troubleshooting of the `op-node` FCU logic ([#15393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/15393)) demonstrates active peer review and performance auditing within the contributor base.
