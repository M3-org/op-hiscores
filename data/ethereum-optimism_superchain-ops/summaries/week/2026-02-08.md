# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Feb 8 - 14, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on scaling operational efficiency and expanding the Superchain ecosystem through refined multi-signature management and new network tasks. Key achievements include the introduction of a template for bulk signer rotations and the deployment of revenue-sharing tasks for Ink and Soneium on the Sepolia testnet. Additionally, the team resolved critical tracking issues for blob priority fees while initiating technical discussions on sequencer redundancy and the production readiness of the blob tip oracle.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Operational Tooling & Multi-Signature Management
To streamline secure contract management, the team introduced a new template that allows for the simultaneous rotation of multiple owners within a single multisig task ([#1368](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1368)). This improvement reduces the operational overhead previously required for sequential signer updates.

### Superchain Expansion & Task Execution
Significant progress was made in onboarding and updating tasks for various L2 chains within the ecosystem:
*   **Ink & Soneium:** New Sepolia tasks were established for revenue sharing, including all necessary configuration and documentation ([#1369](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1369), [#1374](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1374)).
*   **Soneium Minato:** A dedicated task (065) was created for U18 on the Soneium Minato testnet to expand network-specific testing ([#1370](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1370)).
*   **Execution Updates:** Several tasks reached completion, including the Unichain U18 task and the multi-chain "040-U18-op-ink-mmz-arena-swell" task in the ETH environment ([#1372](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1372), [#1375](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1375)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Blob Fee Tracking:** The team successfully closed the issue regarding `op-batcher/txmgr` blob priority fee tracking, marking the completion of the final required fix ([#18737](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18737)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Blob Tip Oracle Activation:** Issue [#18618](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18618) was reopened to address a remaining TODO. The team is seeking production data to confirm that `newBlobSavings == true` consistently before fully activating the oracle.
*   **Sequencer Redundancy:** A technical debate is ongoing regarding support for multiple sequencer RPC endpoints ([#763](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/763)). While the proposed change offers backward-compatible support for comma-separated URLs to prevent outages, some maintainers have raised concerns about user-friendliness versus built-in redundancy.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was characterized by active technical design discussions between contributors like @ajsutton and @brendontan03 regarding protocol reliability. The dialogue around sequencer RPCs highlights a healthy tension between providing flexible power-user features and maintaining a simple developer experience. The steady flow of PRs for new ecosystem participants like Ink and Soneium indicates strong ongoing coordination between the core Optimism team and Superchain partners.
