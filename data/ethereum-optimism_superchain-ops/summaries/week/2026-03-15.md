# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Mar 15 - 21, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on operational maintenance and hardening the stability of the Superchain infrastructure through aggressive flaky test resolution and CI/CD optimization. Significant progress was made in migrating the project's build system from Makefiles to Justfiles, streamlining the developer experience. Meanwhile, technical discussions centered on critical protocol behaviors, specifically addressing `op-node` initial sync correctness and `op-reth` memory stability, ensuring the reliability of the OP Stack as it scales across multiple chains like Base and World Chain.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Operational Task Management
The team managed several rotation and upgrade tasks across various networks, reflecting a shift in near-term operational priorities.
*   **Upgrade Cancellations:** Pending revenue-sharing upgrade tasks for "ink" and "soneium" on Ethereum and Sepolia were canceled ([#1382](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1382)).
*   **Rotations:** Marked previous FUS and FOS rotations as executed on Ethereum ([#1383](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1383)), while canceling several Sepolia-specific rotations for FUS, SC, FOS, and the Chain Governor ([#1385](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1385)).
*   **New Proposer Tasks:** Introduced new Arena-Z proposer rotation tasks for both Sepolia and Mainnet ([#1384](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1384)) and initiated fee vault update tasks ([#1386](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1386), [#1387](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1387)).

### CI/CD & Infrastructure Improvements
A major effort to modernize the repository's tooling was completed this week.
*   **Makefile Migration:** Successfully migrated all Makefiles to `justfiles` across the repository. This included updating CI configurations, Dockerfiles, and documentation to support the new command runner ([#19546](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19546)).
*   **Test Hardening:** Addressed several non-terminating CI jobs. A fix was drafted for `TestChallengerPlaysGame` and other interop tests that were timing out due to RPC calls hanging under CI resource contention ([#19563](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19563)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Flaky Test Resolution:** A significant number of flaky tests were resolved, including `TestBreakTimestampInvariant`, `TestExpiredMessage`, and `TestCLIBootstrapForge`. Fixes generally involved increasing timeouts to 5 minutes to accommodate CI load and resolving nonce races ([#18137](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18137), [#19627](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19627)).
*   **Supernode Reporting:** Fixed an issue where `op-supernode` incorrectly reported the verified L1 instead of the derived L1 ([#18651](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18651)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **`op-node` Sync Correctness:** Ongoing debate persists regarding how `op-node` handles finalization during initial EL sync. Proposals include using a 12-hour fallback window or a separate finalization gadget to prevent the node from incorrectly marking heads as finalized before derivation is complete ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/17631)).
*   **`op-reth` Stability:** Reports of memory leaks and "database read transaction open for too long" warnings continue to affect nodes on World Chain and Base. Additionally, state root mismatches on Base mainnet are being investigated as potential implementation-specific issues ([#19212](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19212), [#19532](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19532)).
*   **Supernode Interop Races:** Investigation is underway for `TestSupernodeSameTimestampInvalidTransitive`, where a race condition in multi-chain invalidation causes the `RewindEngine` to enter an infinite retry loop ([#19570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19570)).
*   **Kona Configuration:** A new issue was raised regarding hardcoded message expiry windows in Kona, suggesting a move toward configurable dependency sets ([#19636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19636)).
