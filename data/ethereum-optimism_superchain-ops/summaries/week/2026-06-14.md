# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Jun 14 - 20, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused heavily on network migration tasks and infrastructure upgrades for the Superchain, specifically targeting Ink Sepolia and Unichain Sepolia. A significant effort was made to clean up the repository, closing numerous stale issues related to UX, testing, and documentation to streamline the development workflow. Technical discussions also advanced the `op-geth` decoupling strategy and the integration of ZK execution support, signaling a shift toward more modular and diverse proof systems within the OP Stack.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Network Migrations and Upgrades
The team executed several critical tasks to support the evolving Superchain ecosystem.
*   **Ink Sepolia Migration:** Initiated migration tasks for Ink Sepolia, transitioning from Gelato to OP Enterprise ([#1464](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1464)).
*   **Unichain Sepolia:** Updated the status and reflected the U19 upgrade for Unichain Sepolia ([#1467](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1467), [#1468](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1468)).
*   **Migration Exercises:** Conducted "Type A" migration exercise tasks for Sepolia to ensure protocol readiness ([#1463](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1463)).

### Protocol Infrastructure & Bug Fixes
Refinements were made to the Optimism Portal and configuration management.
*   **Fee Vault Configuration:** Fixed fee vault setups by transitioning from initialization to setters ([#1469](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1469)).
*   **OPCM Upgrade Templates:** Identified and addressed a structural issue in the `OPCMUpgradeV800` template where the `SUPER_PERMISSIONED_GAME` type required simplified argument structures ([#1466](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/1466)).
*   **Task Execution:** Successfully executed task `099-U19-op-ink-mmz-soneium` ([#1465](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1465)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
A major "spring cleaning" of the repository resulted in the closure of several stale or resolved items:
*   **UX & Tooling:** Issues regarding Ledger detection, wallet support in justfiles, and Safe address environment variables were closed as stale or addressed by existing features ([#400](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/400), [#372](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/372), [#457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/457)).
*   **Testing & Coverage:** Stale issues for test coverage tracking and skeleton upgrade templates were resolved or folded into CI checks ([#637](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/637), [#698](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/698)).
*   **ZK Dispute Games:** Issues regarding `address(0)` bond credit claiming in `ZKDisputeGame` were closed in favor of tracking in a centralized Optimism repository ([#19743](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19743)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **op-geth Decoupling:** Significant technical discussion is ongoing regarding migrating tests and making `op-service/sources` the primary OP-aware client. Key challenges include handling receipt index-correlation and the canonical nature of `PostExecTx` in Lagoon ([#20265](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20265), [#20264](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20264)).
*   **ZK Execution Support:** Developers are debating the implementation of a Rust-based ZK executor for `vm-runner` and the use of mock provers in CI to manage costs while maintaining development velocity ([#18325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18325), [#21409](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21409)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by high-level architectural alignment between maintainers, particularly regarding the `op-geth` decoupling strategy. The decision to move ZK-related bond claiming issues to a separate repository indicates a tightening of project scope and better cross-repo coordination within the Optimism Collective. Furthermore, the resolution of numerous stale issues suggests a concerted effort by the maintainers to reduce technical debt and improve the contributor experience by clarifying the current roadmap.
