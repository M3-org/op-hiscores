# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Mar 29 - Apr 4, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused heavily on enhancing the stability of the Superchain CI/CD pipeline by resolving several long-standing flaky tests across the interop and bedrock suites. Significant progress was made in documenting mainnet task statuses for Arena-Z, specifically regarding proposer rotations and fee vault updates. While many intermittent failures were resolved, new challenges emerged in the fault proofs domain, specifically regarding Kona interop program transitions.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Mainnet Operations & Task Management
Efforts this week centered on maintaining accurate tracking of mainnet deployments and administrative tasks.
*   **Arena-Z Documentation:** Updated the status of critical mainnet tasks for Arena-Z, including proposer rotation and fee vault updates, ensuring the operational state is transparent and up to date ([#1391](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1391)).
*   **Task Execution:** Formally marked tasks 48 and 49 as executed within the repository's chore tracking ([#1392](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1392)).

### Interoperability & Testing Infrastructure
A significant portion of development time was dedicated to fixing "hangs" and flakiness in the interoperability testing suite.
*   **Interop Proofs:** Resolved a critical issue where the `TestProposer` in the interop/proofs suite would hang indefinitely, ensuring smoother local and CI execution ([#19673](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19673)).
*   **Supernode Stability:** Stabilized the `TestSupernodeInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` test, which had previously been identified as unreliable ([#19677](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19677)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
The team successfully cleared several technical hurdles related to test reliability:
*   **Acceptance Test Stabilization:** Resolved intermittent failures in acceptance tests involving database preservation, truncation, and Consensus Layer (CL) unsafe rewinding ([#19661](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19661)).
*   **CI Cleanup:** Closed an undiagnosable flake in the `contracts-bedrock-tests-upgrade` suite. Due to a lack of diagnostic output and no recent recurrences, the issue was shuttered to reduce noise ([#19577](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19577)).

### New & Active Issues
New challenges have surfaced regarding the Kona interop program:
*   **Fault Proof Flakes:** Two new issues were reported concerning flaky tests in interop fault proofs ([#19829](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19829), [#19828](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19828)). Initial investigations suggest the root cause is a missing L1 head check within the Kona no-op transition path, which will likely require a protocol-level fix in the transition logic.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The activity this week reflects a strong internal focus on "quality of life" improvements for contributors. By prioritizing the resolution of flaky tests and indefinite hangs in the test suite, the team is actively reducing the friction required for developers to verify their code. The systematic closure of undiagnosable issues suggests a disciplined approach to triage, focusing resources on actionable bugs while maintaining a clean issue backlog.
