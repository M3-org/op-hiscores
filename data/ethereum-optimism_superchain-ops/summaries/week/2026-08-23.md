# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Aug 23 - 29, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused on significant ownership transitions and infrastructure rehearsals to further decentralize the Superchain ecosystem. Key milestones included the successful handover of ownership for Metal, Mode, Zora, and Dust chains to Conduit, alongside the validation of multi-chain ownership transfer templates. The team also completed a comprehensive L2 deployment and withdrawal lifecycle test on the Plataberget testnet, demonstrating the robustness of the `op-deployer` and OPCM workflows. While progress was made on system configuration and task execution modules, the week also highlighted critical sequencer stability concerns and the need for better developer tooling regarding SuperchainERC20 compatibility.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Ownership and Governance Decentralization
A major operational focus was the transition of control for several Superchain members.
*   **Multi-Chain Ownership Transfers:** The `TransferOwners` and `TransferL2PAOFromL1` templates were enhanced to support ownership transfers across multiple chains within a single Safe transaction, significantly increasing operational efficiency ([#1519](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1519)).
*   **Conduit Handover:** Ownership of L1 ProxyAdmin, DisputeGameFactory, and L2 ProxyAdmin for the Metal, Mode, Zora, and Dust chains was successfully transferred to Conduit Safes on both Mainnet and Sepolia ([#1521](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1521)).
*   **Nested Upgrades:** A rehearsal for a nested upgrade (R3) was added, including updated configuration files and addresses to ensure smooth execution ([#1525](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1525)).

### Infrastructure & Task Execution
Improvements were made to how tasks are executed and how the network is configured.
*   **Unordered Execution:** New tasks were introduced for "nonceless" (hash-once) execution via an `UnorderedExecutionModule` ([#1529](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1529)).
*   **Network Transitions:** Preparation began for Unichain mainnet with the addition of PAO transition tasks ([#1527](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1527)).
*   **Template Cleanup:** Redundant templates and unused inputs, such as the `OPCMMigrateV800` and `cannonPrestate`, were removed to streamline the codebase ([#1532](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1532), [#1531](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1531)).

### Build & Simulation Optimization
*   **Foundry Efficiency:** The build process was optimized by removing redundant `forge build` steps during simulations, as Foundry handles necessary compilation automatically ([#423](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/423)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Supernode Stability:** Analysis of `op-supernode` cross-safe wedges ([#22325](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22325)) concluded that the issue was an isolated parent-hash mismatch rather than a systemic cross-safety bug.
*   **Plataberget Validation:** A successful test deployment of a new L2 chain (ID 78216) was completed, validating the full deposit-prove-finalize withdrawal lifecycle ([#22696](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22696)).
*   **Kona Genesis Leniency:** The team decided to maintain leniency for unknown `rollup.json` keys in `kona-genesis` to ensure backward compatibility with retired keys ([#22682](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22682)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sequencer Stability:** A critical new issue ([#22681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22681)) reports `kona-node` sequencers getting stuck in `forkchoiceUpdated` loops. Additionally, the `NetworkActor` in `kona-node` may stall during remote-signer calls, prompting discussions on overflow policies and latency fixes ([#22646](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22646)).
*   **Glamsterdam Defense:** Updates to Foundry v1.8.1 are required to address test failures related to the Amsterdam upgrade, including gas limit and `OutOfGas` reverts in various contract tests ([#22698](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22698)).
*   **Developer Experience Gap:** There is currently no supported method for developers to verify SuperchainERC20 compatibility, a gap identified as critical for the `SuperchainTokenBridge` ([#1020](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/1020)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw active maintenance regarding the project's footprint. A comprehensive audit of "starter" repositories and documentation links was conducted to prepare for an archive sweep of abandoned repos ([#1014](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/1014), [#1017](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/1017)). Contributors are currently coordinating the consolidation of multiple `superchain-starter` repositories into a single source to improve the onboarding experience for new developers ([#1015](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/1015)). Collaboration remains high between core maintainers and external partners like Conduit to facilitate network-level ownership changes.
