# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Aug 9 - 15, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week focused heavily on the stabilization and benchmarking of the **State Diff Migration (SDM)** on `sdm-net-0`, marking a significant milestone as the network reached a steady state with active fault proofs. The team successfully executed baseline benchmarks, demonstrating substantial gas savings (over 50%) with SDM enabled, while also identifying critical RPC edge cases for future fixes. Preparations for **OPSC Rehearsal 1** and a strategic shift toward **Lightnode deployment** in staging and production environments signal a concerted push toward interoperability readiness.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### SDM Rollout and Performance Benchmarking
The State Diff Migration (SDM) transitioned from initial rollout to active validation.
*   **Steady State Achievement**: `sdm-net-0` is now fully operational with SDM effective, restart-proof, and fault proofs actively proposing ([#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21273)).
*   **Benchmarking Results**: Comparative testing on `sdm-net-0` revealed that SDM reduced gas usage from 849M to 395M at 10 TPS. While builder and execution overhead remained negligible, a slight decrease in throughput at 30 TPS suggests a potential increase in confirmation latency ([#22344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22344)).
*   **Workload Simulation**: A continuous SDM-exercising workload was deployed using a standing daemon to spam StateBloat scenarios, ensuring every block carries same-slot transactions for ongoing testing ([#22343](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22343)).

### Infrastructure and Interoperability Prep
Significant groundwork was laid for upcoming network rehearsals and cross-chain features.
*   **OPSC Rehearsal 1**: New rehearsal configurations were integrated to facilitate CI runs for the upcoming OPSC event ([#1517](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1517)).
*   **Lightnode Strategy**: Initiated plans to re-deploy Lightnodes as the majority configuration in staging and production to validate them ahead of interop activation ([#22352](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22352), [#22354](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22354)).
*   **Multichain Tooling**: Added multichain support for `TransferOwners` and `TransferL2PAOFromL1` templates to streamline cross-chain ownership management ([#1519](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1519)).

### Node Configuration and Recovery
*   **op-reth Fixes**: Resolved a critical issue where missing hardfork override flags prevented superchain nodes from connecting via P2P when registry timestamps were absent ([#20224](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20224)).
*   **Invalidation Recovery**: Closed a loop where invalidation recovery would repeatedly reset during unsafe sync, improving node stability during EL convergence ([#21948](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21948)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Operator Management**: Finalized the SDM operator opt-in mechanism, ensuring it is armed at boot, and updated all relevant documentation ([#22346](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22346), [#22347](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22347)).
*   **Operational Runbooks**: The recovery runbook for invalid interop messages was completed and documented ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21161)).
*   **Ownership Transfers**: Formally marked the transfer of Swell Mainnet ownership to AltLayer as executed ([#1518](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1518)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Flaky P2P Tests**: A new investigation is underway for `TestP2PFull`, which has failed 6 times in 14 days. Failure modes include "no addresses" and "noise: message is too short," suggesting race conditions in the reconnect logic ([#22381](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22381)).
*   **RPC Failures**: Benchmarking revealed that `debug_traceBlockByNumber` fails on specific SDM blocks (0x7d), representing a blocker for full tool compatibility.
*   **Test Coverage**: Discussion on adding integration tests for invalid PostExec transactions in `kona` concluded that current component testing is sufficient, leading to the likely closure of the task to avoid redundant harness complexity ([#21344](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21344)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by high-intensity internal validation and cross-team coordination. The transition of Swell Mainnet ownership to AltLayer demonstrates successful operational handoffs between ecosystem partners. Technical discussions around SDM benchmarks and P2P flakiness show a strong emphasis on data-driven debugging, with contributors actively refining the `op-benchmark` tooling and Grafana monitoring suites to ensure transparent performance metrics for the broader Superchain community.
