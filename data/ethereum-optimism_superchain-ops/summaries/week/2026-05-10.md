# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (May 10 - 16, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused heavily on the V800 upgrade cycle and the expansion of the OPCM (Optimism Contracts Manager) capabilities. Significant progress was made in refining operational templates to reduce reliance on hardcoded values, specifically regarding Safe address configurations and SystemConfig ownership. While the team successfully executed critical tasks on Sepolia and Betanet, a substantial portion of engineering effort was dedicated to resolving persistent synchronization and reorg-related test flakiness to ensure protocol stability.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### V800 Upgrade & Migration Infrastructure
The team prioritized the V800 upgrade path, focusing on both permissioned and permissionless configurations.
*   **Migration Enhancements:** Introduced fallback JSON fixtures for devnets and added support for upgrading permissioned configurations to permissionless ones ([#1423](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1423), [#1418](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1418)).
*   **OPCM Refinements:** Addressed OPCM validation issues, including aligning migration ABIs and handling specific state diff behaviors ([#1424](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1424), [#1422](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1422)).
*   **New Tasks:** Added specific upgrade and migration tasks for `interop-jnt-v2` ([#1427](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1427), [#1428](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1428)).

### Operational Template Refactoring
Efforts were made to harden the templates used for network operations and governance.
*   **Configurable Safes:** Refactored the `SetBatcherAndOrSigner` template to require explicit Safe address configuration, removing previous hardcoded dependencies ([#1413](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1413)).
*   **Ownership & Guards:** Introduced a new template for SystemConfig ownership transfers ([#1406](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1406)) and implemented a guard in `OPCMUpgradeV800.sol` to prevent issues with specific Cannon game types ([#1421](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1421)).

### Network Task Execution
*   **Sepolia:** Successfully executed Task 75 involving the rotation of deputy pause keys ([#1419](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1419)).
*   **Betanet:** Initiated U19 betanet tasks ([#1420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1420)).
*   **Ethereum Mainnet:** Updated the status for ongoing tasks 050, 051, and 052 ([#1426](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1426)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Flaky Test Resolutions:** Investigations into `TestWithdrawal_Cannon` led to a draft fix involving precondition checks for prove transactions ([#19963](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19963)).
*   **Supernode Reporting:** Closed an investigation into `superroot_atTimestamp` reporting; the divergence was determined to be a test-setup artifact rather than a protocol bug ([#20657](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20657)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Sync & Reorg Flakiness:** A cluster of synchronization tests remains a high priority. `TestUnsafeGapFillAfterSafeReorg` ([#19884](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19884)) and `TestFollowL2_ReorgRecovery` ([#19967](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19967)) are being addressed via PR #20651. However, `TestELSyncSafeRetractedByOffset` ([#20334](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20334)) suggests a deeper timing issue.
*   **Interop Proofs Devnet:** Work continues on the Interop Proofs Devnet ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20489)), with the team defining "Done" as successful superdispute games, withdrawals, and flashblock compatibility.
*   **Withdrawal Proving:** An upstream issue in the `viem` library was identified regarding the use of timestamps instead of block numbers for proving withdrawals ([#18614](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18614)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week demonstrated strong cross-project coordination, particularly with upstream dependencies like `viem` to resolve withdrawal proving logic. Internal collaboration was focused on the "Definition of Done" for the Interop Proofs Devnet, involving coordination between the proofs team and infrastructure engineers to ensure flashblocks and conductor re-orgs are functioning correctly. Technical discussions also advanced regarding unsafe head executing message validation, with new implementation ideas proposed to improve `proxyd` filtering ([#20553](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20553)).
