# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Mar 22 - 28, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused heavily on refining the interoperability framework and addressing stability issues within the Superchain ecosystem. Significant progress was made on Interop Phase 3, specifically regarding the removal of chains from shared dispute games and the integration of Custom Gas Token (CGT) chains. Engineers also prioritized system reliability by investigating complex race conditions in the derivation pipeline and enhancing testing infrastructure for the `kona-interop` block hash oracle. The week was characterized by a transition from identifying flaky tests to uncovering and addressing potential underlying architectural bugs.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Interoperability & Proof Systems
The team advanced several workstreams related to cross-chain interoperability and the proofs system.
*   **Kona Interop Testing:** A new fixture-based integration test was introduced for `OracleInteropProvider::header_by_number` ([#19726](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/19726)). This approach utilizes real OP Mainnet state and storage trie proof data to validate EIP-2935 code paths without the overhead of full acceptance tests.
*   **Interop Phase 3:** Technical discussions clarified the scope for removing chains from shared dispute games ([#19692](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19692)), focusing on supporting these removals within the proofs system.
*   **CGT Integration:** Research continued into the integration of Custom Gas Token (CGT) chains into the interop set. Key considerations include the overlap between CGT and `ETHLiquidity` contracts and potential workarounds for enabling correct predeploys in the interop fork ([#19711](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19711), [#19461](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19461)).

### Operational Tooling & Documentation
Minor but essential updates were made to streamline developer workflows for arena-z tasks.
*   **Task Validation:** Updates to the README and validation files included new commands for signing tasks and general "nit" fixes ([#1389](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1389)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Flaky Test Resolution:** `TestSupernodeSameTimestampInvalidTransitive` ([#19570](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19570)) was closed after 43 consecutive successful runs, indicating the previous instability has been resolved.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Derivation Race Conditions:** Investigation into `TestCLELDivergence` ([#19681](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19681)) suggests a potential bug rather than a simple flake. The team identified a race condition where the derivation pipeline resets the `UnsafeL2` status after an `UnsafeUpdateEvent`, rendering intermediate states unobservable.
*   **Kona Deserialization Errors:** A critical issue involving a `Kona` guest panic during `ChainList.json` deserialization is under review ([#19654](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19654)). This appears linked to failures in reading the chain list during proof generation.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week saw deep technical coordination regarding the design of the Superchain's shared security model. Discussions on issues like [#19711](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19711) and [#19461](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19461) demonstrate active collaboration between protocol designers and engineers to reconcile the requirements of CGT chains with existing interop contracts. The use of fixture-based testing for `kona-proof-interop` reflects a community-wide effort to improve contributor experience by reducing CI wait times while maintaining high security standards.
