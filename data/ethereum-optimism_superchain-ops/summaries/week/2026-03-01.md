# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Mar 1 - 7, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused heavily on preparing the Superchain for upcoming network upgrades and refining the operational infrastructure for revenue sharing across multiple chains. A significant portion of the technical effort was dedicated to investigating the impacts of the Glamsterdam upgrade on gas pricing, particularly regarding precompile acceleration and fault proofs. Additionally, the team addressed critical documentation needs for the `L2ContractsManager` to prevent storage collisions and continued refining the synchronization logic within `op-node`.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
While no major pull requests were marked as completed during this specific window, significant progress was made through newly opened PRs aimed at scaling the Superchain's operational tasks.

### Revenue Share (Revshare) Infrastructure
The team is actively expanding the automated task management for revenue sharing across both testnets and mainnets.
*   **Sepolia Expansion:** Implementation of U19 revshare tasks for additional OP-stack chains on Sepolia ([#1380](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1380)).
*   **Mainnet Integration:** New tasks were initiated for Ink and Soneium to handle revenue sharing on Mainnet ([#1379](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1379)).

### Dispute Game & Template Management
Efforts to standardize and update the dispute game mechanisms are ongoing to ensure compatibility with the latest protocol versions.
*   **Template Updates:** A new PR was opened to update the `addGameType` and `setDisputeGame` templates, ensuring that chain deployments remain consistent with current security standards ([#1378](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1378)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
The week saw a high volume of investigative work and documentation planning to mitigate future technical debt and protocol risks.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Glamsterdam Gas Pricing Investigation:** A critical investigation is underway regarding how increased gas costs in the Glamsterdam upgrade affect precompile acceleration ([#19375](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19375)). The team is currently mapping out the necessary bumps to `requiredGas` to ensure L2 costs align with L1, specifically for large preimages.
*   **L2ContractsManager Safety:** A new issue was opened to document the "no-storage-variables" convention for `L2ContractsManager` ([#19391](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19391)). This is a preventative measure to avoid storage slot collisions with `ProxyAdmin`.
*   **op-node Sync Logic:** Discussions continue regarding a bug where `op-node` may incorrectly mark the Execution Layer (EL) sync as finalized ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/17631)). The community is debating whether this is exacerbated by light CL/interop and how it impacts snap sync.
*   **Fault Proof Defaults:** The transition to `CANNON_KONA` as the default respected game type ([#18640](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18640)) was moved back to "in progress" to accommodate upcoming changes in the core `optimism` repository.
*   **Rate Limiting:** Users reported 429 errors on the Flashblocks WS endpoint, indicating a need for revised rate-limiting configurations ([#19366](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19366)).
