# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Weekly Report (Jul 5 - 11, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, development focused heavily on network stability and protocol corrections following the Karst (U19) activation. The team successfully mitigated a critical gas limit discrepancy on OP Mainnet and resolved a high intrinsic gas bug on Optimism Sepolia via an `op-reth` update. Operational efforts also expanded to the broader Superchain, with significant progress on migrations for Ink Mainnet and gas limit adjustments for Soneium. Additionally, the team initiated a strategic review of testing infrastructure, specifically addressing flaky interop tests and evaluating the long-term utility of existing fuzzing strategies.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Network Operations & Gas Limit Mitigations
A primary focus this week was correcting L2 block gas limits that had drifted from their intended `SystemConfig` values following the Karst upgrade.
*   **OP Mainnet:** Implemented a critical mitigation to reset the L2 block gas limit, which was approximately 55 MGas above the stored value ([#1480](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1480)).
*   **Soneium Mainnet:** Opened a similar request for a Karst gas-limit reset on Soneium (060) to ensure network consistency ([#1489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1489)).
*   **General Tasks:** Executed various operational tasks and updates to system configurations ([#1488](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1488)).

### Superchain Expansion & System Configuration
The project continues to support the growth of the Superchain through migration and configuration refactoring.
*   **Ink Mainnet:** Initiated the migration process for Ink Mainnet ([#1490](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1490)).
*   **SystemConfig Refactor:** Opened a PR to remove `SystemConfig` batch inbox getter reads, streamlining how configuration data is accessed ([#1491](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1491)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Protocol Fixes:** The issue regarding high intrinsic gas on Optimism Sepolia after the Karst hard fork was resolved. The fix was integrated into the `op-reth/v2.3.3` release ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21457)).
*   **Infrastructure & Economics:** Closed issues related to Unichain Mainnet Replicas ([#21177](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21177)) and bond sizing models ([#21417](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21417)), as these are being tracked in larger, more centralized efforts within the Optimism repository.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Testing Stability:** A new investigation was launched into a flaky test, `TestSupernodeLightSequencerInteropInvalidMessageReplacement` ([#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21635)). A root cause analysis suggests the need to force resets on active sequencers during local-safe divergence.
*   **Fuzzing Strategy:** The team is debating whether to retire or re-enable current fuzzing jobs ([#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21636)). An audit suggested that current CI fuzz jobs provide low value due to a lack of committed corpora, leading to a recommendation to move toward more meaningful, nightly fuzzing tasks.
*   **Dependencies:** The update for `op-reth` dependencies (specifically `revm`) remains on hold pending a new upstream release ([#21524](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21524)).
