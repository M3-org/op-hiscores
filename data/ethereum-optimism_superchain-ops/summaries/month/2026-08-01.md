# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Monthly Report (August 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
August was characterized by significant operational transitions and infrastructure refinement within the Superchain ecosystem. The primary focus centered on executing ownership transfers for Swell Mainnet and Ink, marking a shift in governance and maintenance responsibilities to AltLayer and FUS. Alongside these operational milestones, the team made progress in decoupling `op-geth` and optimizing CI workflows, while actively troubleshooting persistent synchronization and interop testing issues. The month demonstrated a balanced effort between high-level ecosystem governance and the technical stabilization of the OP Stack.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Superchain Governance & Ownership Transfers
A major portion of the month's activity involved the formal transfer of administrative control for several Superchain participants as part of approved Maintenance Upgrade Proposals.
*   **Swell Mainnet:** Completed the transfer of L1 ownership to AltLayer ([#1511](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1511)) and the L2 ProxyAdmin to an aliased AltLayer Safe ([#1512](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1512)). These tasks involved comprehensive updates to configuration files and address registries.
*   **Ink Infrastructure:** Initiated the transfer of `SystemConfig` ownership from FOS to FUS ([#1515](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1515)) and updated task statuses for Ink Proposer Rotation and Fee Vault Recipient updates ([#1513](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1513)).

### Protocol & Tooling Refinement
The team continued to streamline the codebase and prepare for future upgrades through targeted chores and refactoring.
*   **Op-geth Decoupling:** Successfully completed the audit of fork tools, which involved deleting stale checkers and migrating others to `op-core` ([#21746](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21746)).
*   **Game Type Registration:** Opened a task to register RECA Standard game types, ensuring compatibility with evolving fault proof mechanisms ([#1514](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1514)).
*   **CI Optimization:** Resolved issues regarding CI makespan by adopting a strategy of merging smaller, individual PRs to improve pipeline efficiency ([#21186](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21186)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Execution Engine Stability:** The team addressed a reported lag in `op-reth` proofs history. The resolution determined that consistency should be managed at startup rather than through shutdown logic changes, as the proofs exex is designed to catch up naturally as the chain produces blocks ([#22089](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/22089)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Synchronization Blockers:** A critical issue persists where `op-node` fails to sync using `execution-layer` mode with an `op-reth` backend. Users reported frequent `Ecies` errors, though some nodes eventually established peers after several days of retries ([#20974](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20974)).
*   **Test Flakiness:** Two major integration tests are under heavy investigation. `TestInteropHappyTx` is failing due to sequencer event-loop starvation ([#21940](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21940)), and `peers_negotiate_eth_69` remains flaky; the team has instrumented CI to capture stack traces for the next occurrence ([#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20973)).
*   **Performance Benchmarking:** Ongoing work continues for SDM (Superchain Deployment Manager) performance benchmarking. The team is currently utilizing `op-benchmark` and rudimentary traffic-driving scripts to measure performance on the SSD devnet ([#20176](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20176), [#21273](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21273)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this month was highly technical, focusing on the intersection of protocol development and live operations. The coordination between the Optimism team and external partners like AltLayer for ownership transfers highlights the maturing governance processes of the Superchain. Furthermore, the active debugging of `op-reth` and interop issues shows a high level of engagement between maintainers and node operators, with contributors sharing logs and retry strategies to overcome synchronization hurdles. The introduction of "OPSC Rehearsal 1" ([#1516](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1516)) suggests a structured approach to onboarding or testing new operational procedures within the community.
