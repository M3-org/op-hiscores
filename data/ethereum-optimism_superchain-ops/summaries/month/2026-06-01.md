# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Monthly Report (June 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
June was characterized by intensive preparation for the **U19 protocol upgrade** and the resolution of critical synchronization bottlenecks within the Superchain stack. The team successfully executed several U19 betanet tasks on Sepolia and introduced comprehensive templates for permissioned upgrades and dispute game configurations. Significant engineering effort was dedicated to diagnosing complex deadlocks in `op-reth` and `op-supernode`, leading to a two-patch fix for Consensus Layer (CL) sync recovery stalls. Additionally, the project advanced its interoperability (interop) roadmap by finalizing the Interop Proofs Devnet and refining recovery strategies for invalid interop messages.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### U19 Upgrade & Betanet Execution
The project made substantial progress toward the U19 upgrade, focusing on both automation templates and live execution on testnets.
*   **Betanet Tasks:** Successfully executed U19 betanet tasks (086-089) on Sepolia, covering permissioned/permissionless settings and dispute game implementations ([#1444](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1444)).
*   **Template Standardization:** Introduced `OPCMUpgradeV700` templates and `SetDisputeGameArgs/Impl` mechanisms to streamline future permissioned upgrades and dispute game configurations ([#1448](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1448), [#1445](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1445)).
*   **Karst Rollout:** Implemented the permissionless rollout for `karst-u19-alpha` and corrected configurations for `karst-u19-beta-0` ([#1447](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1447)).

### Infrastructure Robustness & Tooling
Improvements were made to the core operational tools to handle edge cases in gas accounting and state verification.
*   **MultisigTask Enhancements:** Fixed a bug in `MultisigTask` to tolerate negative per-call gas refunds during simulations, preventing unnecessary failures ([#1443](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1443)). Added a config-driven storage-code whitelist to allow specific slots to exempt code presence checks ([#1450](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1450)).
*   **Testing Infrastructure:** Initiated development of `op-reth-test-engine`, utilizing an ephemeral database design to enable one-shot block execution and OP-semantics testing without persistent storage overhead ([#20415](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20415)).

### Interoperability (Interop) Progress
*   **Devnet Completion:** The Interop Proofs Devnet reached a milestone with the final withdrawal successfully proven on an anvil fork of Sepolia ([#20489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20489)).
*   **Failsafe & Recovery:** Resolved an issue where the `op-reth` transaction pool failed to resume after disabling the interop failsafe ([#21149](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21149)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Sync & Stability:** Resolved a critical issue where the follow-mode sequencer failed to re-anchor after a `forceReset` ([#21125](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21125)).
*   **Metrics & Performance:** Fixed high-cardinality metric exports in Kona to improve monitoring performance ([#19927](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19927)).
*   **Devstack:** Corrected a configuration error where the EL was incorrectly shared between the supernode and light node sequencer ([#21158](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21158)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **CL Sync Deadlocks:** A major investigation into `op-supernode/op-reth` stalling revealed a cyclic deadlock in reth's `StateTrieOverlayManager`. A two-patch fix involving eager computation before lock insertion is currently undergoing soak testing ([#21076](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21076)).
*   **Interop Recovery Runbooks:** Following the decision not to implement auto-recovery for `op-conductor` during interop reorgs, the team is now focused on developing manual runbooks to handle invalid interop messages ([#21161](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21161), [#20006](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20006)).
*   **Initialization Flaws:** A new critical issue was identified where `op-supernode` becomes permanently stuck if the Execution Layer (EL) is unreachable at startup ([#21136](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21136)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month saw deep technical collaboration between the Optimism and Paradigm teams (specifically regarding `reth` patches). Discussions around `op-revm` support highlighted a commitment to staying synchronized with upstream `revm` releases, particularly for Amsterdam hardfork support ([#21069](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21069)). The triage of CI flakes in `op-e2e-fuzz` demonstrated a proactive approach to developer experience, with contributors implementing better diagnostic buffering to identify resource-heavy hangs ([#21191](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21191)).
