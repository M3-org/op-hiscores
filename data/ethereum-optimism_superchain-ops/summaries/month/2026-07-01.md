# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Monthly Report (July 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
July was characterized by intensive network operations and protocol stabilization following the Karst hard fork. The team successfully mitigated a critical gas limit bug on OP Mainnet and resolved high intrinsic gas issues on Optimism Sepolia. Significant progress was made in streamlining the Superchain ecosystem through Ink mainnet migration tasks, Soneium gas-limit resets, and the unification of configurations between `op-reth` and `kona`. Efforts also focused on hardening the codebase by auditing fuzzing strategies and addressing persistent integration test flakes to improve CI/CD reliability.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Network Operations & Gas Limit Management
A primary focus this month was correcting gas limit discrepancies across several networks to ensure protocol stability.
*   **OP Mainnet Mitigation:** Implemented a critical fix to reset the L2 block gas limit on OP Mainnet (Karst/U19), correcting a 55 MGas drift from the `SystemConfig` value ([#1480](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1480)).
*   **Soneium & Ink Operations:** Executed a gas-limit reset for Soneium mainnet ([#1489](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1489)) and managed migration tasks for Ink mainnet, including a strategic rollback for specific eth/062-064 tasks ([#1490](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1490), [#1493](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1493)).
*   **Task Execution:** Formally marked the `055-gas-limit-op` task as executed ([#1494](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1494)).

### Protocol Infrastructure & Configuration
The team worked to reduce technical debt and improve the interoperability of core components.
*   **SystemConfig Refactoring:** Removed `SystemConfig` batch inbox getter reads and updated templates to tolerate the removal of `batchInbox` in `OPCMUpgradeV800` ([#1491](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1491), [#1492](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1492)).
*   **Dependency Management:** Bumped the `superchain-registry` submodule to resolve drift and ensure consistency across registry checks ([#1496](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1496)).
*   **Config Unification:** Successfully coupled the synchronization of `kona`, `op-reth`, and `op-node` configurations, closing a major milestone for configuration loading ([#20420](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20420)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Intrinsic Gas Fix:** Resolved a bug where `eth_estimateGas` returned "intrinsic gas too high" on OP Sepolia post-Karst; the fix was included in `op-reth/v2.3.3` ([#21457](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21457)).
*   **Economic Modeling:** Closed an issue regarding bond sizing models, deferring it to a broader scope within the main Optimism repository ([#21417](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21417)).
*   **Infrastructure:** Closed the Unichain Mainnet Replicas Supernode issue, handing off further details to the core team ([#21177](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21177)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Testing Stability:** Significant attention is being paid to a recurring `SIGSEGV` in `reth-optimism-node` integration tests ([#20973](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20973)) and a flaky interop test ([#21635](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21635)). Root causes involve complex race conditions during node teardown and sequencer reorgs.
*   **Pruning & Syncing:** Ongoing discussions regarding `op-reth` reveal that `--minimal` nodes cannot prune block bodies if they need to support EL sync, as L1-info deposit transactions must remain readable ([#21465](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21465)).
*   **Security:** An automated scan identified one high-severity and six low-severity vulnerabilities currently under review ([#1495](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/1495)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month showed strong internal coordination between the `op-reth` and `kona` maintainers to align configuration types. A strategic decision was reached regarding fuzzing: the team will retire low-value PR-time fuzz jobs in favor of deterministic seed tests and potential nightly jobs with persisted corpora ([#21636](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/21636)). Additionally, the team demonstrated proactive ecosystem management by pausing the Uniswap Joint Devnet 4 to better align with the Unichain migration into OPE, ensuring partnership efforts remain synchronized with technical shifts.
