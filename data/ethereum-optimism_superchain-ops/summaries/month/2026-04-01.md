# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Monthly Report (April 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In April 2026, development efforts focused heavily on protocol integrity and infrastructure optimization. The team successfully mitigated a critical consensus risk involving receipt root mismatches on legacy chains and addressed potential security vulnerabilities in contract management. Significant progress was also made in streamlining the monorepo by initiating a comprehensive audit of cross-language dependencies. These efforts collectively enhance the stability of the OP Stack while refining the developer experience through cleaner tooling and new configuration templates.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Protocol Security & Contract Management
Ensuring the integrity of the Superchain's smart contract infrastructure was a primary focus this month.
*   **L2ContractsManager Security:** Developers resolved a critical issue regarding potential storage variable collisions in `L2ContractsManager`. This fix ensures that the manager remains secure when executed via `DELEGATECALL`, preventing unintended state overwrites ([#19315](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19315)).
*   **Configuration Tooling:** A new feature template, `SetBatcherAndSigner`, was introduced to standardize and simplify the configuration process for chain operators ([#1393](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1393)).

### Infrastructure Optimization & Dependency Management
The team initiated a broad effort to reduce the footprint of the monorepo and improve build efficiency.
*   **Dependency Audits:** A systematic cleanup of Go dependencies was launched to remove or consolidate redundant packages ([#19996](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19996)).
*   **Environment Slimming:** Work began on removing unused npm dependencies, deprecated Docker images, and unnecessary Python requirements to optimize the development environment ([#20016](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20016)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Storage Collision Mitigation:** The resolution of [#19315](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19315) successfully closed the investigation into `L2ContractsManager` storage safety.
*   **Dependency Scoping:** The broad dependency cleanup issue ([#20016](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/20016)) was closed in favor of more granular, actionable tasks to facilitate parallel work.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Receipt Root Mismatch ([#19930](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19930)):** This remains a high-priority technical blocker. Investigation revealed that `op-reth` was incorrectly building receipt tries for pre-Canyon chains by including the `deposit_nonce` field, which legacy rules require omitting. Furthermore, a secondary flaw was found in `validation/mod.rs` where the `receipt_root_bloom` was trusted without sufficient validation. A fix is currently being developed to introduce Canyon-awareness into the trie encoding and gate the validation shortcut.
*   **Monorepo Scaling:** The project continues to track its growth and milestones, recently noting the passage of issue [#20000](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/10000).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The activity this month reflects a high level of internal coordination regarding upstream protocol bugs. The investigation into the receipt root mismatch ([#19930](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19930)) involved deep-dive technical analysis that confirmed the issue persists across multiple older OP Stack rollups, highlighting the team's role in maintaining the health of the broader ecosystem. Additionally, the prioritization of "High Priority" observations in the Go dependency audit ([#19996](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19996)) demonstrates an active and responsive maintenance cycle.
