# ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops Monthly Report (March 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
In March 2026, development activity focused heavily on operational readiness for the Superchain ecosystem, specifically regarding revenue sharing (revshare) implementations and network upgrade preparations. Significant effort was directed toward configuring tasks for the Ink and Soneium mainnets and Sepolia-based OP chains. The team also prioritized protocol stability by investigating gas pricing impacts from the Glamsterdam upgrade and addressing critical execution layer (EL) synchronization and memory management issues in `op-node` and `op-reth`.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Operational Task Readiness & Revshare
A major portion of the month's work involved setting up the infrastructure for upcoming operational rotations and revenue-sharing tasks across multiple chains.
*   **Revshare Implementations:** New tasks were initiated to handle U19 revshare for Sepolia additional OP chains ([#1380](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1380)) and Mainnet tasks for Ink and Soneium ([#1379](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1379)).
*   **Rotation Task Preparation:** The team established placeholder configurations for future "fus-rotation" and "fos-rotation" activities (tasks 043 through 047), including environment files and validation documentation ([#1371](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1371)).

### Protocol & Template Updates
Refinements were made to the templates governing dispute games and contract management to ensure consistency across the Superchain.
*   **Dispute Game Templates:** Updates were made to the `addGameType` and `setDisputeGame` templates to streamline how new game types are integrated ([#1378](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/pull/1378)).
*   **Fault Proof Evolution:** Work continued on transitioning the default respected game type to `CANNON_KONA` following Upgrade 18, though the task remains in progress pending further PR dependencies ([#18640](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/18640)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Test Suite Refinement:** The team closed a request for additional time control in batcher and sequencer acceptance tests, determining the current functionality is sufficient for existing needs ([#19009](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19009)).

### New & Active Issues
*   **Execution Layer Sync & Correctness:** A high-priority discussion persists regarding `op-node` marking targets as finalized prematurely during initial EL sync. This investigation explores how light client interoperability might exacerbate the issue and evaluates potential fixes to derivation logic ([#17631](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/17631)).
*   **Performance & Stability:** 
    *   **Glamsterdam Impact:** An investigation was launched into how increased gas costs from the Glamsterdam upgrade affect precompile acceleration and fault proof security ([#19375](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19375)).
    *   **op-reth Memory Leaks:** Developers are monitoring a database read transaction issue in `op-reth` that triggers memory leaks. While stable on `v1.10.2`, the team is tracking the upcoming `v1.11.0` upgrade for a permanent resolution ([#19212](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19212)).
*   **Documentation & Standards:** A new issue was opened to enforce a "no-storage-variables" convention for `L2ContractsManager` to prevent storage slot collisions with `ProxyAdmin` ([#19391](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19391)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The month saw active technical debate between core contributors (including Karlfloersch, trianglesphere, and sebastianst) regarding the nuances of snap sync and finalization. This collaboration is critical as the project moves toward more complex interoperability features. Additionally, the introduction of feature requests for `op-reth`—specifically interop transaction pool filtering and reorg eviction ([#19411](https://github.com/ethereum-optimism/superchain-ops/issues/19411))—indicates a growing focus on enhancing the developer experience and node performance for the broader Superchain ecosystem.
